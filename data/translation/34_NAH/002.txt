2:1	Behold, upon the mountains, the feet of him who brings good news, who publishes peace! Keep your feasts, O Judah; fulfill your vows, for never again shall the worthless pass through you; he is utterly cut off.
2:2	The scatterer has come up against you. Man the ramparts; watch the road; dress for battle; collect all your strength.
2:3	For Yahweh is restoring the majesty of Jacob as the majesty of Israel, for plunderers have plundered them and ruined their branches.
2:4	The shield of his mighty men is red; his soldiers are clothed in scarlet. The chariots come with flashing metal on the day he musters them; the cypress spears are brandished.
2:5	The chariots race madly through the streets; they rush to and fro through the squares; they gleam like torches; they dart like lightning.
2:6	He remembers his officers; they stumble as they go; they hasten to the wall; the siege tower is set up.
2:7	The river gates are opened; the palace melts away;
2:8	it is decreed: she is stripped; she is carried off, her slave girls lamenting, moaning like doves and beating their breasts.
2:9	Nineveh is like a pool whose waters run away. "Halt! Halt!" they cry, but none turns back.
2:10	Plunder the silver, plunder the gold! There is no end of the treasure or of the wealth of all precious things.
2:11	Desolate! Desolation and ruin! Hearts melt and knees tremble; anguish is in all loins; all faces grow pale!
2:12	Where is the lions' den, the feeding place of the young lions, where the lion and lioness went, where his cubs were, with none to disturb?
2:13	The lion tore enough for his cubs and strangled prey for his lionesses; he filled his caves with prey and his dens with torn flesh.
2:14	Behold, I am against you, declares Yahweh of Armies, and I will burn your chariots in smoke, and the sword shall devour your young lions. I will cut off your prey from the earth, and the voice of your messengers shall no longer be heard.
