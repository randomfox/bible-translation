1:1	An oracle concerning Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum of Elkosh.
1:2	Yahweh is a jealous and avenging God; Yahweh is avenging and wrathful; Yahweh takes vengeance on His adversaries and keeps wrath for His enemies.
1:3	Yahweh is slow to anger and great in power, and Yahweh will by no means clear the guilty. His way is in whirlwind and storm, and the clouds are the dust of His feet.
1:4	He rebukes the sea and makes it dry; He dries up all the rivers; Bashan and Carmel wither; the bloom of Lebanon withers.
1:5	The mountains quake before Him; the hills melt; the earth heaves before Him, the world and all who dwell in it.
1:6	Who can stand before His indignation? Who can endure the heat of His anger? His wrath is poured out like fire, and the rocks are broken into pieces by Him.
1:7	Yahweh is good, a stronghold in the day of trouble; He knows those who take refuge in Him.
1:8	But with an overflowing flood He will make a complete end of her place, and will pursue His enemies into darkness.
1:9	What do you plot against Yahweh? He will make a complete end; trouble will not rise up a second time.
1:10	For they are like entangled thorns, like drunkards as they drink; they are consumed like stubble fully dried.
1:11	From you came one who plotted evil against Yahweh, a worthless counselor.
1:12	Thus says Yahweh, "Though they are at full strength and many, they will be cut down and pass away. Though I have afflicted you, I will afflict you no more.
1:13	And now I will break his yoke from off you and will burst your bonds apart."
1:14	Yahweh has given commandment about you: "No more shall your name be perpetuated; from the house of your gods I will cut off the carved image and the metal image. I will make your grave, for you are vile."
