18:1	After these things I saw another angel coming down out of heaven, having great authority, and the earth was lit up by his glory.
18:2	And he cried out with a mighty voice, saying, "Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great! She has become a dwelling place of demons, a haunt of every unclean spirit, a haunt of every unclean bird, and a haunt of every unclean and hated beast.
18:3	For all the nations have fallen from the wine of the passion of her sexual immorality, and the kings of the earth committed sexual immorality with her, and the merchants of the earth grew rich from the power of her luxury."
18:4	Then I heard another voice from heaven saying, "Come out of her, My people, so that you do not take part in her sins, and so that you do not receive any of her plagues;
18:5	for her sins are heaped up as high as heaven, and God has remembered her wrongdoings.
18:6	Pay her back as she herself has paid out, and repay her double according to her deeds; in the cup she mixed, mix her a double portion.
18:7	As much as she glorified herself and lived in luxury, give her that much torment and grief. For in her heart she says, 'I sit as a queen; I am no widow, and I will never see grief.'
18:8	Therefore in a single day her plagues will come, death and grief and famine, and she will be burned up with fire; for mighty is the Lord God Who has judged her.
18:9	And the kings of the earth who committed sexual immorality and lived in luxury with her will weep and wail over her when they see the smoke of her burning,
18:10	standing far off for fear of her torment, saying, 'Woe, woe, the great city, Babylon, the mighty city! For in a single hour your judgment has come.'
18:11	And the merchants of the earth weep and mourn over her, because no one buys their cargo anymore,
18:12	cargo of gold and silver and precious stones and pearls, of fine linen and purple, of silk and scarlet, every kind of citron wood, every article of ivory, every article of very costly wood and bronze and iron and marble,
18:13	cinnamon and spice, incense, perfumed ointment and frankincense, wine and olive oil, fine flour and wheat, cattle and sheep, horses and carriages, and bodies, that is, human souls.
18:14	'The fruit your soul craved has gone from you, and all your luxuries and your splendor have perished from you, and people will never find them again.'
18:15	The merchants of these goods, who grew rich from her, will stand far off for fear of her torment, weeping and mourning,
18:16	saying, 'Woe, woe, the great city that was clothed in fine linen and purple and scarlet, and adorned with gold and precious stones and pearls!
18:17	For in a single hour such great wealth has been laid waste.' And every ship's captain and everyone who sails anywhere, and sailors, and all who make their living from the sea, stood far off
18:18	and cried out as they saw the smoke of her burning, saying, 'What city was like the great city?'
18:19	And they threw dust on their heads and cried out, weeping and mourning, saying, 'Woe, woe, the great city, where all who had ships at sea grew rich from her costly abundance! For in a single hour she has been laid waste.'
18:20	Rejoice over her, O heaven, and you saints and apostles and prophets, for God has pronounced judgment for you against her.
18:21	Then a mighty angel picked up a stone like a great millstone and threw it into the sea, saying, "With such violence will Babylon the great city be thrown down, and will never be found again.
18:22	And the sound of harpists and musicians, of flute players and trumpeters, will never be heard in you again; and no craftsman of any craft will ever be found in you again; and the sound of the millstone will never be heard in you again;
18:23	and the light of a lamp will never shine in you again; and the voice of bridegroom and bride will never be heard in you again; for your merchants were the great ones of the earth, for all the nations were deceived by your sorcery.
18:24	And in her was found the blood of prophets and of saints, and of all who have been slaughtered on the earth."
