7:1	When the king was settled in his house and Yahweh had given him rest from all his enemies around him,
7:2	the king said to Nathan the prophet, "See now, I am living in a house of cedar, but the ark of God stays inside a tent curtain."
7:3	Nathan said to the king, "Go, do all that is in your heart, for Yahweh is with you."
7:4	But that same night the word of Yahweh came to Nathan:
7:5	"Go and tell my servant David, 'Thus says Yahweh: Are you the one to build me a house to live in?
7:6	For I have not lived in a house from the day I brought the Israelites up out of Egypt to this day, but I have been moving about in a tent as my dwelling.
7:7	Wherever I have moved about among all the Israelites, did I ever say a word to any of the tribes of Israel whom I commanded to shepherd my people Israel, saying, "Why have you not built me a house of cedar?"'
7:8	Now therefore, thus you shall say to my servant David, 'Thus says Yahweh of Armies: I took you from the pasture, from following the flock, to be ruler over my people Israel.
7:9	I have been with you wherever you went and have cut off all your enemies before you. And I will make for you a great name, like the name of the great ones on the earth.
7:10	I will appoint a place for my people Israel and plant them, so that they may dwell in their own place and be disturbed no more. Wicked men shall no longer oppress them as before,
7:11	as from the day I appointed judges over my people Israel. And I will give you rest from all your enemies. Moreover, Yahweh declares to you that Yahweh will make you a house.
7:12	When your days are complete and you lie down with your fathers, I will raise up your offspring after you, who will come from your own body, and I will establish his kingdom.
7:13	He shall build a house for my name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever.
7:14	I will be a father to him, and he shall be a son to me. When he does wrong, I will discipline him with the rod of men and with the blows of human beings,
7:15	but my steadfast love will not depart from him, as I took it away from Saul, whom I removed from before you.
7:16	Your house and your kingdom shall be made secure forever before you; your throne shall be established forever.'"
7:17	In accordance with all these words and all this vision, so Nathan spoke to David.
7:18	Then King David went in and sat before Yahweh and said, "Who am I, Lord Yahweh, and what is my house, that you have brought me this far?
7:19	And yet this was a small thing in your eyes, Lord Yahweh; you have also spoken of your servant's house for a long time to come. And this is instruction for mankind, Lord Yahweh!
7:20	What more can David say to you? For you know your servant, Lord Yahweh.
7:21	For the sake of your word and according to your own heart you have done all this great thing, to make your servant know it.
7:22	Therefore you are great, Lord Yahweh, for there is no one like you, and there is no God besides you, according to all that we have heard with our ears.
7:23	And who is like your people, like Israel, the one nation on earth whom God went to redeem for himself as a people, to make a name for himself, and to do for you great and awesome things for your land, before your people whom you redeemed for yourself from Egypt, from nations and their gods?
7:24	You established your people Israel for yourself as your people forever, and you, Yahweh, became their God.
7:25	And now, Yahweh God, confirm forever the word that you have spoken concerning your servant and concerning his house, and do as you have spoken.
7:26	May your name be great forever, so that people say, 'Yahweh of Armies is God over Israel,' and may the house of your servant David be established before you.
7:27	For you, Yahweh of Armies, the God of Israel, have revealed this to your servant, saying, 'I will build you a house.' That is why your servant has found courage to pray this prayer to you.
7:28	And now, Lord Yahweh, you are God, and your words are true, and you have promised this good thing to your servant.
7:29	So now may it please you to bless the house of your servant, so that it may continue forever before you; for you, Lord Yahweh, have spoken, and with your blessing may the house of your servant be blessed forever."
