16:1	When David had gone a little past the summit, there was Ziba, Mephibosheth's servant, coming to meet him with a pair of saddled donkeys, and on them two hundred loaves of bread, a hundred clusters of raisins, a hundred summer fruits, and a skin of wine.
16:2	The king said to Ziba, "What do you mean by these?" Ziba said, "The donkeys are for the king's household to ride, the bread and summer fruit are for the young men to eat, and the wine is for those who grow faint in the wilderness to drink."
16:3	The king said, "And where is your master's son?" Ziba said to the king, "He is staying in Jerusalem, for he said, 'Today the house of Israel will give me back my father's kingdom.'"
16:4	The king said to Ziba, "Look, everything that belonged to Mephibosheth is yours." Ziba said, "I bow down; may I find favor in your sight, my lord the king."
16:5	When King David came to Bahurim, a man of the clan of the house of Saul came out from there; his name was Shimei son of Gera, and he kept coming out and cursing as he came.
16:6	He threw stones at David and at all the servants of King David, though all the people and all the warriors were on his right and on his left.
16:7	This is what Shimei said as he cursed: "Get out, get out, you man of blood, you worthless man!
16:8	Yahweh has brought back on you all the blood of the house of Saul, in whose place you have reigned, and Yahweh has given the kingdom into the hand of your son Absalom. Here you are in your own disaster, because you are a man of blood!"
16:9	Abishai son of Zeruiah said to the king, "Why should this dead dog curse my lord the king? Let me go over and take off his head."
16:10	But the king said, "What have I to do with you, sons of Zeruiah? If he is cursing because Yahweh has said to him, 'Curse David,' who then can say, 'Why have you done so?'"
16:11	And David said to Abishai and to all his servants, "Look, my own son, who came out of my body, is seeking my life; how much more now this Benjaminite! Leave him alone and let him curse, for Yahweh has told him to.
16:12	Perhaps Yahweh will look on my affliction, and Yahweh will repay me with good in place of his cursing today."
16:13	So David and his men went along the road, while Shimei went along the hillside opposite him, cursing as he went, throwing stones at him and flinging dust.
16:14	The king and all the people who were with him arrived exhausted, and he refreshed himself there.
16:15	Meanwhile Absalom and all the people, the men of Israel, came to Jerusalem, and Ahithophel with him.
16:16	When Hushai the Archite, David's friend, came to Absalom, Hushai said to Absalom, "Long live the king! Long live the king!"
16:17	Absalom said to Hushai, "Is this your loyalty to your friend? Why did you not go with your friend?"
16:18	Hushai said to Absalom, "No; the one whom Yahweh and this people and all the men of Israel have chosen, his I will be, and with him I will stay.
16:19	And besides, whom should I serve? Should it not be his son? As I served in your father's presence, so I will be in yours."
16:20	Absalom said to Ahithophel, "Give your counsel: what shall we do?"
16:21	Ahithophel said to Absalom, "Go in to your father's concubines, whom he left to keep the house, and all Israel will hear that you have made yourself odious to your father, and the hands of all who are with you will be strengthened."
16:22	So they pitched the tent for Absalom on the roof, and Absalom went in to his father's concubines in the sight of all Israel.
16:23	Now the counsel that Ahithophel gave in those days was as if one consulted the word of God; so was all the counsel of Ahithophel regarded, both by David and by Absalom.
