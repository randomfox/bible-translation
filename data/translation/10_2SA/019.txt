19:1	The king was shaken. He went up to the room over the gate and wept, and as he went he said, "My son Absalom, my son, my son Absalom! If only I had died instead of you, Absalom, my son, my son!"
19:2	It was reported to Joab, "The king is weeping and mourning for Absalom."
19:3	So the victory that day was turned into mourning for all the troops, for the troops heard that day, "The king is grieving for his son."
19:4	The troops stole into the city that day as troops steal in who are ashamed when they have fled in battle.
19:5	The king covered his face, and the king cried out with a loud voice, "My son Absalom, Absalom, my son, my son!"
19:6	Then Joab came into the house to the king and said, "Today you have covered with shame the faces of all your servants, who today have saved your life and the lives of your sons and daughters, the lives of your wives and the lives of your concubines,
19:7	by loving those who hate you and hating those who love you. For you have made it clear today that commanders and servants are nothing to you; for I know today that if Absalom were alive and all of us were dead today, then you would be pleased.
19:8	Now get up, go out, and speak kindly to your servants, for I swear by Yahweh that if you do not go out, not a man will stay with you tonight; and this will be worse for you than all the trouble that has come upon you from your youth until now."
19:9	So the king got up and sat in the gate. When all the troops were told, "The king is sitting in the gate," all the troops came before the king. Now Israel had fled, each to his own tent.
19:10	All the people throughout all the tribes of Israel were arguing, saying, "The king delivered us from the hand of our enemies and rescued us from the hand of the Philistines, and now he has fled the land because of Absalom.
19:11	But Absalom, whom we anointed over us, has died in battle. So now why are you saying nothing about bringing the king back?"
19:12	King David sent word to Zadok and Abiathar the priests: "Say to the elders of Judah, 'Why should you be the last to bring the king back to his house, when the talk of all Israel has reached the king at his house?
19:13	You are my brothers, you are my bone and my flesh; why then should you be the last to bring the king back?'
19:14	And say to Amasa, 'Are you not my bone and my flesh? May God do so to me and more also if you are not commander of the army before me from now on in place of Joab.'"
19:15	So he swayed the hearts of all the men of Judah as one man, and they sent word to the king, "Come back, you and all your servants."
19:16	So the king came back and reached the Jordan, and Judah came to Gilgal to go and meet the king and to bring the king over the Jordan.
19:17	Shimei son of Gera, the Benjaminite from Bahurim, hurried to come down with the men of Judah to meet King David.
19:18	With him were a thousand men from Benjamin, and Ziba the servant of the house of Saul, with his fifteen sons and his twenty servants; they rushed down to the Jordan ahead of the king.
19:19	The ferry kept crossing to bring over the king's household and to do whatever he wished. Shimei son of Gera fell down before the king as he was about to cross the Jordan
19:20	and said to the king, "Let not my lord hold me guilty, and do not remember the wrong your servant did on the day my lord the king left Jerusalem; may the king not take it to heart.
19:21	For your servant knows that I have sinned; so here I have come today, the first of all the house of Joseph to come down to meet my lord the king."
19:22	Abishai son of Zeruiah answered, "Should not Shimei be put to death for this, because he cursed Yahweh's anointed?"
19:23	But David said, "What have I to do with you, sons of Zeruiah, that you should today become an adversary to me? Should anyone be put to death in Israel today? For do I not know that today I am king over Israel?"
19:24	The king said to Shimei, "You shall not die." And the king swore it to him.
19:25	Mephibosheth the son of Saul came down to meet the king. He had not cared for his feet, nor trimmed his mustache, nor washed his clothes, from the day the king left until the day he came back safely.
19:26	When he came to Jerusalem to meet the king, the king said to him, "Why did you not go with me, Mephibosheth?"
19:27	He said, "My lord the king, my servant deceived me. For your servant said, 'I will saddle a donkey for myself and ride on it and go with the king,' because your servant is lame.
19:28	And he has slandered your servant to my lord the king. But my lord the king is like the angel of God; so do what seems good to you.
19:29	For all my father's house were nothing but men doomed to death before my lord the king, yet you set your servant among those who eat at your table. What further right do I have, then, to cry out any more to the king?"
19:30	The king said to him, "Why speak any more of your affairs? I have decided: you and Ziba shall divide the land."
19:31	Mephibosheth said to the king, "Let him even take it all, since my lord the king has come safely home."
19:32	Barzillai the Gileadite had come down from Rogelim, and he went on with the king to the Jordan to escort him across the Jordan.
19:33	Barzillai was very old, eighty years of age. He had provided the king with food while he stayed at Mahanaim, for he was a very wealthy man.
19:34	The king said to Barzillai, "Come over with me, and I will provide for you with me in Jerusalem."
19:35	But Barzillai said to the king, "How many years of life do I have left, that I should go up with the king to Jerusalem?
19:36	I am eighty years old today. Can I tell good from bad? Can your servant taste what I eat or what I drink? Can I still hear the voice of men and women singing? Why then should your servant be an added burden to my lord the king?
19:37	Your servant will go just a little way across the Jordan with the king. Why should the king repay me with such a reward?
19:38	Please let your servant return, that I may die in my own city near the grave of my father and my mother. But here is your servant Chimham; let him go over with my lord the king, and do for him whatever seems good to you."
19:39	The king said, "Chimham shall go over with me, and I will do for him whatever seems good to you, and whatever you ask of me I will do for you."
19:40	Then all the people crossed the Jordan, and the king crossed. The king kissed Barzillai and blessed him, and he returned to his own place.
19:41	The king went on to Gilgal, and Chimham went on with him. All the people of Judah, and also half the people of Israel, brought the king over.
19:42	Then all the men of Israel came to the king and said to the king, "Why have our brothers the men of Judah stolen you away and brought the king and his household over the Jordan, and all David's men with him?"
19:43	All the men of Judah answered the men of Israel, "Because the king is our close relative. Why then are you angry over this matter? Have we eaten anything at the king's expense? Or has any gift been given to us?"
19:44	The men of Israel answered the men of Judah, "We have ten shares in the king, and in David also we have more than you. Why then did you treat us with contempt? Were we not the first to speak of bringing back our king?" But the words of the men of Judah were harsher than the words of the men of Israel.
