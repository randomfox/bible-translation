2:1	After this David inquired of Yahweh, "Shall I go up into one of the towns of Judah?" Yahweh said to him, "Go up." David said, "Where shall I go up?" He said, "To Hebron."
2:2	So David went up there, along with his two wives, Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail, the widow of Nabal of Carmel.
2:3	David also brought up his men who were with him, each with his household, and they settled in the towns of Hebron.
2:4	Then the men of Judah came, and there they anointed David king over the house of Judah. When they told David, "It was the men of Jabesh-gilead who buried Saul,"
2:5	David sent messengers to the men of Jabesh-gilead and said to them, "May you be blessed by Yahweh, because you showed this loyalty to your lord Saul and buried him.
2:6	Now may Yahweh show you steadfast love and faithfulness. I too will show you this goodness, because you have done this thing.
2:7	So now let your hands be strong, and be valiant, for your lord Saul is dead, and the house of Judah has anointed me king over them."
2:8	But Abner son of Ner, commander of Saul's army, had taken Ish-bosheth son of Saul and brought him over to Mahanaim.
2:9	He made him king over Gilead, the Ashurites, Jezreel, Ephraim, Benjamin, and over all Israel.
2:10	Ish-bosheth son of Saul was forty years old when he became king over Israel, and he reigned two years. But the house of Judah followed David.
2:11	The length of time David was king in Hebron over the house of Judah was seven years and six months.
2:12	Abner son of Ner and the servants of Ish-bosheth son of Saul went out from Mahanaim to Gibeon.
2:13	Joab son of Zeruiah and the servants of David also went out, and they met them at the pool of Gibeon. They sat down, one group on one side of the pool and the other group on the other side of the pool.
2:14	Abner said to Joab, "Let the young men get up and compete before us." Joab said, "Let them get up."
2:15	So they got up and went over by number: twelve for Benjamin and Ish-bosheth son of Saul, and twelve of David's servants.
2:16	Each man grabbed his opponent by the head and thrust his sword into his opponent's side, and they fell down together. So that place, which is in Gibeon, was called Helkath-hazzurim.
2:17	The battle was very fierce that day, and Abner and the men of Israel were beaten before the servants of David.
2:18	The three sons of Zeruiah were there: Joab, Abishai, and Asahel. Asahel was as swift of foot as a gazelle in the open field.
2:19	Asahel chased Abner, and in going he turned neither to the right nor to the left from following Abner.
2:20	Abner looked behind him and said, "Is that you, Asahel?" He said, "It is."
2:21	Abner said to him, "Turn aside to your right or to your left, seize one of the young men, and take his gear for yourself." But Asahel would not turn aside from following him.
2:22	Abner said again to Asahel, "Turn aside from following me. Why should I strike you to the ground? How then could I face your brother Joab?"
2:23	But he refused to turn aside, so Abner struck him in the belly with the butt end of his spear, and the spear came out through his back. He fell there and died on the spot. And everyone who came to the place where Asahel had fallen and died stood still.
2:24	But Joab and Abishai chased Abner. As the sun was setting, they came to the hill of Ammah, which faces Giah on the way to the wilderness of Gibeon.
2:25	The Benjaminites rallied behind Abner and formed a single band, and they took their stand on the top of a hill.
2:26	Then Abner called out to Joab, "Must the sword devour forever? Do you not know that it will be bitter in the end? How long will it be before you tell the troops to turn back from pursuing their brothers?"
2:27	Joab said, "As God lives, if you had not spoken, the troops would not have given up pursuing their brothers until morning."
2:28	Then Joab blew the ram's horn, and all the troops stopped; they no longer pursued Israel, and they fought no more.
2:29	Abner and his men went through the Arabah all that night. They crossed the Jordan, went through the whole Bithron, and came to Mahanaim.
2:30	Joab came back from pursuing Abner and gathered all the troops. Of David's servants, nineteen men were missing, besides Asahel.
2:31	But David's servants had struck down of Benjamin and of Abner's men three hundred and sixty men, who died.
2:32	They took up Asahel and buried him in his father's tomb, which is in Bethlehem. Then Joab and his men marched all night, and day broke on them at Hebron.
