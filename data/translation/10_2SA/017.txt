17:1	Ahithophel said to Absalom, "Let me choose twelve thousand men, and I will set out and pursue David tonight.
17:2	I will come upon him while he is weary and his hands are slack, and I will throw him into a panic; all the people who are with him will flee, and I will strike down the king alone.
17:3	Then I will bring all the people back to you. The return of everyone depends on the man you are seeking; all the people will be at peace."
17:4	The proposal seemed right to Absalom and to all the elders of Israel.
17:5	But Absalom said, "Call Hushai the Archite too, and let us hear what he also has to say."
17:6	When Hushai came to Absalom, Absalom said to him, "This is what Ahithophel has said. Shall we do as he says? If not, you speak."
17:7	Hushai said to Absalom, "This time the counsel Ahithophel has given is not good."
17:8	Hushai went on, "You know your father and his men, that they are warriors and that they are enraged, like a bear robbed of her cubs in the field. Your father is a man of war; he will not spend the night with the troops.
17:9	Even now he is hidden in one of the pits or in some other place. And when some of the troops fall at the first attack, whoever hears it will say, 'There has been a slaughter among the people who follow Absalom.'
17:10	Then even the valiant man, whose heart is like the heart of a lion, will utterly melt with fear, for all Israel knows that your father is a warrior and that those who are with him are valiant men.
17:11	So my counsel is this: let all Israel be gathered to you, from Dan to Beersheba, as numerous as the sand by the sea, and you yourself go into battle.
17:12	Then we will come upon him in whatever place he is found, and we will light on him as the dew falls on the ground; not one will be left of him or of all the men who are with him.
17:13	And if he withdraws into a city, all Israel will bring ropes to that city, and we will drag it down into the valley until not even a pebble is found there."
17:14	Absalom and all the men of Israel said, "The counsel of Hushai the Archite is better than the counsel of Ahithophel." For Yahweh had ordained that the good counsel of Ahithophel be thwarted, so that Yahweh might bring disaster upon Absalom.
17:15	Then Hushai said to Zadok and Abiathar the priests, "Ahithophel counseled Absalom and the elders of Israel thus and so, and I have counseled thus and so.
17:16	Now send quickly and tell David, 'Do not spend the night on the steppes of the wilderness, but be sure to cross over, or the king and all the people with him will be swallowed up.'"
17:17	Jonathan and Ahimaaz were waiting at En-rogel. A servant girl would go and tell them, and they would go and tell King David, for they could not risk being seen entering the city.
17:18	But a young man saw them and told Absalom. So the two of them left quickly and came to the house of a man in Bahurim who had a well in his courtyard, and they went down into it.
17:19	The woman took a covering, spread it over the mouth of the well, and scattered grain on it, so that nothing was known.
17:20	When Absalom's servants came to the woman at the house, they said, "Where are Ahimaaz and Jonathan?" The woman said to them, "They have crossed the stream of water." They searched but did not find them, and they returned to Jerusalem.
17:21	After they had gone, the men came up out of the well and went and told King David. They said to David, "Get up and cross the water quickly, for this is how Ahithophel has counseled against you."
17:22	So David and all the people who were with him set out and crossed the Jordan. By daybreak not one was missing who had not crossed the Jordan.
17:23	When Ahithophel saw that his counsel was not followed, he saddled his donkey and set out and went home to his own city. He gave instructions to his household and hanged himself; he died and was buried in his father's tomb.
17:24	David came to Mahanaim, while Absalom crossed the Jordan, he and all the men of Israel with him.
17:25	Absalom had set Amasa over the army in place of Joab. Amasa was the son of a man named Ithra the Israelite, who had gone in to Abigal daughter of Nahash, sister of Zeruiah, Joab's mother.
17:26	And Israel and Absalom encamped in the land of Gilead.
17:27	When David came to Mahanaim, Shobi son of Nahash from Rabbah of the Ammonites, Machir son of Ammiel from Lo-debar, and Barzillai the Gileadite from Rogelim
17:28	brought bedding, basins, and earthen vessels, wheat, barley, flour, roasted grain, beans, lentils, and roasted seeds,
17:29	honey, curds, sheep, and cheese from the herd for David and the people with him to eat, for they said, "The people are hungry and weary and thirsty in the wilderness."
