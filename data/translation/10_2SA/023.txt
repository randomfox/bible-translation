23:1	These are the last words of David: The oracle of David son of Jesse, the oracle of the man who was raised on high, the anointed of the God of Jacob, the delight of Israel's songs:
23:2	"The Spirit of Yahweh speaks through me; His word is on my tongue.
23:3	The God of Israel has spoken; the Rock of Israel has said to me: One who rules over people justly, who rules in the fear of God,
23:4	is like the morning light when the sun rises, a morning without clouds, like the grass that springs from the earth through sunshine after rain.
23:5	Is not my house like this with God? For He has made with me an everlasting covenant, ordered in all things and secured. Will He not cause all my salvation and all my desire to spring up?
23:6	But the worthless are all like thorns that are thrown away, for they cannot be taken in hand;
23:7	the man who touches them arms himself with iron and the shaft of a spear, and they are utterly burned up with fire on the spot."
23:8	These are the names of the warriors whom David had: Josheb-basshebeth, a Tahchemonite, chief of the officers; he was Adino the Eznite, who fought against eight hundred slain at one time.
23:9	Next to him was Eleazar son of Dodo son of Ahohi, one of the three warriors with David when they defied the Philistines who had gathered there for battle, while the men of Israel withdrew.
23:10	He stood his ground and struck down the Philistines until his hand grew weary and his hand stuck to the sword. Yahweh brought about a great victory that day, and the troops came back after him only to strip the dead.
23:11	Next to him was Shammah son of Agee the Hararite. The Philistines gathered in a troop where there was a plot of ground full of lentils, and the troops fled from the Philistines.
23:12	But he took his stand in the middle of the plot, defended it, and struck down the Philistines; and Yahweh brought about a great victory.
23:13	Three of the thirty chiefs went down and came at harvest time to David at the cave of Adullam, while a troop of Philistines was encamped in the Valley of Rephaim.
23:14	David was then in the stronghold, and the garrison of the Philistines was then at Bethlehem.
23:15	David had a craving and said, "If only someone would give me water to drink from the well of Bethlehem that is by the gate!"
23:16	So the three warriors broke through the camp of the Philistines, drew water from the well of Bethlehem that was by the gate, and carried it and brought it to David. But he would not drink it; he poured it out to Yahweh
23:17	and said, "Far be it from me, Yahweh, that I should do this! Is this not the blood of the men who went at the risk of their lives?" So he would not drink it. These things the three warriors did.
23:18	Abishai, the brother of Joab son of Zeruiah, was chief of the three. He wielded his spear against three hundred and killed them, and he won a name beside the three.
23:19	He was the most honored of the three and became their commander, but he did not attain to the three.
23:20	Benaiah son of Jehoiada was the son of a valiant man from Kabzeel, a doer of great deeds. He struck down the two Ariels of Moab. He also went down and killed a lion in a pit on a snowy day.
23:21	And he struck down an Egyptian, a man of impressive appearance. The Egyptian had a spear in his hand, but Benaiah went down to him with a staff, snatched the spear from the Egyptian's hand, and killed him with his own spear.
23:22	These things Benaiah son of Jehoiada did, and he won a name beside the three warriors.
23:23	He was honored above the thirty, but he did not attain to the three. And David set him over his bodyguard.
23:24	Asahel the brother of Joab was among the thirty; Elhanan son of Dodo of Bethlehem;
23:25	Shammah the Harodite; Elika the Harodite;
23:26	Helez the Paltite; Ira son of Ikkesh the Tekoite;
23:27	Abiezer the Anathothite; Mebunnai the Hushathite;
23:28	Zalmon the Ahohite; Maharai the Netophathite;
23:29	Heleb son of Baanah the Netophathite; Ittai son of Ribai from Gibeah of the Benjaminites;
23:30	Benaiah the Pirathonite; Hiddai of the wadis of Gaash;
23:31	Abi-albon the Arbathite; Azmaveth the Barhumite;
23:32	Eliahba the Shaalbonite; the sons of Jashen; Jonathan;
23:33	Shammah the Hararite; Ahiam son of Sharar the Hararite;
23:34	Eliphelet son of Ahasbai son of the Maacathite; Eliam son of Ahithophel the Gilonite;
23:35	Hezrai the Carmelite; Paarai the Arbite;
23:36	Igal son of Nathan of Zobah; Bani the Gadite;
23:37	Zelek the Ammonite; Naharai the Beerothite, armor-bearer of Joab son of Zeruiah;
23:38	Ira the Ithrite; Gareb the Ithrite;
23:39	Uriah the Hittite: thirty-seven in all.
