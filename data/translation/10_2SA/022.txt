22:1	David spoke to Yahweh the words of this song on the day when Yahweh delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul.
22:2	He said: "Yahweh is my crag, my stronghold, and my deliverer,
22:3	my God, my rock, in whom I take refuge, my shield and the horn of my salvation, my high fortress and my place of escape, my savior; you save me from violence.
22:4	I call upon Yahweh, who is worthy of praise, and I am saved from my enemies.
22:5	For the breakers of death encompassed me; the torrents of destruction terrified me;
22:6	the cords of Sheol surrounded me; the snares of death confronted me.
22:7	In my distress I called upon Yahweh; to my God I called. From his temple he heard my voice, and my cry for help reached his ears.
22:8	Then the earth heaved and quaked; the foundations of the heavens trembled and shook, because he was angry.
22:9	Smoke went up from his nostrils, and devouring fire from his mouth; glowing coals blazed forth from him.
22:10	He bent the heavens and came down; thick darkness was under his feet.
22:11	He rode on a cherub and flew; he was seen on the wings of the wind.
22:12	He made darkness a canopy around him, a mass of waters, thick clouds of the skies.
22:13	Out of the brightness before him coals of fire blazed.
22:14	Yahweh thundered from heaven, and the Most High uttered his voice.
22:15	He sent out arrows and scattered them, lightning, and routed them.
22:16	Then the channels of the sea were seen; the foundations of the world were laid bare at the rebuke of Yahweh, at the blast of the breath of his nostrils.
22:17	He reached down from on high and took me; he drew me out of many waters.
22:18	He rescued me from my strong enemy, from those who hated me, for they were too mighty for me.
22:19	They confronted me in the day of my calamity, but Yahweh was my support.
22:20	He brought me out into a broad place; he rescued me because he delighted in me.
22:21	Yahweh dealt with me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands he rewarded me.
22:22	For I have kept the ways of Yahweh and have not wickedly departed from my God.
22:23	For all his rules were before me, and from his statutes I did not turn aside.
22:24	I was blameless before him, and I kept myself from guilt.
22:25	So Yahweh has rewarded me according to my righteousness, according to my cleanness in his sight.
22:26	With the loyal you show yourself loyal; with the blameless warrior you show yourself blameless;
22:27	with the pure you show yourself pure, and with the crooked you show yourself shrewd.
22:28	You save a humble people, but your eyes are on the haughty, to bring them down.
22:29	For you are my lamp, O Yahweh, and Yahweh lights up my darkness.
22:30	For by you I can rush a troop, and by my God I can leap over a wall.
22:31	This God, his way is perfect; the word of Yahweh is refined; he is a shield to all who take refuge in him.
22:32	For who is God except Yahweh? And who is a rock except our God?
22:33	This God is my strong fortress, and he has made my way blameless.
22:34	He makes my feet like the feet of a deer and sets me on my high places.
22:35	He trains my hands for war, so that my arms can bend a bow of bronze.
22:36	You have given me the shield of your salvation, and your answering has made me great.
22:37	You have widened my step beneath me, and my ankles have not given way.
22:38	I pursued my enemies and destroyed them, and did not turn back until they were finished.
22:39	I finished them off and crushed them, so that they could not rise; they fell under my feet.
22:40	For you girded me with strength for the battle; you made those who rose against me bow beneath me.
22:41	You made my enemies turn their backs to me; those who hated me, I destroyed them.
22:42	They looked, but there was no one to save; they cried to Yahweh, but he did not answer them.
22:43	I beat them fine as the dust of the earth; I crushed them and stamped them down like the mud of the streets.
22:44	You delivered me from the strife of my people; you kept me as the head of nations; a people I had not known served me.
22:45	Foreigners came cringing to me; as soon as they heard of me, they obeyed me.
22:46	Foreigners lost heart and came limping out of their strongholds.
22:47	Yahweh lives! Blessed be my rock, and exalted be my God, the rock of my salvation,
22:48	the God who gives me vengeance and brings peoples down under me,
22:49	who brings me out from my enemies; you lift me up above those who rise against me; you rescue me from the man of violence.
22:50	Therefore I will praise you, O Yahweh, among the nations, and sing praises to your name.
22:51	He is a tower of salvation for his king, and shows steadfast love to his anointed, to David and his offspring forever."
