4:1	When Saul's son heard that Abner had died in Hebron, his courage failed, and all Israel was alarmed.
4:2	Saul's son had two men who were captains of raiding bands; the name of one was Baanah and the name of the other Rechab, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, of the Benjaminites, for Beeroth too is reckoned to Benjamin.
4:3	The Beerothites had fled to Gittaim and have lived there as resident aliens to this day.
4:4	Jonathan son of Saul had a son who was crippled in his feet. He was five years old when the news about Saul and Jonathan came from Jezreel, and his nurse picked him up and fled, and as she hurried to flee, he fell and became lame. His name was Mephibosheth.
4:5	The sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, Rechab and Baanah, set out and came in the heat of the day to the house of Ish-bosheth, while he was taking his midday rest.
4:6	They came into the middle of the house as if fetching wheat, and they struck him in the belly. Then Rechab and his brother Baanah escaped.
4:7	They had come into the house while he was lying on his bed in his bedroom, and they struck him and killed him and cut off his head. They took his head and traveled by the road of the Arabah all night.
4:8	They brought the head of Ish-bosheth to David at Hebron and said to the king, "Here is the head of Ish-bosheth son of Saul, your enemy, who sought your life. Yahweh has avenged my lord the king this day on Saul and on his offspring."
4:9	David answered Rechab and his brother Baanah, the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, and said to them, "As Yahweh lives, who has redeemed my life from all distress,
4:10	when the one who told me, 'Look, Saul is dead,' thought he was bringing good news, I seized him and killed him at Ziklag; that was the reward I gave him for his news.
4:11	How much more, when wicked men have killed a righteous man in his own house on his bed! Now shall I not require his blood from your hand and wipe you off the earth?"
4:12	So David commanded the young men, and they killed them, cut off their hands and feet, and hung them up beside the pool at Hebron. But they took the head of Ish-bosheth and buried it in Abner's tomb at Hebron.
