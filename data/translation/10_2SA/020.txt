20:1	Now there happened to be there a worthless man named Sheba son of Bichri, a Benjaminite. He blew the ram's horn and said, "We have no share in David, and no inheritance in the son of Jesse! Every man to his tents, O Israel!"
20:2	So all the men of Israel withdrew from following David and followed Sheba son of Bichri, but the men of Judah stuck close to their king from the Jordan to Jerusalem.
20:3	David came to his house in Jerusalem. The king took the ten concubines whom he had left to keep the house and put them in a guarded house; he provided for them but did not go in to them. So they were shut up until the day of their death, living as widows.
20:4	Then the king said to Amasa, "Summon the men of Judah to me within three days, and be here yourself."
20:5	So Amasa went to summon Judah, but he delayed beyond the set time that had been appointed him.
20:6	David said to Abishai, "Now Sheba son of Bichri will do us more harm than Absalom. Take your lord's servants and pursue him, or he will find fortified cities for himself and escape from our sight."
20:7	So Joab's men went out after him, along with the Cherethites, the Pelethites, and all the warriors; they went out from Jerusalem to pursue Sheba son of Bichri.
20:8	When they were at the great stone that is in Gibeon, Amasa came to meet them. Joab was dressed in his military garment, and over it was a belt with a sword in its sheath fastened at his waist; and as he went forward, it fell out.
20:9	Joab said to Amasa, "Are you well, my brother?" And Joab took Amasa by the beard with his right hand to kiss him.
20:10	But Amasa was not on guard against the sword in Joab's hand, and Joab struck him with it in the belly and spilled his entrails on the ground; he did not strike him a second time, and he died. Then Joab and Abishai his brother pursued Sheba son of Bichri.
20:11	One of Joab's young men stood over him and said, "Whoever favors Joab, and whoever is for David, follow Joab!"
20:12	Amasa lay wallowing in his blood in the middle of the highway. When the man saw that all the troops stopped, he moved Amasa from the highway into the field and threw a garment over him, since he saw that everyone who came upon him stopped.
20:13	Once he was removed from the highway, all the men went on after Joab to pursue Sheba son of Bichri.
20:14	Sheba passed through all the tribes of Israel to Abel and Beth-maacah, and all the Berites assembled and followed him in as well.
20:15	They came and besieged him in Abel of Beth-maacah. They heaped up a siege ramp against the city, and it stood against the outer rampart, and all the troops who were with Joab were battering the wall to bring it down.
20:16	Then a wise woman called out from the city, "Listen! Listen! Tell Joab, 'Come here so that I may speak with you.'"
20:17	He came near her, and the woman said, "Are you Joab?" He said, "I am." She said to him, "Listen to the words of your servant." He said, "I am listening."
20:18	Then she said, "In the old days they used to say, 'Let them just inquire at Abel,' and so they would settle a matter.
20:19	I am one of the peaceable and faithful in Israel. You are seeking to destroy a city that is a mother in Israel. Why would you swallow up the inheritance of Yahweh?"
20:20	Joab answered, "Far be it, far be it from me that I should swallow up or destroy!
20:21	That is not the case. But a man from the hill country of Ephraim, Sheba son of Bichri by name, has raised his hand against the king, against David. Hand over him alone, and I will withdraw from the city." The woman said to Joab, "His head will be thrown to you over the wall."
20:22	Then the woman went to all the people with her wise counsel, and they cut off the head of Sheba son of Bichri and threw it out to Joab. He blew the ram's horn, and they dispersed from the city, each to his tents, and Joab returned to Jerusalem to the king.
20:23	Joab was over the whole army of Israel; Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and the Pelethites;
20:24	Adoram was over the forced labor; Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was recorder;
20:25	Sheva was secretary; Zadok and Abiathar were priests;
20:26	and Ira the Jairite was also a priest to David.
