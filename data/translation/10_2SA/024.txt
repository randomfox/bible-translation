24:1	Again the anger of Yahweh burned against Israel, and He incited David against them, saying, "Go, count Israel and Judah."
24:2	So the king said to Joab, the commander of the army who was with him, "Go through all the tribes of Israel, from Dan to Beersheba, and register the people, so that I may know the number of the people."
24:3	But Joab said to the king, "May Yahweh your God add to the people a hundred times as many as they are, while the eyes of my lord the king still see it! But why does my lord the king want to do this thing?"
24:4	But the king's word prevailed against Joab and the commanders of the army. So Joab and the commanders of the army went out from the king's presence to register the people of Israel.
24:5	They crossed the Jordan and camped at Aroer, south of the city that is in the middle of the valley, toward Gad and on to Jazer.
24:6	Then they came to Gilead and to the land of Tahtim-hodshi, and they came to Dan-jaan and around toward Sidon.
24:7	They came to the fortress of Tyre and all the cities of the Hivites and the Canaanites, and they went out to the Negev of Judah at Beersheba.
24:8	So when they had gone through the whole land, they came back to Jerusalem at the end of nine months and twenty days.
24:9	Joab gave the king the total of the registration of the people: in Israel there were eight hundred thousand valiant men who drew the sword, and the men of Judah were five hundred thousand.
24:10	But David's heart struck him after he had counted the people. David said to Yahweh, "I have sinned greatly in what I have done. But now, Yahweh, please take away the guilt of Your servant, for I have acted very foolishly."
24:11	When David got up in the morning, the word of Yahweh had come to the prophet Gad, David's seer, saying,
24:12	"Go and say to David, 'Thus says Yahweh: Three things I hold over you; choose one of them, and I will do it to you.'"
24:13	So Gad came to David and told him, and said to him, "Shall seven years of famine come upon you in your land? Or will you flee three months before your foes while they pursue you? Or shall there be three days of plague in your land? Now consider and decide what answer I shall return to the one Who sent me."
24:14	David said to Gad, "I am in great distress. Let us fall into the hand of Yahweh, for His mercies are great; but let me not fall into human hands."
24:15	So Yahweh sent a plague on Israel from the morning until the appointed time, and seventy thousand of the people died, from Dan to Beersheba.
24:16	But when the angel stretched out his hand toward Jerusalem to destroy it, Yahweh relented concerning the disaster and said to the angel who was destroying the people, "Enough! Now hold back your hand." The angel of Yahweh was then by the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.
24:17	When David saw the angel who was striking down the people, he spoke to Yahweh and said, "It is I who have sinned, and I who have done wrong. But these sheep, what have they done? Let Your hand be against me and against my father's house."
24:18	That day Gad came to David and said to him, "Go up and set up an altar to Yahweh on the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite."
24:19	So David went up according to Gad's word, as Yahweh had commanded.
24:20	When Araunah looked down and saw the king and his servants coming over toward him, Araunah went out and bowed down to the king with his face to the ground.
24:21	Araunah said, "Why has my lord the king come to his servant?" David said, "To buy the threshing floor from you in order to build an altar to Yahweh, so that the plague may be held back from the people."
24:22	Araunah said to David, "Let my lord the king take it and offer up whatever seems good to him. Here are the oxen for the burnt offering, and the threshing sledges and the oxen's yokes for the wood.
24:23	All this, O king, Araunah gives to the king." And Araunah said to the king, "May Yahweh your God accept you."
24:24	But the king said to Araunah, "No, I will surely buy it from you for a price. I will not offer burnt offerings to Yahweh my God that cost me nothing." So David bought the threshing floor and the oxen for fifty shekels of silver.
24:25	David built an altar to Yahweh there and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings. So Yahweh responded to prayer for the land, and the plague was held back from Israel.
