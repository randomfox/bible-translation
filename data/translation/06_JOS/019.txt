19:1	The second lot came out for Simeon, for the tribe of the people of Simeon by their clans, and their inheritance was within the inheritance of the people of Judah.
19:2	They had in their inheritance Beersheba, Sheba, Moladah,
19:3	Hazar-shual, Balah, Ezem,
19:4	Eltolad, Bethul, Hormah,
19:5	Ziklag, Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susah,
19:6	Beth-lebaoth, and Sharuhen: thirteen cities with their villages;
19:7	Ain, Rimmon, Ether, and Ashan: four cities with their villages;
19:8	and all the villages that were around these cities as far as Baalath-beer, Ramah of the Negev. This was the inheritance of the tribe of the people of Simeon by their clans.
19:9	The inheritance of the people of Simeon was taken from the portion of the people of Judah, because the share of the people of Judah was too large for them; so the people of Simeon received an inheritance within their inheritance.
19:10	The third lot came up for the people of Zebulun by their clans. The boundary of their inheritance reached as far as Sarid.
19:11	Their boundary went up westward to Maralah, touched Dabbesheth, and touched the wadi that is east of Jokneam.
19:12	From Sarid it turned back eastward, toward the sunrise, to the border of Chisloth-tabor, went out to Daberath, and went up to Japhia.
19:13	From there it passed on eastward, toward the sunrise, to Gath-hepher and to Eth-kazin, and went out to Rimmon, curving toward Neah.
19:14	Then the boundary went around it on the north to Hannathon, and its end was at the Valley of Iphtah-el;
19:15	and Kattath, Nahalal, Shimron, Idalah, and Bethlehem: twelve cities with their villages.
19:16	This was the inheritance of the people of Zebulun by their clans, these cities with their villages.
19:17	For Issachar the fourth lot came out, for the people of Issachar by their clans.
19:18	Their territory went to Jezreel, and included Chesulloth, Shunem,
19:19	Hapharaim, Shion, Anaharath,
19:20	Rabbith, Kishion, Ebez,
19:21	Remeth, En-gannim, En-haddah, and Beth-pazzez.
19:22	The boundary touched Tabor, Shahazumah, and Beth-shemesh, and the end of their boundary was at the Jordan: sixteen cities with their villages.
19:23	This was the inheritance of the tribe of the people of Issachar by their clans, the cities with their villages.
19:24	The fifth lot came out for the tribe of the people of Asher by their clans.
19:25	Their territory included Helkath, Hali, Beten, Achshaph,
19:26	Allammelech, Amad, and Mishal; on the west it touched Carmel and Shihor-libnath.
19:27	Then it turned back toward the sunrise to Beth-dagon, touched Zebulun and the Valley of Iphtah-el northward, Beth-emek and Neiel, and went out to Cabul on the left,
19:28	Ebron, Rehob, Hammon, and Kanah, as far as Great Sidon.
19:29	Then the boundary turned back to Ramah and to the fortified city of Tyre; then the boundary turned to Hosah, and its end was at the sea, from the district of Achzib;
19:30	Ummah, Aphek, and Rehob: twenty-two cities with their villages.
19:31	This was the inheritance of the tribe of the people of Asher by their clans, these cities with their villages.
19:32	For the people of Naphtali the sixth lot came out, for the people of Naphtali by their clans.
19:33	Their boundary ran from Heleph, from the oak at Zaanannim, and Adami-nekeb and Jabneel, as far as Lakkum, and its end was at the Jordan.
19:34	Then the boundary turned westward to Aznoth-tabor and went out from there to Hukkok; it touched Zebulun on the south, touched Asher on the west, and Judah at the Jordan toward the sunrise.
19:35	The fortified cities were Ziddim, Zer, Hammath, Rakkath, Chinnereth,
19:36	Adamah, Ramah, Hazor,
19:37	Kedesh, Edrei, En-hazor,
19:38	Yiron, Migdal-el, Horem, Beth-anath, and Beth-shemesh: nineteen cities with their villages.
19:39	This was the inheritance of the tribe of the people of Naphtali by their clans, the cities with their villages.
19:40	For the tribe of the people of Dan by their clans the seventh lot came out.
19:41	The territory of their inheritance included Zorah, Eshtaol, Ir-shemesh,
19:42	Shaalabbin, Aijalon, Ithlah,
19:43	Elon, Timnah, Ekron,
19:44	Eltekeh, Gibbethon, Baalath,
19:45	Jehud, Bene-berak, Gath-rimmon,
19:46	Me-jarkon and Rakkon, with the territory facing Joppa.
19:47	But the territory of the people of Dan slipped from them, so the people of Dan went up and fought against Leshem; they captured it, struck it with the edge of the sword, took possession of it, and settled in it, and they called Leshem Dan, after the name of Dan their ancestor.
19:48	This was the inheritance of the tribe of the people of Dan by their clans, these cities with their villages.
19:49	When they had finished distributing the land as an inheritance by its boundaries, the people of Israel gave an inheritance among them to Joshua son of Nun.
19:50	At the command of Yahweh they gave him the city he asked for, Timnath-serah in the hill country of Ephraim; he rebuilt the city and settled in it.
19:51	These are the inheritances that Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the ancestral houses of the tribes of the people of Israel distributed by lot at Shiloh before Yahweh, at the entrance of the tent of meeting. So they finished dividing the land.
