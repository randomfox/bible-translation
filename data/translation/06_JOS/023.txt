23:1	A long time afterward, when Yahweh had given Israel rest from all their enemies around them, and Joshua was old and advanced in years,
23:2	Joshua summoned all Israel, their elders, their heads, their judges, and their officers, and said to them, "I am old and advanced in years.
23:3	You yourselves have seen all that Yahweh your God has done to all these nations for your sake, for it is Yahweh your God who has fought for you.
23:4	See, I have allotted to you as an inheritance for your tribes these nations that remain, along with all the nations I have already cut off, from the Jordan to the Great Sea in the west.
23:5	Yahweh your God himself will push them back before you and drive them out of your way, and you will take possession of their land, as Yahweh your God promised you.
23:6	So be very strong to keep and do all that is written in the book of the law of Moses, not turning aside from it to the right or to the left,
23:7	so that you do not mix with these nations, these that remain among you. Do not invoke the names of their gods or swear by them; do not serve them or bow down to them.
23:8	But hold fast to Yahweh your God, as you have done to this day.
23:9	Yahweh has driven out before you great and powerful nations, and as for you, no one has withstood you to this day.
23:10	One man of you puts a thousand to flight, because it is Yahweh your God who fights for you, as he promised you.
23:11	So take great care for your own lives to love Yahweh your God.
23:12	For if you ever turn back and cling to the remnant of these nations, these that remain among you, and intermarry with them, so that you mix with them and they with you,
23:13	know for certain that Yahweh your God will no longer drive these nations out before you. They will become a snare and a trap for you, a whip on your sides and thorns in your eyes, until you perish from this good land that Yahweh your God has given you.
23:14	Now I am about to go the way of all the earth. You know with all your heart and with all your soul that not one word has failed of all the good things that Yahweh your God promised concerning you; all have come true for you; not one word of them has failed.
23:15	But just as every good thing that Yahweh your God promised you has come upon you, so Yahweh will bring upon you every bad thing, until he has destroyed you from this good land that Yahweh your God has given you,
23:16	if you transgress the covenant of Yahweh your God, which he commanded you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them. Then the anger of Yahweh will burn against you, and you will perish quickly from the good land that he has given you."
