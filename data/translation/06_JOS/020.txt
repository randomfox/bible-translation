20:1	Then Yahweh spoke to Joshua:
20:2	"Speak to the people of Israel and say, 'Designate for yourselves the cities of refuge that I spoke to you about through Moses,
20:3	so that a killer who strikes down a person unintentionally, without meaning to, may flee there. They shall be a refuge for you from the avenger of blood.
20:4	He shall flee to one of these cities, stand at the entrance of the city gate, and state his case in the hearing of the elders of that city. Then they shall take him into the city with them, give him a place, and he shall live among them.
20:5	If the avenger of blood pursues him, they shall not hand the killer over to him, because he struck his neighbor without meaning to and had not hated him beforehand.
20:6	He shall live in that city until he stands before the congregation for judgment, until the death of the high priest who is in office at that time. Then the killer may return and go to his own town and his own home, to the town from which he fled.'"
20:7	So they set apart Kedesh in Galilee in the hill country of Naphtali, Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim, and Kiriath-arba (that is, Hebron) in the hill country of Judah.
20:8	Beyond the Jordan, east of Jericho, they designated Bezer in the wilderness on the tableland, from the tribe of Reuben; Ramoth in Gilead, from the tribe of Gad; and Golan in Bashan, from the tribe of Manasseh.
20:9	These were the cities appointed for all the people of Israel and for the foreigner residing among them, so that anyone who struck down a person unintentionally could flee there and not die at the hand of the avenger of blood until he had stood before the congregation.
