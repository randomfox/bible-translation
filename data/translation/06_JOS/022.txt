22:1	Then Joshua summoned the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh,
22:2	and said to them, "You have kept everything that Moses the servant of Yahweh commanded you, and you have obeyed me in everything I commanded you.
22:3	You have not abandoned your brothers these many days, up to this day, but you have carefully kept the charge of the commandment of Yahweh your God.
22:4	And now Yahweh your God has given rest to your brothers, as he promised them. So now turn and go to your tents, to the land of your possession, which Moses the servant of Yahweh gave you beyond the Jordan.
22:5	Only be very careful to carry out the commandment and the law that Moses the servant of Yahweh commanded you: to love Yahweh your God, to walk in all his ways, to keep his commandments, to hold fast to him, and to serve him with all your heart and with all your soul."
22:6	So Joshua blessed them and sent them away, and they went to their tents.
22:7	Now to one half of the tribe of Manasseh Moses had given land in Bashan, and to the other half Joshua had given land with their brothers on the west side of the Jordan. When Joshua sent them away to their tents, he blessed them
22:8	and said to them, "Go back to your tents with great wealth, with very many livestock, with silver, gold, bronze, and iron, and with a great quantity of clothing. Divide the plunder from your enemies with your brothers."
22:9	So the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh returned, leaving the people of Israel at Shiloh in the land of Canaan, to go to the land of Gilead, the land of their possession, which they had acquired at the command of Yahweh through Moses.
22:10	When they came to the region of Geliloth near the Jordan, which is in the land of Canaan, the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh built an altar there by the Jordan, a large, conspicuous altar.
22:11	The people of Israel heard it said, "Look, the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have built the altar on the frontier of the land of Canaan, in the region of Geliloth by the Jordan, on the side belonging to the people of Israel."
22:12	When the people of Israel heard it, the whole congregation of the people of Israel assembled at Shiloh to go up against them in war.
22:13	The people of Israel sent Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead,
22:14	and with him ten chiefs, one chief for each ancestral house from all the tribes of Israel, each of them head of his ancestral house among the clans of Israel.
22:15	They came to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead and said to them,
22:16	"Thus says the whole congregation of Yahweh: 'What is this breach of faith that you have committed against the God of Israel, turning away today from following Yahweh by building yourselves an altar, rebelling today against Yahweh?
22:17	Is the iniquity of Peor not enough for us, from which we have not cleansed ourselves to this day, even though a plague came on the congregation of Yahweh,
22:18	that you must turn away today from following Yahweh? If you rebel against Yahweh today, tomorrow he will be angry with the whole congregation of Israel.
22:19	But if the land of your possession is unclean, cross over into Yahweh's own land, where Yahweh's tabernacle stands, and take a possession among us. Only do not rebel against Yahweh, and do not rebel against us, by building yourselves an altar other than the altar of Yahweh our God.
22:20	Did not Achan son of Zerah break faith in the matter of the devoted things, and wrath fell on the whole congregation of Israel? And he was not the only one who perished for his iniquity.'"
22:21	Then the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh answered the heads of the clans of Israel:
22:22	"The Mighty One, God, Yahweh! The Mighty One, God, Yahweh! He knows, and let Israel itself know! If it was in rebellion or in breach of faith against Yahweh, do not spare us today
22:23	for building ourselves an altar to turn away from following Yahweh. If we did it to offer burnt offerings or grain offerings on it, or to make peace offerings on it, may Yahweh himself call us to account.
22:24	No, we did it out of anxiety over this matter, thinking, 'In time to come your children may say to our children, "What have you to do with Yahweh, the God of Israel?
22:25	Yahweh has made the Jordan a boundary between us and you, you Reubenites and Gadites. You have no share in Yahweh." So your children might make our children stop fearing Yahweh.'
22:26	So we said, 'Let us now act and build an altar, not for burnt offering or for sacrifice,
22:27	but to be a witness between us and you, and between our generations after us, that we may perform the service of Yahweh before him with our burnt offerings, our sacrifices, and our peace offerings, so that your children may not say to our children in time to come, "You have no share in Yahweh."'
22:28	And we said, 'If they should say this to us or to our descendants in time to come, we will say, "Look at the replica of the altar of Yahweh that our ancestors made, not for burnt offering or for sacrifice, but as a witness between us and you."'
22:29	Far be it from us to rebel against Yahweh and turn away today from following Yahweh by building an altar for burnt offering, grain offering, or sacrifice, other than the altar of Yahweh our God that stands before his tabernacle!"
22:30	When Phinehas the priest and the chiefs of the congregation, the heads of the clans of Israel who were with him, heard the words that the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the Manassites spoke, they were satisfied.
22:31	Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest said to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the Manassites, "Today we know that Yahweh is among us, because you have not committed this breach of faith against Yahweh. Now you have rescued the people of Israel from the hand of Yahweh."
22:32	Then Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest and the chiefs returned from the Reubenites and the Gadites in the land of Gilead to the land of Canaan, to the people of Israel, and brought back word to them.
22:33	The report pleased the people of Israel, and the people of Israel blessed God and spoke no more of going up against them in war to destroy the land where the Reubenites and the Gadites lived.
22:34	The Reubenites and the Gadites gave the altar a name: "It is a witness between us that Yahweh is God."
