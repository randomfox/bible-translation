21:1	Then the heads of the ancestral houses of the Levites came to Eleazar the priest, to Joshua son of Nun, and to the heads of the ancestral houses of the tribes of the people of Israel,
21:2	and spoke to them at Shiloh in the land of Canaan: "Yahweh commanded through Moses that we be given cities to live in, with their pasturelands for our livestock."
21:3	So by the command of Yahweh the people of Israel gave the Levites, out of their own inheritance, these cities with their pasturelands.
21:4	The lot came out for the clans of the Kohathites. The descendants of Aaron the priest, among the Levites, received by lot thirteen cities from the tribe of Judah, the tribe of Simeon, and the tribe of Benjamin.
21:5	The rest of the Kohathites received by lot ten cities from the clans of the tribe of Ephraim, the tribe of Dan, and the half-tribe of Manasseh.
21:6	The Gershonites received by lot thirteen cities from the clans of the tribe of Issachar, the tribe of Asher, the tribe of Naphtali, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in Bashan.
21:7	The Merarites by their clans received twelve cities from the tribe of Reuben, the tribe of Gad, and the tribe of Zebulun.
21:8	So the people of Israel gave these cities with their pasturelands to the Levites by lot, as Yahweh had commanded through Moses.
21:9	From the tribe of the people of Judah and the tribe of the people of Simeon they gave these cities, which are here mentioned by name.
21:10	They went to the descendants of Aaron, of the clans of the Kohathites, of the Levites, because the first lot fell to them.
21:11	They gave them Kiriath-arba (Arba was the father of Anak), that is, Hebron, in the hill country of Judah, with the pasturelands around it.
21:12	But the fields of the city and its villages they had given to Caleb son of Jephunneh as his possession.
21:13	To the descendants of Aaron the priest they gave Hebron, the city of refuge for the killer, with its pasturelands, Libnah with its pasturelands,
21:14	Jattir with its pasturelands, Eshtemoa with its pasturelands,
21:15	Holon with its pasturelands, Debir with its pasturelands,
21:16	Ain with its pasturelands, Juttah with its pasturelands, and Beth-shemesh with its pasturelands: nine cities from these two tribes.
21:17	From the tribe of Benjamin: Gibeon with its pasturelands, Geba with its pasturelands,
21:18	Anathoth with its pasturelands, and Almon with its pasturelands: four cities.
21:19	All the cities of the descendants of Aaron, the priests, were thirteen cities with their pasturelands.
21:20	As for the clans of the Kohathites, the Levites who remained of the Kohathites, the cities of their lot were from the tribe of Ephraim.
21:21	They gave them Shechem, the city of refuge for the killer, with its pasturelands in the hill country of Ephraim, Gezer with its pasturelands,
21:22	Kibzaim with its pasturelands, and Beth-horon with its pasturelands: four cities.
21:23	From the tribe of Dan: Elteke with its pasturelands, Gibbethon with its pasturelands,
21:24	Aijalon with its pasturelands, and Gath-rimmon with its pasturelands: four cities.
21:25	From the half-tribe of Manasseh: Taanach with its pasturelands and Gath-rimmon with its pasturelands: two cities.
21:26	All the cities for the clans of the rest of the Kohathites were ten, with their pasturelands.
21:27	To the Gershonites, of the clans of the Levites, from the half-tribe of Manasseh: Golan in Bashan, the city of refuge for the killer, with its pasturelands, and Beeshterah with its pasturelands: two cities.
21:28	From the tribe of Issachar: Kishion with its pasturelands, Daberath with its pasturelands,
21:29	Jarmuth with its pasturelands, and En-gannim with its pasturelands: four cities.
21:30	From the tribe of Asher: Mishal with its pasturelands, Abdon with its pasturelands,
21:31	Helkath with its pasturelands, and Rehob with its pasturelands: four cities.
21:32	From the tribe of Naphtali: Kedesh in Galilee, the city of refuge for the killer, with its pasturelands, Hammoth-dor with its pasturelands, and Kartan with its pasturelands: three cities.
21:33	All the cities of the Gershonites by their clans were thirteen cities with their pasturelands.
21:34	To the clans of the Merarites, the rest of the Levites, from the tribe of Zebulun: Jokneam with its pasturelands, Kartah with its pasturelands,
21:35	Dimnah with its pasturelands, and Nahalal with its pasturelands: four cities.
21:36	From the tribe of Reuben: Bezer with its pasturelands, Jahzah with its pasturelands,
21:37	Kedemoth with its pasturelands, and Mephaath with its pasturelands: four cities.
21:38	From the tribe of Gad: Ramoth in Gilead, the city of refuge for the killer, with its pasturelands, Mahanaim with its pasturelands,
21:39	Heshbon with its pasturelands, and Jazer with its pasturelands: four cities in all.
21:40	All the cities that fell by lot to the Merarites by their clans, the rest of the clans of the Levites, were twelve cities.
21:41	All the cities of the Levites within the possession of the people of Israel were forty-eight cities with their pasturelands.
21:42	Each of these cities had its pasturelands around it; so it was with all these cities.
21:43	So Yahweh gave Israel all the land that he had sworn to give to their ancestors, and they took possession of it and settled in it.
21:44	Yahweh gave them rest on every side, just as he had sworn to their ancestors. Not one of all their enemies withstood them; Yahweh handed all their enemies over to them.
21:45	Not one word of all the good promises that Yahweh had made to the house of Israel failed; all of it came to pass.
