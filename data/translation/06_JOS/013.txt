13:1	Now Joshua was old and advanced in years, and Yahweh said to him, "You are old and advanced in years, and very much of the land remains to be possessed.
13:2	This is the land that remains: all the regions of the Philistines and all those of the Geshurites,
13:3	from the Shihor, which is east of Egypt, northward to the border of Ekron, which is reckoned as Canaanite; the five rulers of the Philistines, those of Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath, and Ekron; and the Avvim
13:4	in the south; all the land of the Canaanites, and Mearah, which belongs to the Sidonians, as far as Aphek, to the border of the Amorites;
13:5	and the land of the Gebalites, and all Lebanon toward the sunrise, from Baal-gad below Mount Hermon to Lebo-hamath;
13:6	all the inhabitants of the hill country from Lebanon to Misrephoth-maim, all the Sidonians. I myself will drive them out before the Israelites. Only allot it to Israel as an inheritance, as I have commanded you.
13:7	Now therefore divide this land as an inheritance for the nine tribes and the half-tribe of Manasseh."
13:8	With the other half of Manasseh the Reubenites and the Gadites received their inheritance, which Moses gave them beyond the Jordan eastward, as Moses the servant of Yahweh gave them:
13:9	from Aroer, which is on the edge of the Wadi Arnon, and the city that is in the middle of the wadi, and all the tableland from Medeba to Dibon;
13:10	and all the cities of Sihon king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon, as far as the border of the Ammonites;
13:11	and Gilead, and the territory of the Geshurites and Maacathites, and all Mount Hermon, and all Bashan as far as Salecah;
13:12	all the kingdom of Og in Bashan, who reigned in Ashtaroth and Edrei; he was left of the remnant of the Rephaim. These Moses struck down and dispossessed.
13:13	Yet the Israelites did not drive out the Geshurites or the Maacathites, and Geshur and Maacath live among Israel to this day.
13:14	Only to the tribe of Levi he gave no inheritance; the offerings by fire to Yahweh the God of Israel are its inheritance, as he told him.
13:15	Moses gave an inheritance to the tribe of the Reubenites according to their clans.
13:16	Their territory was from Aroer, which is on the edge of the Wadi Arnon, and the city that is in the middle of the wadi, and all the tableland by Medeba;
13:17	Heshbon and all its towns that are on the tableland: Dibon, Bamoth-baal, Beth-baal-meon,
13:18	Jahaz, Kedemoth, Mephaath,
13:19	Kiriathaim, Sibmah, Zereth-shahar on the hill of the valley,
13:20	Beth-peor, the slopes of Pisgah, and Beth-jeshimoth,
13:21	all the cities of the tableland, and all the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon, whom Moses struck down with the chiefs of Midian, Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, the princes of Sihon, who lived in the land.
13:22	Balaam son of Beor, the diviner, the Israelites also killed with the sword, along with the rest of those they slew.
13:23	The border of the Reubenites was the Jordan and its bank. This was the inheritance of the Reubenites according to their clans, the cities with their villages.
13:24	Moses gave an inheritance to the tribe of Gad, to the Gadites according to their clans.
13:25	Their territory was Jazer, and all the cities of Gilead, and half the land of the Ammonites, as far as Aroer, which is east of Rabbah,
13:26	and from Heshbon to Ramath-mizpeh and Betonim, and from Mahanaim to the border of Debir,
13:27	and in the valley Beth-haram, Beth-nimrah, Succoth, and Zaphon, the rest of the kingdom of Sihon king of Heshbon, with the Jordan and its bank as far as the lower end of the Sea of Chinnereth, beyond the Jordan eastward.
13:28	This is the inheritance of the Gadites according to their clans, the cities with their villages.
13:29	Moses gave an inheritance to the half-tribe of Manasseh; it was for the half-tribe of the Manassites according to their clans.
13:30	Their territory was from Mahanaim, all Bashan, the whole kingdom of Og king of Bashan, and all the tent villages of Jair, which are in Bashan, sixty towns,
13:31	and half of Gilead, and Ashtaroth and Edrei, the cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan. These were for the descendants of Machir son of Manasseh, for half of the Machirites according to their clans.
13:32	These are the inheritances that Moses distributed in the plains of Moab, beyond the Jordan east of Jericho.
13:33	But to the tribe of Levi Moses gave no inheritance; Yahweh the God of Israel is their inheritance, as he told them.
