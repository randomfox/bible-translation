2:1	Joshua son of Nun secretly sent two men as spies from Shittim, saying, "Go, look over the land, especially Jericho." So they went and came to the house of a prostitute named Rahab, and they lodged there.
2:2	The king of Jericho was told, "Look, some men of the Israelites have come here tonight to search out the land."
2:3	So the king of Jericho sent to Rahab, saying, "Bring out the men who came to you, who entered your house, for they have come to search out the whole land."
2:4	But the woman had taken the two men and hidden them. She said, "Yes, the men came to me, but I did not know where they were from.
2:5	When the gate was about to be shut at dark, the men went out. I do not know where the men went. Pursue them quickly, for you will overtake them."
2:6	But she had brought them up to the roof and hidden them under the stalks of flax that she had laid out on the roof.
2:7	So the men pursued them on the road to the Jordan as far as the fords, and the gate was shut as soon as the pursuers had gone out after them.
2:8	Before they lay down, she came up to them on the roof
2:9	and said to the men, "I know that Yahweh has given you the land, that the dread of you has fallen upon us, and that all the inhabitants of the land melt away before you.
2:10	For we have heard how Yahweh dried up the water of the Red Sea before you when you came out of Egypt, and what you did to the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, to Sihon and Og, whom you devoted to destruction.
2:11	When we heard it, our hearts melted, and no one had any spirit left in him because of you, for Yahweh your God, He is God in the heavens above and on the earth beneath.
2:12	Now then, please swear to me by Yahweh that, since I have dealt kindly with you, you also will deal kindly with my father's house, and give me a sure sign
2:13	that you will spare my father and mother, my brothers and sisters, and all who belong to them, and deliver our lives from death."
2:14	The men said to her, "Our life for yours, to death, if you do not tell about this business of ours. And when Yahweh gives us the land, we will deal with you kindly and faithfully."
2:15	Then she let them down by a rope through the window, for her house was in the wall of the city, and she lived in the wall.
2:16	She said to them, "Go to the hill country, so that the pursuers do not come upon you. Hide there three days until the pursuers return, and afterward go your way."
2:17	The men said to her, "We will be free of this oath of yours that you have made us swear,
2:18	unless, when we come into the land, you tie this cord of scarlet thread in the window through which you let us down, and gather to yourself into the house your father and mother, your brothers, and all your father's household.
2:19	Anyone who goes out of the doors of your house into the street, his blood shall be on his own head, and we will be guiltless; but anyone who is with you in the house, his blood shall be on our head if a hand is laid on him.
2:20	But if you tell about this business of ours, we will be free of the oath that you have made us swear."
2:21	She said, "According to your words, so be it." Then she sent them away and they departed, and she tied the scarlet cord in the window.
2:22	They went and came to the hill country and stayed there three days until the pursuers returned. The pursuers searched all along the road and did not find them.
2:23	Then the two men came back down from the hill country, crossed over, and came to Joshua son of Nun, and told him all that had happened to them.
2:24	They said to Joshua, "Truly Yahweh has given the whole land into our hands; moreover, all the inhabitants of the land melt away before us."
