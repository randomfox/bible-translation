6:1	Now Jericho was shut up tight because of the Israelites; no one went out and no one came in.
6:2	Yahweh said to Joshua, "See, I have given Jericho into your hand, with its king and its mighty warriors.
6:3	You shall march around the city, all the men of war, going around the city once. Thus you shall do for six days.
6:4	Seven priests shall carry seven rams' horn trumpets before the ark. On the seventh day you shall march around the city seven times, and the priests shall blow the trumpets.
6:5	And when they make a long blast with the ram's horn, as soon as you hear the sound of the trumpet, all the people shall shout with a great shout, and the wall of the city will fall down flat, and the people shall go up, each man straight ahead."
6:6	So Joshua son of Nun called the priests and said to them, "Take up the ark of the covenant, and let seven priests carry seven rams' horn trumpets before the ark of Yahweh."
6:7	And he said to the people, "Go forward and march around the city, and let the armed men pass on before the ark of Yahweh."
6:8	When Joshua had spoken to the people, the seven priests carrying the seven rams' horn trumpets before Yahweh went forward and blew the trumpets, with the ark of the covenant of Yahweh following them.
6:9	The armed men went before the priests who blew the trumpets, and the rear guard went after the ark, while the trumpets kept sounding.
6:10	But Joshua commanded the people, "You shall not shout or let your voice be heard, nor shall a word go out of your mouth, until the day I tell you, 'Shout!' Then you shall shout."
6:11	So he had the ark of Yahweh go around the city, circling it once; then they came into the camp and spent the night in the camp.
6:12	Joshua rose early in the morning, and the priests took up the ark of Yahweh.
6:13	The seven priests carrying the seven rams' horn trumpets before the ark of Yahweh went on, blowing the trumpets continually; the armed men went before them, and the rear guard went after the ark of Yahweh, while the trumpets kept sounding.
6:14	They marched around the city once on the second day and returned to the camp. They did this for six days.
6:15	On the seventh day they rose early, at the break of dawn, and marched around the city in the same manner seven times; only on that day did they march around the city seven times.
6:16	At the seventh time, when the priests had blown the trumpets, Joshua said to the people, "Shout, for Yahweh has given you the city!
6:17	The city and all that is in it shall be devoted to Yahweh for destruction. Only Rahab the prostitute shall live, she and all who are with her in the house, because she hid the messengers whom we sent.
6:18	But as for you, keep yourselves from the devoted things, lest you devote them to destruction and then take some of the devoted things, and so make the camp of Israel a thing for destruction and bring trouble upon it.
6:19	All the silver and gold, and the vessels of bronze and iron, are holy to Yahweh; they shall go into the treasury of Yahweh."
6:20	So the people shouted, and the trumpets were blown. As soon as the people heard the sound of the trumpet, the people shouted a great shout, and the wall fell down flat, and the people went up into the city, each man straight ahead, and they captured the city.
6:21	They devoted to destruction everything in the city, both man and woman, young and old, ox, sheep, and donkey, with the edge of the sword.
6:22	To the two men who had spied out the land Joshua said, "Go into the prostitute's house and bring out the woman and all who belong to her, as you swore to her."
6:23	So the young men who had been spies went in and brought out Rahab, her father, her mother, her brothers, and all who belonged to her; they brought out all her clan and settled them outside the camp of Israel.
6:24	They burned the city with fire, and everything in it; only the silver and gold, and the vessels of bronze and iron, they put into the treasury of the house of Yahweh.
6:25	But Rahab the prostitute, with her father's household and all who belonged to her, Joshua spared; and she has lived in the midst of Israel to this day, because she hid the messengers whom Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.
6:26	At that time Joshua laid an oath on them, saying, "Cursed before Yahweh be the man who rises up and rebuilds this city, Jericho! At the cost of his firstborn he shall lay its foundation, and at the cost of his youngest son he shall set up its gates."
6:27	So Yahweh was with Joshua, and his fame spread through all the land.
