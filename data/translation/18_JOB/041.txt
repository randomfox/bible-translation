41:1	Behold, the hope of a man is false; he is laid low even at the sight of him.
41:2	No one is so fierce that he dares to stir him up. Who then is he who can stand before me?
41:3	Who has first given to me, that I should repay him? Whatever is under the whole heaven is mine.
41:4	I will not keep silence concerning his limbs, or his mighty strength, or his goodly frame.
41:5	Who can strip off his outer garment? Who would come near him with a bridle?
41:6	Who can open the doors of his face? Around his teeth is terror.
41:7	His back is made of rows of shields, shut up closely as with a seal.
41:8	One is so near to another that no air can come between them.
41:9	They are joined one to another; they clasp each other and cannot be separated.
41:10	His sneezings flash forth light, and his eyes are like the eyelids of the dawn.
41:11	Out of his mouth go flaming torches; sparks of fire leap forth.
41:12	Out of his nostrils comes forth smoke, as from a boiling pot and burning rushes.
41:13	His breath kindles coals, and a flame comes forth from his mouth.
41:14	In his neck abides strength, and terror dances before him.
41:15	The folds of his flesh stick together, firmly cast on him and immovable.
41:16	His heart is hard as a stone, hard as the lower millstone.
41:17	When he raises himself up, the mighty are afraid; at the crashing they are beside themselves.
41:18	Though the sword reaches him, it does not avail, nor the spear, the dart, or the javelin.
41:19	He counts iron as straw, and bronze as rotten wood.
41:20	The arrow cannot make him flee; for him, sling stones are turned to stubble.
41:21	Clubs are counted as stubble; he laughs at the rattle of javelins.
41:22	His underparts are like sharp potsherds; he spreads himself like a threshing sledge on the mire.
41:23	He makes the deep boil like a pot; he makes the sea like a pot of ointment.
41:24	Behind him he leaves a shining wake; one would think the deep to be white-haired.
41:25	On earth there is not his like, a creature without fear.
41:26	He sees everything that is high; he is king over all the sons of pride."
