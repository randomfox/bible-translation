13:1	The Israelites again did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and Yahweh gave them into the hand of the Philistines for forty years.
13:2	There was a certain man from Zorah, of the clan of the Danites, whose name was Manoah. His wife was barren and had borne no children.
13:3	The angel of Yahweh appeared to the woman and said to her, "Look, you are barren and have not borne children, but you will conceive and bear a son.
13:4	Now be careful not to drink wine or strong drink, and do not eat anything unclean,
13:5	for you are going to conceive and bear a son. No razor shall come upon his head, for the boy shall be a Nazirite to God from the womb, and he will begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines."
13:6	Then the woman came and told her husband, "A man of God came to me, and his appearance was like the appearance of the angel of God, very awe-inspiring. I did not ask him where he was from, and he did not tell me his name.
13:7	But he said to me, 'You are going to conceive and bear a son. So now drink no wine or strong drink, and eat nothing unclean, for the boy shall be a Nazirite to God from the womb to the day of his death.'"
13:8	Then Manoah entreated Yahweh and said, "Please, Lord, let the man of God whom you sent come to us again and teach us what we should do for the boy who is to be born."
13:9	God listened to the voice of Manoah, and the angel of God came again to the woman as she was sitting in the field; but Manoah her husband was not with her.
13:10	So the woman ran quickly and told her husband, "Look, the man who came to me the other day has appeared to me!"
13:11	Manoah got up and followed his wife, came to the man, and said to him, "Are you the man who spoke to the woman?" He said, "I am."
13:12	Manoah said, "Now when your words come true, what is to be the boy's manner of life, and what is he to do?"
13:13	The angel of Yahweh said to Manoah, "Of all that I said to the woman, let her be careful.
13:14	She must not eat anything that comes from the grapevine, she must not drink wine or strong drink, and she must not eat anything unclean. All that I commanded her, she must observe."
13:15	Manoah said to the angel of Yahweh, "Please let us detain you and prepare a young goat for you."
13:16	The angel of Yahweh said to Manoah, "Even if you detain me, I will not eat your food; but if you prepare a burnt offering, offer it to Yahweh." For Manoah did not know that he was the angel of Yahweh.
13:17	Manoah said to the angel of Yahweh, "What is your name, so that when your word comes true we may honor you?"
13:18	The angel of Yahweh said to him, "Why do you ask my name? It is wonderful."
13:19	So Manoah took the young goat and the grain offering and offered them on the rock to Yahweh, and he did a wondrous thing while Manoah and his wife were watching.
13:20	As the flame went up from the altar toward heaven, the angel of Yahweh went up in the flame of the altar, while Manoah and his wife were watching, and they fell on their faces to the ground.
13:21	The angel of Yahweh did not appear again to Manoah and his wife. Then Manoah knew that he was the angel of Yahweh.
13:22	And Manoah said to his wife, "We will surely die, for we have seen God."
13:23	But his wife said to him, "If Yahweh had wanted to kill us, he would not have accepted a burnt offering and a grain offering from our hand, nor shown us all these things, nor now announced to us such things as this."
13:24	The woman bore a son and named him Samson. The boy grew, and Yahweh blessed him.
13:25	And the Spirit of Yahweh began to stir him in Mahaneh-dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.
