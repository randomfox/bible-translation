2:1	The angel of Yahweh went up from Gilgal to Bochim and said, "I brought you up from Egypt and brought you into the land that I swore to give your ancestors. I said, 'I will never break My covenant with you,
2:2	and you, you shall make no covenant with the inhabitants of this land; you shall tear down their altars.' But you have not obeyed My voice. What is this you have done?
2:3	So now I say, I will not drive them out before you; they will be thorns in your sides, and their gods will be a snare to you."
2:4	When the angel of Yahweh spoke these words to all the people of Israel, the people raised their voices and wept.
2:5	They named that place Bochim, and they sacrificed there to Yahweh.
2:6	When Joshua had sent the people away, the people of Israel went each to his own inheritance to take possession of the land.
2:7	The people served Yahweh all the days of Joshua and all the days of the elders who outlived Joshua, who had seen all the great work that Yahweh had done for Israel.
2:8	Joshua son of Nun, the servant of Yahweh, died at the age of a hundred and ten.
2:9	They buried him within the territory of his inheritance at Timnath-heres in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.
2:10	And all that generation also was gathered to their ancestors, and another generation arose after them who did not know Yahweh or the work He had done for Israel.
2:11	The people of Israel did what was evil in the sight of Yahweh and served the Baals.
2:12	They abandoned Yahweh, the God of their ancestors, Who had brought them out of the land of Egypt, and followed other gods, from among the gods of the peoples around them, and bowed down to them; and they provoked Yahweh to anger.
2:13	They abandoned Yahweh and served Baal and the Ashtaroth.
2:14	So the anger of Yahweh burned against Israel, and He gave them into the hands of plunderers who plundered them, and He sold them into the hands of their enemies all around, so that they could no longer stand before their enemies.
2:15	Wherever they went out, the hand of Yahweh was against them for harm, as Yahweh had said and as Yahweh had sworn to them; and they were in great distress.
2:16	Then Yahweh raised up judges, who saved them from the hand of those who plundered them.
2:17	Yet they did not listen even to their judges, for they prostituted themselves after other gods and bowed down to them. They quickly turned aside from the way their ancestors had walked, obeying the commandments of Yahweh; they did not do so.
2:18	Whenever Yahweh raised up judges for them, Yahweh was with the judge and saved them from the hand of their enemies all the days of the judge, for Yahweh was moved to pity by their groaning because of those who oppressed and afflicted them.
2:19	But whenever the judge died, they would turn back and act more corruptly than their ancestors, following other gods to serve them and bow down to them. They did not give up any of their practices or their stubborn ways.
2:20	So the anger of Yahweh burned against Israel, and He said, "Because this nation has transgressed My covenant that I commanded their ancestors and has not obeyed My voice,
2:21	I for My part will no longer drive out before them any of the nations that Joshua left when he died,
2:22	in order to test Israel by them, whether or not they will keep the way of Yahweh and walk in it as their ancestors did."
2:23	So Yahweh left those nations, not driving them out quickly, and He did not give them into the hand of Joshua.
