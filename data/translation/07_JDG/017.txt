17:1	There was a man from the hill country of Ephraim whose name was Micaiah.
17:2	He said to his mother, "The eleven hundred pieces of silver that were taken from you, about which you uttered a curse and even said it in my hearing, look, the silver is with me; I took it." And his mother said, "Blessed be my son by Yahweh!"
17:3	He returned the eleven hundred pieces of silver to his mother, and his mother said, "I solemnly consecrate the silver to Yahweh from my hand for my son, to make a carved image and a cast image. So now I will return it to you."
17:4	When he returned the silver to his mother, his mother took two hundred pieces of silver and gave it to the silversmith, who made it into a carved image and a cast image; and it was in the house of Micaiah.
17:5	Now the man Micah had a shrine, and he made an ephod and household gods, and he ordained one of his sons, who became his priest.
17:6	In those days there was no king in Israel; everyone did what was right in his own eyes.
17:7	There was a young man from Bethlehem in Judah, of the clan of Judah, who was a Levite, and he was residing there as a sojourner.
17:8	The man left the town of Bethlehem in Judah to sojourn wherever he could find a place. As he journeyed, he came to the hill country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah.
17:9	Micah said to him, "Where do you come from?" He said to him, "I am a Levite from Bethlehem in Judah, and I am going to sojourn wherever I can find a place."
17:10	Micah said to him, "Stay with me and be a father and a priest to me, and I will give you ten pieces of silver a year, a set of clothes, and your food." So the Levite went in.
17:11	The Levite agreed to stay with the man, and the young man became to him like one of his sons.
17:12	Micah ordained the Levite, and the young man became his priest and lived in the house of Micah.
17:13	Then Micah said, "Now I know that Yahweh will prosper me, because I have a Levite as priest."
