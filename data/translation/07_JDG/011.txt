11:1	Jephthah the Gileadite was a mighty warrior, but he was the son of a prostitute; Gilead had fathered Jephthah.
11:2	Gilead's wife bore him sons, and when the wife's sons grew up they drove Jephthah out and said to him, "You will not inherit in our father's house, for you are the son of another woman."
11:3	So Jephthah fled from his brothers and lived in the land of Tob, and worthless men gathered around Jephthah and went out with him.
11:4	After some time the Ammonites made war on Israel.
11:5	When the Ammonites made war on Israel, the elders of Gilead went to bring Jephthah from the land of Tob.
11:6	They said to Jephthah, "Come and be our commander, so that we can fight the Ammonites."
11:7	But Jephthah said to the elders of Gilead, "Did you not hate me and drive me out of my father's house? Why have you come to me now, when you are in trouble?"
11:8	The elders of Gilead said to Jephthah, "That is why we have turned to you now: come with us and fight the Ammonites, and you will be our head, over all the inhabitants of Gilead."
11:9	Jephthah said to the elders of Gilead, "If you bring me back to fight the Ammonites and Yahweh gives them over to me, I will be your head."
11:10	The elders of Gilead said to Jephthah, "Yahweh will be witness between us; we will surely do as you say."
11:11	So Jephthah went with the elders of Gilead, and the people made him head and commander over them. And Jephthah spoke all his words before Yahweh at Mizpah.
11:12	Then Jephthah sent messengers to the king of the Ammonites, saying, "What is there between you and me, that you have come against me to fight against my land?"
11:13	The king of the Ammonites said to Jephthah's messengers, "Because Israel took my land when they came up from Egypt, from the Arnon to the Jabbok and to the Jordan. Now give it back peaceably."
11:14	Jephthah again sent messengers to the king of the Ammonites
11:15	and said to him, "Thus says Jephthah: Israel did not take the land of Moab or the land of the Ammonites.
11:16	For when they came up from Egypt, Israel went through the wilderness to the Red Sea and came to Kadesh.
11:17	Israel sent messengers to the king of Edom, saying, 'Please let me pass through your land,' but the king of Edom would not listen. They also sent to the king of Moab, but he was not willing. So Israel stayed at Kadesh.
11:18	Then they went through the wilderness, went around the land of Edom and the land of Moab, came to the east side of the land of Moab, and camped on the other side of the Arnon. They did not enter the territory of Moab, for the Arnon was the border of Moab.
11:19	Israel then sent messengers to Sihon king of the Amorites, king of Heshbon, and Israel said to him, 'Please let us pass through your land to our place.'
11:20	But Sihon did not trust Israel to pass through his territory; Sihon gathered all his people, camped at Jahaz, and fought with Israel.
11:21	And Yahweh the God of Israel gave Sihon and all his people into the hand of Israel, and they struck them down. So Israel took possession of all the land of the Amorites, who inhabited that land.
11:22	They took possession of all the territory of the Amorites, from the Arnon to the Jabbok and from the wilderness to the Jordan.
11:23	So now Yahweh the God of Israel has dispossessed the Amorites before His people Israel; and are you to take possession of it?
11:24	Do you not take possession of whatever Chemosh your god gives you to possess? And all that Yahweh our God has dispossessed before us, we will possess.
11:25	Now are you any better than Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab? Did he ever contend with Israel, or did he ever fight against them?
11:26	While Israel lived in Heshbon and its villages, in Aroer and its villages, and in all the cities along the Arnon for three hundred years, why did you not recover them during that time?
11:27	I have not sinned against you, but you are doing me wrong by making war on me. Let Yahweh, the Judge, judge today between the Israelites and the Ammonites."
11:28	But the king of the Ammonites did not listen to the words that Jephthah sent him.
11:29	Then the Spirit of Yahweh came upon Jephthah, and he passed through Gilead and Manasseh, passed through Mizpah of Gilead, and from Mizpah of Gilead he passed on to the Ammonites.
11:30	And Jephthah made a vow to Yahweh and said, "If You will indeed give the Ammonites into my hand,
11:31	then whatever comes out of the doors of my house to meet me when I return safely from the Ammonites shall be Yahweh's, and I will offer it up as a burnt offering."
11:32	So Jephthah crossed over to the Ammonites to fight against them, and Yahweh gave them into his hand.
11:33	He struck them from Aroer to the approach of Minnith, twenty cities, and as far as Abel-keramim, with a very great slaughter. So the Ammonites were subdued before the Israelites.
11:34	Then Jephthah came to his home at Mizpah, and there was his daughter coming out to meet him with tambourines and dancing! She was his only child; besides her he had neither son nor daughter.
11:35	When he saw her, he tore his clothes and said, "Alas, my daughter! You have brought me very low, and you have become one who troubles me! For I have opened my mouth to Yahweh, and I cannot take it back."
11:36	She said to him, "My father, you have opened your mouth to Yahweh; do to me according to what has gone out of your mouth, now that Yahweh has taken vengeance for you on your enemies, the Ammonites."
11:37	And she said to her father, "Let this be done for me: leave me alone for two months, so that I may go and wander on the mountains and weep over my virginity, I and my companions."
11:38	He said, "Go." And he sent her away for two months. She went with her companions and wept over her virginity on the mountains.
11:39	At the end of two months she returned to her father, and he did to her the vow he had vowed. She had never known a man. And it became a custom in Israel:
11:40	year after year the daughters of Israel go to commemorate the daughter of Jephthah the Gileadite four days in the year.
