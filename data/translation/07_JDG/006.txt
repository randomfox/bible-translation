6:1	The people of Israel did what was evil in the sight of Yahweh, and Yahweh gave them into the hand of Midian for seven years.
6:2	The hand of Midian prevailed over Israel. Because of Midian the people of Israel made for themselves the shelters that are in the mountains, the caves, and the strongholds.
6:3	Whenever Israel had sown seed, Midian and Amalek and the people of the East would come up and attack them.
6:4	They would camp against them and destroy the produce of the land as far as Gaza, leaving no sustenance in Israel, neither sheep nor ox nor donkey.
6:5	For they would come up with their livestock and their tents, coming like a swarm of locusts for number; they and their camels were countless, and they came into the land to ravage it.
6:6	So Israel was brought very low because of Midian, and the people of Israel cried out to Yahweh.
6:7	When the people of Israel cried out to Yahweh on account of Midian,
6:8	Yahweh sent a prophet to the people of Israel, who said to them, "Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel: 'I brought you up from Egypt and brought you out of the house of slavery.
6:9	I rescued you from the hand of the Egyptians and from the hand of all who oppressed you; I drove them out before you and gave you their land.
6:10	And I said to you, "I am Yahweh your God; you shall not fear the gods of the Amorites in whose land you live." But you have not obeyed my voice.'"
6:11	Then the angel of Yahweh came and sat under the oak at Ophrah, which belonged to Joash the Abiezrite, while his son Gideon was beating out wheat in the winepress to hide it from Midian.
6:12	The angel of Yahweh appeared to him and said to him, "Yahweh is with you, mighty warrior."
6:13	Gideon said to him, "Excuse me, my lord, but if Yahweh is with us, why then has all this happened to us? Where are all his wonders that our ancestors told us about, saying, 'Did not Yahweh bring us up from Egypt?' But now Yahweh has abandoned us and given us into the hand of Midian."
6:14	Then Yahweh turned to him and said, "Go in this strength of yours and save Israel from the hand of Midian. Am I not sending you?"
6:15	He said to him, "Excuse me, Lord, but how can I save Israel? Look, my clan is the weakest in Manasseh, and I am the least in my father's house."
6:16	Yahweh said to him, "But I will be with you, and you will strike down Midian as though it were one man."
6:17	He said to him, "If I have found favor in your sight, then give me a sign that it is you who are speaking with me.
6:18	Please do not leave here until I come back to you and bring out my offering and set it before you." He said, "I will stay until you return."
6:19	So Gideon went in and prepared a young goat and unleavened cakes from an ephah of flour. He put the meat in a basket and the broth in a pot, brought them out to him under the oak, and presented them.
6:20	The angel of God said to him, "Take the meat and the unleavened cakes, put them on this rock, and pour out the broth." And he did so.
6:21	Then the angel of Yahweh reached out the tip of the staff that was in his hand and touched the meat and the unleavened cakes, and fire sprang up from the rock and consumed the meat and the unleavened cakes. And the angel of Yahweh vanished from his sight.
6:22	Then Gideon realized that it was the angel of Yahweh, and Gideon said, "Alas, Lord Yahweh! For I have seen the angel of Yahweh face to face."
6:23	But Yahweh said to him, "Peace be with you. Do not be afraid; you will not die."
6:24	So Gideon built an altar there to Yahweh and called it "Yahweh is Peace." To this day it still stands at Ophrah of the Abiezrites.
6:25	That night Yahweh said to him, "Take your father's young bull, and the second bull seven years old, and pull down the altar of Baal that belongs to your father, and cut down the Asherah that is beside it.
6:26	Then build an altar to Yahweh your God on top of this stronghold, with the stones laid in proper order. Take the second bull and offer it as a burnt offering with the wood of the Asherah that you cut down."
6:27	So Gideon took ten men from his servants and did as Yahweh had told him. But because he was afraid of his father's household and the men of the town to do it by day, he did it by night.
6:28	When the men of the town rose early in the morning, there was the altar of Baal torn down, the Asherah beside it cut down, and the second bull offered on the altar that had been built.
6:29	They said to one another, "Who has done this thing?" After they had inquired and searched, they said, "Gideon son of Joash has done this thing."
6:30	Then the men of the town said to Joash, "Bring out your son so that he may die, for he has torn down the altar of Baal and cut down the Asherah beside it."
6:31	But Joash said to all who stood against him, "Will you plead for Baal? Will you save him? Whoever pleads for him shall be put to death by morning. If he is a god, let him plead for himself, since someone has torn down his altar."
6:32	So on that day Gideon was called Jerubbaal, meaning, "Let Baal plead against him," because he had torn down his altar.
6:33	Then all Midian and Amalek and the people of the East gathered together, crossed over, and camped in the Valley of Jezreel.
6:34	But the spirit of Yahweh clothed Gideon; he blew the ram's horn, and the Abiezrites were called out to follow him.
6:35	He sent messengers throughout all Manasseh, and they too were called out to follow him. He also sent messengers to Asher, Zebulun, and Naphtali, and they came up to meet them.
6:36	Then Gideon said to God, "If you are going to save Israel by my hand, as you have said,
6:37	look, I am placing a fleece of wool on the threshing floor. If there is dew on the fleece alone, and all the ground is dry, then I will know that you will save Israel by my hand, as you have said."
6:38	And so it was. When he rose early the next day and squeezed the fleece, he wrung enough dew from the fleece to fill a bowl with water.
6:39	Then Gideon said to God, "Do not let your anger burn against me; let me speak just once more. Please let me make a test with the fleece just once more: let it be dry on the fleece only, and on all the ground let there be dew."
6:40	And God did so that night: it was dry on the fleece only, and on all the ground there was dew.
