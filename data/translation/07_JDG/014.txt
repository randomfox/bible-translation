14:1	Samson went down to Timnah, and at Timnah he saw a woman, one of the daughters of the Philistines.
14:2	He came up and told his father and mother, "I saw a woman at Timnah, one of the daughters of the Philistines. Now get her for me as my wife."
14:3	His father and mother said to him, "Is there no woman among the daughters of your relatives or among all my people, that you must go to take a wife from the uncircumcised Philistines?" But Samson said to his father, "Get her for me, for she is right in my eyes."
14:4	His father and mother did not know that this was from Yahweh, for he was seeking an occasion against the Philistines. At that time the Philistines were ruling over Israel.
14:5	Then Samson went down with his father and mother to Timnah, and they came to the vineyards of Timnah. And suddenly a young lion came roaring toward him.
14:6	The Spirit of Yahweh rushed upon him, and he tore it apart as one tears apart a young goat, though he had nothing in his hand. But he did not tell his father or his mother what he had done.
14:7	Then he went down and talked with the woman, and she was right in Samson's eyes.
14:8	After some time he returned to take her, and he turned aside to see the carcass of the lion, and there was a swarm of bees in the body of the lion, and honey.
14:9	He scraped it out into his palms and went on, eating as he went. He came to his father and mother and gave some to them, and they ate; but he did not tell them that he had scraped the honey out of the lion's body.
14:10	His father went down to the woman, and Samson held a feast there, for that is what young men used to do.
14:11	When they saw him, they brought thirty companions to be with him.
14:12	Samson said to them, "Let me pose a riddle to you. If you can tell me its answer within the seven days of the feast and find it out, I will give you thirty linen garments and thirty changes of clothes.
14:13	But if you cannot tell me, then you shall give me thirty linen garments and thirty changes of clothes." They said to him, "Pose your riddle, and let us hear it."
14:14	He said to them, "Out of the eater came something to eat; out of the strong came something sweet." And for three days they could not tell the riddle.
14:15	On the seventh day they said to Samson's wife, "Coax your husband into telling us the riddle, or we will burn you and your father's house with fire. Did you invite us here to impoverish us?"
14:16	So Samson's wife wept over him and said, "You only hate me; you do not love me. You have posed a riddle to my people, and you have not told me." He said to her, "Look, I have not told my father or my mother; should I tell you?"
14:17	She wept over him the seven days that their feast lasted, and on the seventh day he told her, because she pressed him hard. Then she told the riddle to her people.
14:18	The men of the city said to him on the seventh day, before the sun went down, "What is sweeter than honey? What is stronger than a lion?" He said to them, "If you had not plowed with my heifer, you would not have found out my riddle."
14:19	Then the Spirit of Yahweh rushed upon him, and he went down to Ashkelon and struck down thirty of their men, took their spoil, and gave the changes of clothes to those who had told the riddle. His anger burned, and he went up to his father's house.
14:20	And Samson's wife was given to his companion, who had been his best man.
