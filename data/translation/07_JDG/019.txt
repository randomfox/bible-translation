19:1	In those days, when there was no king in Israel, a Levite was residing as a sojourner in the remote parts of the hill country of Ephraim. He took for himself a concubine from Bethlehem in Judah.
19:2	But his concubine was unfaithful to him, and she left him and went to her father's house at Bethlehem in Judah, and she was there for some time, four months.
19:3	Then her husband set out and went after her to speak to her heart and bring her back. He had his servant with him and a pair of donkeys. She brought him into her father's house, and when the young woman's father saw him, he was glad to meet him.
19:4	His father-in-law, the young woman's father, held him there, and he stayed with him three days; they ate and drank and spent the nights there.
19:5	On the fourth day they got up early in the morning, and he prepared to leave, but the young woman's father said to his son-in-law, "Refresh yourself with a bit of bread, and after that you can go."
19:6	So the two of them sat down and ate and drank together. Then the young woman's father said to the man, "Please agree to spend the night, and let your heart be merry."
19:7	When the man got up to go, his father-in-law pressed him, so he stayed the night there again.
19:8	On the fifth day he got up early in the morning to leave, and the young woman's father said, "Please refresh yourself, and linger until the day declines." So the two of them ate.
19:9	When the man got up to go, he and his concubine and his servant, his father-in-law, the young woman's father, said to him, "Look, the day has waned toward evening. Please spend the night. See, the day is coming to a close; spend the night here and let your heart be merry. Then tomorrow you can rise early for your journey and go home."
19:10	But the man was not willing to spend the night. He got up and left and came opposite Jebus, that is, Jerusalem. He had with him a pair of saddled donkeys, and his concubine was with him.
19:11	When they were near Jebus, the day was far spent, and the servant said to his master, "Come, let us turn aside into this city of the Jebusites and spend the night in it."
19:12	But his master said to him, "We will not turn aside into a city of foreigners who are not of the Israelites; we will go on to Gibeah."
19:13	And he said to his servant, "Come, let us approach one of these places and spend the night at Gibeah or at Ramah."
19:14	So they passed on and went their way, and the sun set on them near Gibeah, which belongs to Benjamin.
19:15	They turned aside there to go in and spend the night at Gibeah. He went in and sat down in the city square, but no one took them into his house to spend the night.
19:16	Then an old man came in from his work in the field in the evening. The man was from the hill country of Ephraim, and he was residing in Gibeah; but the men of the place were Benjaminites.
19:17	He looked up and saw the traveler in the city square, and the old man said, "Where are you going, and where do you come from?"
19:18	He said to him, "We are passing from Bethlehem in Judah to the remote parts of the hill country of Ephraim. I am from there; I went to Bethlehem in Judah, and I am going to the house of Yahweh. But no one is taking me into his house.
19:19	We have both straw and fodder for our donkeys, and also bread and wine for me and your maidservant and the young man with your servants. There is no lack of anything."
19:20	The old man said, "Peace to you! Let all your needs be on me; only do not spend the night in the square."
19:21	So he brought him into his house and gave fodder to the donkeys. They washed their feet, and ate and drank.
19:22	While they were making their hearts merry, the men of the city, worthless men, surrounded the house, beating on the door, and they said to the old man, the master of the house, "Bring out the man who came into your house, so that we may know him."
19:23	The man, the master of the house, went out to them and said to them, "No, my brothers, please do not do evil. Since this man has come into my house, do not do this outrageous thing.
19:24	Here is my virgin daughter and his concubine. Let me bring them out now. Violate them and do to them whatever seems good to you; but against this man do not do this outrageous thing."
19:25	But the men would not listen to him. So the man seized his concubine and put her outside to them. They knew her and abused her all night until morning, and let her go as the dawn was breaking.
19:26	As morning appeared, the woman came and fell down at the entrance of the man's house where her master was, until it was light.
19:27	Her master got up in the morning, opened the doors of the house, and went out to go on his way, and there was the woman, his concubine, lying at the entrance of the house with her hands on the threshold.
19:28	He said to her, "Get up, let us go." But there was no answer. Then he put her on the donkey, and the man set out and went to his home.
19:29	When he came to his house, he took the knife, took hold of his concubine, and cut her up limb by limb into twelve pieces, and sent her throughout all the territory of Israel.
19:30	And everyone who saw it said, "Such a thing has never happened or been seen from the day the Israelites came up from the land of Egypt until this day. Consider it, take counsel, and speak!"
