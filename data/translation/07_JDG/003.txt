3:1	These are the nations that Yahweh left in order to test Israel by them, all those who had not known any of the wars of Canaan,
3:2	only so that the generations of the people of Israel might know war, to teach it to those who had not known it before:
3:3	the five lords of the Philistines, all the Canaanites, the Sidonians, and the Hivites who lived in the Lebanon mountains, from Mount Baal-hermon as far as Lebo-hamath.
3:4	They were there to test Israel by them, to know whether they would obey the commandments of Yahweh, which He had commanded their ancestors through Moses.
3:5	So the people of Israel lived among the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites.
3:6	They took their daughters as wives for themselves and gave their own daughters to their sons, and they served their gods.
3:7	The people of Israel did what was evil in the sight of Yahweh. They forgot Yahweh their God and served the Baals and the Asherahs.
3:8	So the anger of Yahweh burned against Israel, and He sold them into the hand of Cushan-rishathaim king of Aram-naharaim, and the people of Israel served Cushan-rishathaim eight years.
3:9	But when the people of Israel cried out to Yahweh, Yahweh raised up a deliverer for the people of Israel, who saved them: Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother.
3:10	The spirit of Yahweh came upon him, and he judged Israel. He went out to war, and Yahweh gave Cushan-rishathaim king of Aram into his hand, and his hand prevailed over Cushan-rishathaim.
3:11	So the land had rest forty years. Then Othniel son of Kenaz died.
3:12	Again the people of Israel did what was evil in the sight of Yahweh, and Yahweh strengthened Eglon king of Moab against Israel, because they had done what was evil in the sight of Yahweh.
3:13	He gathered the Ammonites and the Amalekites to himself, went and defeated Israel, and they took possession of the City of Palms.
3:14	The people of Israel served Eglon king of Moab eighteen years.
3:15	But when the people of Israel cried out to Yahweh, Yahweh raised up a deliverer for them, Ehud son of Gera, the Benjaminite, a man restricted in his right hand. The people of Israel sent tribute by him to Eglon king of Moab.
3:16	Ehud made himself a sword with two edges, a gomed in length, and he strapped it under his clothes on his right thigh.
3:17	He presented the tribute to Eglon king of Moab. Now Eglon was a very fat man.
3:18	When he had finished presenting the tribute, he sent away the people who had carried the tribute.
3:19	But he himself turned back at the carved images near Gilgal and said, "I have a secret message for you, O king." The king said, "Silence!" and all who were standing by him went out from him.
3:20	Ehud came to him as he was sitting alone in his cool upper room, and Ehud said, "I have a message from God for you." He rose from his seat.
3:21	Then Ehud reached out with his left hand, took the sword from his right thigh, and thrust it into his belly.
3:22	Even the hilt went in after the blade, and the fat closed over the blade, for he did not draw the sword out of his belly; and the dung came out.
3:23	Then Ehud went out into the porch, closed the doors of the upper room behind him, and locked them.
3:24	After he had gone out, the king's servants came, and they saw that the doors of the upper room were locked. They said, "He must be relieving himself in the cool chamber."
3:25	They waited until they were embarrassed, and still he did not open the doors of the upper room. So they took the key and opened them, and there was their lord lying dead on the floor.
3:26	Ehud escaped while they delayed. He passed the carved images and escaped to Seirah.
3:27	When he arrived, he blew the ram's horn in the hill country of Ephraim, and the people of Israel went down with him from the hill country, with him at their head.
3:28	He said to them, "Follow me, for Yahweh has given your enemies the Moabites into your hand." So they went down after him, seized the fords of the Jordan against the Moabites, and allowed no one to cross.
3:29	At that time they struck down about ten thousand Moabites, all strong, able-bodied men; not one escaped.
3:30	So Moab was subdued that day under the hand of Israel, and the land had rest eighty years.
3:31	After him came Shamgar son of Anath, who struck down six hundred Philistines with an oxgoad; he too saved Israel.
