21:1	Now the men of Israel had sworn at Mizpah, "None of us will give his daughter in marriage to Benjamin."
21:2	And the people came to Bethel and sat there before God until evening, and they lifted up their voices and wept bitterly.
21:3	They said, "O Yahweh, God of Israel, why has this happened in Israel, that today one tribe should be missing from Israel?"
21:4	The next day the people rose early and built an altar there, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings.
21:5	Then the Israelites said, "Who from all the tribes of Israel did not come up in the assembly to Yahweh?" For they had taken a great oath concerning anyone who did not come up to Yahweh at Mizpah: "He shall surely be put to death."
21:6	The Israelites felt sorry for Benjamin their brother and said, "Today one tribe has been cut off from Israel.
21:7	What shall we do for wives for those who are left, since we have sworn by Yahweh not to give them any of our daughters as wives?"
21:8	They said, "Which one of the tribes of Israel did not come up to Yahweh at Mizpah?" And it turned out that no one had come to the camp from Jabesh-gilead, to the assembly.
21:9	For when the people were mustered, not one of the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead was there.
21:10	So the congregation sent twelve thousand of their bravest men there and commanded them, "Go and strike the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead with the edge of the sword, including the women and the little ones.
21:11	This is what you shall do: every male and every woman who has lain with a male you shall devote to destruction."
21:12	Among the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead they found four hundred young virgins who had not known a man by lying with a male, and they brought them to the camp at Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan.
21:13	Then the whole congregation sent word and spoke to the Benjaminites who were at the rock of Rimmon, and proclaimed peace to them.
21:14	Benjamin returned at that time, and they gave them the women whom they had kept alive from the women of Jabesh-gilead; but there were not enough for them.
21:15	The people felt sorry for Benjamin, because Yahweh had made a breach in the tribes of Israel.
21:16	Then the elders of the congregation said, "What shall we do for wives for those who are left, since the women of Benjamin have been destroyed?"
21:17	And they said, "There must be an inheritance for the survivors of Benjamin, so that a tribe will not be wiped out from Israel.
21:18	Yet we cannot give them wives from our daughters, for the Israelites have sworn, 'Cursed is anyone who gives a wife to Benjamin.'"
21:19	So they said, "Look, there is the yearly festival of Yahweh at Shiloh, which is north of Bethel, east of the highway that goes up from Bethel to Shechem, and south of Lebonah."
21:20	And they commanded the Benjaminites, "Go and lie in wait in the vineyards,
21:21	and watch. When the daughters of Shiloh come out to dance in the dances, come out of the vineyards, and each of you seize a wife for himself from the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin.
21:22	And when their fathers or their brothers come to complain to us, we will say to them, 'Be gracious to us concerning them, because we did not take a wife for each man in the battle; and you did not give them to them, or you would now be guilty.'"
21:23	The Benjaminites did so; they took wives according to their number from the dancers whom they carried off. Then they went and returned to their inheritance, rebuilt the towns, and lived in them.
21:24	And the Israelites departed from there at that time, each to his tribe and his clan, and each went out from there to his own inheritance.
21:25	In those days there was no king in Israel; everyone did what was right in his own eyes.
