16:1	Samson went to Gaza, and there he saw a prostitute, and he went in to her.
16:2	The Gazites were told, "Samson has come here." So they surrounded the place and lay in wait for him all night at the city gate. They kept quiet all night, saying, "At morning light we will kill him."
16:3	But Samson lay there until midnight. At midnight he got up, took hold of the doors of the city gate and the two gateposts, pulled them up, bar and all, put them on his shoulders, and carried them up to the top of the hill that faces Hebron.
16:4	After this he fell in love with a woman in the Valley of Sorek whose name was Delilah.
16:5	The lords of the Philistines came up to her and said to her, "Coax him and find out where his great strength lies, and how we can overpower him, so that we may bind him to subdue him. And we will each give you eleven hundred pieces of silver."
16:6	So Delilah said to Samson, "Please tell me where your great strength lies, and how you could be bound to subdue you."
16:7	Samson said to her, "If they bind me with seven fresh bowstrings that have not been dried, then I will become weak and be like any other man."
16:8	Then the lords of the Philistines brought up to her seven fresh bowstrings that had not been dried, and she bound him with them.
16:9	She had men lying in wait in an inner room. And she said to him, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" But he snapped the bowstrings as a strand of tow snaps when it touches fire. So his strength was not discovered.
16:10	Then Delilah said to Samson, "Look, you have mocked me and told me lies. Now please tell me how you could be bound."
16:11	He said to her, "If they bind me tightly with new ropes that have never been used for work, then I will become weak and be like any other man."
16:12	So Delilah took new ropes and bound him with them and said to him, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" The men lying in wait were in the inner room. But he snapped them off his arms like thread.
16:13	Then Delilah said to Samson, "Until now you have mocked me and told me lies. Tell me how you could be bound." He said to her, "If you weave the seven locks of my head into the web of the loom."
16:14	So she fastened it with the pin and said to him, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" But he awoke from his sleep and pulled away the pin, the loom, and the web.
16:15	She said to him, "How can you say, 'I love you,' when your heart is not with me? These three times you have mocked me and have not told me where your great strength lies."
16:16	When she pressed him with her words day after day and urged him, his soul was worn out to the point of death.
16:17	So he told her all his heart and said to her, "A razor has never come upon my head, for I have been a Nazirite to God from my mother's womb. If I am shaved, my strength will leave me, and I will become weak and be like all other men."
16:18	When Delilah saw that he had told her all his heart, she sent and called the lords of the Philistines, saying, "Come up this once, for he has told me all his heart." Then the lords of the Philistines came up to her and brought the silver in their hands.
16:19	She made him sleep on her knees, and she called a man and had him shave off the seven locks of his head. Then she began to subdue him, and his strength left him.
16:20	She said, "The Philistines are upon you, Samson!" He awoke from his sleep and said, "I will go out as at other times and shake myself free." But he did not know that Yahweh had left him.
16:21	The Philistines seized him, gouged out his eyes, and brought him down to Gaza. They bound him with bronze shackles, and he became a grinder at the mill in the prison.
16:22	But the hair of his head began to grow again after it had been shaved.
16:23	Now the lords of the Philistines gathered to offer a great sacrifice to Dagon their god and to rejoice, and they said, "Our god has given Samson our enemy into our hand."
16:24	When the people saw him, they praised their god, for they said, "Our god has given into our hand our enemy, the one who laid waste our land and multiplied our slain."
16:25	When their hearts were merry, they said, "Call Samson, so that he may entertain us." So they called Samson out of the prison, and he performed before them. They made him stand between the pillars.
16:26	Samson said to the young man who held him by the hand, "Let me alone, and let me feel the pillars on which the house rests, so that I may lean against them."
16:27	Now the house was full of men and women, and all the lords of the Philistines were there, and on the roof were about three thousand men and women watching Samson perform.
16:28	Then Samson called to Yahweh and said, "Lord Yahweh, please remember me and please strengthen me only this once, O God, so that I may take vengeance on the Philistines, one act of vengeance for my two eyes."
16:29	And Samson grasped the two middle pillars on which the house rested and braced himself against them, one with his right hand and the other with his left.
16:30	Samson said, "Let me die with the Philistines!" Then he pushed with all his might, and the house fell on the lords and on all the people who were in it. So the dead whom he killed at his death were more than those he had killed during his life.
16:31	Then his brothers and all his father's house came down, took him, brought him up, and buried him between Zorah and Eshtaol in the tomb of Manoah his father. He had judged Israel twenty years.
