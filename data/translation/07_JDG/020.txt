20:1	Then all the Israelites came out, from Dan to Beersheba, including the land of Gilead, and the congregation assembled as one man to Yahweh at Mizpah.
20:2	The leaders of all the people, of all the tribes of Israel, took their places in the assembly of the people of God, four hundred thousand foot soldiers who drew the sword.
20:3	(The Benjaminites heard that the Israelites had gone up to Mizpah.) And the Israelites said, "Tell us, how did this evil happen?"
20:4	The Levite, the husband of the murdered woman, answered, "I came with my concubine to Gibeah, which belongs to Benjamin, to spend the night.
20:5	The citizens of Gibeah rose against me and surrounded the house at night because of me. They intended to kill me, and they raped my concubine, and she died.
20:6	So I took my concubine, cut her in pieces, and sent her throughout all the country of Israel's inheritance, for they have committed a lewd and outrageous act in Israel.
20:7	Here you are, all you Israelites; give your word and counsel here."
20:8	All the people rose as one man, saying, "None of us will go to his tent, and none of us will turn back to his house.
20:9	Now this is what we will do to Gibeah: we will go against it by lot.
20:10	We will take ten men out of a hundred from all the tribes of Israel, a hundred out of a thousand, and a thousand out of ten thousand, to bring provisions for the troops, so that when they come to Gibeah of Benjamin they may repay it for all the outrage it has committed in Israel."
20:11	So all the men of Israel gathered against the city, united as one man.
20:12	The tribes of Israel sent men through all the tribe of Benjamin, saying, "What is this evil that has happened among you?
20:13	Now hand over the men, those worthless men in Gibeah, so that we may put them to death and purge the evil from Israel." But the Benjaminites would not listen to the voice of their brothers, the Israelites.
20:14	Instead, the Benjaminites gathered from their cities at Gibeah to go out to battle against the Israelites.
20:15	On that day the Benjaminites mustered from their cities twenty-six thousand men who drew the sword, besides the inhabitants of Gibeah, who mustered seven hundred chosen men.
20:16	Among all these troops there were seven hundred chosen men who were left-handed; every one of them could sling a stone at a hair and not miss.
20:17	The men of Israel, apart from Benjamin, mustered four hundred thousand men who drew the sword, all of them men of war.
20:18	They set out and went up to Bethel and inquired of God. The Israelites said, "Who of us shall go up first to battle against the Benjaminites?" Yahweh said, "Judah first."
20:19	Then the Israelites rose in the morning and camped against Gibeah.
20:20	The men of Israel went out to battle against Benjamin, and the men of Israel drew up their battle lines against them at Gibeah.
20:21	The Benjaminites came out of Gibeah and struck down twenty-two thousand men of Israel to the ground that day.
20:22	But the troops, the men of Israel, took courage and again drew up their battle lines in the same place where they had drawn them up on the first day.
20:23	The Israelites went up and wept before Yahweh until evening, and they inquired of Yahweh, "Shall we again draw near to battle against the Benjaminites, our brothers?" And Yahweh said, "Go up against them."
20:24	So the Israelites advanced against the Benjaminites on the second day.
20:25	Benjamin went out from Gibeah to meet them on the second day and struck down eighteen thousand more of the Israelites to the ground, all of them men who drew the sword.
20:26	Then all the Israelites, the whole army, went up and came to Bethel. They wept and sat there before Yahweh, fasted that day until evening, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before Yahweh.
20:27	And the Israelites inquired of Yahweh (for the ark of the covenant of God was there in those days,
20:28	and Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron, was standing before it in those days), saying, "Shall we yet again go out to battle against the Benjaminites, our brothers, or shall we stop?" Yahweh said, "Go up, for tomorrow I will give them into your hand."
20:29	So Israel set men in ambush all around Gibeah.
20:30	The Israelites went up against the Benjaminites on the third day and drew up their lines against Gibeah as at the other times.
20:31	The Benjaminites came out to meet the troops and were drawn away from the city. As at the other times they began to strike down some of the troops on the highways, one of which goes up to Bethel and the other to Gibeah in the open country, about thirty men of Israel.
20:32	The Benjaminites said, "They are being defeated before us as at first." But the Israelites had said, "Let us flee and draw them away from the city to the highways."
20:33	All the men of Israel rose up from their places and drew up their lines at Baal-tamar, while the Israelite ambush burst out of its place, from the open ground near Geba.
20:34	Ten thousand chosen men from all Israel came against Gibeah, and the battle was fierce; but the Benjaminites did not know that disaster was closing in on them.
20:35	Yahweh struck down Benjamin before Israel, and the Israelites destroyed twenty-five thousand one hundred men of Benjamin that day, all of them men who drew the sword.
20:36	Then the Benjaminites saw that they were defeated. The men of Israel had given ground to Benjamin, because they relied on the ambush they had set against Gibeah.
20:37	The ambush hurried and rushed against Gibeah; the ambush spread out and struck the whole city with the edge of the sword.
20:38	Now the agreed signal between the men of Israel and the ambush was that they would send up a great cloud of smoke from the city.
20:39	The men of Israel turned in the battle. Benjamin had begun to strike down about thirty men of Israel, for they said, "Surely they are defeated before us, as in the first battle."
20:40	But when the signal, a column of smoke, began to rise from the city, Benjamin looked behind them, and there was the whole city going up in smoke to the sky.
20:41	Then the men of Israel turned around, and the men of Benjamin were terrified, for they saw that disaster had closed in on them.
20:42	So they turned before the men of Israel toward the way of the wilderness, but the battle overtook them, and those who came out of the cities destroyed them in the midst of them.
20:43	They surrounded Benjamin, pursued them, and trampled them down at their resting place, as far as opposite Gibeah on the east.
20:44	Eighteen thousand men of Benjamin fell, all of them valiant men.
20:45	They turned and fled toward the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon. Five thousand of them were cut down on the highways, and they pursued them closely as far as Gidom and struck down two thousand more of them.
20:46	So all of Benjamin who fell that day were twenty-five thousand men who drew the sword, all of them valiant men.
20:47	But six hundred men turned and fled toward the wilderness to the rock of Rimmon, and they stayed at the rock of Rimmon four months.
20:48	Then the men of Israel turned back against the Benjaminites and struck them with the edge of the sword, the whole city, the livestock, and everything that was found. They also set on fire all the towns that they found.
