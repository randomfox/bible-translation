18:1	In those days there was no king in Israel. And in those days the tribe of the Danites was seeking for itself an inheritance to live in, for until that day no inheritance had fallen to it among the tribes of Israel.
18:2	So the Danites sent five men from their clan, from their whole number, capable men, from Zorah and Eshtaol, to spy out the land and explore it. They said to them, "Go, explore the land." They came to the hill country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah, and spent the night there.
18:3	When they were near the house of Micah, they recognized the voice of the young Levite, so they turned aside there and said to him, "Who brought you here? What are you doing in this place? What is your business here?"
18:4	He said to them, "Micah did such and such for me; he hired me, and I have become his priest."
18:5	They said to him, "Please inquire of God, so that we may know whether the journey we are on will succeed."
18:6	The priest said to them, "Go in peace. The journey you are on is under Yahweh's eye."
18:7	So the five men went on and came to Laish, and they saw the people who were in it living securely, in the manner of the Sidonians, quiet and unsuspecting. There was no one in the land humiliating them in anything or holding power over them; they were far from the Sidonians and had no dealings with anyone.
18:8	When they came back to their brothers at Zorah and Eshtaol, their brothers said to them, "What do you report?"
18:9	They said, "Get up, let us go up against them, for we have seen the land, and it is very good. And you are doing nothing? Do not be slow to go and enter and take possession of the land.
18:10	When you go, you will come to an unsuspecting people, and the land is broad; indeed God has given it into your hand, a place where there is no lack of anything that is on earth."
18:11	So six hundred men of the clan of the Danites, armed with weapons of war, set out from there, from Zorah and Eshtaol.
18:12	They went up and camped at Kiriath-jearim in Judah. That is why that place is called Mahaneh-dan to this day; it is west of Kiriath-jearim.
18:13	From there they passed on to the hill country of Ephraim and came to the house of Micah.
18:14	Then the five men who had gone to spy out the land of Laish spoke up and said to their brothers, "Do you know that in these houses there are an ephod, household gods, a carved image, and a cast image? Now think about what you should do."
18:15	So they turned aside there and came to the house of the young Levite, at the house of Micah, and asked him how he was.
18:16	The six hundred men of the Danites, armed with their weapons of war, stood at the entrance of the gate.
18:17	And the five men who had gone to spy out the land went up, entered there, and took the carved image, the ephod, the household gods, and the cast image, while the priest stood at the entrance of the gate with the six hundred men armed with weapons of war.
18:18	When these went into Micah's house and took the carved image, the ephod, the household gods, and the cast image, the priest said to them, "What are you doing?"
18:19	They said to him, "Be quiet! Put your hand over your mouth and come with us, and be a father and a priest to us. Is it better for you to be priest to the house of one man, or to be priest to a tribe and a clan in Israel?"
18:20	The priest's heart was glad; he took the ephod, the household gods, and the carved image, and went among the people.
18:21	So they turned and departed, putting the little ones, the livestock, and the goods in front of them.
18:22	When they had gone some distance from the house of Micah, the men who were in the houses near Micah's house were called out, and they overtook the Danites.
18:23	They shouted to the Danites, who turned around and said to Micah, "What is the matter with you, that you have come out with such a company?"
18:24	He said, "You have taken my gods that I made, and the priest, and gone away. What more do I have? How can you say to me, 'What is the matter with you?'"
18:25	The Danites said to him, "Do not let your voice be heard among us, or else bitter-tempered men will attack you, and you will lose your life and the lives of your household."
18:26	Then the Danites went on their way. When Micah saw that they were too strong for him, he turned and went back to his house.
18:27	So they took what Micah had made, and the priest who belonged to him, and came to Laish, to a people quiet and unsuspecting, and struck them with the edge of the sword and burned the city with fire.
18:28	There was no one to rescue them, for it was far from Sidon, and they had no dealings with anyone. It was in the valley that belongs to Beth-rehob. They rebuilt the city and lived in it.
18:29	They named the city Dan, after the name of Dan their ancestor, who was born to Israel; but the name of the city was formerly Laish.
18:30	The Danites set up the carved image for themselves, and Jonathan son of Gershom, son of Manasseh, he and his sons were priests to the tribe of the Danites until the day the land went into exile.
18:31	So they set up for themselves Micah's carved image that he had made, all the time that the house of God was at Shiloh.
