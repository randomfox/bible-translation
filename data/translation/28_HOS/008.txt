8:1	Set the trumpet to your lips! One like a vulture is over the house of Yahweh, because they have transgressed My covenant and rebelled against My law.
8:2	To Me they cry, "My God, we—Israel—know You."
8:3	Israel has spurned the good; the enemy shall pursue him.
8:4	They made kings, but not through Me. They set up princes, but I knew it not. With their silver and gold they made idols for their own destruction.
8:5	I have spurned your calf, O Samaria. My anger burns against them. How long will they be incapable of innocence?
8:6	For it is from Israel; a craftsman made it; it is not God. The calf of Samaria shall be broken to pieces.
8:7	For they sow the wind, and they shall reap the whirlwind. The standing grain has no heads; it shall yield no flour; if it were to yield, strangers would devour it.
8:8	Israel is swallowed up; already they are among the nations as a useless vessel.
8:9	For they have gone up to Assyria, a wild donkey wandering alone; Ephraim has hired lovers.
8:10	Though they hire allies among the nations, I will soon gather them up. And the king and princes shall soon writhe because of the tribute.
8:11	Because Ephraim has multiplied altars for sinning, they have become to him altars for sinning.
8:12	Were I to write for him My laws by the ten thousands, they would be regarded as a strange thing.
8:13	As for My sacrificial offerings, they sacrifice meat and eat it, but Yahweh does not accept them. Now He will remember their iniquity and punish their sins; they shall return to Egypt.
8:14	For Israel has forgotten his Maker and built palaces, and Judah has multiplied fortified cities; so I will send a fire upon his cities, and it shall devour her strongholds.
