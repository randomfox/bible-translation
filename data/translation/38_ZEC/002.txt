2:1	And I lifted my eyes and saw, and behold, four horns!
2:2	And I said to the angel who talked with me, "What are these?" And he said to me, "These are the horns that have scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem."
2:3	Then Yahweh showed me four craftsmen.
2:4	And I said, "What are these coming to do?" He said, "These are the horns that scattered Judah, so that no one raised his head. And these have come to terrify them, to cast down the horns of the nations who lifted up their horns against the land of Judah to scatter it."
2:5	And I lifted my eyes and saw, and behold, a man with a measuring line in his hand!
2:6	Then I said, "Where are you going?" And he said to me, "To measure Jerusalem, to see what is its width and what is its length."
2:7	And behold, the angel who talked with me came forward, and another angel came forward to meet him
2:8	and said to him, "Run, say to that young man, 'Jerusalem shall be inhabited as villages without walls, because of the multitude of people and livestock in it.
2:9	And I will be to her a wall of fire all around, declares Yahweh, and I will be the glory in her midst.'"
2:10	Up! Up! Flee from the land of the north, declares Yahweh. For I have spread you abroad as the four winds of the heavens, declares Yahweh.
2:11	Up! Escape to Zion, you who dwell with the daughter of Babylon.
2:12	For thus said Yahweh of Armies, after glory sent me to the nations who plundered you, for he who touches you touches the apple of his eye:
2:13	"Behold, I will shake my hand over them, and they shall become plunder for those who served them. Then you will know that Yahweh of Armies has sent me.
2:14	Sing and rejoice, O daughter of Zion, for behold, I come and I will dwell in your midst, declares Yahweh.
2:15	And many nations shall join themselves to Yahweh in that day, and shall be my people. And I will dwell in your midst, and you shall know that Yahweh of Armies has sent me to you.
2:16	And Yahweh will inherit Judah as his portion in the holy land, and will again choose Jerusalem."
2:17	Be silent, all flesh, before Yahweh, for he has roused himself from his holy dwelling.
