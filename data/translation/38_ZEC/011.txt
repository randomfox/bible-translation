11:1	Open your doors, Lebanon, so that fire may devour your cedars!
11:2	Wail, you cypress, for the cedar has fallen, because the mighty trees are ruined! Wail, you oaks of Bashan, for the dense forest has been cut down!
11:3	Listen to the wailing of the shepherds, for their glory is ruined; listen to the roaring of the young lions, for the thickets of the Jordan are ruined.
11:4	This is what Yahweh my God says: Shepherd the flock marked for slaughter,
11:5	whose buyers slaughter them and are not held guilty, and whose sellers say, "Blessed be Yahweh, for I have become rich," and whose own shepherds have no pity on them.
11:6	For I will no longer have pity on the inhabitants of the land, declares Yahweh. Look, I will hand people over, each into the hand of his neighbor and into the hand of his king; they will crush the land, and I will not rescue anyone from their hand.
11:7	So I shepherded the flock marked for slaughter, the afflicted of the flock. I took two staffs; one I called Favor and the other I called Union, and I shepherded the flock.
11:8	In one month I got rid of the three shepherds. I lost patience with them, and they also detested me.
11:9	Then I said, "I will not shepherd you. What is dying, let it die; what is being destroyed, let it be destroyed; and let those that are left devour one another's flesh."
11:10	I took my staff Favor and broke it, annulling my covenant that I had made with all the peoples.
11:11	It was annulled on that day, and so the afflicted of the flock who were watching me knew that it was the word of Yahweh.
11:12	I said to them, "If it seems good to you, give me my wages; and if not, keep them." So they weighed out my wages, thirty pieces of silver.
11:13	Then Yahweh said to me, "Throw it to the potter"—the lordly price at which I was valued by them! So I took the thirty pieces of silver and threw them into the house of Yahweh, to the potter.
11:14	Then I broke my second staff, Union, annulling the brotherhood between Judah and Israel.
11:15	Yahweh said to me, "Take again for yourself the equipment of a foolish shepherd.
11:16	For look, I am raising up a shepherd in the land who will not care for those that are perishing, nor seek the young, nor heal the injured, nor sustain the healthy, but he will eat the flesh of the fat ones and tear off their hooves.
11:17	Woe to My worthless shepherd, who deserts the flock! May a sword strike his arm and his right eye! Let his arm wither completely and his right eye go totally blind!"
