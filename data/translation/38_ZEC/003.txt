3:1	Then he showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of Yahweh, and the Accuser standing at his right hand to accuse him.
3:2	And Yahweh said to the Accuser, "Yahweh rebuke you, O Accuser! Yahweh who has chosen Jerusalem rebuke you! Is not this a brand plucked from the fire?"
3:3	Now Joshua was standing before the angel, clothed with filthy garments.
3:4	And the angel said to those who were standing before him, "Remove the filthy garments from him." And to him he said, "Behold, I have taken your iniquity away from you, and I will clothe you with festal robes."
3:5	And I said, "Let them put a clean turban on his head." So they put a clean turban on his head and clothed him with garments. And the angel of Yahweh was standing by.
3:6	And the angel of Yahweh solemnly assured Joshua,
3:7	"Thus says Yahweh of Armies: If you will walk in my ways and keep my charge, then you shall rule my house and have charge of my courts, and I will give you the right of access among those who are standing here.
3:8	Hear now, O Joshua the high priest, you and your friends who sit before you, for they are men who are a sign: behold, I will bring my servant the Branch.
3:9	For behold, on the stone that I have set before Joshua, on a single stone with seven eyes, I will engrave its inscription, declares Yahweh of Armies, and I will remove the iniquity of this land in a single day.
3:10	In that day, declares Yahweh of Armies, every one of you will invite his neighbor to come under his vine and under his fig tree."
