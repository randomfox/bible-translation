12:1	An oracle: the word of Yahweh concerning Israel. Yahweh declares—he who stretches out the heavens, lays the foundation of the earth, and forms the spirit of man within him:
12:2	Look, I am about to make Jerusalem a cup that makes all the surrounding peoples reel; and Judah too will be caught up in the siege against Jerusalem.
12:3	On that day I will make Jerusalem a heavy stone for all the peoples; all who lift it will be badly gashed, and all the nations of the earth will gather against her.
12:4	On that day, declares Yahweh, I will strike every horse with panic and its rider with madness. But I will keep my eyes open over the house of Judah, while I strike every horse of the peoples with blindness.
12:5	Then the clan chiefs of Judah will say in their hearts, "The inhabitants of Jerusalem are my strength through Yahweh of Armies, their God."
12:6	On that day I will make the clan chiefs of Judah like a firepot among sticks of wood and like a flaming torch among sheaves, and they will devour all the surrounding peoples to the right and to the left, while Jerusalem will still dwell in her place, in Jerusalem.
12:7	Yahweh will save the tents of Judah first, so that the glory of the house of David and the glory of the inhabitants of Jerusalem may not be exalted above Judah.
12:8	On that day Yahweh will shield the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so that the feeblest among them on that day will be like David, and the house of David will be like God, like the angel of Yahweh going before them.
12:9	On that day I will set out to destroy all the nations that come against Jerusalem.
12:10	And I will pour out on the house of David and on the inhabitants of Jerusalem a spirit of grace and pleading, and they will look to me, the one they have pierced, and they will mourn for him as one mourns for an only child, and weep bitterly for him as one weeps over a firstborn.
12:11	On that day the mourning in Jerusalem will be as great as the mourning for Hadad-rimmon in the plain of Megiddo.
12:12	The land will mourn, each clan by itself: the clan of the house of David by itself and their women by themselves; the clan of the house of Nathan by itself and their women by themselves;
12:13	the clan of the house of Levi by itself and their women by themselves; the clan of the Shimeites by itself and their women by themselves;
12:14	and all the remaining clans, each clan by itself, and their women by themselves.
