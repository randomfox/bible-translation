9:1	An oracle: the word of Yahweh is against the land of Hadrach, and Damascus is where it comes to rest—for the eyes of mankind, and of all the tribes of Israel, are on Yahweh—
9:2	and also Hamath, which borders on it, Tyre and Sidon, though they are very wise.
9:3	Tyre has built herself a fortress and heaped up silver like dust, and gold like the mud of the streets.
9:4	Look, the Lord will dispossess her and strike down her power on the sea, and she herself will be consumed by fire.
9:5	Ashkelon will see it and be afraid; Gaza too, and writhe in anguish; and Ekron, for her hope has been put to shame. The king will perish from Gaza, and Ashkelon will be uninhabited.
9:6	A mongrel people will live in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines.
9:7	I will take the blood out of his mouth and the abominations from between his teeth, and he too will be a remnant for our God; he will be like a clan chief in Judah, and Ekron will be like the Jebusites.
9:8	I will encamp at my house as a guard against any who march back and forth, and no oppressor will overrun them again, for now I have seen it with my own eyes.
9:9	Rejoice greatly, daughter Zion! Shout aloud, daughter Jerusalem! Look, your king is coming to you; he is righteous and victorious, humble and riding on a donkey, on a colt, the foal of a donkey.
9:10	I will cut off the chariot from Ephraim and the war horse from Jerusalem, and the battle bow will be cut off. He will speak peace to the nations, and his rule will stretch from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth.
9:11	As for you, because of the blood of your covenant, I have set your prisoners free from the pit that has no water in it.
9:12	Return to the stronghold, you prisoners of hope; even today I declare that I will repay you double.
9:13	For I have bent Judah for myself as a bow; I have loaded it with Ephraim. I will rouse your sons, Zion, against your sons, Greece, and make you like a warrior's sword.
9:14	Then Yahweh will appear above them, and his arrow will flash out like lightning; the Lord Yahweh will blow the ram's horn and march out in the storms of the south.
9:15	Yahweh of Armies will shield them, and they will devour and trample down the sling stones; they will drink and roar as if with wine, and be filled like a sprinkling bowl, like the corners of the altar.
9:16	On that day Yahweh their God will save them as the flock of his people, for they are like the jewels of a crown, sparkling over his land.
9:17	How good and how beautiful it will be! Grain will make the young men flourish, and new wine the young women.
