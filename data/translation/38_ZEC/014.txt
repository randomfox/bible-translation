14:1	Look, a day is coming for Yahweh when the plunder taken from you will be divided in your midst.
14:2	I will gather all the nations to Jerusalem for battle; the city will be captured, the houses plundered, and the women raped. Half of the city will go into exile, but the rest of the people will not be cut off from the city.
14:3	Then Yahweh will go out and fight against those nations, as he fights on a day of battle.
14:4	On that day his feet will stand on the Mount of Olives, which lies east of Jerusalem, and the Mount of Olives will be split in two from east to west by a very wide valley, so that half of the mountain moves northward and half of it southward.
14:5	You will flee by the valley of my mountains, for the valley of the mountains will reach to Azel; you will flee as you fled from the earthquake in the days of Uzziah king of Judah. Then Yahweh my God will come, and all the holy ones with you.
14:6	On that day there will be no light; the glorious lights will congeal.
14:7	It will be a unique day—it is known to Yahweh—neither day nor night, for at evening time there will be light.
14:8	On that day living water will flow out from Jerusalem, half of it to the eastern sea and half of it to the western sea; it will continue in summer and in winter.
14:9	Yahweh will be king over all the earth. On that day Yahweh will be one and his name one.
14:10	The whole land will be turned into a plain like the Arabah, from Geba to Rimmon, south of Jerusalem; but Jerusalem will be raised up and stay in her place, from the Benjamin Gate to the site of the First Gate, to the Corner Gate, and from the Tower of Hananel to the king's winepresses.
14:11	People will live in it, and there will never again be a ban of destruction; Jerusalem will dwell in security.
14:12	And this will be the plague with which Yahweh will strike all the peoples who waged war against Jerusalem: their flesh will rot while they are still standing on their feet, their eyes will rot in their sockets, and their tongues will rot in their mouths.
14:13	On that day a great panic from Yahweh will fall on them; each will seize the hand of his neighbor, and raise his hand against his neighbor.
14:14	Judah too will fight at Jerusalem. The wealth of all the surrounding nations will be gathered—gold, silver, and clothing in great abundance.
14:15	A similar plague will strike the horse, the mule, the camel, the donkey, and all the animals that are in those camps—a plague like this one.
14:16	Then all who survive of all the nations that came against Jerusalem will go up year after year to worship the King, Yahweh of Armies, and to celebrate the Festival of Booths.
14:17	If any of the clans of the earth do not go up to Jerusalem to worship the King, Yahweh of Armies, there will be no rain on them.
14:18	And if the clan of Egypt does not go up and come in, then they will have no rain; there will be the plague with which Yahweh strikes the nations that do not go up to celebrate the Festival of Booths.
14:19	This will be the punishment of Egypt and the punishment of all the nations that do not go up to celebrate the Festival of Booths.
14:20	On that day "Holy to Yahweh" will be inscribed on the bells of the horses, and the cooking pots in the house of Yahweh will be like the sprinkling bowls before the altar.
14:21	Every cooking pot in Jerusalem and Judah will be holy to Yahweh of Armies, and all who sacrifice will come and take some of them and cook in them. And there will no longer be a trader in the house of Yahweh of Armies on that day.
