26:1	The Ziphites came to Saul at Gibeah and said, "Is not David hiding on the hill of Hachilah, which faces Jeshimon?"
26:2	So Saul arose and went down to the wilderness of Ziph, with three thousand chosen men of Israel, to seek David in the wilderness of Ziph.
26:3	Saul camped on the hill of Hachilah, which faces Jeshimon, beside the road. David was staying in the wilderness, and when he saw that Saul had come after him into the wilderness,
26:4	David sent out spies and learned that Saul had certainly come.
26:5	Then David arose and came to the place where Saul had camped. David saw the place where Saul lay, with Abner son of Ner, the commander of his army. Saul was lying inside the camp barricade, and the troops were camped around him.
26:6	David spoke up and said to Ahimelech the Hittite and to Abishai son of Zeruiah, Joab's brother, "Who will go down with me to Saul, into the camp?" Abishai said, "I will go down with you."
26:7	So David and Abishai came to the troops by night, and there was Saul lying asleep inside the barricade, with his spear stuck in the ground at his head, and Abner and the troops were lying around him.
26:8	Abishai said to David, "God has handed your enemy over to you today. Now please let me pin him to the ground with the spear in one stroke; I will not need to strike him twice."
26:9	But David said to Abishai, "Do not destroy him, for who can lay a hand on Yahweh's anointed and be guiltless?"
26:10	David said, "As Yahweh lives, Yahweh Himself will strike him; or his day will come and he will die; or he will go down into battle and be swept away.
26:11	Yahweh forbid that I should lay my hand on Yahweh's anointed. Now please take the spear that is at his head and the water jug, and let us go."
26:12	So David took the spear and the water jug from beside Saul's head, and they went away. No one saw, no one knew, no one woke up, for they were all asleep, because a deep sleep from Yahweh had fallen on them.
26:13	Then David crossed over to the other side and stood on top of the hill at a distance, with a wide space between them.
26:14	David called out to the troops and to Abner son of Ner, "Will you not answer, Abner?" Abner answered, "Who are you, calling to the king?"
26:15	David said to Abner, "Are you not a man? And who is like you in Israel? Why then have you not guarded your lord the king? For one of the people came in to destroy the king your lord.
26:16	This thing that you have done is not good. As Yahweh lives, you deserve to die, because you have not guarded your lord, Yahweh's anointed. Now look: where is the king's spear, and the water jug that was at his head?"
26:17	Saul recognized David's voice and said, "Is this your voice, my son David?" David said, "It is my voice, my lord the king."
26:18	And he said, "Why is my lord pursuing his servant? What have I done? What evil is in my hand?
26:19	Now please let my lord the king hear the words of his servant. If it is Yahweh Who has stirred you up against me, may He accept an offering; but if it is human beings, may they be cursed before Yahweh, for they have driven me out today from sharing in Yahweh's inheritance, saying, 'Go, serve other gods.'
26:20	Now do not let my blood fall to the ground away from the presence of Yahweh, for the king of Israel has come out to seek a single flea, as one hunts a partridge in the mountains."
26:21	Then Saul said, "I have sinned. Come back, my son David, for I will not harm you again, because my life was precious in your eyes today. I have acted like a fool and have gone very badly astray."
26:22	David answered, "Here is the king's spear! Let one of the young men come over and get it.
26:23	Yahweh repays each man for his righteousness and his faithfulness; for Yahweh gave you into my hand today, but I would not lay my hand on Yahweh's anointed.
26:24	And just as your life was valued highly in my eyes today, so may my life be valued highly in the eyes of Yahweh, and may He rescue me from all trouble."
26:25	Saul said to David, "Blessed are you, my son David! You will surely do great things and surely succeed." So David went on his way, and Saul returned to his place.
