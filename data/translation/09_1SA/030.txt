30:1	When David and his men came to Ziklag on the third day, the Amalekites had made a raid on the Negev and on Ziklag. They had struck Ziklag and burned it with fire,
30:2	and had taken captive the women who were in it, young and old. They killed no one, but carried them off and went on their way.
30:3	When David and his men came to the town, there it was, burned with fire, and their wives, sons, and daughters had been taken captive.
30:4	Then David and the people who were with him raised their voices and wept until they had no strength left to weep.
30:5	David's two wives had been taken captive, Ahinoam of Jezreel and Abigail, the widow of Nabal of Carmel.
30:6	David was in great distress, for the people spoke of stoning him, because all the people were bitter in spirit, each over his sons and daughters. But David strengthened himself in Yahweh his God.
30:7	David said to Abiathar the priest, son of Ahimelech, "Please bring the ephod here to me." So Abiathar brought the ephod to David.
30:8	David inquired of Yahweh, "Shall I pursue this raiding band? Will I overtake them?" He said to him, "Pursue, for you will surely overtake them and surely rescue the captives."
30:9	So David went, he and the six hundred men who were with him, and they came to the Wadi Besor, where those who were left behind stayed.
30:10	David kept up the pursuit, he and four hundred men, while two hundred men who were too exhausted to cross the Wadi Besor stayed behind.
30:11	They found an Egyptian in the open country and brought him to David. They gave him bread, and he ate, and they gave him water to drink.
30:12	They also gave him a piece of a fig cake and two clusters of raisins, and he ate, and his spirit revived, for he had not eaten bread or drunk water for three days and three nights.
30:13	David said to him, "To whom do you belong, and where are you from?" He said, "I am a young Egyptian, the slave of an Amalekite. My master abandoned me because I fell sick three days ago.
30:14	We made a raid on the Negev of the Cherethites, on the territory belonging to Judah, and on the Negev of Caleb, and we burned Ziklag with fire."
30:15	David said to him, "Will you take me down to this raiding band?" He said, "Swear to me by God that you will not kill me or hand me over to my master, and I will take you down to this band."
30:16	When he had taken him down, there they were, spread out over all the ground, eating and drinking and celebrating because of all the great plunder they had taken from the land of the Philistines and from the land of Judah.
30:17	David struck them down from twilight until the evening of the next day, and not one of them escaped except four hundred young men who mounted camels and fled.
30:18	David recovered all that the Amalekites had taken, and David rescued his two wives.
30:19	Nothing of theirs was missing, small or great, sons or daughters, plunder or anything that had been taken from them; David brought it all back.
30:20	David also took all the flocks and herds, which they drove ahead of the other livestock, saying, "This is David's plunder."
30:21	Then David came to the two hundred men who had been too exhausted to follow David and had been left at the Wadi Besor. They came out to meet David and the people who were with him, and David approached the people and greeted them.
30:22	But all the wicked and worthless men among those who had gone with David spoke up and said, "Because they did not go with us, we will not give them any of the plunder we recovered, except that each man may take his wife and children, and lead them away and go."
30:23	But David said, "You shall not do so, my brothers, with what Yahweh has given us. He has protected us and handed over to us the raiding band that came against us.
30:24	Who would listen to you in this matter? For as is the share of the one who goes down into battle, so shall be the share of the one who stays with the baggage; they shall share alike."
30:25	And from that day forward he made it a statute and a rule for Israel, to this day.
30:26	When David came to Ziklag, he sent some of the plunder to the elders of Judah, his friends, saying, "Here is a gift for you from the plunder of Yahweh's enemies."
30:27	It was for those in Bethel, in Ramoth of the Negev, and in Jattir,
30:28	in Aroer, in Siphmoth, and in Eshtemoa,
30:29	in Racal, in the towns of the Jerahmeelites, and in the towns of the Kenites,
30:30	in Hormah, in Bor-ashan, and in Athach,
30:31	in Hebron, and in all the places where David and his men had roamed.
