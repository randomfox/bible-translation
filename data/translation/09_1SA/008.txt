8:1	When Samuel grew old, he appointed his sons as judges over Israel.
8:2	The name of his firstborn son was Joel, and the name of his second was Abijah; they were judges in Beersheba.
8:3	But his sons did not walk in his ways; they turned aside after dishonest gain, took bribes, and perverted justice.
8:4	So all the elders of Israel gathered together and came to Samuel at Ramah.
8:5	They said to him, "Look, you have grown old, and your sons do not walk in your ways. Now appoint for us a king to judge us, like all the nations."
8:6	But the thing was displeasing in Samuel's eyes when they said, "Give us a king to judge us." And Samuel prayed to Yahweh.
8:7	Yahweh said to Samuel, "Listen to the voice of the people in all that they say to you, for it is not you they have rejected; it is Me they have rejected from being king over them.
8:8	Just as they have done in all their deeds from the day I brought them up out of Egypt until this day, forsaking Me and serving other gods, so they are doing to you as well.
8:9	Now then, listen to their voice; only, you must solemnly warn them and tell them the way of the king who will reign over them."
8:10	So Samuel told all the words of Yahweh to the people who were asking him for a king.
8:11	He said, "This will be the way of the king who will reign over you: he will take your sons and put them for himself among his chariots and his horsemen, and they will run before his chariot.
8:12	He will appoint for himself commanders of thousands and commanders of fifties, and some to plow his ground and to reap his harvest, and to make his weapons of war and the equipment of his chariots.
8:13	He will take your daughters to be perfumers, cooks, and bakers.
8:14	He will take the best of your fields, your vineyards, and your olive groves, and give them to his servants.
8:15	He will take a tenth of your grain and your vineyards and give it to his officials and his servants.
8:16	He will take your male and female slaves, your best young men, and your donkeys, and put them to his own work.
8:17	He will take a tenth of your flocks, and you yourselves will become his slaves.
8:18	On that day you will cry out because of your king whom you have chosen for yourselves, but Yahweh will not answer you on that day."
8:19	But the people refused to listen to the voice of Samuel. They said, "No! There shall be a king over us,
8:20	so that we too may be like all the nations, and our king may judge us and go out before us and fight our battles."
8:21	When Samuel had heard all the words of the people, he repeated them in the ears of Yahweh.
8:22	Yahweh said to Samuel, "Listen to their voice and make them a king." Then Samuel said to the men of Israel, "Go, each of you to his city."
