15:1	Samuel said to Saul, "It was I whom Yahweh sent to anoint you king over his people, over Israel. Now therefore listen to the words of Yahweh.
15:2	Thus says Yahweh of Armies: 'I have taken note of what Amalek did to Israel, how he set himself against them on the way when they came up out of Egypt.
15:3	Now go and strike Amalek, and devote to destruction all that he has. Do not spare him, but put to death man and woman, child and infant, ox and sheep, camel and donkey.'"
15:4	So Saul summoned the people and mustered them at Telaim: two hundred thousand foot soldiers, and ten thousand men of Judah.
15:5	Saul came to the city of Amalek and lay in wait in the valley.
15:6	Saul said to the Kenites, "Go, depart, go down from among the Amalekites, so that I do not destroy you along with them; for you showed kindness to all the Israelites when they came up out of Egypt." So the Kenites departed from among the Amalekites.
15:7	Saul struck down the Amalekites from Havilah as far as Shur, which is east of Egypt.
15:8	He captured Agag king of the Amalekites alive, and devoted all the people to destruction with the edge of the sword.
15:9	But Saul and the people spared Agag, and the best of the sheep and the cattle, the fatlings and the lambs, and all that was good; they were unwilling to devote them to destruction. Everything that was despised and worthless they devoted to destruction.
15:10	Then the word of Yahweh came to Samuel:
15:11	"I regret that I made Saul king, for he has turned back from following me and has not carried out my commands." Samuel was angry, and he cried out to Yahweh all night.
15:12	Samuel rose early in the morning to meet Saul, and it was told to Samuel, "Saul came to Carmel, and there he set up a monument for himself; then he turned and passed on and went down to Gilgal."
15:13	When Samuel came to Saul, Saul said to him, "Blessed are you by Yahweh! I have carried out the command of Yahweh."
15:14	But Samuel said, "Then what is this bleating of sheep in my ears, and the lowing of cattle that I hear?"
15:15	Saul said, "They have brought them from the Amalekites, for the people spared the best of the sheep and the cattle to sacrifice to Yahweh your God; the rest we have devoted to destruction."
15:16	Then Samuel said to Saul, "Stop! Let me tell you what Yahweh said to me last night." He said to him, "Speak."
15:17	Samuel said, "Though you are small in your own eyes, are you not the head of the tribes of Israel? Yahweh anointed you king over Israel.
15:18	And Yahweh sent you on a mission and said, 'Go, devote to destruction the sinners, the Amalekites, and fight against them until they are wiped out.'
15:19	Why then did you not obey the voice of Yahweh? Why did you swoop on the spoil and do what was evil in the sight of Yahweh?"
15:20	Saul said to Samuel, "But I did obey the voice of Yahweh. I went on the mission on which Yahweh sent me. I have brought Agag the king of Amalek, and I have devoted the Amalekites to destruction.
15:21	But the people took from the spoil sheep and cattle, the best of what was devoted to destruction, to sacrifice to Yahweh your God in Gilgal."
15:22	And Samuel said, "Does Yahweh delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices as much as in obeying the voice of Yahweh? Look, to obey is better than sacrifice, and to heed is better than the fat of rams.
15:23	For rebellion is like the sin of divination, and stubbornness is like iniquity and household idols. Because you have rejected the word of Yahweh, he has rejected you from being king."
15:24	Saul said to Samuel, "I have sinned, for I have transgressed the command of Yahweh and your words, because I feared the people and listened to their voice.
15:25	Now therefore, please forgive my sin and return with me, so that I may worship Yahweh."
15:26	Samuel said to Saul, "I will not return with you, for you have rejected the word of Yahweh, and Yahweh has rejected you from being king over Israel."
15:27	As Samuel turned to go, Saul seized the hem of his robe, and it tore.
15:28	Samuel said to him, "Yahweh has torn the kingdom of Israel from you today and has given it to a neighbor of yours who is better than you.
15:29	Moreover, the Glory of Israel will not lie or change his mind, for he is not a human being, that he should change his mind."
15:30	Then Saul said, "I have sinned. Yet honor me now, please, before the elders of my people and before Israel, and return with me, so that I may worship Yahweh your God."
15:31	So Samuel turned back after Saul, and Saul worshiped Yahweh.
15:32	Then Samuel said, "Bring Agag king of the Amalekites here to me." Agag came to him confidently, and Agag said, "Surely the bitterness of death is past."
15:33	Samuel said, "As your sword has made women childless, so your mother shall be childless among women." And Samuel hacked Agag to pieces before Yahweh in Gilgal.
15:34	Then Samuel went to Ramah, and Saul went up to his house at Gibeah of Saul.
15:35	Samuel did not see Saul again until the day of his death, but Samuel grieved over Saul. And Yahweh regretted that he had made Saul king over Israel.
