28:1	In those days the Philistines gathered their forces for war, to fight against Israel. Achish said to David, "You must understand that you and your men will go out with me in the army."
28:2	David said to Achish, "Very well, you yourself will know what your servant can do." Achish said to David, "Very well, I will make you my bodyguard for life."
28:3	Now Samuel had died, and all Israel had mourned for him and buried him in Ramah, his own town. And Saul had removed the mediums and the spiritists from the land.
28:4	The Philistines assembled and came and camped at Shunem, and Saul gathered all Israel, and they camped at Gilboa.
28:5	When Saul saw the Philistine army, he was afraid, and his heart trembled greatly.
28:6	Saul inquired of Yahweh, but Yahweh did not answer him, neither by dreams, nor by Urim, nor by prophets.
28:7	Then Saul said to his servants, "Find me a woman who is a medium, so that I may go to her and inquire through her." His servants said to him, "There is a woman who is a medium at En-dor."
28:8	So Saul disguised himself, putting on other clothes, and went, he and two men with him. They came to the woman by night, and he said, "Please divine for me by a spirit, and bring up for me the one I name to you."
28:9	The woman said to him, "Surely you know what Saul has done, how he has cut off the mediums and the spiritists from the land. Why then are you laying a trap for my life, to get me killed?"
28:10	But Saul swore to her by Yahweh, "As Yahweh lives, no punishment shall come upon you for this thing."
28:11	Then the woman said, "Whom shall I bring up for you?" He said, "Bring up Samuel for me."
28:12	When the woman saw Samuel, she cried out with a loud voice, and the woman said to Saul, "Why have you deceived me? You are Saul!"
28:13	The king said to her, "Do not be afraid. What do you see?" The woman said to Saul, "I see a divine being coming up out of the earth."
28:14	He said to her, "What does he look like?" She said, "An old man is coming up, and he is wrapped in a robe." Then Saul knew that it was Samuel, and he bowed with his face to the ground and prostrated himself.
28:15	Samuel said to Saul, "Why have you disturbed me by bringing me up?" Saul said, "I am in great distress, for the Philistines are fighting against me, and God has turned away from me and no longer answers me, either by prophets or by dreams. So I have called you to tell me what I should do."
28:16	Samuel said, "Why then do you ask me, when Yahweh has turned away from you and has become your adversary?
28:17	Yahweh has done for himself just as he spoke through me: Yahweh has torn the kingdom out of your hand and given it to your neighbor, to David.
28:18	Because you did not obey the voice of Yahweh and did not carry out his fierce anger against Amalek, therefore Yahweh has done this thing to you today.
28:19	Moreover, Yahweh will give Israel along with you into the hand of the Philistines, and tomorrow you and your sons will be with me. Yahweh will also give the army of Israel into the hand of the Philistines."
28:20	Immediately Saul fell full length on the ground, terrified by Samuel's words. There was no strength left in him, for he had eaten no food all day and all night.
28:21	The woman came to Saul and saw that he was badly shaken, and she said to him, "Look, your servant has listened to you; I took my life in my hands and obeyed the words you spoke to me.
28:22	Now, please, you also listen to your servant: let me set a bit of bread before you, and eat, so that you may have strength when you go on your way."
28:23	He refused and said, "I will not eat." But his servants, together with the woman, pressed him, and he listened to them. He got up from the ground and sat on the bed.
28:24	The woman had a fattened calf in the house; she quickly slaughtered it, took flour, kneaded it, and baked unleavened bread from it.
28:25	She set it before Saul and his servants, and they ate. Then they rose and went away that same night.
