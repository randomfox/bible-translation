9:1	There was a man of Benjamin whose name was Kish son of Abiel, son of Zeror, son of Becorath, son of Aphiah, a Benjaminite, a man of substance.
9:2	He had a son whose name was Saul, a fine young man; there was no one among the Israelites more handsome than he. From his shoulders upward he was taller than all the people.
9:3	Now the donkeys of Kish, Saul's father, had strayed. So Kish said to his son Saul, "Take one of the servants with you, and get up and go look for the donkeys."
9:4	He passed through the hill country of Ephraim and passed through the land of Shalishah, but they did not find them. They passed through the land of Shaalim, but they were not there. Then he passed through the land of Benjamin, but they did not find them.
9:5	When they came to the land of Zuph, Saul said to the servant who was with him, "Come, let us go back, or my father will stop thinking about the donkeys and start worrying about us."
9:6	But he said to him, "Look, there is a man of God in this city, and the man is highly respected; everything he says surely comes true. Let us go there now; perhaps he can tell us about the journey we have set out on."
9:7	Saul said to his servant, "But if we go, what can we bring the man? The bread in our bags is gone, and there is no present to bring to the man of God. What do we have?"
9:8	The servant answered Saul again, "Look, I have in my hand a quarter of a shekel of silver. I will give it to the man of God, and he will tell us our way."
9:9	(Formerly in Israel, when a man went to inquire of God, he would say, "Come, let us go to the seer," for the one who is now called a prophet was formerly called a seer.)
9:10	Saul said to his servant, "Well said. Come, let us go." So they went to the city where the man of God was.
9:11	As they were going up the slope to the city, they met young women coming out to draw water and said to them, "Is the seer here?"
9:12	They answered them, "He is. Look, he is just ahead of you. Hurry now, for he has come to the city today, because the people have a sacrifice today at the high place.
9:13	As soon as you enter the city you will find him, before he goes up to the high place to eat. The people will not eat until he comes, for he blesses the sacrifice; after that those who are invited eat. So go up now, for you will find him right away."
9:14	So they went up to the city. As they were coming into the city, there was Samuel coming out toward them on his way up to the high place.
9:15	Now the day before Saul came, Yahweh had revealed to Samuel:
9:16	"About this time tomorrow I will send you a man from the land of Benjamin, and you shall anoint him to be ruler over My people Israel. He will save My people from the hand of the Philistines, for I have looked upon My people, because their cry has come to Me."
9:17	When Samuel saw Saul, Yahweh told him, "Here is the man of whom I spoke to you. He is the one who will govern My people."
9:18	Saul approached Samuel in the gateway and said, "Please tell me where the seer's house is."
9:19	Samuel answered Saul, "I am the seer. Go up ahead of me to the high place, for today you will eat with me. In the morning I will send you on your way and tell you all that is on your mind.
9:20	As for the donkeys that strayed from you three days ago, do not set your mind on them, for they have been found. And for whom is all that is desirable in Israel? Is it not for you and for all your father's house?"
9:21	Saul answered, "Am I not a Benjaminite, from the smallest of the tribes of Israel, and is not my clan the least of all the clans of the tribe of Benjamin? Why then have you spoken to me like this?"
9:22	Then Samuel took Saul and his servant, brought them into the hall, and gave them a place at the head of those who had been invited, who were about thirty men.
9:23	Samuel said to the cook, "Bring the portion I gave you, the one I told you to set aside."
9:24	So the cook lifted up the thigh and what was on it and set it before Saul. Samuel said, "Here is what has been kept back. Set it before you and eat, for it has been kept for you for this appointed time, since I said, 'I have invited the people.'" So Saul ate with Samuel that day.
9:25	When they came down from the high place to the city, Samuel spoke with Saul on the roof.
9:26	They rose early, and at the break of dawn Samuel called to Saul on the roof, "Get up, so that I may send you on your way." Saul got up, and both of them, he and Samuel, went outside.
9:27	As they were going down to the edge of the city, Samuel said to Saul, "Tell the servant to go on ahead of us"—and he went on—"but you stand still here for a moment, so that I may make known to you the word of God."
