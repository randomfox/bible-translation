13:1	Saul was [number missing] years old when he became king, and he reigned two years over Israel.
13:2	Saul chose for himself three thousand men of Israel. Two thousand were with Saul at Michmash and in the hill country of Bethel, and a thousand were with Jonathan at Gibeah of Benjamin; the rest of the people he sent home, each to his tent.
13:3	Jonathan struck down the Philistine garrison that was at Geba, and the Philistines heard of it. And Saul blew the ram's horn throughout all the land, saying, "Let the Hebrews hear!"
13:4	All Israel heard the news: "Saul has struck down the Philistine garrison, and Israel has also become a stench to the Philistines." And the people were called out to join Saul at Gilgal.
13:5	The Philistines gathered to fight with Israel: thirty thousand chariots, six thousand horsemen, and troops as numerous as the sand on the seashore. They came up and encamped at Michmash, east of Beth-aven.
13:6	When the men of Israel saw that they were in a tight spot, for the troops were hard pressed, the people hid themselves in caves, in thickets, in rocks, in tombs, and in cisterns.
13:7	Some Hebrews crossed the Jordan to the land of Gad and Gilead. Saul was still at Gilgal, and all the people followed him trembling.
13:8	He waited seven days, the time appointed by Samuel; but Samuel did not come to Gilgal, and the people began to scatter from him.
13:9	So Saul said, "Bring me the burnt offering and the peace offerings." And he offered the burnt offering.
13:10	Just as he finished offering the burnt offering, Samuel arrived, and Saul went out to meet him and greet him.
13:11	Samuel said, "What have you done?" Saul said, "When I saw that the people were scattering from me, and that you had not come within the appointed days, and that the Philistines were assembling at Michmash,
13:12	I said, 'Now the Philistines will come down against me at Gilgal, and I have not sought the favor of Yahweh.' So I forced myself and offered the burnt offering."
13:13	Samuel said to Saul, "You have acted foolishly. You have not kept the command of Yahweh your God that he commanded you. For now Yahweh would have established your kingdom over Israel forever.
13:14	But now your kingdom will not endure. Yahweh has sought out for himself a man after his own heart, and Yahweh has appointed him ruler over his people, because you have not kept what Yahweh commanded you."
13:15	Then Samuel arose and went up from Gilgal to Gibeah of Benjamin. Saul mustered the people who were present with him, about six hundred men.
13:16	Saul and his son Jonathan, and the people who were present with them, stayed in Geba of Benjamin, while the Philistines encamped at Michmash.
13:17	Raiders came out of the Philistine camp in three companies: one company turned toward Ophrah, to the land of Shual,
13:18	another company turned toward Beth-horon, and another company turned toward the border that looks down on the Valley of Zeboim toward the wilderness.
13:19	Now no smith could be found in all the land of Israel, for the Philistines said, "Otherwise the Hebrews will make themselves swords or spears."
13:20	So all Israel went down to the Philistines to sharpen each his plowshare, his mattock, his axe, and his hoe.
13:21	The charge was a pim for the plowshares and the mattocks, and a third of a shekel for the forks and the axes, and for setting the goads.
13:22	So on the day of battle neither sword nor spear was found in the hand of any of the people who were with Saul and Jonathan; only Saul and his son Jonathan had them.
13:23	And the Philistine garrison went out to the pass of Michmash.
