16:1	Yahweh said to Samuel, "How long will you grieve over Saul, when I have rejected him from being king over Israel? Fill your horn with oil and go; I am sending you to Jesse of Bethlehem, for I have provided for Myself a king among his sons."
16:2	Samuel said, "How can I go? If Saul hears of it, he will kill me." Yahweh said, "Take a heifer with you and say, 'I have come to sacrifice to Yahweh.'
16:3	Invite Jesse to the sacrifice, and I will make known to you what you shall do; and you shall anoint for Me the one whom I name to you."
16:4	Samuel did what Yahweh said and came to Bethlehem. The elders of the city came trembling to meet him and said, "Do you come in peace?"
16:5	He said, "In peace. I have come to sacrifice to Yahweh. Consecrate yourselves and come with me to the sacrifice." He consecrated Jesse and his sons and invited them to the sacrifice.
16:6	When they came, he looked at Eliab and thought, "Surely Yahweh's anointed stands before him."
16:7	But Yahweh said to Samuel, "Do not look at his appearance or the height of his stature, for I have rejected him. For it is not as a human sees: a human looks on the outward appearance, but Yahweh looks on the heart."
16:8	Then Jesse called Abinadab and made him pass before Samuel. He said, "Yahweh has not chosen this one either."
16:9	Then Jesse made Shammah pass by. He said, "Yahweh has not chosen this one either."
16:10	Jesse made seven of his sons pass before Samuel, and Samuel said to Jesse, "Yahweh has not chosen these."
16:11	Then Samuel said to Jesse, "Are these all the young men?" He said, "There remains yet the youngest, but he is tending the sheep." Samuel said to Jesse, "Send and bring him, for we will not sit down to eat until he comes here."
16:12	So he sent and brought him in. He was ruddy, with beautiful eyes, and handsome in appearance. Yahweh said, "Rise, anoint him, for this is he."
16:13	Then Samuel took the horn of oil and anointed him in the midst of his brothers, and the Spirit of Yahweh rushed upon David from that day forward. And Samuel rose and went to Ramah.
16:14	Now the Spirit of Yahweh had departed from Saul, and an evil spirit from Yahweh tormented him.
16:15	Saul's servants said to him, "See now, an evil spirit from God is tormenting you.
16:16	Let our lord command your servants who are before you to look for a man who knows how to play the lyre; and whenever the evil spirit from God is upon you, he will play it with his hand, and you will feel better."
16:17	So Saul said to his servants, "Find me a man who plays well and bring him to me."
16:18	One of the young men answered, "I have seen a son of Jesse of Bethlehem who knows how to play, a valiant man, a warrior, skillful in speech, and a man of good appearance; and Yahweh is with him."
16:19	So Saul sent messengers to Jesse and said, "Send me your son David, who is with the sheep."
16:20	Jesse took a donkey loaded with bread, a skin of wine, and a young goat, and sent them by his son David to Saul.
16:21	David came to Saul and entered his service. Saul loved him greatly, and he became his armor-bearer.
16:22	Saul sent word to Jesse, "Let David remain in my service, for he has found favor in my eyes."
16:23	Whenever the spirit from God came upon Saul, David would take the lyre and play it with his hand; then Saul would be relieved and feel better, and the evil spirit would depart from him.
