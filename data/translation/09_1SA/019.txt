19:1	Saul spoke to his son Jonathan and to all his servants about killing David. But Jonathan, Saul's son, delighted greatly in David.
19:2	Jonathan told David, "My father Saul is seeking to kill you. Now be on your guard in the morning; stay in a hiding place and keep yourself hidden.
19:3	I will go out and stand beside my father in the field where you are, and I will speak about you to my father. If I learn anything, I will tell you."
19:4	Jonathan spoke well of David to his father Saul and said to him, "Let the king not sin against his servant David, for he has not sinned against you, and his deeds have been very good for you.
19:5	He took his life in his hand and struck down the Philistine, and Yahweh brought about a great deliverance for all Israel. You saw it and rejoiced. Why then would you sin against innocent blood by killing David without cause?"
19:6	Saul listened to the voice of Jonathan, and Saul swore, "As Yahweh lives, he shall not be put to death."
19:7	So Jonathan called David, and Jonathan told him all these things. Then Jonathan brought David to Saul, and he was in his presence as before.
19:8	Once again there was war. David went out and fought with the Philistines and struck them with a great blow, so that they fled before him.
19:9	Then an evil spirit from Yahweh came upon Saul as he sat in his house with his spear in his hand, while David was playing the lyre.
19:10	Saul sought to pin David to the wall with the spear, but he slipped away from Saul, so that he struck the spear into the wall. David fled and escaped that night.
19:11	Saul sent messengers to David's house to watch him and to kill him in the morning. But Michal, David's wife, told him, "If you do not save your life tonight, tomorrow you will be killed."
19:12	So Michal let David down through the window, and he went away, fled, and escaped.
19:13	Michal took the household idol and laid it on the bed, put a net of goats' hair at its head, and covered it with the clothes.
19:14	When Saul sent messengers to take David, she said, "He is sick."
19:15	Then Saul sent the messengers to see David, saying, "Bring him up to me in the bed, so that I may kill him."
19:16	When the messengers came in, there was the household idol in the bed, with the net of goats' hair at its head.
19:17	Saul said to Michal, "Why have you deceived me like this and let my enemy go, so that he has escaped?" Michal answered Saul, "He said to me, 'Let me go. Why should I kill you?'"
19:18	Now David fled and escaped, and came to Samuel at Ramah and told him all that Saul had done to him. He and Samuel went and stayed at Naioth.
19:19	It was told to Saul, "David is at Naioth in Ramah."
19:20	Then Saul sent messengers to take David. When they saw the company of the prophets prophesying, with Samuel standing as head over them, the Spirit of God came upon Saul's messengers, and they also prophesied.
19:21	When it was told to Saul, he sent other messengers, and they also prophesied. Saul sent messengers again a third time, and they also prophesied.
19:22	Then he himself went to Ramah and came to the great cistern that is in Secu. He asked, "Where are Samuel and David?" Someone said, "They are at Naioth in Ramah."
19:23	He went there, to Naioth in Ramah, and the Spirit of God came upon him too, and he went along prophesying until he came to Naioth in Ramah.
19:24	He too stripped off his clothes, and he too prophesied before Samuel, and lay naked all that day and all that night. Therefore it is said, "Is Saul also among the prophets?"
