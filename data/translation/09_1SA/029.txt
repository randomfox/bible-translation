29:1	The Philistines gathered all their forces at Aphek, while Israel was camped by the spring that is in Jezreel.
29:2	As the Philistine rulers were passing on by hundreds and by thousands, and David and his men were passing on in the rear with Achish,
29:3	the Philistine commanders said, "What are these Hebrews doing here?" Achish said to the Philistine commanders, "Is this not David, the servant of Saul king of Israel, who has been with me for days, even years? I have found no fault in him from the day he defected until today."
29:4	But the Philistine commanders were angry with him, and the Philistine commanders said to him, "Send the man back, so that he may return to the place you assigned him. He must not go down with us into battle, or he may become an adversary to us in the battle. For how could this man regain his master's favor? Would it not be with the heads of these men here?
29:5	Is this not David, of whom they sang to one another in the dances, 'Saul has struck down his thousands, and David his tens of thousands'?"
29:6	So Achish called David and said to him, "As Yahweh lives, you are upright, and in my view your going out and coming in with me in the army is good, for I have found nothing wrong in you from the day you came to me until today. But in the eyes of the rulers you are not good.
29:7	So now go back, and go in peace, and do nothing that displeases the Philistine rulers."
29:8	David said to Achish, "But what have I done? What have you found in your servant from the day I entered your service until today, that I may not go and fight against the enemies of my lord the king?"
29:9	Achish answered David, "I know that you are as good in my eyes as an angel of God. But the Philistine commanders have said, 'He must not go up with us into battle.'
29:10	So now rise early in the morning, you and the servants of your lord who came with you. Rise early in the morning, and as soon as it is light, go."
29:11	So David rose early, he and his men, to set out in the morning and return to the land of the Philistines, and the Philistines went up to Jezreel.
