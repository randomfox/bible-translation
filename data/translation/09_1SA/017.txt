17:1	The Philistines gathered their forces for war. They assembled at Socoh, which belongs to Judah, and encamped between Socoh and Azekah, in Ephes-dammim.
17:2	Saul and the men of Israel assembled and encamped in the Valley of Elah, and drew up in battle formation to meet the Philistines.
17:3	The Philistines stood on the mountain on one side, and Israel stood on the mountain on the other side, with the valley between them.
17:4	Then a champion came out from the Philistine camp, named Goliath, of Gath. His height was six cubits and a span.
17:5	He had a bronze helmet on his head and was clothed in scale armor; the weight of the armor was five thousand shekels of bronze.
17:6	He had bronze greaves on his legs, and a bronze javelin slung between his shoulders.
17:7	The shaft of his spear was like a weaver's beam, and the head of his spear weighed six hundred shekels of iron. His shield-bearer went before him.
17:8	He stood and shouted to the ranks of Israel, "Why have you come out to draw up for battle? Am I not the Philistine, and are you not servants of Saul? Choose a man for yourselves, and let him come down to me.
17:9	If he is able to fight with me and kill me, then we will be your slaves; but if I prevail against him and kill him, then you shall be our slaves and serve us."
17:10	And the Philistine said, "I defy the ranks of Israel this day! Give me a man, and let us fight together."
17:11	When Saul and all Israel heard these words of the Philistine, they were dismayed and greatly afraid.
17:12	Now David was the son of that Ephrathite of Bethlehem in Judah, whose name was Jesse; he had eight sons. In the days of Saul the man was old, advanced in years among men.
17:13	The three eldest sons of Jesse had followed Saul to the war. The names of his three sons who went to the war were Eliab the firstborn, next to him Abinadab, and the third Shammah.
17:14	David was the youngest; the three eldest followed Saul.
17:15	But David used to go back and forth from Saul to tend his father's sheep at Bethlehem.
17:16	For forty days the Philistine came forward morning and evening and took his stand.
17:17	Jesse said to his son David, "Take for your brothers an ephah of this parched grain and these ten loaves, and hurry to the camp to your brothers.
17:18	Also take these ten cheeses to the commander of their thousand. See how your brothers are doing, and bring back some token from them."
17:19	Now Saul and they and all the men of Israel were in the Valley of Elah, fighting with the Philistines.
17:20	David rose early in the morning, left the sheep with a keeper, loaded up, and went as Jesse had commanded him. He came to the encampment as the army was going out to the battle line, shouting the war cry.
17:21	Israel and the Philistines drew up for battle, rank against rank.
17:22	David left his baggage in the care of the keeper of the baggage, ran to the battle line, and went and asked his brothers how they were.
17:23	As he was talking with them, there was the champion, the Philistine of Gath named Goliath, coming up out of the Philistine ranks, and he spoke the same words as before, and David heard him.
17:24	All the men of Israel, when they saw the man, fled from him and were very much afraid.
17:25	The men of Israel said, "Have you seen this man who has come up? Surely he comes up to defy Israel. The king will greatly enrich the man who kills him, will give him his daughter, and will make his father's house free in Israel."
17:26	David said to the men standing by him, "What will be done for the man who kills this Philistine and removes the disgrace from Israel? For who is this uncircumcised Philistine, that he should defy the armies of the living God?"
17:27	The people answered him in the same way, "So shall it be done for the man who kills him."
17:28	His eldest brother Eliab heard him speaking to the men, and Eliab's anger burned against David. He said, "Why have you come down? And with whom have you left those few sheep in the wilderness? I know your presumption and the evil of your heart; you have come down to watch the battle."
17:29	David said, "What have I done now? Was it not just a word?"
17:30	He turned away from him toward another and spoke in the same way, and the people answered him again as before.
17:31	When the words David had spoken were heard, they reported them to Saul, and he sent for him.
17:32	David said to Saul, "Let no one's heart fail because of him. Your servant will go and fight with this Philistine."
17:33	Saul said to David, "You cannot go against this Philistine to fight with him, for you are only a youth, and he has been a warrior from his youth."
17:34	But David said to Saul, "Your servant used to tend sheep for his father. Whenever a lion or a bear came and took a lamb from the flock,
17:35	I went out after it, struck it, and rescued it from its mouth; and if it rose against me, I seized it by its beard, struck it, and killed it.
17:36	Your servant has struck down both lion and bear, and this uncircumcised Philistine shall be like one of them, for he has defied the armies of the living God."
17:37	David said, "Yahweh, Who rescued me from the paw of the lion and from the paw of the bear, will rescue me from the hand of this Philistine." Saul said to David, "Go, and may Yahweh be with you."
17:38	Then Saul clothed David with his own garments. He put a bronze helmet on his head and clothed him with armor.
17:39	David strapped Saul's sword over his garments and tried to walk, for he had not tested them. Then David said to Saul, "I cannot walk in these, for I have not tested them." So David took them off.
17:40	He took his staff in his hand, chose five smooth stones from the wadi, and put them in his shepherd's bag, in the pouch. With his sling in his hand, he approached the Philistine.
17:41	The Philistine came on and drew near to David, with the shield-bearer in front of him.
17:42	When the Philistine looked and saw David, he despised him, for he was only a youth, ruddy and handsome in appearance.
17:43	The Philistine said to David, "Am I a dog, that you come to me with sticks?" And the Philistine cursed David by his gods.
17:44	The Philistine said to David, "Come to me, and I will give your flesh to the birds of the air and to the beasts of the field."
17:45	But David said to the Philistine, "You come to me with sword and spear and javelin, but I come to you in the name of Yahweh of Armies, the God of the ranks of Israel, Whom you have defied.
17:46	This day Yahweh will deliver you into my hand. I will strike you down and cut off your head, and I will give the corpses of the Philistine army this day to the birds of the air and the wild animals of the earth, so that all the earth may know that there is a God in Israel,
17:47	and that all this assembly may know that Yahweh does not save by sword and spear; for the battle is Yahweh's, and He will give you into our hand."
17:48	When the Philistine rose and came closer to meet David, David ran quickly toward the battle line to meet the Philistine.
17:49	David put his hand into the bag, took out a stone, slung it, and struck the Philistine on his forehead. The stone sank into his forehead, and he fell on his face to the ground.
17:50	So David prevailed over the Philistine with a sling and a stone, and struck the Philistine and killed him; there was no sword in David's hand.
17:51	Then David ran and stood over the Philistine, took his sword, drew it out of its sheath, killed him, and cut off his head with it. When the Philistines saw that their champion was dead, they fled.
17:52	The men of Israel and Judah rose up with a shout and pursued the Philistines as far as Gai and the gates of Ekron, and the Philistine dead fell along the road from Shaaraim as far as Gath and Ekron.
17:53	The Israelites came back from chasing the Philistines and plundered their camp.
17:54	David took the head of the Philistine and brought it to Jerusalem, but he put his weapons in his own tent.
17:55	When Saul saw David going out to meet the Philistine, he said to Abner, the commander of the army, "Abner, whose son is this young man?" Abner said, "As surely as you live, O king, I do not know."
17:56	The king said, "Find out whose son the lad is."
17:57	When David returned from killing the Philistine, Abner took him and brought him before Saul with the Philistine's head in his hand.
17:58	Saul said to him, "Whose son are you, young man?" David said, "The son of your servant Jesse of Bethlehem."
