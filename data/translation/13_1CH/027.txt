27:1	This is the list of the people of Israel, the heads of fathers' houses, the commanders of thousands and hundreds, and their officers who served the king in all matters of the divisions that came and went month after month throughout all the months of the year, each division numbering 24,000:
27:2	Over the first division for the first month was Jashobeam the son of Zabdiel; in his division were 24,000.
27:3	He was a descendant of Perez and was chief of all the commanders of the army for the first month.
27:4	Over the division of the second month was Dodai the Ahohite, with his division; Mikloth was the chief officer; in his division were 24,000.
27:5	The third commander of the army, for the third month, was Benaiah the son of Jehoiada the priest, as chief; in his division were 24,000.
27:6	This is the Benaiah who was a mighty man of the thirty and in command of the thirty; Ammizabad his son was over his division.
27:7	Fourth, for the fourth month, was Asahel the brother of Joab, and Zebadiah his son after him; in his division were 24,000.
27:8	Fifth, for the fifth month, was the commander Shamhuth the Izrahite; in his division were 24,000.
27:9	Sixth, for the sixth month, was Ira the son of Ikkesh the Tekoite; in his division were 24,000.
27:10	Seventh, for the seventh month, was Helez the Pelonite, of the sons of Ephraim; in his division were 24,000.
27:11	Eighth, for the eighth month, was Sibbecai the Hushathite, of the Zerahites; in his division were 24,000.
27:12	Ninth, for the ninth month, was Abiezer of Anathoth, a Benjaminite; in his division were 24,000.
27:13	Tenth, for the tenth month, was Maharai of Netophah, of the Zerahites; in his division were 24,000.
27:14	Eleventh, for the eleventh month, was Benaiah of Pirathon, of the sons of Ephraim; in his division were 24,000.
27:15	Twelfth, for the twelfth month, was Heldai the Netophathite, of Othniel; in his division were 24,000.
27:16	Over the tribes of Israel: for the Reubenites Eliezer the son of Zichri was chief officer; for the Simeonites, Shephatiah the son of Maacah;
27:17	for Levi, Hashabiah the son of Kemuel; for Aaron, Zadok;
27:18	for Judah, Elihu, one of David's brothers; for Issachar, Omri the son of Michael;
27:19	for Zebulun, Ishmaiah the son of Obadiah; for Naphtali, Jerimoth the son of Azriel;
27:20	for the Ephraimites, Hoshea the son of Azaziah; for the half-tribe of Manasseh, Joel the son of Pedaiah;
27:21	for the half of Manasseh in Gilead, Iddo the son of Zechariah; for Benjamin, Jaasiel the son of Abner;
27:22	for Dan, Azarel the son of Jeroham. These were the officers of the tribes of Israel.
27:23	David did not take the number of those twenty years old and under, for Yahweh had promised to make Israel as many as the stars of the heavens.
27:24	Joab the son of Zeruiah began to count, but did not finish; and wrath came upon Israel because of this, and the number was not entered in the chronicles of King David.
27:25	Over the king's treasuries was Azmaveth the son of Adiel; and over the treasuries in the country, in the cities, in the villages, and in the towers, was Jonathan the son of Uzziah.
27:26	Over those who did the work of the field, tilling the soil, was Ezri the son of Chelub.
27:27	Over the vineyards was Shimei the Ramathite; and over the produce of the vineyards for the wine cellars was Zabdi the Shiphmite.
27:28	Over the olive and sycamore trees in the Shephelah was Baal-hanan the Gederite; and over the stores of oil was Joash.
27:29	Over the herds that pastured in Sharon was Shitrai the Sharonite; over the herds in the valleys was Shaphat the son of Adlai.
27:30	Over the camels was Obil the Ishmaelite; and over the donkeys was Jehdeiah the Meronothite.
27:31	Over the flocks was Jaziz the Hagrite. All these were stewards of King David's property.
27:32	Jonathan, David's uncle, was a counselor, a man of understanding and a scribe; he and Jehiel the son of Hachmoni attended the king's sons.
27:33	Ahithophel was the king's counselor, and Hushai the Archite was the king's friend.
27:34	Ahithophel was succeeded by Jehoiada the son of Benaiah, and Abiathar; Joab was commander of the king's army.
