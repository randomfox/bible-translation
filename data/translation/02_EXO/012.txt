12:1	Yahweh said to Moses and Aaron in the land of Egypt,
12:2	"This month shall be for you the beginning of months; it shall be the first month of the year for you.
12:3	Tell the whole community of Israel that on the tenth day of this month each man is to take a lamb for his father's house, a lamb for each household.
12:4	If the household is too small for a lamb, then he and his nearest neighbor shall take one according to the number of persons; you shall divide the lamb according to what each person can eat.
12:5	Your lamb shall be without blemish, a year-old male; you may take it from the sheep or from the goats.
12:6	You shall keep it until the fourteenth day of this month, and then the whole assembly of the community of Israel shall slaughter it at twilight.
12:7	They shall take some of the blood and put it on the two doorposts and on the lintel of the houses in which they eat it.
12:8	They shall eat the meat that night, roasted over the fire; with unleavened bread and with bitter herbs they shall eat it.
12:9	Do not eat any of it raw or boiled in water, but roasted over the fire, its head with its legs and its inner parts.
12:10	You shall leave none of it until morning; whatever remains of it until morning you shall burn with fire.
12:11	This is how you shall eat it: with your belt fastened, your sandals on your feet, and your staff in your hand. You shall eat it in haste. It is Yahweh's Passover.
12:12	For I will pass through the land of Egypt this night and strike down every firstborn in the land of Egypt, both human and animal, and on all the gods of Egypt I will execute judgments. I am Yahweh.
12:13	The blood shall be a sign for you on the houses where you are. When I see the blood, I will pass over you, and no plague will come upon you to destroy you when I strike the land of Egypt.
12:14	This day shall be a memorial day for you, and you shall celebrate it as a festival to Yahweh; throughout your generations you shall celebrate it as a permanent statute.
12:15	For seven days you shall eat unleavened bread. On the very first day you shall remove leaven from your houses, for whoever eats anything leavened from the first day until the seventh day, that person shall be cut off from Israel.
12:16	On the first day you shall hold a sacred assembly, and on the seventh day a sacred assembly. No work shall be done on them, except what every person must eat; that alone may be prepared by you.
12:17	You shall observe the Feast of Unleavened Bread, for on this very day I brought your divisions out of the land of Egypt. So you shall observe this day throughout your generations as a permanent statute.
12:18	In the first month, from the evening of the fourteenth day of the month, you shall eat unleavened bread until the evening of the twenty-first day of the month.
12:19	For seven days no leaven shall be found in your houses, for whoever eats anything leavened, that person shall be cut off from the community of Israel, whether a resident foreigner or a native of the land.
12:20	You shall eat nothing leavened; in all your settlements you shall eat unleavened bread."
12:21	Then Moses summoned all the elders of Israel and said to them, "Go, select lambs for your clans, and slaughter the Passover lamb.
12:22	Take a bunch of hyssop, dip it in the blood that is in the basin, and touch the lintel and the two doorposts with the blood that is in the basin. None of you shall go out of the door of his house until morning.
12:23	For Yahweh will pass through to strike the Egyptians, and when he sees the blood on the lintel and on the two doorposts, Yahweh will pass over the door and will not allow the destroyer to enter your houses to strike you.
12:24	You shall observe this as a statute for you and for your children forever.
12:25	When you come to the land that Yahweh will give you, as he has promised, you shall observe this rite.
12:26	And when your children say to you, 'What does this rite mean to you?'
12:27	you shall say, 'It is the Passover sacrifice to Yahweh, for he passed over the houses of the Israelites in Egypt when he struck the Egyptians, but spared our houses.'" And the people bowed down and worshiped.
12:28	Then the Israelites went and did so; just as Yahweh had commanded Moses and Aaron, so they did.
12:29	At midnight Yahweh struck down every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of Pharaoh who sat on his throne to the firstborn of the captive who was in the dungeon, and all the firstborn of the livestock.
12:30	Pharaoh rose up in the night, he and all his servants and all the Egyptians, and there was a great cry in Egypt, for there was not a house where someone was not dead.
12:31	He summoned Moses and Aaron by night and said, "Get up, get out from among my people, both you and the Israelites! Go, serve Yahweh as you have said.
12:32	Take your flocks and your herds too, as you have said, and go. And bless me also!"
12:33	The Egyptians urged the people on, hurrying to send them out of the land, for they said, "We are all dead men."
12:34	So the people took their dough before it was leavened, with their kneading bowls bound up in their cloaks on their shoulders.
12:35	The Israelites had done as Moses said, and had asked the Egyptians for articles of silver and articles of gold and for clothing.
12:36	And Yahweh had given the people favor in the eyes of the Egyptians, so that they granted what they asked. Thus they plundered the Egyptians.
12:37	The Israelites set out from Rameses to Succoth, about six hundred thousand men on foot, besides children.
12:38	A mixed crowd also went up with them, and flocks and herds, a very large number of livestock.
12:39	They baked the dough that they had brought out of Egypt into round loaves of unleavened bread, for it was not leavened, because they had been driven out of Egypt and could not delay, nor had they prepared any provisions for themselves.
12:40	The time that the Israelites lived in Egypt was four hundred thirty years.
12:41	At the end of four hundred thirty years, on that very day, all the divisions of Yahweh went out from the land of Egypt.
12:42	It was a night of vigil for Yahweh, to bring them out of the land of Egypt; this same night is a vigil to Yahweh for all the Israelites throughout their generations.
12:43	Yahweh said to Moses and Aaron, "This is the statute of the Passover: no foreigner shall eat of it,
12:44	but any man's slave who has been bought with money may eat of it after you have circumcised him.
12:45	No temporary resident or hired worker may eat of it.
12:46	It shall be eaten in one house; you shall not take any of the meat outside the house, and you shall not break any of its bones.
12:47	The whole community of Israel shall keep it.
12:48	If a foreigner resides with you and wants to keep the Passover to Yahweh, all his males must be circumcised; then he may come near to keep it, and he shall be like a native of the land. But no uncircumcised person shall eat of it.
12:49	There shall be one law for the native and for the foreigner who resides among you."
12:50	All the Israelites did just as Yahweh had commanded Moses and Aaron; so they did.
12:51	On that very day Yahweh brought the Israelites out of the land of Egypt by their divisions.
