5:1	Afterward Moses and Aaron went and said to Pharaoh, "Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel: 'Let My people go, so that they may hold a festival for Me in the wilderness.'"
5:2	But Pharaoh said, "Who is Yahweh, that I should obey His voice and let Israel go? I do not know Yahweh, and moreover I will not let Israel go."
5:3	They said, "The God of the Hebrews has met with us. Please let us go a three days' journey into the wilderness and sacrifice to Yahweh our God, or He may fall upon us with plague or with the sword."
5:4	The king of Egypt said to them, "Moses and Aaron, why are you taking the people away from their work? Get back to your labors!"
5:5	And Pharaoh said, "Look, the people of the land are now many, and you would make them stop their labors!"
5:6	That same day Pharaoh commanded the taskmasters over the people and their foremen,
5:7	"You shall no longer give the people straw to make bricks, as before. Let them go and gather straw for themselves.
5:8	But you shall require of them the same quota of bricks that they made before; do not reduce it. For they are lazy; that is why they cry out, 'Let us go and sacrifice to our God.'
5:9	Let the work be heavier on the men so that they are kept busy with it and pay no attention to lying words."
5:10	So the taskmasters of the people and their foremen went out and said to the people, "Thus says Pharaoh: 'I will not give you straw.
5:11	Go yourselves and get straw wherever you can find it, for none of your work will be reduced in the least.'"
5:12	So the people scattered throughout all the land of Egypt to gather stubble for straw.
5:13	The taskmasters kept pressing them, saying, "Complete your work, each day's quota on its day, just as when there was straw."
5:14	And the foremen of the Israelites, whom Pharaoh's taskmasters had set over them, were beaten and asked, "Why have you not completed your required amount of brickmaking yesterday and today, as before?"
5:15	Then the foremen of the Israelites came and cried out to Pharaoh, "Why do you treat your servants like this?
5:16	No straw is given to your servants, yet they say to us, 'Make bricks!' And look, your servants are being beaten, but the fault lies with your own people."
5:17	He said, "You are lazy, lazy! That is why you say, 'Let us go and sacrifice to Yahweh.'
5:18	Now go and work. No straw will be given to you, but you must deliver the same quota of bricks."
5:19	The foremen of the Israelites saw that they were in trouble when they were told, "You shall not reduce your bricks, each day's quota on its day."
5:20	As they came out from Pharaoh, they met Moses and Aaron, who were standing there to meet them.
5:21	They said to them, "May Yahweh look upon you and judge, because you have made us stink in the eyes of Pharaoh and his servants, putting a sword in their hand to kill us."
5:22	Then Moses turned back to Yahweh and said, "Lord, why have You brought harm on this people? Why did You ever send me?
5:23	Since I came to Pharaoh to speak in Your name, he has done harm to this people, and You have not rescued Your people at all."
