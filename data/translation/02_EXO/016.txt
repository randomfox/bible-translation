16:1	They set out from Elim, and the whole congregation of the Israelites came to the wilderness of Sin, which is between Elim and Sinai, on the fifteenth day of the second month after they had left the land of Egypt.
16:2	The whole congregation of the Israelites grumbled against Moses and Aaron in the wilderness.
16:3	The Israelites said to them, "If only we had died by the hand of Yahweh in the land of Egypt, when we sat by the pots of meat and ate bread to the full! For you have brought us out into this wilderness to kill this whole assembly with hunger."
16:4	Then Yahweh said to Moses, "Look, I am going to rain bread from heaven for you. The people shall go out and gather a day's portion every day, so that I may test them, whether they will walk in My instruction or not.
16:5	On the sixth day, when they prepare what they bring in, it will be twice as much as they gather on other days."
16:6	So Moses and Aaron said to all the Israelites, "In the evening you will know that it was Yahweh Who brought you out of the land of Egypt,
16:7	and in the morning you will see the glory of Yahweh, because He has heard your grumbling against Yahweh. What are we, that you grumble against us?"
16:8	Moses said, "When Yahweh gives you meat to eat in the evening and bread to the full in the morning, because Yahweh has heard the grumbling that you grumble against Him—what are we? Your grumbling is not against us but against Yahweh."
16:9	Then Moses said to Aaron, "Say to the whole congregation of the Israelites, 'Come near before Yahweh, for He has heard your grumbling.'"
16:10	As Aaron spoke to the whole congregation of the Israelites, they looked toward the wilderness, and there the glory of Yahweh appeared in the cloud.
16:11	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
16:12	"I have heard the grumbling of the Israelites. Say to them, 'At twilight you shall eat meat, and in the morning you shall be filled with bread. Then you shall know that I am Yahweh your God.'"
16:13	In the evening quail came up and covered the camp, and in the morning there was a layer of dew around the camp.
16:14	When the layer of dew had lifted, there on the surface of the wilderness was a fine, flaky substance, fine as frost on the ground.
16:15	When the Israelites saw it, they said to one another, "What is it?"—for they did not know what it was. Moses said to them, "It is the bread that Yahweh has given you to eat.
16:16	This is what Yahweh has commanded: 'Gather of it, each of you, as much as he can eat, an omer a head, according to the number of persons; each of you shall take for those in his tent.'"
16:17	The Israelites did so; some gathered more, some less.
16:18	But when they measured it with an omer, the one who gathered much had nothing left over, and the one who gathered little had no shortage; each had gathered as much as he could eat.
16:19	Moses said to them, "Let no one leave any of it until morning."
16:20	But they did not listen to Moses; some left part of it until morning, and it bred worms and stank. And Moses was angry with them.
16:21	Morning by morning they gathered it, each as much as he could eat, but when the sun grew hot, it melted.
16:22	On the sixth day they gathered twice as much bread, two omers for each person, and all the leaders of the congregation came and told Moses.
16:23	He said to them, "This is what Yahweh has said: 'Tomorrow is a day of solemn rest, a holy Sabbath to Yahweh. Bake what you want to bake and boil what you want to boil, and all that is left over put aside to be kept until morning.'"
16:24	So they put it aside until morning, as Moses commanded, and it did not stink, and there were no worms in it.
16:25	Moses said, "Eat it today, for today is a Sabbath to Yahweh; today you will not find it in the field.
16:26	Six days you shall gather it, but on the seventh day, the Sabbath, there will be none."
16:27	On the seventh day some of the people went out to gather, but they found none.
16:28	Yahweh said to Moses, "How long will you refuse to keep My commandments and My instructions?
16:29	See, Yahweh has given you the Sabbath; that is why on the sixth day He gives you bread for two days. Each of you stay where you are; let no one go out of his place on the seventh day."
16:30	So the people rested on the seventh day.
16:31	The house of Israel called it manna. It was like coriander seed, white, and it tasted like wafers made with honey.
16:32	Moses said, "This is what Yahweh has commanded: 'Let an omer of it be kept throughout your generations, so that they may see the bread with which I fed you in the wilderness when I brought you out of the land of Egypt.'"
16:33	And Moses said to Aaron, "Take a jar, put an omer of manna in it, and place it before Yahweh, to be kept throughout your generations."
16:34	As Yahweh commanded Moses, Aaron placed it before the testimony, to be kept.
16:35	The Israelites ate manna forty years, until they came to a habitable land; they ate the manna until they came to the border of the land of Canaan.
16:36	(An omer is a tenth of an ephah.)
