26:1	"Moreover, you shall make the tabernacle with ten curtains of fine twisted linen and blue, purple, and scarlet yarn; with cherubim worked into them by a skilled designer you shall make them.
26:2	The length of each curtain shall be twenty-eight cubits, and the width of each curtain four cubits; all the curtains shall have the same measurement.
26:3	Five curtains shall be joined to one another, and the other five curtains shall be joined to one another.
26:4	You shall make loops of blue on the edge of the outermost curtain in the first set, and likewise you shall make them on the edge of the outermost curtain in the second set.
26:5	Fifty loops you shall make on the one curtain, and fifty loops you shall make on the edge of the curtain that is in the second set; the loops shall be opposite one another.
26:6	And you shall make fifty clasps of gold and join the curtains one to the other with the clasps, so that the tabernacle may be a single whole.
26:7	"You shall also make curtains of goats' hair for a tent over the tabernacle; you shall make eleven curtains.
26:8	The length of each curtain shall be thirty cubits, and the width of each curtain four cubits; the eleven curtains shall have the same measurement.
26:9	You shall join five curtains by themselves and six curtains by themselves, and the sixth curtain you shall fold double at the front of the tent.
26:10	You shall make fifty loops on the edge of the outermost curtain in the first set, and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain of the second set.
26:11	You shall make fifty clasps of bronze, put the clasps into the loops, and join the tent together so that it may be a single whole.
26:12	As for the overhang that remains of the curtains of the tent, the half curtain that remains shall hang over the back of the tabernacle.
26:13	And the cubit on the one side and the cubit on the other side, of what remains in the length of the curtains of the tent, shall hang over the sides of the tabernacle, on this side and that, to cover it.
26:14	And you shall make for the tent a covering of rams' skins dyed red, and a covering of fine leather above that.
26:15	"You shall make upright frames of acacia wood for the tabernacle.
26:16	Ten cubits shall be the length of a frame, and a cubit and a half the width of each frame.
26:17	There shall be two tenons in each frame, fitted to each other; so shall you do for all the frames of the tabernacle.
26:18	You shall make the frames for the tabernacle: twenty frames for the south side, toward the south.
26:19	And you shall make forty bases of silver under the twenty frames, two bases under one frame for its two tenons, and two bases under the next frame for its two tenons;
26:20	and for the second side of the tabernacle, on the north side, twenty frames,
26:21	and their forty bases of silver, two bases under one frame and two bases under the next frame.
26:22	For the rear of the tabernacle, westward, you shall make six frames.
26:23	And you shall make two frames for the corners of the tabernacle at the rear.
26:24	They shall be doubled at the bottom, and likewise complete to the top, at the first ring; thus shall it be with both of them; they shall form the two corners.
26:25	So there shall be eight frames with their bases of silver, sixteen bases: two bases under one frame and two bases under the next frame.
26:26	"You shall make bars of acacia wood, five for the frames of the one side of the tabernacle,
26:27	and five bars for the frames of the other side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the frames of the side of the tabernacle at the rear, westward.
26:28	The middle bar, halfway up the frames, shall run from end to end.
26:29	You shall overlay the frames with gold and make their rings of gold as holders for the bars, and you shall overlay the bars with gold.
26:30	Then you shall set up the tabernacle according to its plan that you were shown on the mountain.
26:31	"You shall make a veil of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen; with cherubim it shall be made, the work of a skilled designer.
26:32	And you shall hang it on four pillars of acacia overlaid with gold, with hooks of gold, on four bases of silver.
26:33	You shall hang the veil under the clasps, and bring the ark of the testimony in there within the veil. The veil shall separate for you the Holy Place from the Most Holy Place.
26:34	You shall put the atonement cover on the ark of the testimony in the Most Holy Place.
26:35	And you shall set the table outside the veil, and the lampstand opposite the table on the south side of the tabernacle; and you shall put the table on the north side.
26:36	"You shall make a screen for the entrance of the tent, of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer.
26:37	And you shall make for the screen five pillars of acacia and overlay them with gold; their hooks shall be of gold, and you shall cast five bases of bronze for them.
