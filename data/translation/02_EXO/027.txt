27:1	"You shall make the altar of acacia wood, five cubits long and five cubits wide; the altar shall be square, and its height shall be three cubits.
27:2	You shall make horns for it on its four corners; its horns shall be of one piece with it, and you shall overlay it with bronze.
27:3	You shall make its pots for removing its fatty ashes, and its shovels, basins, forks, and firepans; all its utensils you shall make of bronze.
27:4	You shall also make for it a grating, a network of bronze, and on the network you shall make four bronze rings at its four corners.
27:5	And you shall set it under the ledge of the altar, below, so that the network extends halfway up the altar.
27:6	You shall make poles for the altar, poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with bronze.
27:7	Its poles shall be put through the rings, so that the poles are on the two sides of the altar when it is carried.
27:8	You shall make it hollow, of boards; as it was shown you on the mountain, so shall they make it.
27:9	"You shall make the court of the tabernacle. On the south side, toward the south, the court shall have hangings of fine twisted linen a hundred cubits long for one side.
27:10	Its pillars shall be twenty and their bases twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands shall be of silver.
27:11	Likewise for the north side lengthwise there shall be hangings a hundred cubits long, its pillars twenty and their bases twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands shall be of silver.
27:12	For the width of the court on the west side there shall be hangings of fifty cubits, with their ten pillars and their ten bases.
27:13	The width of the court on the front, toward the sunrise, shall be fifty cubits.
27:14	The hangings for one side of the entrance shall be fifteen cubits, with their three pillars and their three bases.
27:15	On the other side the hangings shall be fifteen cubits, with their three pillars and their three bases.
27:16	For the gate of the court there shall be a screen twenty cubits long, of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, the work of an embroiderer, with its four pillars and their four bases.
27:17	All the pillars around the court shall be banded with silver; their hooks shall be of silver and their bases of bronze.
27:18	The length of the court shall be a hundred cubits, the width fifty throughout, and the height five cubits, of fine twisted linen, with bases of bronze.
27:19	All the utensils of the tabernacle for every use, and all its pegs and all the pegs of the court, shall be of bronze.
27:20	"You shall command the people of Israel to bring you pure oil of beaten olives for the light, so that a lamp may be kept burning continually.
27:21	In the tent of meeting, outside the veil that is before the testimony, Aaron and his sons shall tend it from evening to morning before Yahweh. It shall be a statute forever throughout their generations, to be observed by the people of Israel.
