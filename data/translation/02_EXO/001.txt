1:1	These are the names of the sons of Israel who came to Egypt with Jacob, each with his household:
1:2	Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah,
1:3	Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin,
1:4	Dan and Naphtali, Gad and Asher.
1:5	All the persons descended from Jacob were seventy; Joseph was already in Egypt.
1:6	Then Joseph died, and all his brothers, and all that generation.
1:7	But the Israelites were fruitful and swarmed; they multiplied and grew exceedingly strong, so that the land was filled with them.
1:8	Then a new king arose over Egypt who did not know Joseph.
1:9	He said to his people, "Look, the Israelite people are more numerous and stronger than we are.
1:10	Come, let us deal shrewdly with them, or they will multiply, and if war breaks out, they will join our enemies, fight against us, and go up from the land."
1:11	So they set taskmasters over them to oppress them with their forced labor, and they built storage cities for Pharaoh, Pithom and Rameses.
1:12	But the more they oppressed them, the more they multiplied and spread, and the Egyptians came to dread the Israelites.
1:13	So the Egyptians made the Israelites work ruthlessly,
1:14	and made their lives bitter with hard labor in mortar and brick and in every kind of work in the field; in all their work they made them serve ruthlessly.
1:15	The king of Egypt spoke to the Hebrew midwives, one of whom was named Shiphrah and the other Puah,
1:16	and said, "When you help the Hebrew women give birth and see them on the birthstool, if it is a son, kill him; but if it is a daughter, she may live."
1:17	But the midwives feared God and did not do as the king of Egypt had told them; they let the boys live.
1:18	So the king of Egypt called the midwives and said to them, "Why have you done this and let the boys live?"
1:19	The midwives said to Pharaoh, "Because the Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women; they are vigorous and give birth before the midwife comes to them."
1:20	So God dealt well with the midwives, and the people multiplied and grew very strong.
1:21	And because the midwives feared God, he gave them households of their own.
1:22	Then Pharaoh commanded all his people, "Every son that is born you shall throw into the Nile, but you shall let every daughter live."
