7:1	Yahweh said to Moses, "See, I have made you as God to Pharaoh, and Aaron your brother shall be your prophet.
7:2	You shall speak all that I command you, and Aaron your brother shall speak to Pharaoh so that he will let the Israelites go out of his land.
7:3	But I will harden Pharaoh's heart, and I will multiply my signs and my wonders in the land of Egypt.
7:4	Pharaoh will not listen to you. Then I will lay my hand on Egypt and bring my divisions, my people the Israelites, out of the land of Egypt with great acts of judgment.
7:5	The Egyptians shall know that I am Yahweh when I stretch out my hand against Egypt and bring the Israelites out from among them."
7:6	Moses and Aaron did so; as Yahweh commanded them, so they did.
7:7	Moses was eighty years old and Aaron eighty-three years old when they spoke to Pharaoh.
7:8	Yahweh said to Moses and Aaron,
7:9	"When Pharaoh says to you, 'Perform a wonder,' then you shall say to Aaron, 'Take your staff and throw it down before Pharaoh, so that it becomes a serpent.'"
7:10	So Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh and did just as Yahweh had commanded. Aaron threw down his staff before Pharaoh and his servants, and it became a serpent.
7:11	Then Pharaoh in turn summoned the wise men and the sorcerers, and they too, the magicians of Egypt, did the same by their secret arts.
7:12	Each of them threw down his staff, and they became serpents. But Aaron's staff swallowed up their staffs.
7:13	Yet Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he did not listen to them, just as Yahweh had said.
7:14	Then Yahweh said to Moses, "Pharaoh's heart is stubborn; he refuses to let the people go.
7:15	Go to Pharaoh in the morning, as he is going out to the water. Stand to meet him on the bank of the Nile, and take in your hand the staff that was turned into a snake.
7:16	Say to him, 'Yahweh, the God of the Hebrews, sent me to you, saying, "Let my people go, so that they may serve me in the wilderness." But so far you have not listened.
7:17	Thus says Yahweh: "By this you shall know that I am Yahweh: look, I will strike the water in the Nile with the staff that is in my hand, and it will be turned into blood.
7:18	The fish in the Nile will die, the Nile will stink, and the Egyptians will be unable to drink water from the Nile."'"
7:19	Yahweh said to Moses, "Say to Aaron, 'Take your staff and stretch out your hand over the waters of Egypt, over their rivers, over their canals, over their ponds, and over all their pools of water, so that they may become blood.' There will be blood throughout all the land of Egypt, even in vessels of wood and in vessels of stone."
7:20	Moses and Aaron did so, as Yahweh commanded. He raised the staff and struck the water in the Nile in the sight of Pharaoh and his servants, and all the water in the Nile was turned into blood.
7:21	The fish in the Nile died, and the Nile stank so that the Egyptians could not drink water from the Nile. There was blood throughout all the land of Egypt.
7:22	But the magicians of Egypt did the same by their secret arts. So Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he did not listen to them, just as Yahweh had said.
7:23	Pharaoh turned and went into his house, and he did not take even this to heart.
7:24	All the Egyptians dug around the Nile for water to drink, because they could not drink the water of the Nile.
7:25	Seven days passed after Yahweh had struck the Nile.
7:26	Then Yahweh said to Moses, "Go in to Pharaoh and say to him, 'Thus says Yahweh: Let my people go, so that they may serve me.
7:27	But if you refuse to let them go, I will plague all your territory with frogs.
7:28	The Nile will swarm with frogs; they will come up and go into your house, into your bedroom and onto your bed, into the houses of your servants and among your people, into your ovens and your kneading bowls.
7:29	The frogs will come up on you, on your people, and on all your servants.'"
