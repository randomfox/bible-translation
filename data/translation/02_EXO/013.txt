13:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
13:2	"Consecrate to Me every firstborn; whatever first opens the womb among the Israelites, both of humans and of animals, is Mine."
13:3	Moses said to the people, "Remember this day on which you came out of Egypt, out of the house of slavery, for by strength of hand Yahweh brought you out from here. No leavened bread shall be eaten.
13:4	Today, in the month of Abib, you are going out.
13:5	When Yahweh brings you into the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, which He swore to your fathers to give you, a land flowing with milk and honey, you shall keep this rite in this month.
13:6	For seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day there shall be a festival to Yahweh.
13:7	Unleavened bread shall be eaten for the seven days; no leavened bread shall be seen with you, and no leaven shall be seen with you in all your territory.
13:8	You shall tell your son on that day, 'It is because of what Yahweh did for me when I came out of Egypt.'
13:9	It shall be for you a sign on your hand and a reminder between your eyes, so that the law of Yahweh may be in your mouth, for with a mighty hand Yahweh brought you out of Egypt.
13:10	You shall keep this statute at its appointed time from year to year.
13:11	When Yahweh brings you into the land of the Canaanites, as He swore to you and your fathers, and gives it to you,
13:12	you shall set apart to Yahweh everything that first opens the womb. Every firstling that your livestock bear, the males shall be Yahweh's.
13:13	Every firstling of a donkey you shall redeem with a lamb, and if you do not redeem it, you shall break its neck. Every firstborn among your sons you shall redeem.
13:14	When in time to come your son asks you, 'What does this mean?' you shall say to him, 'By strength of hand Yahweh brought us out of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.
13:15	When Pharaoh stubbornly refused to let us go, Yahweh killed every firstborn in the land of Egypt, both the firstborn of humans and the firstborn of animals. That is why I sacrifice to Yahweh every male that first opens the womb, but every firstborn of my sons I redeem.'
13:16	It shall be a sign on your hand and a band between your eyes, for by strength of hand Yahweh brought us out of Egypt."
13:17	When Pharaoh let the people go, God did not lead them by the road through the land of the Philistines, although it was nearer, for God said, "The people might change their minds when they see war and return to Egypt."
13:18	So God led the people around by the wilderness road toward the Red Sea. The Israelites went up out of the land of Egypt equipped for battle.
13:19	Moses took the bones of Joseph with him, for Joseph had made the Israelites solemnly swear, saying, "God will surely take note of you, and then you must carry my bones up from here with you."
13:20	They set out from Succoth and camped at Etham, on the edge of the wilderness.
13:21	Yahweh went before them by day in a pillar of cloud to guide them on the way, and by night in a pillar of fire to give them light, so that they could travel by day and by night.
13:22	The pillar of cloud by day and the pillar of fire by night did not depart from before the people.
