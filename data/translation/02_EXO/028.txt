28:1	"Then bring near to you Aaron your brother, and his sons with him, from among the people of Israel, to serve me as priests: Aaron and Aaron's sons, Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.
28:2	And you shall make holy garments for Aaron your brother, for glory and for beauty.
28:3	You shall speak to all the skilled, whom I have filled with a spirit of skill, that they make Aaron's garments to consecrate him to serve me as priest.
28:4	These are the garments that they shall make: a breastpiece, an ephod, a robe, a tunic of checkered weave, a turban, and a sash. They shall make holy garments for Aaron your brother and his sons to serve me as priests.
28:5	They shall receive the gold, the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and the fine linen.
28:6	"They shall make the ephod of gold, of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and of fine twisted linen, the work of a skilled designer.
28:7	It shall have two shoulder pieces attached to its two edges, so that it may be joined together.
28:8	And the skillfully woven band on it, to fasten it, shall be of the same workmanship and of one piece with it, of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine twisted linen.
28:9	You shall take two onyx stones and engrave on them the names of the sons of Israel,
28:10	six of their names on the one stone and the names of the remaining six on the other stone, in the order of their birth.
28:11	As a gem-cutter engraves signets, so shall you engrave the two stones with the names of the sons of Israel; you shall set them in settings of gold filigree.
28:12	And you shall set the two stones on the shoulder pieces of the ephod, as stones of remembrance for the sons of Israel. And Aaron shall bear their names before Yahweh on his two shoulders for remembrance.
28:13	You shall make settings of gold filigree,
28:14	and two chains of pure gold, twisted like cords; you shall make them of corded work and fasten the corded chains to the settings.
28:15	"You shall make a breastpiece of judgment, the work of a skilled designer; in the style of the ephod you shall make it, of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine twisted linen you shall make it.
28:16	It shall be square and doubled, a span in length and a span in width.
28:17	You shall set in it a setting of stones, four rows of stones: a row of carnelian, chrysolite, and emerald shall be the first row;
28:18	and the second row a turquoise, a lapis lazuli, and a moonstone;
28:19	and the third row a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst;
28:20	and the fourth row a beryl, an onyx, and a jasper. They shall be set in gold filigree in their settings.
28:21	The stones shall correspond to the names of the sons of Israel, twelve, according to their names; they shall be like signets, each engraved with its name, for the twelve tribes.
28:22	You shall make on the breastpiece chains like cords, of corded work in pure gold.
28:23	And you shall make on the breastpiece two rings of gold, and put the two rings on the two ends of the breastpiece.
28:24	You shall put the two cords of gold in the two rings at the ends of the breastpiece.
28:25	The two ends of the two cords you shall attach to the two settings, and so attach it to the shoulder pieces of the ephod at the front.
28:26	You shall make two rings of gold and put them at the two ends of the breastpiece, on its inside edge that faces the ephod.
28:27	And you shall make two rings of gold and attach them to the lower part of the two shoulder pieces of the ephod at its front, near its seam, above the woven band of the ephod.
28:28	They shall bind the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a cord of blue, so that it may lie on the woven band of the ephod, and so that the breastpiece will not come loose from the ephod.
28:29	So Aaron shall bear the names of the sons of Israel in the breastpiece of judgment over his heart when he goes into the Holy Place, as a continual remembrance before Yahweh.
28:30	And in the breastpiece of judgment you shall put the Urim and the Thummim, and they shall be over Aaron's heart when he goes in before Yahweh. Thus Aaron shall bear the judgment of the people of Israel over his heart before Yahweh continually.
28:31	"You shall make the robe of the ephod entirely of blue.
28:32	It shall have an opening for the head in the middle of it, with a woven binding around its opening, like the opening of a coat of mail, so that it may not tear.
28:33	On its hem you shall make pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, around its hem, with bells of gold between them all around:
28:34	a golden bell and a pomegranate, a golden bell and a pomegranate, around the hem of the robe.
28:35	It shall be on Aaron when he ministers, and its sound shall be heard when he goes into the Holy Place before Yahweh and when he comes out, so that he does not die.
28:36	"You shall make a plate of pure gold and engrave on it, like the engraving of a signet, 'Holy to Yahweh.'
28:37	You shall fasten it on a cord of blue, and it shall be on the turban; it shall be on the front of the turban.
28:38	It shall be on Aaron's forehead, and Aaron shall bear the guilt connected with the holy things that the people of Israel consecrate, in all their holy gifts; it shall be on his forehead continually, so that they may be accepted before Yahweh.
28:39	"You shall weave the tunic in checkered work of fine linen, and you shall make a turban of fine linen, and you shall make a sash, the work of an embroiderer.
28:40	For Aaron's sons you shall make tunics, and you shall make sashes for them, and you shall make caps for them, for glory and for beauty.
28:41	And you shall put them on Aaron your brother and on his sons with him, and anoint them and ordain them and consecrate them, that they may serve me as priests.
28:42	You shall make for them linen undergarments to cover their naked flesh; they shall reach from the hips to the thighs.
28:43	They shall be on Aaron and on his sons when they go into the tent of meeting or when they approach the altar to minister in the Holy Place, so that they do not incur guilt and die. It shall be a statute forever for him and for his offspring after him.
