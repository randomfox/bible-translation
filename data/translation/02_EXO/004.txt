4:1	Moses answered, "But suppose they do not believe me or listen to my voice, but say, 'Yahweh did not appear to you'?"
4:2	Yahweh said to him, "What is that in your hand?" He said, "A staff."
4:3	He said, "Throw it on the ground." So he threw it on the ground, and it became a snake, and Moses ran from it.
4:4	Then Yahweh said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand and grab it by the tail." So he stretched out his hand and caught hold of it, and it became a staff in his palm.
4:5	"This is so that they may believe that Yahweh, the God of their fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, has appeared to you."
4:6	Yahweh also said to him, "Put your hand inside your cloak." So he put his hand inside his cloak, and when he took it out, his hand was diseased, white as snow.
4:7	He said, "Put your hand back inside your cloak." So he put his hand back inside his cloak, and when he took it out of his cloak, it had become again like the rest of his flesh.
4:8	"If they will not believe you or heed the voice of the first sign, they will believe the voice of the latter sign.
4:9	And if they will not believe even these two signs or listen to your voice, you shall take some water from the Nile and pour it on the dry ground, and the water that you take from the Nile will become blood on the dry ground."
4:10	Moses said to Yahweh, "Please, Lord, I am not a man of words, neither in the past nor since You have spoken to Your servant, for I am heavy of mouth and heavy of tongue."
4:11	Yahweh said to him, "Who has made a mouth for human beings? Or who makes a person mute or deaf, seeing or blind? Is it not I, Yahweh?
4:12	So now go, and I will be with your mouth and teach you what you are to say."
4:13	But he said, "Please, Lord, send by the hand of whomever else You will send."
4:14	Then Yahweh's anger burned against Moses, and He said, "Is there not Aaron your brother, the Levite? I know that he can certainly speak. And look, he is coming out to meet you, and when he sees you he will be glad in his heart.
4:15	You shall speak to him and put the words in his mouth, and I will be with your mouth and with his mouth, and I will teach you both what you are to do.
4:16	He shall speak for you to the people; he will be a mouth for you, and you will be as God to him.
4:17	And take this staff in your hand, with which you will perform the signs."
4:18	Moses went back to his father-in-law Jether and said to him, "Please let me go back to my relatives in Egypt and see whether they are still alive." And Jethro said to Moses, "Go in peace."
4:19	Yahweh said to Moses in Midian, "Go back to Egypt, for all the men who were seeking your life are dead."
4:20	So Moses took his wife and his sons, set them on the donkey, and returned to the land of Egypt. And Moses took the staff of God in his hand.
4:21	Yahweh said to Moses, "As you go back to Egypt, see that you perform before Pharaoh all the wonders that I have put in your power. But I will harden his heart, and he will not let the people go.
4:22	Then you shall say to Pharaoh, 'Thus says Yahweh: Israel is My son, My firstborn,
4:23	and I said to you, Let My son go so that he may serve Me, but you refused to let him go. Now I am going to kill your son, your firstborn.'"
4:24	At a lodging place on the way, Yahweh met him and sought to put him to death.
4:25	Then Zipporah took a flint, cut off her son's foreskin, and touched his feet with it, and she said, "Surely you are a bridegroom of blood to me!"
4:26	So He let him alone. It was then that she said, "A bridegroom of blood," because of the circumcision.
4:27	Yahweh said to Aaron, "Go into the wilderness to meet Moses." So he went and met him at the mountain of God and kissed him.
4:28	Moses told Aaron all the words of Yahweh with which He had sent him, and all the signs that He had commanded him to do.
4:29	Then Moses and Aaron went and gathered all the elders of the Israelites.
4:30	Aaron spoke all the words that Yahweh had spoken to Moses and performed the signs in the sight of the people.
4:31	And the people believed; and when they heard that Yahweh had taken note of the Israelites and that He had seen their affliction, they bowed down and worshiped.
