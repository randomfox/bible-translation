19:1	In the third month after the Israelites had gone out of the land of Egypt, on that very day, they came into the wilderness of Sinai.
19:2	They set out from Rephidim, came into the wilderness of Sinai, and camped in the wilderness; Israel camped there in front of the mountain.
19:3	Moses went up to God, and Yahweh called to him from the mountain, saying, "Thus you shall say to the house of Jacob and tell the Israelites:
19:4	You yourselves have seen what I did to the Egyptians, and how I carried you on eagles' wings and brought you to Myself.
19:5	Now therefore, if you will indeed obey My voice and keep My covenant, you shall be My treasured possession among all peoples, for all the earth is Mine;
19:6	and you shall be to Me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation. These are the words that you shall speak to the Israelites."
19:7	So Moses came and summoned the elders of the people and set before them all these words that Yahweh had commanded him.
19:8	All the people answered together and said, "All that Yahweh has spoken we will do." And Moses reported the words of the people to Yahweh.
19:9	Yahweh said to Moses, "Look, I am coming to you in a thick cloud, so that the people may hear when I speak with you and may also trust you forever." Then Moses told the words of the people to Yahweh.
19:10	Yahweh said to Moses, "Go to the people and consecrate them today and tomorrow, and let them wash their garments,
19:11	and be ready for the third day. For on the third day Yahweh will come down on Mount Sinai in the sight of all the people.
19:12	And you shall set limits for the people all around, saying, 'Take care not to go up the mountain or touch its edge. Whoever touches the mountain shall surely be put to death.
19:13	No hand shall touch him; he shall be stoned or shot; whether beast or man, he shall not live.' When the ram's horn sounds a long blast, they may go up the mountain."
19:14	So Moses went down from the mountain to the people and consecrated the people, and they washed their garments.
19:15	He said to the people, "Be ready for the third day; do not go near a woman."
19:16	On the morning of the third day there were thunders and lightnings and a thick cloud on the mountain, and a very loud blast of the ram's horn, so that all the people in the camp trembled.
19:17	Then Moses brought the people out of the camp to meet God, and they took their stand at the foot of the mountain.
19:18	Mount Sinai was wrapped in smoke, because Yahweh had descended on it in fire. Its smoke went up like the smoke of a kiln, and the whole mountain trembled greatly.
19:19	As the blast of the horn grew louder and louder, Moses would speak, and God would answer him in thunder.
19:20	Yahweh came down on Mount Sinai, to the top of the mountain, and Yahweh called Moses to the top of the mountain, and Moses went up.
19:21	Yahweh said to Moses, "Go down and warn the people, so that they do not break through to Yahweh to look and many of them perish.
19:22	Even the priests who come near to Yahweh must consecrate themselves, or Yahweh will break out against them."
19:23	Moses said to Yahweh, "The people cannot come up to Mount Sinai, for You Yourself warned us, saying, 'Set limits around the mountain and consecrate it.'"
19:24	Yahweh said to him, "Go down, and come up bringing Aaron with you; but do not let the priests and the people break through to come up to Yahweh, or He will break out against them."
19:25	So Moses went down to the people and told them.
