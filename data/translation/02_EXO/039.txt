39:1	From the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn they made finely woven garments for ministering in the sanctuary, and they made the holy garments for Aaron, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.
39:2	He made the ephod of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine twisted linen.
39:3	They hammered out sheets of gold, and he cut them into threads to work into the blue, the purple, the scarlet yarn, and the fine linen, the work of a skilled designer.
39:4	They made shoulder pieces for it, joined together; it was joined at its two edges.
39:5	The woven band on it, for fastening it, was of one piece with it and of the same workmanship: of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine twisted linen, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.
39:6	They prepared the onyx stones, mounted in gold settings, engraved as a seal is engraved, with the names of the sons of Israel.
39:7	He set them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod as stones of remembrance for the sons of Israel, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.
39:8	He made the breastpiece, the work of a skilled designer, like the work of the ephod: of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine twisted linen.
39:9	It was square; they made the breastpiece doubled, a span long and a span wide when doubled.
39:10	They set in it four rows of stones: a row of carnelian, topaz, and emerald was the first row;
39:11	the second row, turquoise, sapphire, and diamond;
39:12	the third row, jacinth, agate, and amethyst;
39:13	and the fourth row, beryl, onyx, and jasper. They were mounted in gold settings in their fillings.
39:14	The stones corresponded to the names of the sons of Israel; there were twelve, according to their names, engraved like a seal, each with its name, for the twelve tribes.
39:15	On the breastpiece they made chains like cords, braided work of pure gold.
39:16	They made two gold settings and two gold rings, and put the two rings on the two ends of the breastpiece.
39:17	They put the two gold cords on the two rings at the ends of the breastpiece.
39:18	The two ends of the two cords they attached to the two settings, and put them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod at its front.
39:19	They made two gold rings and put them on the two ends of the breastpiece, on its inner edge facing the ephod.
39:20	They made two gold rings and put them on the two shoulder pieces of the ephod, low on its front, near its seam, above the woven band of the ephod.
39:21	They bound the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a cord of blue, so that it would lie on the woven band of the ephod and the breastpiece would not come loose from the ephod, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.
39:22	He made the robe of the ephod, woven work, all of blue.
39:23	The opening of the robe was in its center, like the opening of a coat of mail, with a binding around its opening so that it would not tear.
39:24	On the hem of the robe they made pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, twisted.
39:25	They made bells of pure gold and put the bells between the pomegranates on the hem of the robe all around, between the pomegranates:
39:26	a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, on the hem of the robe all around, for ministering, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.
39:27	They made the tunics of fine linen, woven work, for Aaron and his sons,
39:28	the turban of fine linen, the ornamental caps of fine linen, and the linen undergarments of fine twisted linen,
39:29	and the sash of fine twisted linen and blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, embroiderer's work, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.
39:30	They made the plate, the holy diadem, of pure gold, and wrote on it an inscription engraved like a seal: "Holy to Yahweh."
39:31	They fastened a cord of blue to it to attach it to the turban above, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.
39:32	So all the work of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting was finished. The Israelites did everything just as Yahweh had commanded Moses; so they did.
39:33	They brought the tabernacle to Moses: the tent and all its furnishings, its clasps, its frames, its bars, its pillars, and its bases;
39:34	the covering of rams' skins dyed red, the covering of fine leather, and the screening veil;
39:35	the ark of the testimony with its poles and the atonement cover;
39:36	the table with all its utensils and the bread of the Presence;
39:37	the pure lampstand with its lamps, the lamps set in order, and all its utensils, and the oil for the light;
39:38	the gold altar, the anointing oil, the fragrant incense, and the screen for the entrance of the tent;
39:39	the bronze altar with its bronze grating, its poles, and all its utensils; the basin and its stand;
39:40	the hangings of the courtyard, its pillars and its bases, the screen for the courtyard gate, its cords and its pegs, and all the equipment for the service of the tabernacle, for the tent of meeting;
39:41	the finely woven garments for ministering in the sanctuary, the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons for serving as priests.
39:42	Just as Yahweh had commanded Moses, so the Israelites had done all the work.
39:43	Moses saw all the work, and indeed they had done it; as Yahweh had commanded, so they had done it. And Moses blessed them.
