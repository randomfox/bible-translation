8:1	Yahweh said to Moses, "Say to Aaron, 'Stretch out your hand with your staff over the rivers, over the canals, and over the ponds, and make the frogs come up on the land of Egypt.'"
8:2	So Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters of Egypt, and the frogs came up and covered the land of Egypt.
8:3	But the magicians did the same by their secret arts and made frogs come up on the land of Egypt.
8:4	Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron and said, "Plead with Yahweh to take the frogs away from me and from my people, and I will let the people go to sacrifice to Yahweh."
8:5	Moses said to Pharaoh, "You may have the honor over me: for when shall I plead for you, for your servants, and for your people, that the frogs be removed from you and your houses and remain only in the Nile?"
8:6	He said, "Tomorrow." Moses said, "As you say, so that you may know that there is no one like Yahweh our God.
8:7	The frogs will depart from you, from your houses, from your servants, and from your people; they will remain only in the Nile."
8:8	So Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh, and Moses cried out to Yahweh about the frogs that he had brought upon Pharaoh.
8:9	Yahweh did as Moses asked: the frogs died out in the houses, the courtyards, and the fields.
8:10	They piled them up in heaps, and the land stank.
8:11	But when Pharaoh saw that there was relief, he made his heart stubborn and did not listen to them, just as Yahweh had said.
8:12	Then Yahweh said to Moses, "Say to Aaron, 'Stretch out your staff and strike the dust of the earth, so that it becomes gnats throughout all the land of Egypt.'"
8:13	They did so. Aaron stretched out his hand with his staff and struck the dust of the earth, and gnats came on people and on animals. All the dust of the earth became gnats throughout all the land of Egypt.
8:14	The magicians tried to produce gnats by their secret arts, but they could not. And the gnats were on people and on animals.
8:15	Then the magicians said to Pharaoh, "This is the finger of God." But Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he did not listen to them, just as Yahweh had said.
8:16	Then Yahweh said to Moses, "Rise early in the morning and present yourself before Pharaoh as he goes out to the water, and say to him, 'Thus says Yahweh: Let My people go, so that they may serve Me.
8:17	For if you will not let My people go, I am going to send swarms against you, your servants, your people, and your houses. The houses of the Egyptians will be filled with swarms, and so will the ground on which they stand.
8:18	But on that day I will set apart the land of Goshen, where My people live, so that no swarms will be there, so that you may know that I am Yahweh in the midst of the land.
8:19	I will put redemption between My people and your people. Tomorrow this sign will happen.'"
8:20	Yahweh did so. Heavy swarms came into Pharaoh's house and into his servants' houses, and in all the land of Egypt the land was ruined because of the swarms.
8:21	Then Pharaoh summoned Moses and Aaron and said, "Go, sacrifice to your God within the land."
8:22	But Moses said, "It would not be right to do so, for what we sacrifice to Yahweh our God is an abomination to the Egyptians. If we sacrifice what is an abomination to the Egyptians before their eyes, will they not stone us?
8:23	We must go a three days' journey into the wilderness and sacrifice to Yahweh our God as He tells us."
8:24	So Pharaoh said, "I will let you go to sacrifice to Yahweh your God in the wilderness; only you must not go very far away. Plead for me."
8:25	Moses said, "As soon as I go out from you I will plead with Yahweh, and the swarms will depart from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people tomorrow. Only let Pharaoh not again deal deceitfully by not letting the people go to sacrifice to Yahweh."
8:26	So Moses went out from Pharaoh and pleaded with Yahweh.
8:27	Yahweh did as Moses asked and removed the swarms from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people; not one remained.
8:28	But Pharaoh made his heart stubborn this time also, and he did not let the people go.
