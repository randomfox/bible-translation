17:1	The whole congregation of the Israelites moved on from the wilderness of Sin by stages, according to the command of Yahweh, and camped at Rephidim, but there was no water for the people to drink.
17:2	So the people quarreled with Moses and said, "Give us water to drink." Moses said to them, "Why do you quarrel with me? Why do you test Yahweh?"
17:3	But the people thirsted there for water, and the people grumbled against Moses and said, "Why did you bring us up out of Egypt, to kill us and our children and our livestock with thirst?"
17:4	So Moses cried out to Yahweh, "What shall I do with this people? A little more and they will stone me."
17:5	Yahweh said to Moses, "Pass on before the people, taking with you some of the elders of Israel, and take in your hand the staff with which you struck the Nile, and go.
17:6	I will stand before you there on the rock at Horeb. Strike the rock, and water will come out of it, and the people will drink." And Moses did so in the sight of the elders of Israel.
17:7	He called the name of the place Massah and Meribah, because of the quarreling of the Israelites, and because they tested Yahweh by saying, "Is Yahweh among us or not?"
17:8	Then Amalek came and fought with Israel at Rephidim.
17:9	Moses said to Joshua, "Choose men for us, and go out and fight with Amalek. Tomorrow I will stand on the top of the hill with the staff of God in my hand."
17:10	So Joshua did as Moses told him and fought with Amalek, while Moses, Aaron, and Hur went up to the top of the hill.
17:11	Whenever Moses held up his hand, Israel prevailed, and whenever he lowered his hand, Amalek prevailed.
17:12	But Moses' hands grew heavy, so they took a stone and put it under him, and he sat on it, while Aaron and Hur held up his hands, one on one side and one on the other. So his hands were steady until the sun went down.
17:13	And Joshua overwhelmed Amalek and his people with the sword.
17:14	Then Yahweh said to Moses, "Write this as a memorial in a book and recite it in the hearing of Joshua: I will utterly blot out the memory of Amalek from under heaven."
17:15	And Moses built an altar and called its name Yahweh-nissi, "Yahweh is my banner,"
17:16	saying, "A hand upon the throne of Yah! Yahweh will have war with Amalek from generation to generation."
