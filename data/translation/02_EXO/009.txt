9:1	Then Yahweh said to Moses, "Go in to Pharaoh and tell him, 'Thus says Yahweh, the God of the Hebrews: Let My people go, so that they may serve Me.
9:2	For if you refuse to let them go and still hold on to them,
9:3	then the hand of Yahweh will come upon your livestock in the field, on the horses, the donkeys, the camels, the cattle, and the flocks, with a very severe plague.
9:4	But Yahweh will make a distinction between the livestock of Israel and the livestock of Egypt, so that nothing belonging to the Israelites will die.'"
9:5	Yahweh set a time, saying, "Tomorrow Yahweh will do this thing in the land."
9:6	And the next day Yahweh did this thing. All the livestock of Egypt died, but of the livestock of the Israelites not one died.
9:7	Pharaoh sent, and indeed not one of the livestock of Israel had died. But Pharaoh's heart was stubborn, and he did not let the people go.
9:8	Then Yahweh said to Moses and Aaron, "Take handfuls of soot from a kiln, and let Moses throw it toward the sky in the sight of Pharaoh.
9:9	It will become fine dust over all the land of Egypt and will become boils breaking out in blisters on people and animals throughout all the land of Egypt."
9:10	So they took soot from a kiln and stood before Pharaoh, and Moses threw it toward the sky, and it became boils breaking out in blisters on people and animals.
9:11	The magicians could not stand before Moses because of the boils, for the boils were on the magicians and on all the Egyptians.
9:12	But Yahweh hardened Pharaoh's heart, and he did not listen to them, just as Yahweh had told Moses.
9:13	Then Yahweh said to Moses, "Rise early in the morning, present yourself before Pharaoh, and say to him, 'Thus says Yahweh, the God of the Hebrews: Let My people go, so that they may serve Me.
9:14	For this time I am sending all My plagues upon your very heart, and on your servants and your people, so that you may know that there is no one like Me in all the earth.
9:15	For by now I could have stretched out My hand and struck you and your people with plague, and you would have been wiped off the earth.
9:16	But in fact, for this reason I have let you remain: to show you My power, and so that My name may be declared in all the earth.
9:17	You are still exalting yourself against My people by not letting them go.
9:18	Look, about this time tomorrow I will rain down very heavy hail, such as has never been in Egypt from the day it was founded until now.
9:19	So now send and bring your livestock and everything you have in the field into shelter. Every person and animal that is found in the field and not brought home, the hail will come down on them, and they will die.'"
9:20	Whoever among Pharaoh's servants feared the word of Yahweh hurried his slaves and his livestock into the houses,
9:21	but whoever did not pay attention to the word of Yahweh left his slaves and his livestock in the field.
9:22	Yahweh said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand toward the sky, so that there may be hail in all the land of Egypt, on people and animals and on every plant of the field in the land of Egypt."
9:23	Moses stretched out his staff toward the sky, and Yahweh sent thunder and hail, and fire came down to the earth. Yahweh rained hail on the land of Egypt.
9:24	There was hail with fire flashing continually in the midst of the hail, very heavy, such as had never been in all the land of Egypt since it became a nation.
9:25	The hail struck everything that was in the field throughout all the land of Egypt, both people and animals. The hail struck every plant of the field and shattered every tree of the field.
9:26	Only in the land of Goshen, where the Israelites were, was there no hail.
9:27	Then Pharaoh sent and summoned Moses and Aaron and said to them, "This time I have sinned. Yahweh is in the right, and I and my people are in the wrong.
9:28	Plead with Yahweh, for there has been enough of God's thunder and hail. I will let you go; you need not stay any longer."
9:29	Moses said to him, "As soon as I have gone out of the city, I will spread out my hands to Yahweh. The thunder will stop, and there will be no more hail, so that you may know that the earth belongs to Yahweh.
9:30	But as for you and your servants, I know that you do not yet fear Yahweh God."
9:31	(The flax and the barley were struck down, for the barley was in the ear and the flax was in bud.
9:32	But the wheat and the spelt were not struck down, for they ripen late.)
9:33	So Moses went out of the city from Pharaoh and spread out his hands to Yahweh. The thunder and the hail stopped, and the rain no longer poured down on the earth.
9:34	But when Pharaoh saw that the rain and the hail and the thunder had stopped, he sinned yet again and made his heart stubborn, he and his servants.
9:35	So Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he did not let the Israelites go, just as Yahweh had said through Moses.
