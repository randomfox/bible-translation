35:1	Moses assembled the whole congregation of the Israelites and said to them, "These are the things that Yahweh has commanded you to do.
35:2	Six days work shall be done, but the seventh day shall be holy to you, a Sabbath of solemn rest to Yahweh. Whoever does any work on it shall be put to death.
35:3	You shall kindle no fire in any of your dwellings on the Sabbath day."
35:4	Moses said to the whole congregation of the Israelites, "This is what Yahweh has commanded:
35:5	Take from among you a contribution for Yahweh. Whoever is of a willing heart, let him bring Yahweh's contribution: gold, silver, and bronze;
35:6	blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, fine linen, and goats' hair;
35:7	rams' skins dyed red, fine leather, and acacia wood;
35:8	oil for the light, spices for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense;
35:9	and onyx stones and stones for setting, for the ephod and for the breastpiece.
35:10	Let every skilled person among you come and make all that Yahweh has commanded:
35:11	the tabernacle, its tent and its covering, its clasps and its frames, its bars, its pillars, and its bases;
35:12	the ark with its poles, the atonement cover, and the curtain of the screen;
35:13	the table with its poles and all its utensils, and the bread of the Presence;
35:14	the lampstand for the light with its utensils and its lamps, and the oil for the light;
35:15	the altar of incense with its poles, the anointing oil, the fragrant incense, and the screen for the entrance, at the entrance of the tabernacle;
35:16	the altar of burnt offering with its bronze grating, its poles, and all its utensils, the basin and its stand;
35:17	the hangings of the court, its pillars and its bases, and the screen for the gate of the court;
35:18	the pegs of the tabernacle and the pegs of the court, and their cords;
35:19	the finely worked garments for ministering in the holy place, the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons for serving as priests."
35:20	Then the whole congregation of the Israelites departed from Moses' presence.
35:21	And everyone whose heart stirred him and everyone whose spirit moved him came and brought Yahweh's contribution for the work of the tent of meeting, for all its service, and for the holy garments.
35:22	Men and women came, all who were of a willing heart, and brought brooches, earrings, signet rings, and pendants, all sorts of gold objects, everyone presenting an offering of gold to Yahweh.
35:23	And everyone who possessed blue, purple, or scarlet yarn, fine linen, goats' hair, rams' skins dyed red, or fine leather brought them.
35:24	Everyone who could make a contribution of silver or bronze brought it as Yahweh's contribution, and everyone who possessed acacia wood for any work of the service brought it.
35:25	Every skilled woman spun with her hands and brought what she had spun in blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine linen.
35:26	All the women whose hearts stirred them with skill spun the goats' hair.
35:27	The leaders brought onyx stones and stones for setting, for the ephod and for the breastpiece,
35:28	and spices and oil for the light, for the anointing oil, and for the fragrant incense.
35:29	All the men and women of Israel whose hearts moved them to bring anything for the work that Yahweh had commanded through Moses to be done brought it as a freewill offering to Yahweh.
35:30	Then Moses said to the Israelites, "See, Yahweh has called by name Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah,
35:31	and he has filled him with the spirit of God, with skill, with understanding, with knowledge, and with all craftsmanship,
35:32	to devise artistic designs, to work in gold, silver, and bronze,
35:33	in cutting stones for setting, and in carving wood, for work in every skilled craft.
35:34	And he has put it in his heart to teach, both him and Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan.
35:35	He has filled them with skill to do every sort of work done by an engraver or designer or embroiderer in blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine linen, or by a weaver—by any sort of workman or skilled designer.
