18:1	Jethro, the priest of Midian, Moses' father-in-law, heard of all that God had done for Moses and for Israel His people, how Yahweh had brought Israel out of Egypt.
18:2	So Jethro, Moses' father-in-law, took Zipporah, Moses' wife, after he had sent her away,
18:3	and her two sons. The name of one was Gershom, for he said, "I have been a foreigner in a foreign land,"
18:4	and the name of the other was Eliezer, for he said, "The God of my father was my help, and He rescued me from the sword of Pharaoh."
18:5	Jethro, Moses' father-in-law, came with Moses' sons and his wife to Moses in the wilderness where he was camped at the mountain of God.
18:6	He sent word to Moses, "I, your father-in-law Jethro, am coming to you, with your wife and her two sons with her."
18:7	Moses went out to meet his father-in-law, bowed down and kissed him, and they asked each other about their welfare and went into the tent.
18:8	Then Moses told his father-in-law all that Yahweh had done to Pharaoh and to the Egyptians for Israel's sake, all the hardship that had come upon them on the way, and how Yahweh had rescued them.
18:9	Jethro rejoiced over all the good that Yahweh had done to Israel in rescuing them from the hand of the Egyptians.
18:10	Jethro said, "Blessed be Yahweh, Who has rescued you from the hand of the Egyptians and from the hand of Pharaoh, Who has rescued the people from under the hand of the Egyptians.
18:11	Now I know that Yahweh is greater than all gods, because of what happened when they acted arrogantly against the people."
18:12	And Jethro, Moses' father-in-law, brought a burnt offering and sacrifices to God, and Aaron came with all the elders of Israel to eat bread with Moses' father-in-law before God.
18:13	The next day Moses sat to judge the people, and the people stood around Moses from morning till evening.
18:14	When Moses' father-in-law saw all that he was doing for the people, he said, "What is this that you are doing for the people? Why do you sit alone, with all the people standing around you from morning till evening?"
18:15	Moses said to his father-in-law, "Because the people come to me to inquire of God.
18:16	When they have a dispute, they come to me, and I decide between one person and another, and I make known the statutes of God and His instructions."
18:17	Moses' father-in-law said to him, "What you are doing is not good.
18:18	You will surely wear yourself out, both you and these people with you, for the task is too heavy for you; you cannot do it alone.
18:19	Now listen to me; I will give you advice, and may God be with you. You represent the people before God and bring their cases to God,
18:20	and you warn them about the statutes and the instructions, and make known to them the way they must walk and what they must do.
18:21	But select from all the people capable men who fear God, trustworthy men who hate dishonest gain, and set them over the people as officers of thousands, of hundreds, of fifties, and of tens.
18:22	Let them judge the people at all times. Every major matter they shall bring to you, but every minor matter they shall decide themselves. So it will be easier for you, and they will bear the burden with you.
18:23	If you do this, and God so commands you, you will be able to endure, and all these people also will go to their place in peace."
18:24	So Moses listened to his father-in-law and did all that he had said.
18:25	Moses chose capable men out of all Israel and made them heads over the people, officers of thousands, of hundreds, of fifties, and of tens.
18:26	They judged the people at all times; the hard cases they brought to Moses, but every minor matter they decided themselves.
18:27	Then Moses let his father-in-law depart, and he went away to his own land.
