22:1	To the choirmaster: according to The Doe of the Dawn. A Psalm of David.
22:2	My God, My God, why have You forsaken Me? Why are You so far from saving Me, from the words of My groaning?
22:3	O My God, I cry by day, but You do not answer, and by night, but I find no rest.
22:4	Yet You are holy, enthroned on the praises of Israel.
22:5	In You our fathers trusted; they trusted, and You delivered them.
22:6	To You they cried and were rescued; in You they trusted and were not put to shame.
22:7	But I am a worm and not a man, scorned by mankind and despised by the people.
22:8	All who see Me mock Me; they make mouths at Me; they wag their heads;
22:9	"He trusts in Yahweh; let Him deliver Him; let Him rescue Him, for He delights in Him!"
22:10	Yet You are He Who took Me from the womb; You made Me trust You at My mother's breasts.
22:11	On You was I cast from My birth, and from My mother's womb You have been My God.
22:12	Be not far from Me, for trouble is near, and there is none to help.
22:13	Many bulls encompass Me; strong bulls of Bashan surround Me;
22:14	they open wide their mouths at Me, like a ravening and roaring lion.
22:15	I am poured out like water, and all My bones are out of joint; My heart is like wax; it is melted within My breast;
22:16	My strength is dried up like a potsherd, and My tongue sticks to My jaws; You lay Me in the dust of death.
22:17	For dogs encompass Me; a company of evildoers encircles Me; they pierce My hands and My feet.
22:18	I can count all My bones; they stare and gloat over Me;
22:19	they divide My garments among them, and for My clothing they cast lots.
22:20	But You, O Yahweh, do not be far off! O My help, come quickly to My aid!
22:21	Deliver My soul from the sword, My precious life from the power of the dog!
22:22	Save Me from the mouth of the lion! From the horns of the wild oxen You have answered Me!
22:23	I will tell of Your name to My brothers; in the midst of the congregation I will praise You:
22:24	You who fear Yahweh, praise Him! All you offspring of Jacob, glorify Him, and stand in awe of Him, all you offspring of Israel!
22:25	For He has not despised or abhorred the affliction of the afflicted, and He has not hidden His face from Him, but has heard, when He cried to Him.
22:26	From You comes My praise in the great congregation; My vows I will perform before those who fear Him.
22:27	The afflicted shall eat and be satisfied; those who seek Him shall praise Yahweh! May your hearts live forever!
22:28	All the ends of the earth shall remember and turn to Yahweh, and all the families of the nations shall worship before You.
22:29	For kingship belongs to Yahweh, and He rules over the nations.
22:30	All the prosperous of the earth eat and worship; before Him shall bow all who go down to the dust, even the one who could not keep himself alive.
22:31	Posterity shall serve Him; it shall be told of the Lord to the coming generation;
22:32	they shall come and proclaim His righteousness to a people yet unborn, that He has done it.
