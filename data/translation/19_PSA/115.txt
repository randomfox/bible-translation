115:1	Not to us, O Yahweh, not to us, but to Your name give glory, for the sake of Your steadfast love and Your faithfulness!
115:2	Why should the nations say, "Where is their God?"
115:3	Our God is in the heavens; He does all that He pleases.
115:4	Their idols are silver and gold, the work of human hands.
115:5	They have mouths, but do not speak; eyes, but do not see.
115:6	They have ears, but do not hear; noses, but do not smell.
115:7	They have hands, but do not feel; feet, but do not walk; and they do not make a sound in their throat.
115:8	Those who make them become like them; so do all who trust in them.
115:9	O Israel, trust in Yahweh! He is their help and their shield.
115:10	O house of Aaron, trust in Yahweh! He is their help and their shield.
115:11	You who fear Yahweh, trust in Yahweh! He is their help and their shield.
115:12	Yahweh has remembered us; He will bless us; He will bless the house of Israel; He will bless the house of Aaron;
115:13	He will bless those who fear Yahweh, both the small and the great.
115:14	May Yahweh give you increase, you and your children!
115:15	May you be blessed by Yahweh, Who made heaven and earth!
115:16	The heavens are Yahweh's heavens, but the earth He has given to the children of man.
115:17	The dead do not praise Yah, nor do any who go down into silence.
115:18	But we will bless Yah from this time forth and forevermore. Praise Yah!
