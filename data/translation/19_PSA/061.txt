61:1	To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments. Of David.
61:2	Hear my cry, O God, listen to my prayer;
61:3	from the end of the earth I call to You when my heart is faint. Lead me to the rock that is higher than I,
61:4	for You have been my refuge, a strong tower against the enemy.
61:5	Let me dwell in Your tent forever! Let me take refuge under the shelter of Your wings! Selah
61:6	For You, O God, have heard my vows; You have given me the heritage of those who fear Your name.
61:7	Prolong the life of the king; may his years endure to all generations!
61:8	May he be enthroned forever before God; appoint steadfast love and faithfulness to watch over him!
61:9	So will I ever sing praises to Your name, as I perform my vows day after day.
