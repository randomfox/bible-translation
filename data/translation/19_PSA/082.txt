82:1	A Psalm of Asaph. God has taken his place in the divine council; in the midst of the gods he holds judgment:
82:2	"How long will you judge unjustly and show partiality to the wicked? Selah
82:3	Give justice to the weak and the fatherless; maintain the right of the afflicted and the destitute.
82:4	Rescue the weak and the needy; deliver them from the hand of the wicked."
82:5	They have neither knowledge nor understanding; they walk about in darkness; all the foundations of the earth are shaken.
82:6	I said, "You are gods, sons of the Most High, all of you;
82:7	nevertheless, like men you shall die, and fall like any prince."
82:8	Arise, O God, judge the earth; for you shall inherit all the nations!
