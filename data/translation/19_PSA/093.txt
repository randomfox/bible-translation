93:1	Yahweh reigns; He is robed in majesty; Yahweh is robed; He has put on strength as His belt. Yes, the world is established; it shall never be moved.
93:2	Your throne is established from of old; You are from everlasting.
93:3	The floods have lifted up, O Yahweh, the floods have lifted up their voice; the floods lift up their roaring.
93:4	Mightier than the thunders of many waters, mightier than the waves of the sea, Yahweh on high is mighty!
93:5	Your decrees are very trustworthy; holiness befits Your house, O Yahweh, forevermore.
