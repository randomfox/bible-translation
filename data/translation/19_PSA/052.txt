52:1	To the choirmaster. A Maskil of David,
52:2	when Doeg the Edomite came and told Saul, "David has come to the house of Ahimelech."
52:3	Why do you boast of evil, O mighty man? The steadfast love of God endures all the day.
52:4	Your tongue plots destruction, like a sharp razor, you worker of deceit.
52:5	You love evil more than good, and lying more than speaking what is right. Selah
52:6	You love all words that devour, O deceitful tongue.
52:7	But God will break you down forever; he will snatch and tear you from your tent; he will uproot you from the land of the living. Selah
52:8	The righteous shall see and fear, and shall laugh at him, saying,
52:9	"See the man who would not make God his refuge, but trusted in the abundance of his riches and sought strength in his own destruction!"
52:10	But I am like a green olive tree in the house of God. I trust in the steadfast love of God forever and ever.
52:11	I will thank you forever, because you have done it. I will wait for your name, for it is good, in the presence of your faithful ones.
