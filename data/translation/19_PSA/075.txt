75:1	To the choirmaster: according to Do Not Destroy. A Psalm of Asaph. A Song.
75:2	We give thanks to You, O God; we give thanks, for Your name is near. We recount Your wondrous deeds.
75:3	"At the set time that I appoint I will judge with equity.
75:4	When the earth totters, and all its inhabitants, it is I Who keep steady its pillars. Selah
75:5	I say to the boastful, 'Do not boast,' and to the wicked, 'Do not lift up your horn;
75:6	do not lift up your horn on high, or speak with haughty neck.'"
75:7	For not from the east or from the west and not from the wilderness comes lifting up,
75:8	but it is God Who executes judgment, putting down one and lifting up another.
75:9	For in the hand of Yahweh there is a cup with foaming wine, well mixed, and He pours out from it, and all the wicked of the earth shall drain it down to the dregs.
75:10	But I will declare it forever; I will sing praises to the God of Jacob.
75:11	All the horns of the wicked I will cut off, but the horns of the righteous shall be lifted up.
