83:1	A Song. A Psalm of Asaph.
83:2	O God, do not keep silence; do not hold your peace or be still, O God!
83:3	For behold, your enemies make an uproar; those who hate you have raised their heads.
83:4	They lay crafty plans against your people; they consult together against your treasured ones.
83:5	They say, "Come, let us wipe them out as a nation; let the name of Israel be remembered no more!"
83:6	For they conspire with one accord; against you they make a covenant—
83:7	the tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites, Moab and the Hagrites,
83:8	Gebal and Ammon and Amalek, Philistia with the inhabitants of Tyre;
83:9	Asshur also has joined them; they are the strong arm of the children of Lot. Selah
83:10	Do to them as you did to Midian, as to Sisera and Jabin at the river Kishon,
83:11	who were destroyed at En-dor, who became dung for the ground.
83:12	Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb, all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna,
83:13	who said, "Let us take possession for ourselves of the pastures of God."
83:14	O my God, make them like whirling dust, like chaff before the wind.
83:15	As fire consumes the forest, as the flame sets the mountains ablaze,
83:16	so may you pursue them with your tempest and terrify them with your hurricane!
83:17	Fill their faces with shame, that they may seek your name, O Yahweh.
83:18	Let them be put to shame and dismayed forever; let them perish in disgrace,
83:19	that they may know that you alone, whose name is Yahweh, are the Most High over all the earth.
