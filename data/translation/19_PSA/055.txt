55:1	To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments. A Maskil of David.
55:2	Give ear to my prayer, O God, and hide not Yourself from my plea for mercy!
55:3	Attend to me, and answer me; I am restless in my complaint and I moan,
55:4	because of the noise of the enemy, because of the oppression of the wicked. For they drop trouble upon me, and in anger they bear a grudge against me.
55:5	My heart is in anguish within me; the terrors of death have fallen upon me.
55:6	Fear and trembling come upon me, and horror overwhelms me.
55:7	And I say, "Oh, that I had wings like a dove! I would fly away and be at rest;
55:8	yes, I would wander far away; I would lodge in the wilderness; Selah
55:9	I would hurry to find a shelter from the raging wind and tempest."
55:10	Destroy, O Lord, divide their tongues; for I see violence and strife in the city.
55:11	Day and night they go around it on its walls, and iniquity and trouble are within it;
55:12	ruin is in its midst; oppression and fraud do not depart from its marketplace.
55:13	For it is not an enemy who taunts me—then I could bear it; it is not an adversary who deals insolently with me—then I could hide from him.
55:14	But it is you, a man, my equal, my companion, my familiar friend.
55:15	We used to take sweet counsel together; within God's house we walked in the throng.
55:16	Let death steal over them; let them go down to Sheol alive; for evil is in their dwelling place and in their heart.
55:17	But I call to God, and Yahweh will save me.
55:18	Evening and morning and at noon I utter my complaint and moan, and He hears my voice.
55:19	He redeems my soul in safety from the battle that I wage, for many are arrayed against me.
55:20	God will give ear and humble them, He Who is enthroned from of old, Selah, because they do not change and do not fear God.
55:21	My companion stretched out his hand against his friends; he violated his covenant.
55:22	His speech was smooth as butter, yet war was in his heart; his words were softer than oil, yet they were drawn swords.
55:23	Cast your burden on Yahweh, and He will sustain you; He will never permit the righteous to be moved.
55:24	But You, O God, will cast them down into the pit of destruction; men of blood and treachery shall not live out half their days. But I will trust in You.
