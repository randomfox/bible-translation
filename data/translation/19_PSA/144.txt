144:1	Of David. Blessed be Yahweh, my rock, who trains my hands for war and my fingers for battle;
144:2	my steadfast love and my fortress, my stronghold and my deliverer, my shield and he in whom I take refuge, who subdues my people under me.
144:3	O Yahweh, what is man that you take note of him, or a son of man that you think of him?
144:4	Man is like a breath; his days are like a passing shadow.
144:5	O Yahweh, bend your heavens and come down! Touch the mountains so that they smoke!
144:6	Flash forth lightning and scatter them; send out your arrows and rout them!
144:7	Stretch out your hand from on high; rescue me and deliver me from many waters, from the hand of foreigners,
144:8	whose mouths speak falsehood and whose right hand is a right hand of lies.
144:9	O God, I will sing a new song to you; on a ten-stringed harp I will make music to you,
144:10	who gives victory to kings, who rescues David his servant from the evil sword.
144:11	Rescue me and deliver me from the hand of foreigners, whose mouths speak falsehood and whose right hand is a right hand of lies.
144:12	May our sons in their youth be like plants full grown, our daughters like corner pillars carved to adorn a palace;
144:13	may our barns be full, providing every kind of produce; may our flocks increase by thousands, by tens of thousands in our fields;
144:14	may our cattle be heavy with young; may there be no breach in the walls and no going into exile, and no outcry in our streets!
144:15	Happy the people to whom it is so! Happy the people whose God is Yahweh!
