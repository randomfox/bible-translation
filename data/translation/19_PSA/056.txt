56:1	To the choirmaster: according to The Dove on Far-off Terebinths. A Miktam of David, when the Philistines seized him in Gath.
56:2	Be gracious to me, O God, for man tramples on me; all day long an attacker oppresses me;
56:3	my enemies trample on me all day long, for many attack me proudly.
56:4	When I am afraid, I put my trust in you.
56:5	In God, whose word I praise, in God I trust; I shall not be afraid. What can flesh do to me?
56:6	All day long they injure my cause; all their thoughts are against me for evil.
56:7	They stir up strife, they lurk; they watch my steps, as they have waited for my life.
56:8	For their crime will they escape? In wrath cast down the peoples, O God!
56:9	You have kept count of my wanderings; put my tears in your bottle. Are they not in your book?
56:10	Then my enemies will turn back in the day when I call. This I know, that God is for me.
56:11	In God, whose word I praise, in Yahweh, whose word I praise,
56:12	in God I trust; I shall not be afraid. What can man do to me?
56:13	I must perform my vows to you, O God; I will render thank offerings to you.
56:14	For you have delivered my soul from death, yes, my feet from falling, that I may walk before God in the light of life.
