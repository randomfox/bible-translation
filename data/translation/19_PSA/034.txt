34:1	Of David, when he changed his behavior before Abimelech, so that he drove him out, and he went away.
34:2	I will bless Yahweh at all times; his praise shall continually be in my mouth.
34:3	My soul makes its boast in Yahweh; let the humble hear and be glad.
34:4	Oh, magnify Yahweh with me, and let us exalt his name together!
34:5	I sought Yahweh, and he answered me and delivered me from all my fears.
34:6	Those who look to him are radiant, and their faces shall never be ashamed.
34:7	This poor man cried, and Yahweh heard him and saved him out of all his troubles.
34:8	The angel of Yahweh encamps around those who fear him, and delivers them.
34:9	Oh, taste and see that Yahweh is good! Blessed is the man who takes refuge in him!
34:10	Oh, fear Yahweh, you his holy ones, for those who fear him have no lack!
34:11	The young lions suffer want and hunger; but those who seek Yahweh lack no good thing.
34:12	Come, O children, listen to me; I will teach you the fear of Yahweh.
34:13	What man is there who desires life and loves many days, that he may see good?
34:14	Keep your tongue from evil and your lips from speaking deceit.
34:15	Turn away from evil and do good; seek peace and pursue it.
34:16	The eyes of Yahweh are toward the righteous and his ears toward their cry.
34:17	The face of Yahweh is against those who do evil, to cut off the memory of them from the earth.
34:18	When the righteous cry for help, Yahweh hears and delivers them out of all their troubles.
34:19	Yahweh is near to the brokenhearted and saves the crushed in spirit.
34:20	Many are the afflictions of the righteous, but Yahweh delivers him out of them all.
34:21	He keeps all his bones; not one of them is broken.
34:22	Affliction will slay the wicked, and those who hate the righteous will be condemned.
34:23	Yahweh redeems the life of his servants; none of those who take refuge in him will be condemned.
