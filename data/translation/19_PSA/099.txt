99:1	Yahweh reigns; let the peoples tremble! He sits enthroned upon the cherubim; let the earth quake!
99:2	Yahweh is great in Zion; he is exalted over all the peoples.
99:3	Let them praise your great and awesome name! Holy is he!
99:4	The King in his might loves justice. You have established equity; you have executed justice and righteousness in Jacob.
99:5	Exalt Yahweh our God; worship at his footstool! Holy is he!
99:6	Moses and Aaron were among his priests, Samuel also was among those who called upon his name. They called to Yahweh, and he answered them.
99:7	In the pillar of the cloud he spoke to them; they kept his testimonies and the statute that he gave them.
99:8	O Yahweh our God, you answered them; you were a forgiving God to them, but an avenger of their wrongdoings.
99:9	Exalt Yahweh our God, and worship at his holy mountain; for Yahweh our God is holy!
