13:1	To the choirmaster. A Psalm of David.
13:2	How long, O Yahweh? Will You forget me forever? How long will You hide Your face from me?
13:3	How long must I take counsel in my soul and have sorrow in my heart all the day? How long shall my enemy be exalted over me?
13:4	Consider and answer me, O Yahweh my God; light up my eyes, lest I sleep the sleep of death,
13:5	lest my enemy say, "I have prevailed over him," lest my foes rejoice because I am shaken.
13:6	But I have trusted in Your steadfast love; my heart shall rejoice in Your salvation. I will sing to Yahweh, because He has dealt bountifully with me.
