57:1	To the choirmaster: according to Do Not Destroy. A Miktam of David, when he fled from Saul, in the cave.
57:2	Be gracious to me, O God, be gracious to me, for in You my soul takes refuge; in the shadow of Your wings I will take refuge, till the storms of destruction pass by.
57:3	I cry out to God Most High, to God Who fulfills His purpose for me.
57:4	He will send from heaven and save me; He will put to shame him who tramples on me. Selah. God will send out His steadfast love and His faithfulness!
57:5	My soul is in the midst of lions; I lie down amid fiery beasts—the children of man, whose teeth are spears and arrows, whose tongues are sharp swords.
57:6	Be exalted, O God, above the heavens! Let Your glory be over all the earth!
57:7	They set a net for my steps; my soul was bowed down. They dug a pit in my way, but they have fallen into it themselves. Selah
57:8	My heart is steadfast, O God, my heart is steadfast! I will sing and make melody!
57:9	Awake, my glory! Awake, O harp and lyre! I will awake the dawn!
57:10	I will give thanks to You, O Lord, among the peoples; I will sing praises to You among the nations.
57:11	For Your steadfast love is great to the heavens, Your faithfulness to the clouds.
57:12	Be exalted, O God, above the heavens! Let Your glory be over all the earth!
