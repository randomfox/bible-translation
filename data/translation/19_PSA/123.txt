123:1	A Song of Ascents. To you I lift up my eyes, O you who are enthroned in the heavens!
123:2	Behold, as the eyes of servants look to the hand of their master, as the eyes of a maidservant to the hand of her mistress, so our eyes look to Yahweh our God, till he has mercy upon us.
123:3	Have mercy upon us, O Yahweh, have mercy upon us, for we have had more than enough of contempt.
123:4	Our soul has had more than enough of the scorn of those who are at ease, of the contempt of the proud.
