70:1	To the choirmaster. Of David, for the memorial offering.
70:2	Make haste, O God, to deliver me! O Yahweh, make haste to help me!
70:3	Let them be put to shame and confusion who seek my life! Let them be turned back and brought to dishonor who delight in my hurt!
70:4	Let them turn back because of their shame who say, "Aha, Aha!"
70:5	May all who seek You rejoice and be glad in You! May those who love Your salvation say evermore, "God is great!"
70:6	But I am poor and needy; hasten to me, O God! You are my help and my deliverer; O Yahweh, do not delay!
