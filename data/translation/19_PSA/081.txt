81:1	To the choirmaster: according to The Gittith. Of Asaph.
81:2	Sing aloud to God our strength; shout for joy to the God of Jacob!
81:3	Raise a song; sound the tambourine, the sweet lyre with the harp.
81:4	Blow the trumpet at the new moon, at the full moon, on our feast day.
81:5	For it is a statute for Israel, a rule of the God of Jacob.
81:6	He made it a decree in Joseph when He went out over the land of Egypt. I hear a language I had not known:
81:7	"I relieved your shoulder of the burden; your hands were freed from the basket.
81:8	In distress you called, and I delivered you; I answered you in the secret place of thunder; I tested you at the waters of Meribah. Selah
81:9	Hear, O My people, while I admonish you! O Israel, if you would but listen to Me!
81:10	There shall be no strange god among you; you shall not bow down to a foreign god.
81:11	I am Yahweh your God, Who brought you up out of the land of Egypt. Open your mouth wide, and I will fill it.
81:12	But My people did not listen to My voice; Israel would not submit to Me.
81:13	So I gave them over to their stubborn hearts, to follow their own counsels.
81:14	Oh, that My people would listen to Me, that Israel would walk in My ways!
81:15	I would soon subdue their enemies and turn My hand against their foes.
81:16	Those who hate Yahweh would cringe toward Him, and their fate would last forever.
81:17	But He would feed you with the finest of the wheat, and with honey from the rock I would satisfy you."
