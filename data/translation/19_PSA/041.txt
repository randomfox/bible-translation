41:1	To the choirmaster. A Psalm of David.
41:2	Blessed is the one who considers the poor! In the day of trouble Yahweh delivers him;
41:3	Yahweh protects him and keeps him alive; he is called blessed in the land; You do not give him up to the will of his enemies.
41:4	Yahweh sustains him on his sickbed; in his illness You restore him to full health.
41:5	As for me, I said, "O Yahweh, be gracious to me; heal me, for I have sinned against You!"
41:6	My enemies say of me in malice, "When will he die, and his name perish?"
41:7	And when one comes to see me, he utters empty words, while his heart gathers iniquity; when he goes out, he tells it abroad.
41:8	All who hate me whisper together about me; they imagine the worst for me.
41:9	They say, "A deadly thing is poured out on him; he will not rise again from where he lies."
41:10	Even my close friend in whom I trusted, who ate my bread, has lifted his heel against me.
41:11	But You, O Yahweh, be gracious to me, and raise me up, that I may repay them!
41:12	By this I know that You delight in me: my enemy will not shout in triumph over me.
41:13	But You have upheld me because of my integrity, and set me in Your presence forever.
41:14	Blessed be Yahweh, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting! Amen and Amen.
