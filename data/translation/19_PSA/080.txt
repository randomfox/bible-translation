80:1	To the choirmaster: according to Lilies. A Testimony. Of Asaph, a Psalm.
80:2	Give ear, O Shepherd of Israel, you who lead Joseph like a flock. You who are enthroned upon the cherubim, shine forth.
80:3	Before Ephraim and Benjamin and Manasseh, stir up your might and come to save us!
80:4	Restore us, O God; let your face shine, that we may be saved!
80:5	O Yahweh God of Armies, how long will you be angry with your people's prayers?
80:6	You have fed them with the bread of tears and given them tears to drink in full measure.
80:7	You make us an object of contention for our neighbors, and our enemies laugh among themselves.
80:8	Restore us, O God of Armies; let your face shine, that we may be saved!
80:9	You brought a vine out of Egypt; you drove out the nations and planted it.
80:10	You cleared the ground for it; it took deep root and filled the land.
80:11	The mountains were covered with its shade, the mighty cedars with its branches.
80:12	It sent out its branches to the sea and its shoots to the River.
80:13	Why then have you broken down its walls, so that all who pass along the way pluck its fruit?
80:14	The boar from the forest ravages it, and all that move in the field feed on it.
80:15	Turn again, O God of Armies! Look down from heaven, and see; have regard for this vine,
80:16	the stock that your right hand planted, and for the son whom you made strong for yourself.
80:17	They have burned it with fire; they have cut it down; may they perish at the rebuke of your face!
80:18	But let your hand be on the man of your right hand, the son of man whom you have made strong for yourself!
80:19	Then we shall not turn back from you; give us life, and we will call upon your name!
80:20	Restore us, O Yahweh God of Armies! Let your face shine, that we may be saved!
