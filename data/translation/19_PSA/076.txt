76:1	To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments. A Psalm of Asaph. A Song.
76:2	In Judah God is known; his name is great in Israel.
76:3	His abode has been established in Salem, his dwelling place in Zion.
76:4	There he broke the flashing arrows, the shield, the sword, and the weapons of war. Selah
76:5	Glorious are you, more majestic than the mountains of prey.
76:6	The stouthearted were stripped of their spoil; they sank into sleep; all the men of war were unable to use their hands.
76:7	At your rebuke, O God of Jacob, both rider and horse lay stunned.
76:8	But you, you are to be feared! Who can stand before you when once your anger is roused?
76:9	From the heavens you uttered judgment; the earth feared and was still,
76:10	when God arose to establish judgment, to save all the humble of the earth. Selah
76:11	Surely the wrath of man shall praise you; the remnant of wrath you will put on like a belt.
76:12	Make your vows to Yahweh your God and perform them; let all around him bring gifts to him who is to be feared,
76:13	who cuts off the spirit of princes, who is to be feared by the kings of the earth.
