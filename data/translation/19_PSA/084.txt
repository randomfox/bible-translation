84:1	To the choirmaster: according to The Gittith. A Psalm of the Sons of Korah.
84:2	How lovely is your dwelling place, O Yahweh of Armies!
84:3	My soul longs, yes, faints for the courts of Yahweh; my heart and flesh sing for joy to the living God.
84:4	Even the sparrow finds a home, and the swallow a nest for herself, where she may lay her young, at your altars, O Yahweh of Armies, my King and my God.
84:5	Blessed are those who dwell in your house, ever singing your praise! Selah
84:6	Blessed are those whose strength is in you, in whose heart are the highways to Zion.
84:7	As they go through the Valley of Baca they make it a place of springs; the early rain also covers it with pools.
84:8	They go from strength to strength; each one appears before God in Zion.
84:9	O Yahweh God of Armies, hear my prayer; give ear, O God of Jacob! Selah
84:10	Behold our shield, O God; look on the face of your anointed!
84:11	For a day in your courts is better than a thousand elsewhere. I would rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God than dwell in the tents of wickedness.
84:12	For Yahweh God is a sun and shield; Yahweh bestows favor and honor. No good thing does he withhold from those who walk uprightly.
84:13	O Yahweh of Armies, blessed is the one who trusts in you!
