5:1	To the choirmaster: for the flutes. A Psalm of David.
5:2	Give ear to my words, O Yahweh; consider my groaning.
5:3	Give attention to the sound of my cry, my King and my God, for to you do I pray.
5:4	O Yahweh, in the morning you hear my voice; in the morning I prepare a sacrifice for you and watch.
5:5	For you are not a God who delights in wickedness; evil may not dwell with you.
5:6	The boastful shall not stand before your eyes; you hate all evildoers.
5:7	You destroy those who speak lies; Yahweh abhors the bloodthirsty and deceitful man.
5:8	But I, through the abundance of your steadfast love, will enter your house. I will bow down toward your holy temple in the fear of you.
5:9	Lead me, O Yahweh, in your righteousness because of my enemies; make your way straight before me.
5:10	For there is no truth in their mouth; their inmost self is destruction; their throat is an open grave; they flatter with their tongue.
5:11	Make them bear their guilt, O God; let them fall by their own counsels; because of the abundance of their transgressions cast them out, for they have rebelled against you.
5:12	But let all who take refuge in you rejoice; let them ever sing for joy, and spread your protection over them, that those who love your name may exult in you.
5:13	For you bless the righteous, O Yahweh; you cover him with favor as with a shield.
