36:1	To the choirmaster. Of David, the servant of Yahweh.
36:2	Transgression speaks to the wicked deep in his heart; there is no fear of God before his eyes.
36:3	For he flatters himself in his own eyes that his iniquity cannot be found out and hated.
36:4	The words of his mouth are trouble and deceit; he has ceased to act wisely and do good.
36:5	He plots trouble while on his bed; he sets himself in a way that is not good; he does not reject evil.
36:6	Your steadfast love, O Yahweh, extends to the heavens, Your faithfulness to the clouds.
36:7	Your righteousness is like the mountains of God; Your judgments are like the great deep; man and beast You save, O Yahweh.
36:8	How precious is Your steadfast love, O God! The children of mankind take refuge in the shadow of Your wings.
36:9	They feast on the abundance of Your house, and You give them drink from the river of Your delights.
36:10	For with You is the fountain of life; in Your light do we see light.
36:11	Oh, continue Your steadfast love to those who know You, and Your righteousness to the upright of heart!
36:12	Let not the foot of arrogance come upon me, nor the hand of the wicked drive me away.
36:13	There the evildoers lie fallen; they are thrust down, unable to rise.
