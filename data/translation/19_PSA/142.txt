142:1	A Maskil of David, when he was in the cave. A Prayer.
142:2	With my voice I cry out to Yahweh; with my voice I plead for mercy to Yahweh.
142:3	I pour out my complaint before him; I tell my trouble before him.
142:4	When my spirit faints within me, you know my way! In the path where I walk they have hidden a trap for me.
142:5	Look to the right and see: there is none who takes notice of me; no refuge remains to me; no one cares for my soul.
142:6	I cry to you, O Yahweh; I say, "You are my refuge, my portion in the land of the living."
142:7	Attend to my cry, for I am brought very low! Deliver me from my persecutors, for they are too strong for me!
142:8	Bring me out of prison, that I may give thanks to your name! The righteous will surround me, for you will deal bountifully with me.
