67:1	To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments. A Psalm. A Song.
67:2	May God be gracious to us and bless us and make His face to shine upon us, Selah
67:3	that Your way may be known on earth, Your saving power among all nations.
67:4	Let the peoples praise You, O God; let all the peoples praise You!
67:5	Let the nations be glad and sing for joy, for You judge the peoples with equity and guide the nations upon earth. Selah
67:6	Let the peoples praise You, O God; let all the peoples praise You!
67:7	The earth has yielded its increase; God, our God, shall bless us.
67:8	God shall bless us; let all the ends of the earth fear Him!
