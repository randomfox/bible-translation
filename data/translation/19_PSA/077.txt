77:1	To the choirmaster: according to Jeduthun. Of Asaph. A Psalm.
77:2	I cry aloud to God, aloud to God, and he will hear me.
77:3	In the day of my trouble I seek the Lord; in the night my hand is stretched out without wearying; my soul refuses to be comforted.
77:4	When I remember God, I moan; when I meditate, my spirit faints. Selah
77:5	You hold my eyelids open; I am so troubled that I cannot speak.
77:6	I consider the days of old, the years long ago.
77:7	I said, "Let me remember my song in the night; let me meditate in my heart." Then my spirit made a diligent search:
77:8	"Will the Lord spurn forever, and never again be favorable?
77:9	Has his steadfast love forever ceased? Are his promises at an end for all time?
77:10	Has God forgotten to be gracious? Has he in anger shut up his compassion?" Selah
77:11	Then I said, "This is my grief: that the right hand of the Most High has changed."
77:12	I will remember the deeds of Yah; yes, I will remember your wonders of old.
77:13	I will ponder all your work, and meditate on your mighty deeds.
77:14	Your way, O God, is holy. What god is great like our God?
77:15	You are the God who works wonders; you have made known your might among the peoples.
77:16	You with your arm redeemed your people, the children of Jacob and Joseph. Selah
77:17	When the waters saw you, O God, when the waters saw you, they were afraid; indeed, the deep trembled.
77:18	The clouds poured out water; the skies gave forth thunder; your arrows flashed on every side.
77:19	The crash of your thunder was in the whirlwind; your lightnings lighted up the world; the earth trembled and shook.
77:20	Your way was through the sea, your path through the great waters; yet your footprints were unseen.
77:21	You led your people like a flock by the hand of Moses and Aaron.
