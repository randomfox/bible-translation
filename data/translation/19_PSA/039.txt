39:1	To the choirmaster: to Jeduthun. A Psalm of David.
39:2	I said, "I will guard my ways, that I may not sin with my tongue; I will guard my mouth with a muzzle, so long as the wicked are in my presence."
39:3	I was mute and silent; I held my peace to no avail, and my distress grew worse.
39:4	My heart became hot within me. As I mused, the fire burned; then I spoke with my tongue:
39:5	"O Yahweh, make me know my end and what is the measure of my days; let me know how fleeting I am!
39:6	Behold, you have made my days a few handbreadths, and my lifetime is as nothing before you. Surely all mankind stands as a mere breath! Selah
39:7	Surely a man goes about as a shadow! Surely for nothing they are in turmoil; man heaps up wealth and does not know who will gather!
39:8	And now, O Lord, for what do I wait? My hope is in you.
39:9	Deliver me from all my transgressions. Do not make me the scorn of the fool!
39:10	I am mute; I do not open my mouth, for it is you who have done it.
39:11	Remove your stroke from me; I am spent by the hostility of your hand.
39:12	When you discipline a man with rebukes for sin, you consume like a moth what is dear to him; surely all mankind is a mere breath! Selah
39:13	Hear my prayer, O Yahweh, and give ear to my cry; hold not your peace at my tears! For I am a sojourner with you, a guest, like all my fathers.
39:14	Look away from me, that I may smile again, before I depart and am no more!"
