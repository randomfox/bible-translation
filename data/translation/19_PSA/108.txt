108:1	A Song. A Psalm of David.
108:2	My heart is steadfast, O God! I will sing and make melody with all my being!
108:3	Awake, O harp and lyre! I will awake the dawn!
108:4	I will give thanks to you, O Yahweh, among the peoples; I will sing praises to you among the nations.
108:5	For your steadfast love is great above the heavens; your faithfulness reaches to the clouds.
108:6	Be exalted, O God, above the heavens! Let your glory be over all the earth!
108:7	That your beloved ones may be delivered, give salvation by your right hand and answer me!
108:8	God has promised in his holiness: "With exultation I will divide up Shechem and portion out the Valley of Succoth.
108:9	Gilead is mine; Manasseh is mine; Ephraim is my helmet, Judah my scepter.
108:10	Moab is my washbasin; upon Edom I cast my shoe; over Philistia I shout in triumph."
108:11	Who will bring me to the fortified city? Who will lead me to Edom?
108:12	Have you not rejected us, O God? You do not go out, O God, with our armies.
108:13	Oh, grant us help against the foe, for vain is the salvation of man!
108:14	With God we shall do valiantly; it is he who will tread down our foes.
