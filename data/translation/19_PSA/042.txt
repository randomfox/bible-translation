42:1	To the choirmaster. A Maskil of the Sons of Korah.
42:2	As a deer pants for flowing streams, so pants my soul for you, O God.
42:3	My soul thirsts for God, for the living God. When shall I come and appear before God?
42:4	My tears have been my food day and night, while they say to me all the day long, "Where is your God?"
42:5	These things I remember, as I pour out my soul: how I would go with the throng and lead them in procession to the house of God with glad shouts and songs of praise, a multitude keeping festival.
42:6	Why are you cast down, O my soul, and why are you in turmoil within me? Hope in God; for I shall again praise him for the salvation of his presence.
42:7	O my God, my soul is cast down within me; therefore I remember you from the land of Jordan and of Hermon, from Mount Mizar.
42:8	Deep calls to deep at the roar of your waterfalls; all your breakers and your waves have gone over me.
42:9	By day Yahweh commands his steadfast love, and at night his song is with me, a prayer to the God of my life.
42:10	I say to God, my rock: "Why have you forgotten me? Why do I go mourning because of the oppression of the enemy?"
42:11	As with a deadly wound in my bones, my adversaries taunt me, while they say to me all the day long, "Where is your God?"
42:12	Why are you cast down, O my soul, and why are you in turmoil within me? Hope in God; for I shall again praise him, my salvation and my God.
