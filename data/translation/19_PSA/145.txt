145:1	A Song of Praise. Of David. I will exalt you, my God the King, and bless your name forever and ever.
145:2	Every day I will bless you and praise your name forever and ever.
145:3	Great is Yahweh and greatly to be praised, and his greatness is unsearchable.
145:4	One generation shall commend your works to another and shall declare your mighty acts.
145:5	On the glorious splendor of your majesty, and on the record of your wondrous works, I will meditate.
145:6	They shall speak of the might of your awesome deeds, and I will recount your greatness.
145:7	They shall pour forth the fame of your abundant goodness and shall sing aloud of your righteousness.
145:8	Yahweh is gracious and merciful, slow to anger and great in steadfast love.
145:9	Yahweh is good to all, and his mercy is over all that he has made.
145:10	All your works shall give thanks to you, O Yahweh, and your faithful ones shall bless you!
145:11	They shall speak of the glory of your kingdom and tell of your power,
145:12	to make known to the children of man your mighty deeds, and the glorious splendor of your kingdom.
145:13	Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and your dominion endures throughout all generations.
145:14	Yahweh upholds all who are falling and raises up all who are bowed down.
145:15	The eyes of all look to you, and you give them their food in due season.
145:16	You open your hand; you satisfy the desire of every living thing.
145:17	Yahweh is righteous in all his ways and faithful in all his works.
145:18	Yahweh is near to all who call on him, to all who call on him in truth.
145:19	He fulfills the desire of those who fear him; he hears their cry and saves them.
145:20	Yahweh preserves all who love him, but all the wicked he will destroy.
145:21	My mouth will speak the praise of Yahweh, and let all flesh bless his holy name forever and ever.
