12:1	To the choirmaster: according to The Sheminith. A Psalm of David.
12:2	Save, O Yahweh, for the godly one is gone; for the faithful have vanished from among the children of man.
12:3	Everyone utters lies to his neighbor; with flattering lips and a double heart they speak.
12:4	May Yahweh cut off all flattering lips, the tongue that makes great boasts,
12:5	those who say, "With our tongue we will prevail, our lips are with us; who is master over us?"
12:6	"Because the poor are plundered, because the needy groan, I will now arise," says Yahweh; "I will place him in the safety for which he longs."
12:7	The words of Yahweh are pure words, like silver refined in a furnace on the ground, purified seven times.
12:8	You, O Yahweh, will keep them; You will guard us from this generation forever.
12:9	On every side the wicked prowl, as vileness is exalted among the children of man.
