92:1	A Psalm. A Song for the Sabbath.
92:2	It is good to give thanks to Yahweh, to sing praises to Your name, O Most High;
92:3	to declare Your steadfast love in the morning, and Your faithfulness by night,
92:4	to the music of the ten-stringed instrument and the harp, to the melody of the lyre.
92:5	For You, O Yahweh, have made me glad by Your work; at the works of Your hands I sing for joy.
92:6	How great are Your works, O Yahweh! Your thoughts are very deep!
92:7	The stupid man cannot know; the fool cannot understand this:
92:8	that though the wicked sprout like grass and all evildoers flourish, they are doomed to destruction forever;
92:9	but You, O Yahweh, are on high forever.
92:10	For behold, Your enemies, O Yahweh, for behold, Your enemies shall perish; all evildoers shall be scattered.
92:11	But You have exalted my horn like that of the wild ox; You have poured over me fresh oil.
92:12	My eyes have seen the downfall of my enemies; my ears have heard the doom of my evil assailants.
92:13	The righteous flourish like the palm tree and grow like a cedar in Lebanon.
92:14	They are planted in the house of Yahweh; they flourish in the courts of our God.
92:15	They still bear fruit in old age; they are ever full of sap and green,
92:16	to declare that Yahweh is upright; He is my rock, and there is no unrighteousness in Him.
