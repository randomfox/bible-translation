113:1	Praise Yah! Praise, O servants of Yahweh, praise the name of Yahweh!
113:2	Blessed be the name of Yahweh from this time forth and forevermore!
113:3	From the rising of the sun to its setting, the name of Yahweh is to be praised!
113:4	Yahweh is high above all nations, and his glory above the heavens!
113:5	Who is like Yahweh our God, who is seated on high,
113:6	who looks far down on the heavens and the earth?
113:7	He raises the poor from the dust and lifts the needy from the ash heap,
113:8	to make them sit with princes, with the princes of his people.
113:9	He gives the barren woman a home, making her the joyous mother of children. Praise Yah!
