59:1	To the choirmaster: according to Do Not Destroy. A Miktam of David, when Saul sent men to watch his house in order to kill him.
59:2	Deliver me from my enemies, O my God; protect me from those who rise up against me;
59:3	deliver me from those who work evil, and save me from bloodthirsty men.
59:4	For behold, they lie in wait for my life; fierce men stir up strife against me. For no transgression or sin of mine, O Yahweh,
59:5	for no fault of mine, they run and make ready. Awake, come to meet me, and see!
59:6	You, Yahweh God of Armies, the God of Israel, rouse Yourself to punish all the nations; spare none of those who treacherously plot evil. Selah
59:7	Each evening they come back, howling like dogs and prowling about the city.
59:8	There they are, bellowing with their mouths with swords in their lips—for "Who," they think, "will hear us?"
59:9	But You, O Yahweh, laugh at them; You hold all the nations in derision.
59:10	O my Strength, I will watch for You, for You, O God, are my fortress.
59:11	My God in His steadfast love will meet me; God will let me look in triumph on my enemies.
59:12	Kill them not, lest my people forget; make them totter by Your power and bring them down, O Lord, our shield!
59:13	For the sin of their mouths, the words of their lips, let them be trapped in their pride. For the cursing and lies that they utter,
59:14	consume them in wrath; consume them till they are no more, that they may know that God rules over Jacob to the ends of the earth. Selah
59:15	Each evening they come back, howling like dogs and prowling about the city.
59:16	They wander about for food and growl if they do not get their fill.
59:17	But I will sing of Your strength; I will sing aloud of Your steadfast love in the morning. For You have been to me a fortress and a refuge in the day of my distress.
59:18	O my Strength, I will sing praises to You, for You, O God, are my fortress, the God Who shows me steadfast love.
