7:1	A Shiggaion of David, which he sang to Yahweh concerning the words of Cush, a Benjaminite.
7:2	O Yahweh my God, in You I take refuge; save me from all my pursuers and deliver me,
7:3	lest like a lion they tear my soul apart, rending it in pieces, with none to deliver.
7:4	O Yahweh my God, if I have done this, if there is wrong in my hands,
7:5	if I have repaid my friend with evil or plundered my enemy without cause,
7:6	let the enemy pursue my soul and overtake it, and let him trample my life to the ground and lay my glory in the dust. Selah
7:7	Arise, O Yahweh, in Your anger; lift Yourself up against the fury of my enemies; awake for me; You have appointed a judgment.
7:8	Let the assembly of the peoples be gathered about You; over it return on high.
7:9	Yahweh judges the peoples; judge me, O Yahweh, according to my righteousness and according to the integrity that is in me.
7:10	Oh, let the evil of the wicked come to an end, and may You establish the righteous—You Who test the minds and hearts, O righteous God!
7:11	My shield is with God, Who saves the upright in heart.
7:12	God is a righteous judge, and a God Who feels indignation every day.
7:13	If a man does not repent, God will whet His sword; He has bent and readied His bow;
7:14	He has prepared for him His deadly weapons, making His arrows fiery shafts.
7:15	Behold, the wicked man conceives evil and is pregnant with mischief and gives birth to lies.
7:16	He makes a pit, digging it out, and falls into the hole that he has made.
7:17	His mischief returns upon his own head, and on his own skull his violence descends.
7:18	I will give to Yahweh the thanks due to His righteousness, and I will sing praise to the name of Yahweh, the Most High.
