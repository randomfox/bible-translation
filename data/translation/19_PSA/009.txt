9:1	To the choirmaster: according to Muth-labben. A Psalm of David.
9:2	I will give thanks to Yahweh with my whole heart; I will recount all of Your wonderful deeds.
9:3	I will be glad and exult in You; I will sing praise to Your name, O Most High.
9:4	When my enemies turn back, they stumble and perish before Your presence.
9:5	For You have maintained my just cause; You have sat on the throne, giving righteous judgment.
9:6	You have rebuked the nations; You have made the wicked perish; You have blotted out their name forever and ever.
9:7	The enemy came to an end in everlasting ruins; their cities You rooted out; the very memory of them has perished.
9:8	But Yahweh sits enthroned forever; He has established His throne for justice,
9:9	and He judges the world with righteousness; He judges the peoples with uprightness.
9:10	Yahweh is a stronghold for the oppressed, a stronghold in times of trouble.
9:11	And those who know Your name put their trust in You, for You, O Yahweh, have not forsaken those who seek You.
9:12	Sing praises to Yahweh, Who dwells in Zion! Tell among the peoples His deeds!
9:13	For He Who avenges blood is mindful of them; He does not forget the cry of the afflicted.
9:14	Be gracious to me, O Yahweh! See my affliction from those who hate me, O You Who lift me up from the gates of death,
9:15	that I may recount all Your praises, that in the gates of the daughter of Zion I may rejoice in Your salvation.
9:16	The nations have sunk in the pit that they made; in the net that they hid, their own foot has been caught.
9:17	Yahweh has made Himself known; He has executed judgment; the wicked are snared in the work of their own hands. Higgaion. Selah
9:18	The wicked shall return to Sheol, all the nations that forget God.
9:19	For the needy shall not always be forgotten, and the hope of the poor shall not perish forever.
9:20	Arise, O Yahweh! Let not man prevail; let the nations be judged before You!
9:21	Put them in fear, O Yahweh! Let the nations know that they are but men! Selah
