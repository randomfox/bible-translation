30:1	A Psalm of David. A song at the dedication of the temple.
30:2	I will extol you, O Yahweh, for you have drawn me up and have not let my foes rejoice over me.
30:3	O Yahweh my God, I cried to you for help, and you have healed me.
30:4	O Yahweh, you have brought up my soul from Sheol; you restored me to life from among those who go down to the pit.
30:5	Sing praises to Yahweh, O you his faithful ones, and give thanks to his holy name.
30:6	For his anger is but for a moment, and his favor is for a lifetime. Weeping may tarry for the night, but joy comes with the morning.
30:7	As for me, I said in my prosperity, "I shall never be moved."
30:8	By your favor, O Yahweh, you made my mountain stand strong; you hid your face; I was dismayed.
30:9	To you, O Yahweh, I cry, and to the Lord I plead for mercy:
30:10	"What profit is there in my death, if I go down to the pit? Will the dust praise you? Will it tell of your faithfulness?
30:11	Hear, O Yahweh, and be merciful to me! O Yahweh, be my helper!"
30:12	You have turned for me my mourning into dancing; you have loosed my sackcloth and clothed me with gladness,
30:13	that my glory may sing your praise and not be silent. O Yahweh my God, I will give thanks to you forever!
