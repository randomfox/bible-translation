147:1	Praise Yah! For it is good to sing praises to our God; for it is pleasant, and praise is fitting.
147:2	Yahweh builds up Jerusalem; he gathers the outcasts of Israel.
147:3	He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds.
147:4	He determines the number of the stars; he gives to all of them their names.
147:5	Great is our Lord and abundant in power; his understanding is beyond measure.
147:6	Yahweh lifts up the humble; he casts the wicked to the ground.
147:7	Sing to Yahweh with thanksgiving; make music to our God on the lyre!
147:8	He covers the heavens with clouds; he prepares rain for the earth; he makes grass grow on the hills.
147:9	He gives to the beasts their food, and to the young ravens that cry.
147:10	He does not delight in the strength of the horse, nor take pleasure in the legs of a man,
147:11	but Yahweh takes pleasure in those who fear him, in those who hope in his steadfast love.
147:12	Praise Yahweh, O Jerusalem! Praise your God, O Zion!
147:13	For he strengthens the bars of your gates; he blesses your children within you.
147:14	He makes peace in your borders; he fills you with the finest of the wheat.
147:15	He sends out his command to the earth; his word runs swiftly.
147:16	He gives snow like wool; he scatters frost like ashes.
147:17	He hurls down his hail like crumbs; who can stand before his cold?
147:18	He sends out his word and melts them; he makes his wind blow, and the waters flow.
147:19	He declares his words to Jacob, his statutes and rules to Israel.
147:20	He has not dealt thus with any other nation; they do not know his rules. Praise Yah!
