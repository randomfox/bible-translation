130:1	A Song of Ascents. Out of the depths I cry to you, O Yahweh!
130:2	O Lord, hear my voice! Let your ears be attentive to the voice of my pleas for mercy!
130:3	If you, O Yah, should mark iniquities, O Lord, who could stand?
130:4	But with you there is forgiveness, that you may be feared.
130:5	I wait for Yahweh, my soul waits, and in his word I hope;
130:6	my soul waits for the Lord more than watchmen for the morning, more than watchmen for the morning.
130:7	O Israel, hope in Yahweh! For with Yahweh there is steadfast love, and with him is plentiful redemption.
130:8	And he will redeem Israel from all his iniquities.
