89:1	A Maskil of Ethan the Ezrahite.
89:2	I will sing of the steadfast love of Yahweh forever; with my mouth I will make known your faithfulness to all generations.
89:3	For I said, "Steadfast love will be built up forever; in the heavens you will establish your faithfulness."
89:4	You have said, "I have made a covenant with my chosen one; I have sworn to David my servant:
89:5	'I will establish your offspring forever, and build your throne for all generations.'" Selah
89:6	Let the heavens praise your wonders, O Yahweh, your faithfulness in the assembly of the holy ones!
89:7	For who in the skies can be compared to Yahweh? Who among the heavenly beings is like Yahweh,
89:8	a God greatly to be feared in the council of the holy ones, and awesome above all who are around him?
89:9	O Yahweh God of Armies, who is mighty as you are, O Yah, with your faithfulness all around you?
89:10	You rule the raging of the sea; when its waves rise, you still them.
89:11	You crushed Rahab like a carcass; you scattered your enemies with your mighty arm.
89:12	The heavens are yours; the earth also is yours; the world and all that is in it, you have founded them.
89:13	The north and the south, you have created them; Tabor and Hermon joyously praise your name.
89:14	You have a mighty arm; strong is your hand, high your right hand.
89:15	Righteousness and justice are the foundation of your throne; steadfast love and faithfulness go before you.
89:16	Blessed are the people who know the festal shout, who walk, O Yahweh, in the light of your face,
89:17	who exult in your name all the day and in your righteousness are exalted.
89:18	For you are the glory of their strength; by your favor our horn is exalted.
89:19	For our shield belongs to Yahweh, our king to the Holy One of Israel.
89:20	Of old you spoke in a vision to your faithful one, and said: "I have granted help to one who is mighty; I have exalted one chosen from the people.
89:21	I have found David, my servant; with my holy oil I have anointed him,
89:22	so that my hand shall be established with him; my arm also shall strengthen him.
89:23	The enemy shall not outwit him; the wicked shall not humble him.
89:24	I will crush his foes before him and strike down those who hate him.
89:25	My faithfulness and my steadfast love shall be with him, and in my name shall his horn be exalted.
89:26	I will set his hand on the sea and his right hand on the rivers.
89:27	He shall cry to me, 'You are my Father, my God, and the Rock of my salvation.'
89:28	And I will make him the firstborn, the highest of the kings of the earth.
89:29	My steadfast love I will keep for him forever, and my covenant will stand firm for him.
89:30	I will establish his offspring forever and his throne as the days of the heavens.
89:31	If his children forsake my law and do not walk according to my rules,
89:32	if they violate my statutes and do not keep my commandments,
89:33	then I will punish their transgression with the rod and their iniquity with stripes,
89:34	but I will not remove from him my steadfast love or be false to my faithfulness.
89:35	I will not violate my covenant or alter the word that went forth from my lips.
89:36	Once for all I have sworn by my holiness; I will not lie to David.
89:37	His offspring shall endure forever, his throne as long as the sun before me.
89:38	Like the moon it shall be established forever, a faithful witness in the skies." Selah
89:39	But now you have cast off and rejected; you are full of wrath against your anointed.
89:40	You have renounced the covenant with your servant; you have defiled his crown in the dust.
89:41	You have breached all his walls; you have laid his strongholds in ruins.
89:42	All who pass by plunder him; he has become the scorn of his neighbors.
89:43	You have exalted the right hand of his foes; you have made all his enemies rejoice.
89:44	You have also turned back the edge of his sword, and you have not made him stand in battle.
89:45	You have made his splendor to cease and cast his throne to the ground.
89:46	You have cut short the days of his youth; you have covered him with shame. Selah
89:47	How long, O Yahweh? Will you hide yourself forever? How long will your wrath burn like fire?
89:48	Remember how short my time is! For what vanity you have created all the children of man!
89:49	What man can live and never see death? Who can deliver his soul from the power of Sheol? Selah
89:50	Lord, where is your steadfast love of old, which by your faithfulness you swore to David?
89:51	Remember, O Lord, how your servants are mocked, and how I bear in my heart the insults of all the many nations,
89:52	with which your enemies mock, O Yahweh, with which they mock the footsteps of your anointed.
89:53	Blessed be Yahweh forever! Amen and Amen.
