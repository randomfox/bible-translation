51:1	To the choirmaster. A Psalm of David,
51:2	when Nathan the prophet went to him, after he had gone in to Bathsheba.
51:3	Have mercy on me, O God, according to your steadfast love; according to your abundant mercy blot out my transgressions.
51:4	Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin!
51:5	For I know my transgressions, and my sin is ever before me.
51:6	Against you, you only, have I sinned and done what is evil in your sight, so that you may be justified in your words and blameless in your judgment.
51:7	Behold, I was brought forth in iniquity, and in sin did my mother conceive me.
51:8	Behold, you delight in truth in the inward being, and you teach me wisdom in the secret heart.
51:9	Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean; wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow.
51:10	Let me hear joy and gladness; let the bones that you have broken rejoice.
51:11	Hide your face from my sins, and blot out all my iniquities.
51:12	Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me.
51:13	Cast me not away from your presence, and take not your Holy Spirit from me.
51:14	Restore to me the joy of your salvation, and uphold me with a willing spirit.
51:15	Then I will teach transgressors your ways, and sinners will return to you.
51:16	Deliver me from bloodguiltiness, O God, O God of my salvation, and my tongue will sing aloud of your righteousness.
51:17	O Lord, open my lips, and my mouth will declare your praise.
51:18	For you will not delight in sacrifice, or I would give it; you will not be pleased with a burnt offering.
51:19	The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit; a broken and contrite heart, O God, you will not despise.
51:20	Do good to Zion in your good pleasure; build up the walls of Jerusalem;
51:21	then will you delight in right sacrifices, in burnt offerings and whole burnt offerings; then bulls will be offered on your altar.
