8:1	To the choirmaster: according to The Gittith. A Psalm of David.
8:2	O Yahweh, our Lord, how majestic is your name in all the earth! You have set your glory above the heavens.
8:3	Out of the mouth of babies and infants you have established strength because of your foes, to still the enemy and the avenger.
8:4	When I look at your heavens, the work of your fingers, the moon and the stars, which you have set in place,
8:5	what is man that you are mindful of him, and the son of man that you care for him?
8:6	Yet you have made him a little lower than God and crowned him with glory and honor.
8:7	You have given him dominion over the works of your hands; you have put all things under his feet,
8:8	all sheep and oxen, and also the beasts of the field,
8:9	the birds of the heavens, and the fish of the sea, whatever passes along the paths of the seas.
8:10	O Yahweh, our Lord, how majestic is your name in all the earth!
