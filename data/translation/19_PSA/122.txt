122:1	A Song of Ascents. Of David. I was glad when they said to me, "Let us go to the house of Yahweh!"
122:2	Our feet have been standing within your gates, O Jerusalem!
122:3	Jerusalem—built as a city that is bound firmly together,
122:4	to which the tribes go up, the tribes of Yah, as was decreed for Israel, to give thanks to the name of Yahweh.
122:5	There thrones for judgment were set, the thrones of the house of David.
122:6	Pray for the peace of Jerusalem! "May they be secure who love you!
122:7	Peace be within your walls and security within your towers!"
122:8	For my brothers and companions' sake I will say, "Peace be within you!"
122:9	For the sake of the house of Yahweh our God, I will seek your good.
