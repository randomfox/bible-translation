62:1	To the choirmaster: according to Jeduthun. A Psalm of David.
62:2	For God alone my soul waits in silence; from Him comes my salvation.
62:3	He alone is my rock and my salvation, my fortress; I shall not be greatly shaken.
62:4	How long will all of you attack a man to batter him, like a leaning wall, a tottering fence?
62:5	They only plan to thrust him down from his high position. They take pleasure in falsehood. They bless with their mouths, but inwardly they curse. Selah
62:6	For God alone, O my soul, wait in silence, for my hope is from Him.
62:7	He alone is my rock and my salvation, my fortress; I shall not be shaken.
62:8	On God rests my salvation and my glory; my mighty rock, my refuge is God.
62:9	Trust in Him at all times, O people; pour out your heart before Him; God is a refuge for us. Selah
62:10	Those of low estate are but a breath; those of high estate are a delusion; in the balances they go up; they are together lighter than a breath.
62:11	Put no trust in extortion; set no vain hopes on robbery; if riches increase, set not your heart on them.
62:12	Once God has spoken; twice have I heard this: that power belongs to God,
62:13	and that to You, O Lord, belongs steadfast love. For You will render to a man according to his work.
