60:1	To the choirmaster: according to Shushan Eduth. A Miktam of David; for instruction;
60:2	when he strove with Aram-naharaim and with Aram-zobah, and when Joab on his return struck down twelve thousand of Edom in the Valley of Salt.
60:3	O God, you have rejected us, broken our defenses; you have been angry; oh, restore us.
60:4	You have made the land to quake; you have torn it open; repair its breaches, for it totters.
60:5	You have made your people see hard things; you have given us wine to drink that made us stagger.
60:6	You have set up a banner for those who fear you, that they may flee to it from the bow. Selah
60:7	That your beloved ones may be delivered, give salvation by your right hand and answer me!
60:8	God has spoken in his holiness: "With exultation I will divide up Shechem and portion out the Valley of Succoth.
60:9	Gilead is mine; Manasseh is mine; Ephraim is my helmet; Judah is my scepter.
60:10	Moab is my washbasin; upon Edom I cast my shoe; over Philistia I shout in triumph."
60:11	Who will bring me to the fortified city? Who will lead me to Edom?
60:12	Have you not rejected us, O God? You do not go forth, O God, with our armies.
60:13	Oh, grant us help against the foe, for vain is the salvation of man!
60:14	With God we shall do valiantly; it is he who will tread down our foes.
