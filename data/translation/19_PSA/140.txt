140:1	To the choirmaster. A Psalm of David.
140:2	Deliver me, O Yahweh, from evil men; preserve me from violent men,
140:3	who plan evil things in their heart and stir up wars continually.
140:4	They make their tongue sharp as a serpent's, and under their lips is the venom of asps. Selah
140:5	Guard me, O Yahweh, from the hands of the wicked; preserve me from violent men, who have planned to trip up my feet.
140:6	The arrogant have hidden a trap for me, and with cords they have spread a net; beside the way they have set snares for me. Selah
140:7	I say to Yahweh, You are my God; give ear to the voice of my pleas for mercy, O Yahweh!
140:8	O Lord Yahweh, my strong deliverer, you have covered my head in the day of battle.
140:9	Grant not, O Yahweh, the desires of the wicked; do not further their evil plot, or they will be exalted! Selah
140:10	As for the head of those who surround me, let the mischief of their lips overwhelm them!
140:11	Let burning coals fall upon them! Let them be cast into fire, into miry pits, no more to rise!
140:12	Let not the slanderer be established in the land; let evil hunt down the violent man speedily!
140:13	I know that Yahweh will maintain the cause of the afflicted, and will execute justice for the needy.
140:14	Surely the righteous shall give thanks to your name; the upright shall dwell in your presence.
