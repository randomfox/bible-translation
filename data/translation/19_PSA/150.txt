150:1	Praise Yah! Praise God in His sanctuary; praise Him in His mighty firmament!
150:2	Praise Him for His mighty deeds; praise Him according to His abundant greatness!
150:3	Praise Him with the blast of the shofar; praise Him with harp and lyre!
150:4	Praise Him with tambourine and dance; praise Him with strings and pipe!
150:5	Praise Him with sounding cymbals; praise Him with clashing cymbals!
150:6	Let everything that has breath praise Yah! Praise Yah!
