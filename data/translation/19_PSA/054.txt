54:1	To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments. A Maskil of David,
54:2	when the Ziphites went and told Saul, "Is not David hiding among us?"
54:3	O God, save me by your name, and vindicate me by your might.
54:4	O God, hear my prayer; give ear to the words of my mouth.
54:5	For strangers have risen against me; ruthless men seek my life; they do not set God before themselves. Selah
54:6	Behold, God is my helper; the Lord is the upholder of my life.
54:7	He will return the evil to my enemies; in your faithfulness put an end to them.
54:8	With a freewill offering I will sacrifice to you; I will give thanks to your name, O Yahweh, for it is good.
54:9	For he has delivered me from every trouble, and my eye has looked in triumph on my enemies.
