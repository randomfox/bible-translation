85:1	To the choirmaster. A Psalm of the Sons of Korah.
85:2	Yahweh, You were favorable to Your land; You restored the fortunes of Jacob.
85:3	You forgave the iniquity of Your people; You covered all their sin. Selah
85:4	You withdrew all Your wrath; You turned from Your hot anger.
85:5	Restore us again, O God of our salvation, and put away Your indignation toward us!
85:6	Will You be angry with us forever? Will You prolong Your anger to all generations?
85:7	Will You not revive us again, that Your people may rejoice in You?
85:8	Show us Your steadfast love, O Yahweh, and grant us Your salvation.
85:9	Let me hear what God Yahweh will speak, for He will speak peace to His people, to His faithful ones; but let them not turn back to folly.
85:10	Surely His salvation is near to those who fear Him, that glory may dwell in our land.
85:11	Steadfast love and faithfulness meet; righteousness and peace kiss each other.
85:12	Faithfulness springs up from the ground, and righteousness looks down from the sky.
85:13	Yes, Yahweh will give what is good, and our land will yield its increase.
85:14	Righteousness will go before Him and make His footsteps a way.
