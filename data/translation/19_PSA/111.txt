111:1	Praise Yah! I will give thanks to Yahweh with my whole heart, in the company of the upright, in the congregation.
111:2	Great are the works of Yahweh, studied by all who delight in them.
111:3	Full of splendor and majesty is his work, and his righteousness endures forever.
111:4	He has caused his wondrous works to be remembered; Yahweh is gracious and merciful.
111:5	He provides food for those who fear him; he remembers his covenant forever.
111:6	He has shown his people the power of his works, in giving them the inheritance of the nations.
111:7	The works of his hands are faithful and just; all his precepts are trustworthy;
111:8	they are established forever and ever, to be performed with faithfulness and uprightness.
111:9	He sent redemption to his people; he has commanded his covenant forever. Holy and awesome is his name!
111:10	The fear of Yahweh is the beginning of wisdom; all those who practice it have a good understanding. His praise endures forever!
