47:1	To the choirmaster. A Psalm of the Sons of Korah.
47:2	Clap your hands, all peoples! Shout to God with loud songs of joy!
47:3	For Yahweh, the Most High, is to be feared, a great king over all the earth.
47:4	He subdued peoples under us, and nations under our feet.
47:5	He chose our heritage for us, the pride of Jacob whom he loves. Selah
47:6	God has gone up with a shout, Yahweh with the sound of a trumpet.
47:7	Sing praises to God, sing praises! Sing praises to our King, sing praises!
47:8	For God is the King of all the earth; sing praises with a skillful psalm!
47:9	God reigns over the nations; God sits on his holy throne.
47:10	The princes of the peoples gather as the people of the God of Abraham. For the shields of the earth belong to God; he is highly exalted!
