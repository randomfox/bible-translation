46:1	To the choirmaster. Of the Sons of Korah. According to Alamoth. A Song.
46:2	God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.
46:3	Therefore we will not fear though the earth gives way, though the mountains be moved into the heart of the sea,
46:4	though its waters roar and foam, though the mountains tremble at its swelling. Selah
46:5	There is a river whose streams make glad the city of God, the holy habitation of the Most High.
46:6	God is in the midst of her; she shall not be moved; God will help her when morning dawns.
46:7	The nations rage, the kingdoms totter; he utters his voice, the earth melts.
46:8	Yahweh of Armies is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress. Selah
46:9	Come, behold the works of Yahweh, how he has brought desolations on the earth.
46:10	He makes wars cease to the end of the earth; he breaks the bow and shatters the spear; he burns the chariots with fire.
46:11	"Be still, and know that I am God. I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted in the earth!"
46:12	Yahweh of Armies is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress. Selah
