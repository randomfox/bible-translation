102:1	A Prayer of one afflicted, when he is faint and pours out his complaint before Yahweh.
102:2	Hear my prayer, O Yahweh; let my cry come to You!
102:3	Do not hide Your face from me in the day of my distress! Incline Your ear to me; answer me speedily in the day when I call!
102:4	For my days pass away like smoke, and my bones burn like a furnace.
102:5	My heart is struck down like grass and has withered; I forget to eat my bread.
102:6	Because of my loud groaning my bones cling to my flesh.
102:7	I am like a desert owl of the wilderness, like an owl of the waste places;
102:8	I lie awake; I am like a lonely sparrow on the housetop.
102:9	All the day my enemies taunt me; those who deride me use my name for a curse.
102:10	For I eat ashes like bread and mingle tears with my drink,
102:11	because of Your indignation and anger; for You have taken me up and thrown me down.
102:12	My days are like a lengthening shadow; I wither away like grass.
102:13	But You, O Yahweh, are enthroned forever; You are remembered throughout all generations.
102:14	You will arise and have pity on Zion; it is the time to favor her; the appointed time has come.
102:15	For Your servants hold her stones dear and have pity on her dust.
102:16	Nations will fear the name of Yahweh, and all the kings of the earth will fear Your glory.
102:17	For Yahweh builds up Zion; He appears in His glory;
102:18	He regards the prayer of the destitute and does not despise their prayer.
102:19	Let this be recorded for a generation to come, so that a people yet to be created may praise Yah:
102:20	that He looked down from His holy height; from heaven Yahweh looked at the earth,
102:21	to hear the groans of the prisoners, to set free those who were doomed to die,
102:22	that they may declare in Zion the name of Yahweh, and in Jerusalem His praise,
102:23	when peoples gather together, and kingdoms, to worship Yahweh.
102:24	He has broken my strength in midcourse; He has shortened my days.
102:25	"O my God," I say, "take me not away in the midst of my days—You Whose years endure throughout all generations!"
102:26	Of old You laid the foundation of the earth, and the heavens are the work of Your hands.
102:27	They will perish, but You will remain; they will all wear out like a garment. You will change them like a robe, and they will pass away,
102:28	but You are the same, and Your years have no end.
102:29	The children of Your servants shall dwell secure; their offspring shall be established before You.
