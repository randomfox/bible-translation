63:1	A Psalm of David, when he was in the wilderness of Judah.
63:2	O God, you are my God; earnestly I seek you; my soul thirsts for you; my flesh faints for you, as in a dry and weary land where there is no water.
63:3	So I have looked upon you in the sanctuary, beholding your power and glory.
63:4	Because your steadfast love is better than life, my lips will praise you.
63:5	So I will bless you as long as I live; in your name I will lift up my hands.
63:6	My soul will be satisfied as with fat and rich food, and my mouth will praise you with joyful lips,
63:7	when I remember you upon my bed, and meditate on you in the watches of the night;
63:8	for you have been my help, and in the shadow of your wings I will sing for joy.
63:9	My soul clings to you; your right hand upholds me.
63:10	But those who seek to destroy my life shall go down into the depths of the earth;
63:11	they shall be given over to the power of the sword; they shall be a portion for jackals.
63:12	But the king shall rejoice in God; all who swear by him shall exult, for the mouths of liars will be stopped.
