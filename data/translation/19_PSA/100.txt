100:1	A Psalm for giving thanks. Make a joyful noise to Yahweh, all the earth!
100:2	Serve Yahweh with gladness! Come into his presence with singing!
100:3	Know that Yahweh, he is God! It is he who made us, and we are his; we are his people, and the sheep of his pasture.
100:4	Enter his gates with thanksgiving, and his courts with praise! Give thanks to him; bless his name!
100:5	For Yahweh is good; his steadfast love endures forever, and his faithfulness to all generations.
