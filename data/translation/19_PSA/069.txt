69:1	To the choirmaster: according to Lilies. Of David.
69:2	Save me, O God! For the waters have come up to my neck.
69:3	I sink in deep mire, where there is no foothold; I have come into deep waters, and the flood sweeps over me.
69:4	I am weary with my crying out; my throat is parched. My eyes grow dim with waiting for my God.
69:5	More in number than the hairs of my head are those who hate me without cause; mighty are those who would destroy me, those who attack me with lies. What I did not steal must I now restore?
69:6	O God, You know my folly; the wrongs I have done are not hidden from You.
69:7	Let not those who hope in You be put to shame through me, O Lord Yahweh of Armies; let not those who seek You be brought to dishonor through me, O God of Israel.
69:8	For it is for Your sake that I have borne reproach, that dishonor has covered my face.
69:9	I have become a stranger to my brothers, an alien to my mother's sons.
69:10	For zeal for Your house has consumed me, and the reproaches of those who reproach You have fallen on me.
69:11	When I wept and humbled my soul with fasting, it became my reproach.
69:12	When I made sackcloth my clothing, I became a byword to them.
69:13	I am the talk of those who sit in the gate, and the drunkards make songs about me.
69:14	But as for me, my prayer is to You, O Yahweh. At an acceptable time, O God, in the abundance of Your steadfast love answer me in Your saving faithfulness.
69:15	Deliver me from sinking in the mire; let me be delivered from my enemies and from the deep waters.
69:16	Let not the flood sweep over me, or the deep swallow me up, or the pit close its mouth over me.
69:17	Answer me, O Yahweh, for Your steadfast love is good; according to Your abundant mercy, turn to me.
69:18	Hide not Your face from Your servant, for I am in distress; make haste to answer me.
69:19	Draw near to my soul, redeem me; ransom me because of my enemies!
69:20	You know my reproach, and my shame and my dishonor; my foes are all known to You.
69:21	Reproaches have broken my heart, so that I am in despair. I looked for pity, but there was none, and for comforters, but I found none.
69:22	They gave me poison for food, and for my thirst they gave me sour wine to drink.
69:23	Let their own table before them become a snare; and when they are at peace, let it become a trap.
69:24	Let their eyes be darkened, so that they cannot see, and make their loins tremble continually.
69:25	Pour out Your indignation upon them, and let Your burning anger overtake them.
69:26	May their camp be a desolation; let no one dwell in their tents.
69:27	For they persecute him whom You have struck down, and they recount the pain of those You have wounded.
69:28	Add to them punishment upon punishment; may they have no acquittal from You.
69:29	Let them be blotted out of the book of the living; let them not be enrolled among the righteous.
69:30	But I am afflicted and in pain; let Your salvation, O God, set me on high!
69:31	I will praise the name of God with a song; I will magnify Him with thanksgiving.
69:32	This will please Yahweh more than an ox or a bull with horns and hoofs.
69:33	When the humble see it they will be glad; you who seek God, let your hearts revive.
69:34	For Yahweh hears the needy and does not despise His own people who are prisoners.
69:35	Let heaven and earth praise Him, the seas and everything that moves in them.
69:36	For God will save Zion and build up the cities of Judah, and people shall dwell there and possess it;
69:37	the offspring of His servants shall inherit it, and those who love His name shall dwell in it.
