21:1	To the choirmaster. A Psalm of David.
21:2	O Yahweh, in Your strength the king rejoices, and in Your salvation how greatly he exults!
21:3	You have given him his heart's desire and have not withheld the request of his lips. Selah
21:4	For You meet him with rich blessings; You set a crown of fine gold upon his head.
21:5	He asked life of You; You gave it to him, length of days forever and ever.
21:6	His glory is great through Your salvation; splendor and majesty You bestow on him.
21:7	For You make him most blessed forever; You make him glad with the joy of Your presence.
21:8	For the king trusts in Yahweh, and through the steadfast love of the Most High he shall not be moved.
21:9	Your hand will find out all your enemies; your right hand will find out those who hate you.
21:10	You will make them as a blazing oven when you appear. Yahweh will swallow them up in His wrath, and fire will consume them.
21:11	You will destroy their descendants from the earth, and their offspring from among the children of man.
21:12	Though they plan evil against you, though they devise mischief, they will not succeed.
21:13	For you will put them to flight; you will aim at their faces with your bows.
21:14	Be exalted, O Yahweh, in Your strength! We will sing and praise Your power.
