3:1	A Psalm of David, when he fled from Absalom his son.
3:2	O Yahweh, how many are my foes! Many are rising against me;
3:3	many are saying of my soul, "There is no salvation for him in God." Selah
3:4	But You, O Yahweh, are a shield about me, my glory, and the lifter of my head.
3:5	I cried aloud to Yahweh, and He answered me from His holy hill. Selah
3:6	I lay down and slept; I woke again, for Yahweh sustained me.
3:7	I will not be afraid of many thousands of people who have set themselves against me all around.
3:8	Arise, O Yahweh! Save me, O my God! For You strike all my enemies on the cheek; You break the teeth of the wicked.
3:9	Salvation belongs to Yahweh; Your blessing be on Your people! Selah
