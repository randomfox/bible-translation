58:1	To the choirmaster: according to Do Not Destroy. A Miktam of David.
58:2	Do you indeed decree what is right, you gods? Do you judge the children of man uprightly?
58:3	No, in your hearts you devise wrongs; your hands deal out violence on earth.
58:4	The wicked are estranged from the womb; they go astray from birth, speaking lies.
58:5	They have venom like the venom of a serpent, like the deaf adder that stops its ear,
58:6	so that it does not hear the voice of charmers or of the cunning enchanter.
58:7	O God, break the teeth in their mouths; tear out the fangs of the young lions, O Yahweh!
58:8	Let them vanish like water that runs away; when he aims his arrows, let them be blunted.
58:9	Let them be like the snail that dissolves into slime, like the stillborn child who never sees the sun.
58:10	Sooner than your pots can feel the heat of thorns, whether green or ablaze, may he sweep them away!
58:11	The righteous will rejoice when he sees the vengeance; he will bathe his feet in the blood of the wicked.
58:12	Mankind will say, "Surely there is a reward for the righteous; surely there is a God who judges on earth."
