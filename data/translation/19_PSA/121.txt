121:1	A Song of Ascents. I lift up my eyes to the hills. From where does my help come?
121:2	My help comes from Yahweh, who made heaven and earth.
121:3	He will not let your foot be moved; he who keeps you will not slumber.
121:4	Behold, he who keeps Israel will neither slumber nor sleep.
121:5	Yahweh is your keeper; Yahweh is your shade on your right hand.
121:6	The sun shall not strike you by day, nor the moon by night.
121:7	Yahweh will keep you from all evil; he will keep your life.
121:8	Yahweh will keep your going out and your coming in from this time forth and forevermore.
