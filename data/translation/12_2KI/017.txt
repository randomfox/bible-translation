17:1	In the twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah, Hoshea son of Elah became king over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned nine years.
17:2	He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, though not like the kings of Israel who were before him.
17:3	Shalmaneser king of Assyria came up against him, and Hoshea became his vassal and paid him tribute.
17:4	But the king of Assyria found conspiracy in Hoshea, for he had sent messengers to So king of Egypt and had not brought up tribute to the king of Assyria as he had year by year; so the king of Assyria confined him and bound him in prison.
17:5	Then the king of Assyria came up against the whole land; he came up to Samaria and besieged it for three years.
17:6	In the ninth year of Hoshea the king of Assyria captured Samaria and carried Israel into exile to Assyria. He settled them in Halah, and on the Habor, the river of Gozan, and in the cities of the Medes.
17:7	This happened because the people of Israel had sinned against Yahweh their God, who had brought them up out of the land of Egypt from under the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and they had feared other gods.
17:8	They walked in the statutes of the nations whom Yahweh had driven out before the people of Israel, and in those that the kings of Israel had made.
17:9	The people of Israel secretly did things that were not right against Yahweh their God. They built for themselves high places in all their towns, from watchtower to fortified city.
17:10	They set up for themselves standing stones and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every leafy tree.
17:11	There they burned offerings on all the high places, like the nations whom Yahweh had carried away before them, and they did evil things to provoke Yahweh to anger.
17:12	They served idols, of which Yahweh had said to them, "You shall not do this thing."
17:13	Yet Yahweh warned Israel and Judah by every prophet and every seer, saying, "Turn back from your evil ways and keep my commandments and my statutes, according to all the law that I commanded your ancestors and that I sent to you by my servants the prophets."
17:14	But they did not listen; they stiffened their necks like the neck of their ancestors, who did not trust in Yahweh their God.
17:15	They rejected his statutes and his covenant that he had made with their ancestors, and the warnings he had given them. They went after worthless things and became worthless, and they went after the nations around them, concerning whom Yahweh had commanded them not to do as they did.
17:16	They abandoned all the commandments of Yahweh their God and made for themselves cast images, two calves; they made an Asherah, bowed down to all the host of heaven, and served Baal.
17:17	They made their sons and their daughters pass through the fire, practiced divination and sought omens, and sold themselves to do what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, provoking him to anger.
17:18	So Yahweh was very angry with Israel and removed them from his presence; none was left but the tribe of Judah alone.
17:19	Judah too did not keep the commandments of Yahweh their God, but walked in the statutes that Israel had made.
17:20	Yahweh rejected all the descendants of Israel; he afflicted them and gave them into the hand of plunderers until he had cast them out of his presence.
17:21	When he tore Israel away from the house of David, they made Jeroboam son of Nebat king. Jeroboam drove Israel away from following Yahweh and made them commit a great sin.
17:22	The people of Israel walked in all the sins that Jeroboam did; they did not turn away from them,
17:23	until Yahweh removed Israel from his presence, as he had spoken by all his servants the prophets. So Israel was exiled from its own land to Assyria, as it is to this day.
17:24	The king of Assyria brought people from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath, and Sepharvaim and settled them in the cities of Samaria in place of the people of Israel. They took possession of Samaria and lived in its cities.
17:25	At the beginning of their living there they did not fear Yahweh, so Yahweh sent lions among them, and they kept killing some of them.
17:26	So it was said to the king of Assyria, "The nations you have deported and settled in the cities of Samaria do not know the custom of the god of the land, so he has sent lions among them, and they are killing them, because they do not know the custom of the god of the land."
17:27	Then the king of Assyria commanded, "Take back there one of the priests whom you deported from there, and let him go and live there and teach them the custom of the god of the land."
17:28	So one of the priests whom they had deported from Samaria came and lived in Bethel, and he taught them how they should fear Yahweh.
17:29	But each nation kept making its own gods and put them in the shrines of the high places that the Samarians had made, each nation in the cities where they lived.
17:30	The people of Babylon made Succoth-benoth, the people of Cuth made Nergal, the people of Hamath made Ashima,
17:31	and the Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak; and the Sepharvites burned their children in the fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.
17:32	They also feared Yahweh, and they appointed from among themselves, from all ranks of people, priests of the high places, who officiated for them in the shrines of the high places.
17:33	They feared Yahweh, but they served their own gods, according to the custom of the nations from among whom they had been deported.
17:34	To this day they do according to their former customs. They do not fear Yahweh, nor do they act according to their statutes and their ordinances, or according to the law and the commandment that Yahweh commanded the children of Jacob, whom he named Israel.
17:35	Yahweh made a covenant with them and commanded them, "You shall not fear other gods or bow down to them or serve them or sacrifice to them,
17:36	but Yahweh, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt with great power and with an outstretched arm, him you shall fear, to him you shall bow down, and to him you shall sacrifice.
17:37	The statutes and the ordinances and the law and the commandment that he wrote for you, you shall be careful to do always; you shall not fear other gods.
17:38	The covenant that I have made with you, you shall not forget, and you shall not fear other gods.
17:39	But Yahweh your God you shall fear, and he will deliver you from the hand of all your enemies."
17:40	However, they did not listen, but they kept doing according to their former custom.
17:41	So these nations feared Yahweh and also served their carved images; their children and their children's children likewise, as their fathers did, so they do to this day.
