6:1	The sons of the prophets said to Elisha, "Look, the place where we live under your charge is too cramped for us.
6:2	Let us go to the Jordan, and let each of us take a beam from there, and let us make a place there for us to live." He said, "Go."
6:3	Then one of them said, "Please be willing to go with your servants." He said, "I will go."
6:4	So he went with them. When they came to the Jordan, they cut down trees.
6:5	But as one of them was felling a beam, the iron axe head fell into the water. He cried out and said, "Alas, my lord! It was borrowed."
6:6	The man of God said, "Where did it fall?" When he showed him the place, he cut off a stick and threw it in there, and made the iron float.
6:7	He said, "Pick it up." So he reached out his hand and took it.
6:8	Now the king of Aram was at war with Israel. He took counsel with his servants, saying, "At such and such a place will be my camp."
6:9	But the man of God sent word to the king of Israel, saying, "Beware of passing that place, for the Arameans are going down there."
6:10	The king of Israel sent to the place that the man of God told him of; he warned him about it, and he was on guard there, not once and not twice.
6:11	The mind of the king of Aram was greatly troubled over this matter. He called his servants and said to them, "Will you not tell me which of us is for the king of Israel?"
6:12	One of his servants said, "No one, my lord the king. It is Elisha, the prophet who is in Israel, who tells the king of Israel the words that you speak in your bedroom."
6:13	He said, "Go and see where he is, so that I may send and seize him." It was told him, "He is in Dothan."
6:14	So he sent horses and chariots and a strong force there. They came by night and surrounded the city.
6:15	When the attendant of the man of God rose early and went out, there was an army surrounding the city, with horses and chariots. His servant said to him, "Alas, my lord! What shall we do?"
6:16	He said, "Do not be afraid, for those who are with us are more than those who are with them."
6:17	Then Elisha prayed and said, "Yahweh, please open his eyes so that he may see." So Yahweh opened the servant's eyes, and he saw: the mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire all around Elisha.
6:18	When they came down toward him, Elisha prayed to Yahweh and said, "Please strike this nation with blindness." So He struck them with blindness according to the word of Elisha.
6:19	Then Elisha said to them, "This is not the way, and this is not the city. Follow me, and I will bring you to the man you are seeking." And he led them to Samaria.
6:20	As soon as they entered Samaria, Elisha said, "Yahweh, open the eyes of these men so that they may see." Yahweh opened their eyes, and they saw: they were in the middle of Samaria.
6:21	When the king of Israel saw them, he said to Elisha, "Shall I strike them down? Shall I strike them down, my father?"
6:22	He said, "Do not strike them down. Would you strike down those you have taken captive with your sword and your bow? Set bread and water before them, so that they may eat and drink and go to their master."
6:23	So he prepared a great feast for them, and they ate and drank. Then he sent them away, and they went to their master. And the Aramean raiding bands no longer came into the land of Israel.
6:24	Some time after this, Ben-hadad king of Aram mustered his whole army, went up, and besieged Samaria.
6:25	There was a great famine in Samaria; they besieged it until a donkey's head sold for eighty shekels of silver, and a quarter of a kab of dove's dung for five shekels of silver.
6:26	As the king of Israel was passing by on the wall, a woman cried out to him, "Help, my lord the king!"
6:27	He said, "If Yahweh does not help you, where am I to get help for you? From the threshing floor or from the winepress?"
6:28	Then the king said to her, "What is your trouble?" She said, "This woman said to me, 'Give your son, so that we may eat him today, and we will eat my son tomorrow.'
6:29	So we boiled my son and ate him. On the next day I said to her, 'Give your son, so that we may eat him,' but she has hidden her son."
6:30	When the king heard the woman's words, he tore his clothes. As he was passing by on the wall, the people looked, and there was sackcloth on his body underneath.
6:31	He said, "May God do so to me and more also, if the head of Elisha son of Shaphat stays on him today!"
6:32	Elisha was sitting in his house, and the elders were sitting with him. The king sent a man ahead of him, but before the messenger arrived, Elisha said to the elders, "Do you see how this son of a murderer has sent to take off my head? Look, when the messenger comes, shut the door and hold the door fast against him. Is not the sound of his master's feet behind him?"
6:33	While he was still speaking with them, the messenger came down to him and said, "This disaster is from Yahweh! Why should I wait for Yahweh any longer?"
