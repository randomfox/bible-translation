24:1	In his days Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up, and Jehoiakim became his vassal for three years; then he turned and rebelled against him.
24:2	Yahweh sent against him raiding bands of Chaldeans, raiding bands of Aram, raiding bands of Moab, and raiding bands of the Ammonites; he sent them against Judah to destroy it, according to the word of Yahweh that he had spoken by his servants the prophets.
24:3	Surely this came upon Judah at the command of Yahweh, to remove them from his presence for the sins of Manasseh, according to all that he had done,
24:4	and also for the innocent blood that he had shed, for he filled Jerusalem with innocent blood, and Yahweh was not willing to pardon.
24:5	The rest of the deeds of Jehoiakim and all that he did, are they not written in the Book of the Annals of the Kings of Judah?
24:6	Jehoiakim slept with his fathers, and Jehoiachin his son reigned in his place.
24:7	The king of Egypt did not come out of his land again, for the king of Babylon had taken all that belonged to the king of Egypt from the Wadi of Egypt to the river Euphrates.
24:8	Jehoiachin was eighteen years old when he became king, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Nehushta daughter of Elnathan of Jerusalem.
24:9	He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, according to all that his father had done.
24:10	At that time the servants of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up to Jerusalem, and the city came under siege.
24:11	Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to the city while his servants were besieging it,
24:12	and Jehoiachin king of Judah went out to the king of Babylon, he and his mother, his servants, his officials, and his eunuchs. The king of Babylon took him in the eighth year of his reign.
24:13	He carried out from there all the treasures of the house of Yahweh and the treasures of the king's house, and he cut in pieces all the vessels of gold that Solomon king of Israel had made in the temple of Yahweh, as Yahweh had spoken.
24:14	He carried into exile all Jerusalem: all the officials and all the mighty warriors, ten thousand exiles, and all the craftsmen and the smiths. None remained except the poorest people of the land.
24:15	He carried Jehoiachin into exile to Babylon; the king's mother, the king's wives, his eunuchs, and the leading men of the land he took into exile from Jerusalem to Babylon.
24:16	All the men of valor, seven thousand, and the craftsmen and smiths, one thousand, all of them strong and fit for war—the king of Babylon brought them into exile to Babylon.
24:17	The king of Babylon made Mattaniah, Jehoiachin's uncle, king in his place and changed his name to Zedekiah.
24:18	Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah.
24:19	He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, according to all that Jehoiakim had done.
24:20	For because of the anger of Yahweh it came to this in Jerusalem and Judah, until he cast them out from his presence. And Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.
