19:1	When King Hezekiah heard it, he tore his clothes, covered himself with sackcloth, and went into the house of Yahweh.
19:2	He sent Eliakim, who was over the household, Shebna the secretary, and the senior priests, covered in sackcloth, to Isaiah the prophet son of Amoz.
19:3	They said to him, "Thus says Hezekiah: 'This day is a day of distress, of rebuke, and of disgrace; for children have come to the point of birth, and there is no strength to deliver them.
19:4	Perhaps Yahweh your God will hear all the words of the Rabshakeh, whom his master the king of Assyria has sent to taunt the living God, and will rebuke the words that Yahweh your God has heard. So lift up a prayer for the remnant that is left.'"
19:5	When the servants of King Hezekiah came to Isaiah,
19:6	Isaiah said to them, "Say this to your master: 'Thus says Yahweh: Do not be afraid because of the words you have heard, with which the attendants of the king of Assyria have reviled me.
19:7	I am about to put a spirit in him, so that he will hear a report and return to his own land, and I will make him fall by the sword in his own land.'"
19:8	The Rabshakeh returned and found the king of Assyria fighting against Libnah, for he had heard that the king had moved on from Lachish.
19:9	Then the king heard concerning Tirhakah king of Cush, "Look, he has come out to fight against you." So he again sent messengers to Hezekiah, saying,
19:10	"Say this to Hezekiah king of Judah: 'Do not let your God on whom you rely deceive you by saying, "Jerusalem will not be given into the hand of the king of Assyria."
19:11	Look, you have heard what the kings of Assyria have done to all the lands, devoting them to destruction. And will you be delivered?
19:12	Did the gods of the nations deliver them, the nations my fathers destroyed—Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, and the people of Eden who were in Telassar?
19:13	Where is the king of Hamath, the king of Arpad, the king of the city of Sepharvaim, of Hena, and of Ivvah?'"
19:14	Hezekiah took the letter from the hand of the messengers and read it; then Hezekiah went up to the house of Yahweh and spread it out before Yahweh.
19:15	And Hezekiah prayed before Yahweh and said, "Yahweh, God of Israel, enthroned above the cherubim, you are God, you alone, of all the kingdoms of the earth; you made the heavens and the earth.
19:16	Incline your ear, Yahweh, and hear; open your eyes, Yahweh, and see; hear the words of Sennacherib, which he has sent to taunt the living God.
19:17	Truly, Yahweh, the kings of Assyria have laid waste the nations and their lands,
19:18	and have thrown their gods into the fire, for they were not gods but the work of human hands, wood and stone; so they destroyed them.
19:19	So now, Yahweh our God, save us, please, from his hand, so that all the kingdoms of the earth may know that you, Yahweh, alone are God."
19:20	Then Isaiah son of Amoz sent to Hezekiah, saying, "Thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel: Your prayer to me about Sennacherib king of Assyria I have heard.
19:21	This is the word that Yahweh has spoken concerning him: She despises you, she mocks you, the virgin daughter Zion; behind you she shakes her head, the daughter Jerusalem.
19:22	Whom have you taunted and reviled? Against whom have you raised your voice and lifted your eyes on high? Against the Holy One of Israel!
19:23	By your messengers you have taunted the Lord, and you said, 'With my many chariots I have gone up to the heights of the mountains, to the farthest reaches of Lebanon. I cut down its tallest cedars, its choicest junipers; I entered its farthest lodging place, its densest forest.
19:24	I dug wells and drank foreign waters, and with the sole of my feet I dried up all the streams of Egypt.'
19:25	Have you not heard? Long ago I made it; from days of old I planned it; now I have brought it about, that you should turn fortified cities into ruined heaps,
19:26	while their inhabitants, short of strength, were dismayed and put to shame. They became like plants of the field and green grass, like grass on the rooftops, blighted before it grows up.
19:27	But I know your sitting down and your going out and your coming in, and your raging against me.
19:28	Because you have raged against me and your arrogance has come up to my ears, I will put my hook in your nose and my bit in your lips, and I will turn you back by the way you came.
19:29	And this shall be the sign for you: this year you will eat what grows by itself, and in the second year what springs up from that; then in the third year sow and reap, plant vineyards and eat their fruit.
19:30	The surviving remnant of the house of Judah shall again take root downward and bear fruit upward.
19:31	For out of Jerusalem shall go a remnant, and survivors out of Mount Zion. The zeal of Yahweh of Armies will do this.
19:32	Therefore thus says Yahweh concerning the king of Assyria: He shall not come into this city or shoot an arrow there; he shall not come before it with a shield or cast up a siege ramp against it.
19:33	By the way that he came, by it he shall return, and he shall not come into this city, declares Yahweh.
19:34	For I will defend this city to save it, for my own sake and for the sake of David my servant."
19:35	That night the angel of Yahweh went out and struck down one hundred eighty-five thousand in the camp of Assyria. When people rose early in the morning, there they all were, dead bodies.
19:36	Then Sennacherib king of Assyria broke camp and went home, and lived in Nineveh.
19:37	As he was worshiping in the house of Nisroch his god, Adrammelech and Sharezer his sons struck him down with the sword and escaped to the land of Ararat. And Esarhaddon his son reigned in his place.
