4:1	Now a certain woman, one of the wives of the sons of the prophets, cried out to Elisha, saying, "Your servant my husband is dead, and you know that your servant feared Yahweh. But the creditor has come to take my two children as his slaves."
4:2	Elisha said to her, "What shall I do for you? Tell me, what do you have in the house?" She said, "Your servant has nothing at all in the house except a jar of oil."
4:3	He said, "Go, borrow vessels for yourself from outside, from all your neighbors, empty vessels, and not too few.
4:4	Then go in and shut the door behind you and your sons, and pour into all these vessels, and set aside each one that is full."
4:5	So she went from him and shut the door behind her and her sons. They kept bringing vessels to her, and she kept pouring.
4:6	When the vessels were full, she said to her son, "Bring me another vessel." He said to her, "There is not another vessel." Then the oil stopped.
4:7	She came and told the man of God, and he said, "Go, sell the oil and pay your debt, and you and your sons can live on the rest."
4:8	One day Elisha passed through Shunem, where there was a wealthy woman, and she urged him to eat a meal. So whenever he passed by, he would turn in there to eat a meal.
4:9	She said to her husband, "Look, I know that this is a holy man of God who is constantly passing our way.
4:10	Let us make a small walled upper room and put a bed there for him, a table, a chair, and a lamp, so that whenever he comes to us he can turn in there."
4:11	One day he came there, and he turned in to the upper room and lay down there.
4:12	He said to Gehazi his servant, "Call this Shunammite." He called her, and she stood before him.
4:13	He said to him, "Say to her, 'Look, you have gone to all this trouble for us. What can be done for you? Can I speak on your behalf to the king or to the commander of the army?'" She said, "I live among my own people."
4:14	He said, "Then what can be done for her?" Gehazi said, "Well, she has no son, and her husband is old."
4:15	He said, "Call her." He called her, and she stood in the doorway.
4:16	He said, "At this season, when the time comes round, you will be embracing a son." She said, "No, my lord, man of God, do not lie to your servant."
4:17	But the woman conceived and bore a son at that season, when the time came round, as Elisha had told her.
4:18	When the child had grown, one day he went out to his father among the reapers.
4:19	He said to his father, "My head, my head!" His father said to the servant, "Carry him to his mother."
4:20	He carried him and brought him to his mother, and he sat on her lap until noon, and then he died.
4:21	She went up and laid him on the bed of the man of God, shut the door on him, and went out.
4:22	She called to her husband and said, "Please send me one of the servants and one of the donkeys, so that I may hurry to the man of God and come back."
4:23	He said, "Why are you going to him today? It is neither new moon nor sabbath." She said, "It is all right."
4:24	She saddled the donkey and said to her servant, "Drive on and go; do not slow down the pace for me unless I tell you."
4:25	So she went and came to the man of God at Mount Carmel. When the man of God saw her at a distance, he said to Gehazi his servant, "Look, there is the Shunammite.
4:26	Now run to meet her and say to her, 'Is all well with you? Is all well with your husband? Is all well with the child?'" She said, "All is well."
4:27	When she came to the man of God on the mountain, she caught hold of his feet. Gehazi came near to push her away, but the man of God said, "Leave her alone, for her soul is bitter within her, and Yahweh has hidden it from me and has not told me."
4:28	She said, "Did I ask my lord for a son? Did I not say, 'Do not deceive me'?"
4:29	He said to Gehazi, "Tie up your garments, take my staff in your hand, and go. If you meet anyone, do not greet him, and if anyone greets you, do not answer him. And lay my staff on the boy's face."
4:30	But the boy's mother said, "As Yahweh lives and as you yourself live, I will not leave you." So he got up and followed her.
4:31	Gehazi went on ahead of them and laid the staff on the boy's face, but there was no sound and no response. So he went back to meet him and told him, "The boy has not woken up."
4:32	When Elisha came into the house, there was the boy, dead, laid on his bed.
4:33	He went in and shut the door behind the two of them and prayed to Yahweh.
4:34	Then he went up and lay on the child, putting his mouth on his mouth, his eyes on his eyes, and his hands on his hands. As he stretched himself over him, the child's flesh grew warm.
4:35	He got down and walked once back and forth in the house, then went up and stretched himself over him. The boy sneezed seven times, and the boy opened his eyes.
4:36	He called Gehazi and said, "Call this Shunammite." So he called her, and she came to him. He said, "Pick up your son."
4:37	She came and fell at his feet and bowed to the ground. Then she picked up her son and went out.
4:38	Elisha returned to Gilgal when there was a famine in the land. As the sons of the prophets were sitting before him, he said to his servant, "Put on the large pot and boil stew for the sons of the prophets."
4:39	One of them went out into the field to gather herbs, and he found a wild vine and gathered from it a lapful of wild gourds. He came and cut them up into the pot of stew, for they did not know what they were.
4:40	They poured it out for the men to eat. But as they were eating the stew, they cried out, "Man of God, there is death in the pot!" And they could not eat it.
4:41	He said, "Then bring some flour." He threw it into the pot and said, "Pour it out for the people, so that they may eat." And there was nothing harmful in the pot.
4:42	A man came from Baal-shalishah, bringing the man of God bread from the first fruits: twenty loaves of barley and fresh ears of grain in his sack. Elisha said, "Give it to the people, so that they may eat."
4:43	But his attendant said, "How can I set this before a hundred men?" He said, "Give it to the people, so that they may eat, for thus says Yahweh: They will eat and have some left over."
4:44	So he set it before them, and they ate and had some left over, according to the word of Yahweh.
