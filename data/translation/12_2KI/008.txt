8:1	Now Elisha had said to the woman whose son he had restored to life, "Get up and go, you and your household, and stay as a foreigner wherever you can, for Yahweh has called for a famine, and it will come on the land for seven years."
8:2	So the woman got up and did as the man of God said. She went with her household and lived as a foreigner in the land of the Philistines for seven years.
8:3	At the end of the seven years, the woman returned from the land of the Philistines, and she went to appeal to the king for her house and her field.
8:4	Now the king was talking with Gehazi, the servant of the man of God, saying, "Tell me all the great things that Elisha has done."
8:5	Just as he was telling the king how Elisha had restored the dead to life, the woman whose son he had restored to life came appealing to the king for her house and her field. Gehazi said, "My lord the king, this is the woman, and this is her son whom Elisha restored to life."
8:6	When the king questioned the woman, she told him. So the king appointed an official for her, saying, "Restore all that was hers, together with all the produce of the field from the day she left the land until now."
8:7	Elisha came to Damascus while Ben-hadad king of Aram was ill, and he was told, "The man of God has come here."
8:8	The king said to Hazael, "Take a gift with you and go to meet the man of God, and inquire of Yahweh through him, saying, 'Will I recover from this illness?'"
8:9	So Hazael went to meet him, taking a gift with him, all kinds of the good things of Damascus, forty camel loads. He came and stood before him and said, "Your son Ben-hadad king of Aram has sent me to you, saying, 'Will I recover from this illness?'"
8:10	Elisha said to him, "Go, say to him, 'You will certainly recover.' But Yahweh has shown me that he will certainly die."
8:11	He kept his face fixed and stared until he was ashamed, and then the man of God wept.
8:12	Hazael said, "Why is my lord weeping?" He said, "Because I know the harm you will do to the people of Israel. You will set their fortresses on fire, kill their young men with the sword, dash their little ones to pieces, and rip open their pregnant women."
8:13	Hazael said, "But what is your servant, a mere dog, that he should do this great thing?" Elisha said, "Yahweh has shown me that you will be king over Aram."
8:14	Then he left Elisha and came to his master, who said to him, "What did Elisha say to you?" He said, "He told me that you would certainly recover."
8:15	But the next day he took the blanket, dipped it in water, and spread it over his face, and he died. And Hazael became king in his place.
8:16	In the fifth year of Joram son of Ahab king of Israel, Jehoshaphat being king of Judah, Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat king of Judah became king.
8:17	He was thirty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem.
8:18	He walked in the way of the kings of Israel, as the house of Ahab had done, for the daughter of Ahab was his wife; and he did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh.
8:19	Yet Yahweh was not willing to destroy Judah, for the sake of David His servant, since He had promised to give him a lamp for his sons forever.
8:20	In his days Edom revolted from the rule of Judah and set up a king of their own.
8:21	So Joram crossed over to Zair with all his chariots. He rose by night and struck the Edomites who had surrounded him and the chariot commanders, but the army fled to their tents.
8:22	So Edom has been in revolt from the rule of Judah to this day. Libnah also revolted at that time.
8:23	Now the rest of the acts of Joram and all that he did, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?
8:24	Joram slept with his fathers and was buried with his fathers in the city of David, and Ahaziah his son reigned in his place.
8:25	In the twelfth year of Joram son of Ahab king of Israel, Ahaziah son of Jehoram king of Judah became king.
8:26	Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned one year in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Athaliah, granddaughter of Omri king of Israel.
8:27	He walked in the way of the house of Ahab and did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, as the house of Ahab had done, for he was a son-in-law of the house of Ahab.
8:28	He went with Joram son of Ahab to war against Hazael king of Aram at Ramoth-gilead, and the Arameans wounded Joram.
8:29	King Joram returned to Jezreel to recover from the wounds that the Arameans had inflicted on him at Ramah, when he fought against Hazael king of Aram. And Ahaziah son of Jehoram king of Judah went down to see Joram son of Ahab in Jezreel, because he was wounded.
