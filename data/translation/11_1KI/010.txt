10:1	When the queen of Sheba heard of Solomon's fame, connected with the name of Yahweh, she came to test him with riddles.
10:2	She came to Jerusalem with a very large retinue, with camels carrying spices, a great quantity of gold and precious stones. She came to Solomon and spoke with him about everything that was on her mind.
10:3	Solomon answered all her questions; there was nothing hidden from the king that he could not explain to her.
10:4	When the queen of Sheba had seen all Solomon's wisdom, the house he had built,
10:5	the food of his table, the seating of his officials, the attendance of his servants and their clothing, his cupbearers, and the burnt offerings he offered at the house of Yahweh, it left her breathless.
10:6	She said to the king, "The report I heard in my own land about your words and your wisdom was true.
10:7	But I did not believe the reports until I came and my own eyes saw it. And in fact, not even half was told me. You surpass in wisdom and prosperity the report that I heard.
10:8	How fortunate are your men! How fortunate are these servants of yours, who stand before you continually and hear your wisdom!
10:9	Blessed be Yahweh your God, Who has delighted in you and set you on the throne of Israel! Because Yahweh loves Israel forever, He has made you king, to do justice and righteousness."
10:10	Then she gave the king a hundred and twenty talents of gold, a very great quantity of spices and precious stones. Never again did such an abundance of spices come as the queen of Sheba gave to King Solomon.
10:11	Moreover, Hiram's fleet, which carried gold from Ophir, brought from Ophir a very great quantity of almug wood and precious stones.
10:12	The king made the almug wood into supports for the house of Yahweh and for the king's house, and into lyres and harps for the singers. No such almug wood has come in or been seen to this day.
10:13	King Solomon gave the queen of Sheba everything she desired, whatever she asked, besides what he gave her out of King Solomon's royal bounty. Then she turned and went back to her own land, she and her servants.
10:14	The weight of the gold that came to Solomon in one year was six hundred and sixty-six talents of gold,
10:15	besides what came from the traders and the commerce of the merchants, and from all the kings of Arabia and the governors of the land.
10:16	King Solomon made two hundred large shields of beaten gold; six hundred shekels of gold went into each shield.
10:17	He also made three hundred small shields of beaten gold; three minas of gold went into each shield. The king put them in the House of the Forest of Lebanon.
10:18	The king also made a great ivory throne and overlaid it with fine gold.
10:19	The throne had six steps, and the back of the throne had a rounded top; on each side of the seat were armrests, with two lions standing beside the armrests.
10:20	Twelve lions stood there on the six steps, one on each side. Nothing like it was ever made in any kingdom.
10:21	All King Solomon's drinking vessels were of gold, and all the vessels of the House of the Forest of Lebanon were of pure gold. None were of silver; silver was considered as nothing in the days of Solomon.
10:22	For the king had a fleet of Tarshish ships at sea along with Hiram's fleet. Once every three years the fleet of Tarshish ships would come, bringing gold, silver, ivory, apes and baboons.
10:23	So King Solomon surpassed all the kings of the earth in riches and wisdom.
10:24	The whole world sought an audience with Solomon to hear the wisdom that God had put in his heart.
10:25	Each of them brought his gift: articles of silver and gold, garments, weapons, spices, horses and mules, so much year by year.
10:26	Solomon gathered chariots and horsemen. He had fourteen hundred chariots and twelve thousand horsemen, whom he stationed in the chariot cities and with the king in Jerusalem.
10:27	The king made silver as common in Jerusalem as stones, and cedar as plentiful as the sycamore-fig trees of the Shephelah.
10:28	Solomon's horses were imported from Egypt and from Kue; the king's traders acquired them from Kue at the current price.
10:29	A chariot could be imported from Egypt for six hundred shekels of silver, and a horse for a hundred and fifty. In the same way they were exported through them to all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Aram.
