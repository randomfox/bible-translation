7:1	Solomon spent thirteen years building his own house, and he finished his whole house.
7:2	He built the House of the Forest of Lebanon: it was a hundred cubits long, fifty cubits wide and thirty cubits high, on four rows of cedar pillars, with cedar beams on the pillars.
7:3	It was roofed with cedar above the beams that rested on the pillars, forty-five of them, fifteen to a row.
7:4	There were window frames in three rows, with window facing window three times.
7:5	All the doorways and doorposts had square frames, and window was opposite window three times.
7:6	He made the Hall of Pillars, fifty cubits long and thirty cubits wide, with a porch in front of it, and pillars with a canopy in front of them.
7:7	He made the Hall of the Throne where he was to judge, the Hall of Judgment; it was paneled with cedar from floor to floor.
7:8	His own house where he was to live, in the other court set back from the hall, was of the same workmanship. Solomon also made a house like this hall for Pharaoh's daughter, whom he had married.
7:9	All these were of costly stones, cut to measure like hewn stone, trimmed with saws on the inner and outer faces, from the foundation up to the coping, and on the outside as far as the great court.
7:10	The foundation was laid with costly stones, great stones, stones of ten cubits and stones of eight cubits.
7:11	Above were costly stones, cut to measure like hewn stone, and cedar.
7:12	The great court all around had three courses of hewn stone and a course of trimmed cedar beams, like the inner court of the house of Yahweh and the porch of the house.
7:13	King Solomon sent and brought Hiram from Tyre.
7:14	He was the son of a widow from the tribe of Naphtali, and his father was a man of Tyre, a worker in bronze. He was full of skill, understanding and knowledge for doing every kind of work in bronze. He came to King Solomon and did all his work.
7:15	He cast the two bronze pillars. Each pillar was eighteen cubits high, and a cord of twelve cubits went around the second pillar.
7:16	He made two capitals of cast bronze to set on the tops of the pillars; one capital was five cubits high and the other capital was five cubits high.
7:17	There were lattices of checker work and wreaths of chain work for the capitals on top of the pillars, seven for one capital and seven for the other.
7:18	So he made the pillars, and two rows of pomegranates all around on each lattice to cover the capitals that were on top of the pillars; he did the same for the other capital.
7:19	The capitals on top of the pillars in the porch were of lily work, four cubits high.
7:20	The capitals on the two pillars also had pomegranates above, next to the rounded bulge beside the lattice; there were two hundred pomegranates in rows all around; so also on the second capital.
7:21	He set up the pillars at the porch of the temple. He set up the pillar on the right and named it Jachin, and he set up the pillar on the left and named it Boaz.
7:22	On top of the pillars was lily work. So the work on the pillars was finished.
7:23	He made the Sea of cast metal, ten cubits from rim to rim, round all over, five cubits high; a line of thirty cubits would go around it.
7:24	Under its rim, all around it, were gourds encircling it, ten to a cubit, going all the way around the Sea; the gourds were in two rows, cast together with it.
7:25	It stood on twelve oxen, three facing north, three facing west, three facing south and three facing east; the Sea rested on top of them, and all their hindquarters were toward the inside.
7:26	It was a handbreadth thick, and its rim was made like the rim of a cup, like a lily blossom. It held two thousand baths.
7:27	He made the ten bronze stands. Each stand was four cubits long, four cubits wide and three cubits high.
7:28	This is how the stands were made: they had panels, and the panels were set between the frames.
7:29	On the panels between the frames were lions, oxen and cherubim; on the frames as well, above and below the lions and oxen, were wreaths of hanging work.
7:30	Each stand had four bronze wheels with bronze axles, and its four feet had supports. The supports were cast beneath the basin, with wreaths at the side of each.
7:31	Its opening, inside the crown and projecting upward, was a cubit; its opening was round, made like a pedestal, a cubit and a half. On its opening there were also engravings. Their panels were square, not round.
7:32	The four wheels were under the panels, and the axles of the wheels were fixed in the stand. Each wheel was a cubit and a half high.
7:33	The wheels were made like chariot wheels; their axles, rims, spokes and hubs were all cast metal.
7:34	There were four supports at the four corners of each stand; the supports were of one piece with the stand.
7:35	On top of the stand there was a round band half a cubit high, and on top of the stand its brackets and its panels were of one piece with it.
7:36	On the surfaces of its brackets and on its panels he engraved cherubim, lions and palm trees, wherever there was space, with wreaths all around.
7:37	This is how he made the ten stands: all of them had the same casting, the same measure and the same shape.
7:38	He made ten bronze basins. Each basin held forty baths, and each basin measured four cubits; there was one basin on each of the ten stands.
7:39	He set the stands, five on the right side of the house and five on the left side; and he set the Sea on the right side of the house, toward the southeast.
7:40	Hiram also made the basins, the shovels and the sprinkling bowls. So Hiram finished all the work that he did for King Solomon on the house of Yahweh:
7:41	the two pillars; the two bowl-shaped capitals on top of the pillars; the two lattices to cover the two bowls of the capitals on top of the pillars;
7:42	the four hundred pomegranates for the two lattices, two rows of pomegranates for each lattice, to cover the two bowls of the capitals on top of the pillars;
7:43	the ten stands, and the ten basins on the stands;
7:44	the one Sea, and the twelve oxen under the Sea;
7:45	and the pots, the shovels and the sprinkling bowls. All these vessels that Hiram made for King Solomon for the house of Yahweh were of burnished bronze.
7:46	The king cast them in the plain of the Jordan, in the clay ground between Succoth and Zarethan.
7:47	Solomon left all the vessels unweighed because there were so very many; the weight of the bronze was never determined.
7:48	Solomon made all the furnishings that were in the house of Yahweh: the golden altar; the golden table on which the bread of the Presence was set;
7:49	the lampstands of pure gold, five on the right and five on the left in front of the inner sanctuary; the flowers, the lamps and the tongs, of gold;
7:50	the cups, the snuffers, the sprinkling bowls, the ladles and the fire pans, of pure gold; and the sockets for the doors of the inner house, the Most Holy Place, and for the doors of the house, the nave, of gold.
7:51	So all the work that King Solomon did on the house of Yahweh was completed. Then Solomon brought in the things his father David had dedicated, the silver, the gold and the vessels, and put them in the treasuries of the house of Yahweh.
