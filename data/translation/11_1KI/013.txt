13:1	Just then a man of God came from Judah to Bethel by the word of Yahweh, while Jeroboam was standing by the altar to burn incense.
13:2	He cried out against the altar by the word of Yahweh, "Altar, altar, thus says Yahweh: 'A son will be born to the house of David, Josiah by name, and on you he will sacrifice the priests of the high places who burn incense on you, and human bones will be burned on you.'"
13:3	He gave a sign that same day, saying, "This is the sign that Yahweh has spoken: the altar will be split apart, and the ashes on it will be poured out."
13:4	When the king heard what the man of God cried out against the altar in Bethel, Jeroboam stretched out his hand from the altar and said, "Seize him!" But the hand he stretched out against him withered, so that he could not draw it back to himself.
13:5	The altar also split apart, and the ashes poured out from the altar, according to the sign that the man of God had given by the word of Yahweh.
13:6	The king said to the man of God, "Please entreat the favor of Yahweh your God and pray for me, so that my hand may be restored to me." The man of God entreated Yahweh, and the king's hand was restored to him and became as it was before.
13:7	The king said to the man of God, "Come home with me and refresh yourself, and I will give you a gift."
13:8	But the man of God said to the king, "Even if you gave me half your house, I would not go in with you, nor would I eat bread or drink water in this place.
13:9	For so I was commanded by the word of Yahweh: 'You shall not eat bread or drink water, and you shall not return by the way you came.'"
13:10	So he went another way and did not return by the way he had come to Bethel.
13:11	Now an old prophet was living in Bethel. His sons came and told him all that the man of God had done that day in Bethel; they also told their father the words he had spoken to the king.
13:12	Their father said to them, "Which way did he go?" His sons had seen the way the man of God who came from Judah had gone.
13:13	He said to his sons, "Saddle the donkey for me." They saddled the donkey for him, and he mounted it.
13:14	He went after the man of God and found him sitting under the oak. He said to him, "Are you the man of God who came from Judah?" He said, "I am."
13:15	He said to him, "Come home with me and eat some bread."
13:16	He said, "I cannot go back with you or go in with you, and I will not eat bread or drink water with you in this place,
13:17	for I was told by the word of Yahweh, 'You shall not eat bread or drink water there, and you shall not go back by the way you came.'"
13:18	He said to him, "I too am a prophet like you, and an angel spoke to me by the word of Yahweh, saying, 'Bring him back with you to your house so that he may eat bread and drink water.'" But he was lying to him.
13:19	So he went back with him and ate bread in his house and drank water.
13:20	As they were sitting at the table, the word of Yahweh came to the prophet who had brought him back,
13:21	and he cried out to the man of God who had come from Judah, "Thus says Yahweh: 'Because you have defied the command of Yahweh and have not kept the commandment that Yahweh your God commanded you,
13:22	but have come back and eaten bread and drunk water in the place of which he said to you, "Do not eat bread or drink water," your body shall not come to the tomb of your fathers.'"
13:23	After he had eaten bread and after he had drunk, he saddled the donkey for him, for the prophet whom he had brought back.
13:24	He went on his way, and a lion met him on the road and killed him. His body lay thrown down on the road, with the donkey standing beside it and the lion standing beside the body.
13:25	Some men passing by saw the body thrown down on the road and the lion standing beside the body, and they came and told it in the city where the old prophet lived.
13:26	When the prophet who had brought him back from the road heard it, he said, "It is the man of God who defied the command of Yahweh; Yahweh has given him to the lion, which has mauled him and killed him, according to the word Yahweh spoke to him."
13:27	He said to his sons, "Saddle the donkey for me." And they saddled it.
13:28	He went and found the body thrown down on the road, with the donkey and the lion standing beside the body. The lion had not eaten the body or mauled the donkey.
13:29	The prophet lifted up the body of the man of God, laid it on the donkey and brought it back; the old prophet came into the city to mourn and to bury him.
13:30	He laid the body in his own tomb, and they mourned over him, "Alas, my brother!"
13:31	After he had buried him, he said to his sons, "When I die, bury me in the tomb where the man of God is buried; lay my bones beside his bones.
13:32	For the word that he cried out by the word of Yahweh against the altar in Bethel and against all the shrines of the high places in the cities of Samaria will surely come to pass."
13:33	Even after this Jeroboam did not turn from his evil way, but again appointed priests for the high places from among all the people; anyone who wanted it, he ordained, so that he became one of the priests of the high places.
13:34	This thing became the sin of the house of Jeroboam, so as to wipe it out and destroy it from the face of the earth.
