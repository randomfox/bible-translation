24:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
24:2	"Command the people of Israel to bring you pure oil from beaten olives for the light, so that a lamp may be kept burning continually.
24:3	Outside the veil of the testimony, in the tent of meeting, Aaron shall keep it in order from evening to morning before Yahweh continually; it is a permanent statute throughout your generations.
24:4	He shall keep the lamps in order on the lampstand of pure gold before Yahweh continually.
24:5	"You shall take fine flour and bake twelve loaves from it; two tenths of an ephah shall be in each loaf.
24:6	And you shall set them in two rows, six in a row, on the table of pure gold before Yahweh.
24:7	You shall put pure frankincense on each row, so that it may be a memorial portion for the bread, a food offering to Yahweh.
24:8	Every sabbath day he shall set it in order before Yahweh continually; it is from the people of Israel as a perpetual covenant.
24:9	It shall belong to Aaron and his sons, and they shall eat it in a holy place, for it is most holy to him, from Yahweh's food offerings, a permanent due."
24:10	Now the son of an Israelite woman, whose father was an Egyptian, went out among the people of Israel, and the son of the Israelite woman and an Israelite man fought in the camp.
24:11	The son of the Israelite woman blasphemed the Name and cursed. So they brought him to Moses. His mother's name was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.
24:12	They put him in custody until the will of Yahweh should be made clear to them.
24:13	Then Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
24:14	"Bring the one who cursed outside the camp, and let all who heard him lay their hands on his head, and let the whole congregation stone him.
24:15	And speak to the people of Israel, saying: Anyone who curses his God shall bear his sin.
24:16	Whoever blasphemes the name of Yahweh shall surely be put to death; the whole congregation shall surely stone him. The foreigner as well as the native-born, when he blasphemes the Name, shall be put to death.
24:17	"Whoever strikes down any human life shall surely be put to death.
24:18	Whoever strikes down the life of an animal shall make restitution for it, life for life.
24:19	If anyone injures his neighbor, as he has done, so it shall be done to him:
24:20	fracture for fracture, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; as he has injured a person, so it shall be done to him.
24:21	Whoever kills an animal shall make restitution for it, but whoever kills a person shall be put to death.
24:22	You shall have one rule for the foreigner and for the native-born alike, for I am Yahweh your God."
24:23	So Moses spoke to the people of Israel, and they brought the one who had cursed outside the camp and stoned him with stones. Thus the people of Israel did as Yahweh commanded Moses.
