8:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
8:2	"Take Aaron and his sons with him, the garments, the anointing oil, the bull of the sin offering, the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread,
8:3	and assemble the whole congregation at the entrance of the tent of meeting."
8:4	Moses did as Yahweh commanded him, and the congregation assembled at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
8:5	Moses said to the congregation, "This is what Yahweh has commanded to be done."
8:6	Then Moses brought Aaron and his sons forward and washed them with water.
8:7	He put the tunic on him, tied the sash around him, clothed him with the robe, and put the ephod on him; he tied the woven band of the ephod around him and fastened the ephod to him with it.
8:8	He placed the breastpiece on him and put the Urim and the Thummim into the breastpiece.
8:9	He set the turban on his head, and on the turban, at its front, he set the gold plate, the holy diadem, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.
8:10	Then Moses took the anointing oil and anointed the tabernacle and everything in it, and consecrated them.
8:11	He sprinkled some of it on the altar seven times and anointed the altar and all its utensils, and the basin and its stand, to consecrate them.
8:12	He poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron's head and anointed him to consecrate him.
8:13	Moses brought Aaron's sons forward, clothed them with tunics, tied sashes around them, and bound caps on them, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.
8:14	Then he brought the bull of the sin offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the head of the bull of the sin offering.
8:15	It was slaughtered, and Moses took the blood and with his finger put it on the horns of the altar all around and purified the altar; then he poured out the blood at the base of the altar and consecrated it, to make atonement for it.
8:16	He took all the fat that was on the entrails, the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys with their fat, and Moses burned them on the altar.
8:17	But the bull, its hide, its flesh, and its dung he burned with fire outside the camp, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.
8:18	Then he presented the ram of the burnt offering, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the ram's head.
8:19	It was slaughtered, and Moses splashed the blood against all sides of the altar.
8:20	The ram was cut into its pieces, and Moses burned the head, the pieces, and the fat.
8:21	The entrails and the legs were washed with water, and Moses burned the whole ram on the altar. It was a burnt offering for a soothing aroma, a food offering to Yahweh, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.
8:22	Then he presented the second ram, the ram of ordination, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on the ram's head.
8:23	It was slaughtered, and Moses took some of its blood and put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear, on the thumb of his right hand, and on the big toe of his right foot.
8:24	Then Moses brought Aaron's sons forward and put some of the blood on the lobe of their right ears, on the thumbs of their right hands, and on the big toes of their right feet; and Moses splashed the blood against all sides of the altar.
8:25	He took the fat, the fat tail, all the fat that was on the entrails, the lobe of the liver, the two kidneys with their fat, and the right thigh;
8:26	and from the basket of unleavened bread that was before Yahweh he took one unleavened loaf, one loaf of bread made with oil, and one wafer, and placed them on the fat portions and on the right thigh.
8:27	He put all these on the palms of Aaron and on the palms of his sons, and waved them as a wave offering before Yahweh.
8:28	Then Moses took them from their palms and burned them on the altar on top of the burnt offering. They were an ordination offering for a soothing aroma; it was a food offering to Yahweh.
8:29	Moses took the breast and waved it as a wave offering before Yahweh; it was Moses' portion of the ram of ordination, as Yahweh had commanded Moses.
8:30	Then Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood that was on the altar and sprinkled it on Aaron and his garments, and on his sons and his sons' garments with him; so he consecrated Aaron and his garments, and his sons and his sons' garments with him.
8:31	Moses said to Aaron and his sons, "Boil the meat at the entrance of the tent of meeting and eat it there with the bread that is in the basket of ordination, as I commanded, saying, 'Aaron and his sons shall eat it.'
8:32	Whatever is left of the meat and the bread you shall burn with fire.
8:33	You shall not go out from the entrance of the tent of meeting for seven days, until the days of your ordination are completed, for it will take seven days to ordain you.
8:34	As has been done today, Yahweh has commanded to be done to make atonement for you.
8:35	You shall remain at the entrance of the tent of meeting day and night for seven days, keeping Yahweh's charge, so that you do not die; for so I have been commanded."
8:36	Aaron and his sons did all the things that Yahweh had commanded through Moses.
