11:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying to them,
11:2	"Speak to the Israelites, saying: These are the creatures you may eat among all the animals that are on the land.
11:3	Any animal that has divided hooves, split into two, and chews the cud, you may eat.
11:4	But among those that chew the cud or have divided hooves, you shall not eat these: the camel, because it chews the cud but does not have a divided hoof; it is unclean for you.
11:5	The rock hyrax, because it chews the cud but does not have a divided hoof; it is unclean for you.
11:6	The hare, because it chews the cud but does not have a divided hoof; it is unclean for you.
11:7	And the pig, because it has a divided hoof, split into two, but does not chew the cud; it is unclean for you.
11:8	You shall not eat their flesh, and you shall not touch their carcasses; they are unclean for you.
11:9	Of all that live in the water you may eat these: anything in the water, whether in the seas or in the streams, that has fins and scales, you may eat.
11:10	But anything in the seas or the streams that does not have fins and scales, of all the swarming creatures of the water and of all the living creatures that are in the water, is detestable to you.
11:11	They shall remain detestable to you; you shall not eat their flesh, and you shall detest their carcasses.
11:12	Everything in the water that does not have fins and scales is detestable to you.
11:13	These you shall detest among the birds; they shall not be eaten, they are detestable: the eagle, the bearded vulture, the black vulture,
11:14	the kite, the falcon of every kind,
11:15	every raven of every kind,
11:16	the ostrich, the nighthawk, the seagull, the hawk of every kind,
11:17	the little owl, the cormorant, the great owl,
11:18	the barn owl, the desert owl, the carrion vulture,
11:19	the stork, the heron of every kind, the hoopoe, and the bat.
11:20	All winged swarming creatures that walk on all fours are detestable to you.
11:21	Yet among all the winged swarming creatures that walk on all fours you may eat those that have jointed legs above their feet with which to leap on the ground.
11:22	Of them you may eat these: the locust of every kind, the bald locust of every kind, the cricket of every kind, and the grasshopper of every kind.
11:23	But all other winged swarming creatures that have four feet are detestable to you.
11:24	By these you will become unclean; whoever touches their carcasses will be unclean until evening,
11:25	and whoever carries any part of their carcasses shall wash his clothes and be unclean until evening.
11:26	Every animal that has hooves but not split into two and does not chew the cud is unclean for you; whoever touches them will become unclean.
11:27	All that walk on their paws, among all the creatures that walk on all fours, are unclean for you; whoever touches their carcasses will be unclean until evening,
11:28	and whoever carries their carcasses shall wash his clothes and be unclean until evening; they are unclean for you.
11:29	These are unclean for you among the swarming creatures that swarm on the ground: the mole rat, the mouse, the great lizard of every kind,
11:30	the gecko, the monitor lizard, the lizard, the skink, and the chameleon.
11:31	These are unclean for you among all the swarming creatures; whoever touches them when they are dead will be unclean until evening.
11:32	Anything on which any of them falls when they are dead will be unclean, whether any article of wood or clothing or skin or sackcloth—any article that is used for work. It shall be put into water, and it will be unclean until evening; then it will be clean.
11:33	And any earthenware vessel into which any of them falls—everything in it will be unclean, and you shall break the vessel.
11:34	Any food that may be eaten, on which water from such a vessel comes, will be unclean, and any drink that may be drunk from any such vessel will be unclean.
11:35	Everything on which any part of their carcass falls will be unclean; an oven or a stove shall be broken down. They are unclean, and they shall remain unclean for you.
11:36	But a spring or a cistern holding water will remain clean, though whoever touches their carcass will be unclean.
11:37	If any part of their carcass falls on any seed for sowing that is to be sown, it is clean;
11:38	but if water is put on the seed and any part of their carcass falls on it, it is unclean for you.
11:39	If any animal that you may eat dies, whoever touches its carcass will be unclean until evening.
11:40	Whoever eats of its carcass shall wash his clothes and be unclean until evening, and whoever carries its carcass shall wash his clothes and be unclean until evening.
11:41	Every swarming creature that swarms on the ground is detestable; it shall not be eaten.
11:42	Whatever moves on its belly, and whatever walks on all fours, or whatever has many feet—all the swarming creatures that swarm on the ground—you shall not eat them, for they are detestable.
11:43	You shall not make yourselves detestable with any swarming creature that swarms, and you shall not defile yourselves with them and so become unclean through them.
11:44	For I am Yahweh your God. Consecrate yourselves therefore, and be holy, for I am holy. You shall not defile yourselves with any swarming creature that creeps on the ground.
11:45	For I am Yahweh Who brought you up out of the land of Egypt to be your God; you shall be holy, for I am holy.
11:46	This is the law concerning animals and birds and every living creature that moves in the waters and every creature that swarms on the ground,
11:47	to make a distinction between the unclean and the clean, and between the creature that may be eaten and the creature that may not be eaten."
