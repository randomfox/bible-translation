5:1	"If anyone sins in that he hears a public adjuration to testify, and he is a witness, whether he has seen or come to know the matter, yet does not speak up, he shall bear his iniquity.
5:2	Or if anyone touches anything unclean—whether the carcass of an unclean wild animal, or the carcass of unclean livestock, or the carcass of an unclean swarming creature—and it is hidden from him, he has become unclean and is guilty.
5:3	Or if he touches human uncleanness, any uncleanness by which one becomes unclean, and it is hidden from him, and then he comes to know it, he is guilty.
5:4	Or if anyone rashly swears with his lips to do harm or to do good—any matter about which a person may swear rashly—and it is hidden from him, and then he comes to know it, he is guilty in any of these.
5:5	When he becomes guilty in any of these, he shall confess the sin he has committed,
5:6	and he shall bring to Yahweh his penalty for the sin he has committed: a female from the flock, a lamb or a goat, as a sin offering; and the priest shall make atonement for him for his sin.
5:7	But if he cannot afford a sheep, he shall bring to Yahweh as his penalty for the sin he has committed two turtledoves or two young pigeons, one for a sin offering and the other for a burnt offering.
5:8	He shall bring them to the priest, who shall first offer the one for the sin offering; he shall wring its head at the back of its neck but not sever it.
5:9	He shall sprinkle some of the blood of the sin offering on the side of the altar, and the rest of the blood shall be drained out at the base of the altar; it is a sin offering.
5:10	The second he shall offer as a burnt offering according to the regulation. So the priest shall make atonement for him for the sin he has committed, and he will be forgiven.
5:11	But if he cannot afford two turtledoves or two young pigeons, he shall bring as his offering for the sin he has committed a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a sin offering. He shall put no oil on it and place no frankincense on it, for it is a sin offering.
5:12	He shall bring it to the priest, and the priest shall take a handful of it as its memorial portion and burn it on the altar on top of Yahweh's food offerings; it is a sin offering.
5:13	So the priest shall make atonement for him for the sin he has committed in any of these, and he will be forgiven. The rest shall belong to the priest, as with the grain offering."
5:14	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
5:15	"If anyone commits a breach of faith and sins unintentionally in regard to Yahweh's holy things, he shall bring to Yahweh as his guilt offering a ram without blemish from the flock, valued by you in silver shekels by the sanctuary shekel, as a guilt offering.
5:16	He shall make restitution for what he has sinned against the holy thing, adding a fifth to it, and give it to the priest; and the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering, and he will be forgiven.
5:17	If anyone sins and does any of Yahweh's commandments about things that must not be done, though he does not know it, he is guilty and shall bear his iniquity.
5:18	He shall bring to the priest a ram without blemish from the flock, at your valuation, as a guilt offering, and the priest shall make atonement for him for the error he committed unintentionally without knowing it, and he will be forgiven.
5:19	It is a guilt offering; he is indeed guilty before Yahweh."
5:20	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
5:21	"If anyone sins and commits a breach of faith against Yahweh by deceiving his neighbor about a deposit or a pledge, or by robbery, or by extorting from his neighbor,
5:22	or has found something lost and lied about it, and swears falsely—in any of all the things people do and sin thereby—
5:23	then when he has sinned and is guilty, he shall return what he took by robbery, or what he got by extortion, or the deposit that was entrusted to him, or the lost thing that he found,
5:24	or anything about which he swore falsely. He shall repay it in full and add a fifth to it, and give it to the one to whom it belongs on the day he is found guilty.
5:25	And he shall bring to the priest his guilt offering to Yahweh: a ram without blemish from the flock, at your valuation, as a guilt offering.
5:26	The priest shall make atonement for him before Yahweh, and he will be forgiven for any of all the things that one may do and so incur guilt."
