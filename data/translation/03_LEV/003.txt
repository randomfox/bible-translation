3:1	"If his offering is a sacrifice of peace offering, if he offers from the herd, whether male or female, he shall offer one without blemish before Yahweh.
3:2	He shall lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and Aaron's sons the priests shall splash the blood against all sides of the altar.
3:3	From the sacrifice of peace offering he shall present as a food offering to Yahweh the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails,
3:4	the two kidneys with the fat that is on them at the loins, and the lobe on the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.
3:5	Aaron's sons shall burn it on the altar on top of the burnt offering that is on the wood on the fire, a food offering, a soothing aroma to Yahweh.
3:6	If his offering for a sacrifice of peace offering to Yahweh is from the flock, male or female, he shall offer one without blemish.
3:7	If he offers a lamb as his offering, he shall present it before Yahweh.
3:8	He shall lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it in front of the tent of meeting, and Aaron's sons shall splash its blood against all sides of the altar.
3:9	From the sacrifice of peace offering he shall present as a food offering to Yahweh its fat: the whole fat tail, which he shall remove close to the backbone, the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails,
3:10	the two kidneys with the fat that is on them at the loins, and the lobe on the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.
3:11	The priest shall burn it on the altar as food, a food offering to Yahweh.
3:12	If his offering is a goat, he shall present it before Yahweh.
3:13	He shall lay his hand on its head and slaughter it in front of the tent of meeting, and Aaron's sons shall splash its blood against all sides of the altar.
3:14	From it he shall present his offering as a food offering to Yahweh: the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails,
3:15	the two kidneys with the fat that is on them at the loins, and the lobe on the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys.
3:16	The priest shall burn them on the altar as food, a food offering for a soothing aroma. All fat belongs to Yahweh.
3:17	It is a perpetual statute throughout your generations, in all your dwellings: you shall not eat any fat or any blood."
