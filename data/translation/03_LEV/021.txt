21:1	Yahweh said to Moses, "Speak to the priests, the sons of Aaron, and say to them: None of them shall make himself unclean for a dead person among his people,
21:2	except for his closest relatives: his mother, his father, his son, his daughter, his brother,
21:3	or his virgin sister, who is close to him because she has not had a husband; for her he may make himself unclean.
21:4	He shall not make himself unclean as a husband among his people, and so profane himself.
21:5	They shall not make bald patches on their heads, or shave off the edges of their beards, or make any cuts on their bodies.
21:6	They shall be holy to their God and not profane the name of their God, for they offer Yahweh's food offerings, the food of their God; therefore they shall be holy.
21:7	They shall not marry a woman who is a prostitute or who has been defiled, nor shall they marry a woman divorced from her husband, for the priest is holy to his God.
21:8	You shall consecrate him, for he offers the food of your God; he shall be holy to you, for I, Yahweh, who sanctify you, am holy.
21:9	And if the daughter of any priest profanes herself by prostitution, she profanes her father; she shall be burned with fire.
21:10	"The priest who is chief among his brothers, on whose head the anointing oil has been poured and who has been ordained to wear the garments, shall not let the hair of his head hang loose or tear his clothes.
21:11	He shall not go in to any dead body, nor make himself unclean even for his father or his mother.
21:12	He shall not go out of the sanctuary, or profane the sanctuary of his God, for the consecration of the anointing oil of his God is on him. I am Yahweh.
21:13	He shall take a wife in her virginity.
21:14	A widow, or a divorced woman, or one who has been defiled, a prostitute, these he shall not marry; but he shall take as his wife a virgin from his own people,
21:15	so that he does not profane his offspring among his people, for I am Yahweh who sanctifies him."
21:16	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
21:17	"Speak to Aaron, saying: None of your offspring throughout their generations who has a defect may approach to offer the food of his God.
21:18	For no one who has a defect shall approach: a man who is blind or lame, or has a disfigured face or a limb too long,
21:19	or a man who has a broken foot or a broken hand,
21:20	or a hunchback or a dwarf, or a man with a defect in his eye, or an itching disease or scabs, or crushed testicles.
21:21	No man of the offspring of Aaron the priest who has a defect shall come near to offer Yahweh's food offerings; since he has a defect, he shall not come near to offer the food of his God.
21:22	He may eat the food of his God, both of the most holy and of the holy things,
21:23	but he shall not go in to the veil or come near the altar, because he has a defect, so that he does not profane my sanctuaries; for I am Yahweh who sanctifies them."
21:24	So Moses spoke to Aaron and to his sons and to all the people of Israel.
