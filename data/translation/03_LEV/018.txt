18:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
18:2	"Speak to the people of Israel and say to them: I am Yahweh your God.
18:3	You shall not do as they do in the land of Egypt, where you lived, and you shall not do as they do in the land of Canaan, to which I am bringing you. You shall not walk in their statutes.
18:4	You shall do My rules and keep My statutes, walking in them. I am Yahweh your God.
18:5	You shall keep My statutes and My rules; the person who does them shall live by them. I am Yahweh.
18:6	"None of you shall approach any close relative of his own flesh to uncover nakedness. I am Yahweh.
18:7	You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father, that is, the nakedness of your mother; she is your mother, you shall not uncover her nakedness.
18:8	You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's wife; it is your father's nakedness.
18:9	You shall not uncover the nakedness of your sister, your father's daughter or your mother's daughter, whether born in the household or born outside it.
18:10	You shall not uncover the nakedness of your son's daughter or your daughter's daughter, for their nakedness is your own nakedness.
18:11	You shall not uncover the nakedness of the daughter of your father's wife, born to your father; she is your sister.
18:12	You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's sister; she is your father's close relative.
18:13	You shall not uncover the nakedness of your mother's sister, for she is your mother's close relative.
18:14	You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's brother; you shall not approach his wife; she is your aunt.
18:15	You shall not uncover the nakedness of your daughter-in-law; she is your son's wife, you shall not uncover her nakedness.
18:16	You shall not uncover the nakedness of your brother's wife; it is your brother's nakedness.
18:17	You shall not uncover the nakedness of a woman and her daughter, and you shall not take her son's daughter or her daughter's daughter to uncover her nakedness; they are close relatives; it is depravity.
18:18	And you shall not take a woman as a rival wife to her sister, uncovering her nakedness while her sister is still alive.
18:19	"You shall not approach a woman to uncover her nakedness while she is in her menstrual impurity.
18:20	And you shall not lie sexually with your neighbor's wife and so defile yourself with her.
18:21	You shall not give any of your offspring to pass them over to Molech, and so profane the name of your God. I am Yahweh.
18:22	You shall not lie with a male as one lies with a woman; it is an abomination.
18:23	And you shall not lie with any animal and so defile yourself with it, nor shall a woman stand before an animal to mate with it; it is perversion.
18:24	"Do not defile yourselves by any of these things, for by all these the nations that I am driving out before you defiled themselves,
18:25	and the land became defiled, so that I punished its iniquity upon it, and the land vomited out its inhabitants.
18:26	But you shall keep My statutes and My rules and do none of these abominations, neither the native-born nor the foreigner who resides among you,
18:27	for the people of the land who were before you did all these abominations, and the land became defiled,
18:28	so that the land will not vomit you out when you defile it, as it vomited out the nation that was before you.
18:29	For whoever does any of these abominations, the persons who do them shall be cut off from among their people.
18:30	So keep My charge, not to practice any of the abominable customs that were practiced before you, and do not defile yourselves by them. I am Yahweh your God."
