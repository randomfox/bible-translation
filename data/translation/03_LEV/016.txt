16:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses after the death of Aaron's two sons, when they drew near before Yahweh and died.
16:2	Yahweh said to Moses, "Tell Aaron your brother that he must not come at just any time into the holy place inside the veil, before the mercy seat that is on the ark, so that he does not die; for I appear in the cloud over the mercy seat.
16:3	This is how Aaron shall come into the holy place: with a young bull for a sin offering and a ram for a burnt offering.
16:4	He shall put on the holy linen tunic, and linen undergarments shall be on his body, and he shall tie on the linen sash and wrap on the linen turban. These are holy garments; he shall bathe his body in water and then put them on.
16:5	And from the congregation of the people of Israel he shall take two male goats for a sin offering and one ram for a burnt offering.
16:6	Aaron shall present the bull as a sin offering for himself and make atonement for himself and for his household.
16:7	Then he shall take the two goats and set them before Yahweh at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
16:8	And Aaron shall cast lots over the two goats, one lot for Yahweh and the other lot for Azazel.
16:9	Aaron shall present the goat on which the lot for Yahweh fell and offer it as a sin offering,
16:10	but the goat on which the lot for Azazel fell shall be stationed alive before Yahweh to make atonement over it, to send it away into the wilderness to Azazel.
16:11	Aaron shall present the bull as a sin offering for himself, and make atonement for himself and for his household; he shall slaughter the bull as a sin offering for himself.
16:12	He shall take a censer full of coals of fire from the altar before Yahweh, and two handfuls of finely ground fragrant incense, and bring it inside the veil.
16:13	He shall put the incense on the fire before Yahweh, so that the cloud of the incense covers the mercy seat that is over the testimony, so that he does not die.
16:14	He shall take some of the blood of the bull and sprinkle it with his finger on the front of the mercy seat, on the east side; and in front of the mercy seat he shall sprinkle some of the blood with his finger seven times.
16:15	Then he shall slaughter the goat of the sin offering that is for the people and bring its blood inside the veil, and do with its blood as he did with the blood of the bull, sprinkling it on the mercy seat and in front of the mercy seat.
16:16	Thus he shall make atonement for the holy place because of the uncleannesses of the people of Israel and because of their transgressions, all their sins; and so he shall do for the tent of meeting, which dwells with them in the midst of their uncleannesses.
16:17	No one shall be in the tent of meeting from the time he enters to make atonement in the holy place until he comes out and has made atonement for himself and for his household and for all the assembly of Israel.
16:18	Then he shall go out to the altar that is before Yahweh and make atonement for it; he shall take some of the blood of the bull and some of the blood of the goat and put it on the horns of the altar all around.
16:19	He shall sprinkle some of the blood on it with his finger seven times, and cleanse it and consecrate it from the uncleannesses of the people of Israel.
16:20	When he has finished atoning for the holy place and the tent of meeting and the altar, he shall present the live goat.
16:21	Aaron shall lay both his hands on the head of the live goat and confess over it all the iniquities of the people of Israel and all their transgressions, all their sins; he shall put them on the head of the goat and send it away into the wilderness by the hand of a man appointed for the task.
16:22	The goat shall carry on itself all their iniquities to a cut-off land, and he shall release the goat in the wilderness.
16:23	Then Aaron shall come into the tent of meeting and take off the linen garments that he put on when he went into the holy place, and leave them there.
16:24	He shall bathe his body in water in a holy place and put on his garments, and come out and offer his burnt offering and the burnt offering of the people, and make atonement for himself and for the people.
16:25	And the fat of the sin offering he shall burn on the altar.
16:26	The man who sent away the goat for Azazel shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and after that he may come into the camp.
16:27	The bull of the sin offering and the goat of the sin offering, whose blood was brought in to make atonement in the holy place, shall be carried outside the camp, and their skins, their flesh, and their dung shall be burned with fire.
16:28	The one who burns them shall wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and after that he may come into the camp.
16:29	"This shall be a permanent statute for you: in the seventh month, on the tenth day of the month, you shall humble yourselves and do no work at all, neither the native-born nor the foreigner who resides among you.
16:30	For on this day atonement shall be made for you to cleanse you; from all your sins you shall be clean before Yahweh.
16:31	It is a sabbath of complete rest for you, and you shall humble yourselves; it is a permanent statute.
16:32	The priest who is anointed and ordained to serve as priest in his father's place shall make atonement, wearing the linen garments, the holy garments.
16:33	He shall make atonement for the holy sanctuary, and he shall make atonement for the tent of meeting and for the altar, and he shall make atonement for the priests and for all the people of the assembly.
16:34	This shall be a permanent statute for you, to make atonement for the people of Israel for all their sins once a year." And he did as Yahweh commanded Moses.
