19:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
19:2	"Speak to all the congregation of the people of Israel and say to them: You shall be holy, for I, Yahweh your God, am holy.
19:3	Each of you shall revere his mother and his father, and you shall keep My sabbaths. I am Yahweh your God.
19:4	Do not turn to worthless idols or make for yourselves gods of cast metal. I am Yahweh your God.
19:5	"When you sacrifice a peace offering to Yahweh, sacrifice it so that you may be accepted.
19:6	It shall be eaten on the day you sacrifice it, or on the next day, and whatever remains until the third day shall be burned with fire.
19:7	If it is eaten at all on the third day, it is tainted; it will not be accepted,
19:8	and whoever eats it shall bear his iniquity, because he has profaned what is holy to Yahweh; that person shall be cut off from his people.
19:9	"When you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not reap your field right up to its edge, nor shall you gather the gleanings of your harvest.
19:10	And you shall not strip your vineyard bare, nor gather the fallen grapes of your vineyard. You shall leave them for the poor and for the foreigner. I am Yahweh your God.
19:11	"You shall not steal; you shall not deal falsely; you shall not lie to one another.
19:12	You shall not swear by My name falsely, and so profane the name of your God. I am Yahweh.
19:13	"You shall not oppress your neighbor or rob him. The wages of a hired worker shall not remain with you all night until morning.
19:14	You shall not curse the deaf or put a stumbling block before the blind, but you shall fear your God. I am Yahweh.
19:15	"You shall do no injustice in judgment. You shall not be partial to the poor or defer to the great; in righteousness you shall judge your neighbor.
19:16	You shall not go around as a slanderer among your people; you shall not stand idly by when your neighbor's life is at stake. I am Yahweh.
19:17	"You shall not hate your brother in your heart; you shall surely rebuke your neighbor, so that you do not incur sin because of him.
19:18	You shall not take vengeance or bear a grudge against the members of your people, but you shall love your neighbor as yourself. I am Yahweh.
19:19	"You shall keep My statutes. You shall not let two kinds of your cattle breed together. You shall not sow your field with two kinds of seed, nor shall a garment of two kinds of material, of mixed fabric, come upon you.
19:20	"If a man lies sexually with a woman who is a slave, assigned to another man, and she has not been ransomed or given her freedom, there shall be an inquiry; they shall not be put to death, because she was not free.
19:21	He shall bring his guilt offering to Yahweh, to the entrance of the tent of meeting, a ram as a guilt offering.
19:22	And the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering before Yahweh for his sin that he has committed, and he shall be forgiven for the sin that he has committed.
19:23	"When you come into the land and plant any kind of tree for food, you shall regard its fruit as forbidden, uncircumcised. For three years it shall be forbidden to you, as uncircumcised; it shall not be eaten.
19:24	In the fourth year all its fruit shall be holy, an offering of praise to Yahweh.
19:25	And in the fifth year you may eat of its fruit, to increase its yield for you. I am Yahweh your God.
19:26	"You shall not eat anything with the blood. You shall not practice divination or soothsaying.
19:27	You shall not round off the hair at the sides of your head or mar the edges of your beard.
19:28	You shall not make any cuts on your body for the dead or put tattoo marks on yourselves. I am Yahweh.
19:29	"Do not profane your daughter by making her a prostitute, so that the land does not fall into prostitution and the land become full of depravity.
19:30	You shall keep My sabbaths and revere My sanctuary. I am Yahweh.
19:31	"Do not turn to the spirits of the dead or to the spiritists; do not seek them out, to be defiled by them. I am Yahweh your God.
19:32	"You shall stand up before the gray head and honor the face of an old man, and you shall fear your God. I am Yahweh.
19:33	"When a foreigner resides with you in your land, you shall not mistreat him.
19:34	The foreigner who resides with you shall be to you as the native-born among you, and you shall love him as yourself, for you were foreigners in the land of Egypt. I am Yahweh your God.
19:35	"You shall do no wrong in judgment, in measures of length, weight, or quantity.
19:36	You shall have just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin. I am Yahweh your God, Who brought you out of the land of Egypt.
19:37	And you shall keep all My statutes and all My rules, and do them. I am Yahweh."
