23:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
23:2	"Speak to the people of Israel and say to them: These are the appointed festivals of Yahweh, which you shall proclaim as holy assemblies; these are My appointed festivals.
23:3	Six days shall work be done, but on the seventh day is a sabbath of complete rest, a holy assembly. You shall do no work at all; it is a sabbath to Yahweh in all your dwelling places.
23:4	"These are the appointed festivals of Yahweh, the holy assemblies, which you shall proclaim at their appointed times.
23:5	In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, at twilight, is Yahweh's Passover.
23:6	And on the fifteenth day of the same month is the Festival of Unleavened Bread to Yahweh; for seven days you shall eat unleavened bread.
23:7	On the first day you shall have a holy assembly; you shall do no ordinary work.
23:8	For seven days you shall present a food offering to Yahweh. On the seventh day is a holy assembly; you shall do no ordinary work."
23:9	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
23:10	"Speak to the people of Israel and say to them: When you come into the land that I am giving you and reap its harvest, you shall bring the sheaf of the first portion of your harvest to the priest,
23:11	and he shall wave the sheaf before Yahweh, so that you may be accepted; on the day after the sabbath the priest shall wave it.
23:12	And on the day when you wave the sheaf, you shall offer a male lamb a year old without blemish as a burnt offering to Yahweh,
23:13	and its grain offering shall be two tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil, a food offering to Yahweh, a soothing aroma; and its drink offering shall be of wine, a quarter of a hin.
23:14	You shall eat no bread or roasted grain or fresh grain until this very day, until you have brought the offering of your God; it is a permanent statute throughout your generations in all your dwelling places.
23:15	"You shall count for yourselves from the day after the sabbath, from the day that you brought the sheaf of the wave offering: seven full weeks shall there be.
23:16	You shall count fifty days to the day after the seventh sabbath; then you shall present a new grain offering to Yahweh.
23:17	From your dwelling places you shall bring two loaves of bread for a wave offering; they shall be of two tenths of an ephah of fine flour, baked with leaven, as firstfruits to Yahweh.
23:18	Along with the bread you shall present seven lambs a year old without blemish, one young bull, and two rams; they shall be a burnt offering to Yahweh, with their grain offering and their drink offerings, a food offering with a soothing aroma to Yahweh.
23:19	And you shall offer one male goat as a sin offering, and two male lambs a year old as a sacrifice of peace offerings.
23:20	The priest shall wave them with the bread of the firstfruits as a wave offering before Yahweh, together with the two lambs; they shall be holy to Yahweh for the priest.
23:21	On that very day you shall make a proclamation; you shall have a holy assembly; you shall do no ordinary work. It is a permanent statute in all your dwelling places throughout your generations.
23:22	"And when you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not reap your field right up to its edge, nor shall you gather the gleanings of your harvest. You shall leave them for the poor and for the foreigner. I am Yahweh your God."
23:23	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
23:24	"Speak to the people of Israel, saying: In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, you shall have a day of complete rest, a memorial proclaimed with trumpet blasts, a holy assembly.
23:25	You shall do no ordinary work, and you shall present a food offering to Yahweh."
23:26	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
23:27	"But on the tenth day of this seventh month is the Day of Atonement. It shall be a holy assembly for you, and you shall humble yourselves and present a food offering to Yahweh.
23:28	You shall do no work on that very day, for it is a Day of Atonement, to make atonement for you before Yahweh your God.
23:29	For any person who does not humble himself on that very day shall be cut off from his people.
23:30	And any person who does any work on that very day, that person I will destroy from among his people.
23:31	You shall do no work at all. It is a permanent statute throughout your generations in all your dwelling places.
23:32	It shall be to you a sabbath of complete rest, and you shall humble yourselves; on the ninth day of the month in the evening, from evening to evening, you shall keep your sabbath."
23:33	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
23:34	"Speak to the people of Israel, saying: On the fifteenth day of this seventh month is the Festival of Booths, for seven days, to Yahweh.
23:35	On the first day is a holy assembly; you shall do no ordinary work.
23:36	For seven days you shall present food offerings to Yahweh. On the eighth day you shall have a holy assembly and present a food offering to Yahweh; it is a solemn closing assembly; you shall do no ordinary work.
23:37	"These are the appointed festivals of Yahweh, which you shall proclaim as holy assemblies, for presenting food offerings to Yahweh, burnt offerings and grain offerings, sacrifices and drink offerings, each on its own day,
23:38	besides the sabbaths of Yahweh, and besides your gifts, and besides all your vows, and besides all your freewill offerings, which you give to Yahweh.
23:39	"But on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when you have gathered in the produce of the land, you shall celebrate the festival of Yahweh for seven days. On the first day shall be a complete rest, and on the eighth day shall be a complete rest.
23:40	On the first day you shall take for yourselves the fruit of splendid trees, branches of palm trees, boughs of leafy trees, and willows of the brook, and you shall rejoice before Yahweh your God for seven days.
23:41	You shall celebrate it as a festival to Yahweh for seven days in the year. It is a permanent statute throughout your generations; you shall celebrate it in the seventh month.
23:42	You shall live in booths for seven days; all native-born Israelites shall live in booths,
23:43	so that your generations may know that I made the people of Israel live in booths when I brought them out of the land of Egypt. I am Yahweh your God."
23:44	So Moses declared the appointed festivals of Yahweh to the people of Israel.
