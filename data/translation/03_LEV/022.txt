22:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
22:2	"Tell Aaron and his sons to be careful with the holy things of the people of Israel, which they dedicate to Me, so that they do not profane My holy name. I am Yahweh.
22:3	Say to them: Throughout your generations, any man of all your offspring who approaches the holy things that the people of Israel dedicate to Yahweh while he is unclean, that person shall be cut off from My presence. I am Yahweh.
22:4	No man of the offspring of Aaron who has a skin disease or a discharge may eat of the holy things until he is clean. Whoever touches anything made unclean by a corpse, or a man who has had an emission of semen,
22:5	or a man who touches any swarming creature by which he is made unclean, or a person from whom he becomes unclean, whatever his uncleanness may be,
22:6	the person who touches any such thing shall be unclean until evening and shall not eat of the holy things unless he has bathed his body in water.
22:7	When the sun goes down he shall be clean, and afterward he may eat of the holy things, because they are his food.
22:8	He shall not eat an animal that died of itself or was torn by beasts, and so become unclean by it. I am Yahweh.
22:9	They shall therefore keep My charge, so that they do not incur sin for it and die by it when they profane it. I am Yahweh Who sanctifies them.
22:10	"No outsider shall eat of a holy thing; no resident alien of a priest's household or hired worker shall eat of a holy thing.
22:11	But if a priest buys a person as his property for money, that one may eat of it, and those born in his house may eat of his food.
22:12	If a priest's daughter is married to an outsider, she shall not eat of the contribution of the holy things.
22:13	But if a priest's daughter is widowed or divorced and has no child, and returns to her father's house as in her youth, she may eat of her father's food; but no outsider shall eat of it.
22:14	And if anyone eats a holy thing unintentionally, he shall add a fifth of its value to it and give the holy thing to the priest.
22:15	They shall not profane the holy things of the people of Israel, which they contribute to Yahweh,
22:16	and so cause them to bear iniquity requiring a guilt offering by eating their holy things; for I am Yahweh Who sanctifies them."
22:17	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
22:18	"Speak to Aaron and his sons and to all the people of Israel and say to them: When anyone of the house of Israel or of the foreigners in Israel presents his offering, for any of their vows or any of their freewill offerings that they present to Yahweh as a burnt offering,
22:19	if it is to be accepted for you, it must be a male without blemish, from the cattle, the sheep, or the goats.
22:20	You shall not offer anything that has a defect, for it will not be accepted for you.
22:21	And when anyone presents a sacrifice of peace offerings to Yahweh to fulfill a vow or as a freewill offering, from the herd or from the flock, it must be perfect to be accepted; there shall be no defect in it.
22:22	Anything blind or injured or maimed, or having a running sore or an itch or scabs, you shall not offer to Yahweh or give any of them as a food offering on the altar to Yahweh.
22:23	An ox or a sheep that has a limb too long or too short you may present as a freewill offering, but for a vow it will not be accepted.
22:24	Any animal with its testicles bruised or crushed or torn or cut you shall not offer to Yahweh; you shall not do this in your land.
22:25	Nor shall you offer as the food of your God any such animals obtained from a foreigner, for their mutilation is in them; they have a defect; they will not be accepted for you."
22:26	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
22:27	"When an ox or a sheep or a goat is born, it shall remain seven days with its mother, and from the eighth day on it shall be acceptable as an offering, a food offering to Yahweh.
22:28	But you shall not slaughter an ox or a sheep and its young on the same day.
22:29	When you sacrifice a thanksgiving sacrifice to Yahweh, sacrifice it so that you may be accepted.
22:30	It shall be eaten on the same day; you shall leave none of it until morning. I am Yahweh.
22:31	"So you shall keep My commandments and do them. I am Yahweh.
22:32	You shall not profane My holy name, but I will be sanctified among the people of Israel. I am Yahweh Who sanctifies you,
22:33	Who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God. I am Yahweh."
