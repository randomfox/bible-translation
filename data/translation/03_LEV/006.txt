6:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
6:2	"Command Aaron and his sons, saying: This is the law of the burnt offering. The burnt offering shall remain on the hearth on the altar all night until the morning, and the fire of the altar shall be kept burning on it.
6:3	The priest shall put on his linen garment and put linen undergarments on his body, and he shall take up the ashes to which the fire has reduced the burnt offering on the altar and put them beside the altar.
6:4	Then he shall take off his garments and put on other garments, and carry the ashes outside the camp to a clean place.
6:5	The fire on the altar shall be kept burning on it; it shall not go out. The priest shall burn wood on it every morning, arrange the burnt offering on it, and burn on it the fat of the peace offerings.
6:6	A fire shall be kept burning on the altar continually; it shall not go out.
6:7	This is the law of the grain offering. The sons of Aaron shall present it before Yahweh in front of the altar.
6:8	One of them shall take from it a handful of the fine flour of the grain offering and its oil, with all the frankincense that is on the grain offering, and burn it on the altar as a soothing aroma, its memorial portion to Yahweh.
6:9	What is left of it Aaron and his sons shall eat. It shall be eaten unleavened in a holy place; they shall eat it in the courtyard of the tent of meeting.
6:10	It shall not be baked with leaven. I have given it as their portion from my food offerings; it is most holy, like the sin offering and the guilt offering.
6:11	Every male among the descendants of Aaron may eat it, as a perpetual due throughout your generations from Yahweh's food offerings. Whatever touches them will become holy."
6:12	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
6:13	"This is the offering that Aaron and his sons shall present to Yahweh on the day he is anointed: a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a regular grain offering, half of it in the morning and half in the evening.
6:14	It shall be made with oil on a griddle; you shall bring it well soaked, and present it as a grain offering of baked pieces, a soothing aroma to Yahweh.
6:15	The priest from among his sons who is anointed to succeed him shall prepare it. It is Yahweh's perpetual due; it shall be wholly burned.
6:16	Every grain offering of a priest shall be wholly burned; it shall not be eaten."
6:17	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
6:18	"Speak to Aaron and his sons, saying: This is the law of the sin offering. In the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered, the sin offering shall be slaughtered before Yahweh; it is most holy.
6:19	The priest who offers it for sin shall eat it. It shall be eaten in a holy place, in the courtyard of the tent of meeting.
6:20	Whatever touches its flesh will become holy, and if any of its blood is spattered on a garment, you shall wash the spattered part in a holy place.
6:21	An earthenware vessel in which it is boiled shall be broken; but if it is boiled in a bronze vessel, that shall be scoured and rinsed with water.
6:22	Every male among the priests may eat it; it is most holy.
6:23	But no sin offering shall be eaten from which any blood is brought into the tent of meeting to make atonement in the sanctuary; it shall be burned with fire."
