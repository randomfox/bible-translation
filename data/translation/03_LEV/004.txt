4:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
4:2	"Speak to the Israelites, saying: When anyone sins unintentionally against any of Yahweh's commandments about things that must not be done, and does any one of them—
4:3	if it is the anointed priest who sins, bringing guilt on the people, then he shall offer to Yahweh for the sin he has committed a young bull without blemish as a sin offering.
4:4	He shall bring the bull to the entrance of the tent of meeting before Yahweh, lay his hand on the bull's head, and slaughter the bull before Yahweh.
4:5	The anointed priest shall take some of the bull's blood and bring it into the tent of meeting.
4:6	The priest shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle some of the blood seven times before Yahweh, in front of the veil of the sanctuary.
4:7	The priest shall put some of the blood on the horns of the altar of fragrant incense before Yahweh, which is in the tent of meeting, and all the rest of the bull's blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of burnt offering, which is at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
4:8	All the fat of the bull of the sin offering he shall remove from it: the fat that covers the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails,
4:9	the two kidneys with the fat that is on them at the loins, and the lobe on the liver, which he shall remove with the kidneys,
4:10	just as these are removed from the ox of the sacrifice of peace offering; and the priest shall burn them on the altar of burnt offering.
4:11	But the hide of the bull and all its flesh, with its head and its legs, its entrails and its dung—
4:12	all the rest of the bull—he shall carry outside the camp to a clean place, to the ash heap, and burn it on a wood fire; it shall be burned on the ash heap.
4:13	If the whole congregation of Israel errs unintentionally and the matter is hidden from the eyes of the assembly, and they do any one of Yahweh's commandments about things that must not be done, and they become guilty,
4:14	when the sin they have committed becomes known, the assembly shall offer a young bull as a sin offering and bring it before the tent of meeting.
4:15	The elders of the congregation shall lay their hands on the bull's head before Yahweh, and the bull shall be slaughtered before Yahweh.
4:16	The anointed priest shall bring some of the bull's blood into the tent of meeting,
4:17	and the priest shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle it seven times before Yahweh, in front of the veil.
4:18	He shall put some of the blood on the horns of the altar that is before Yahweh in the tent of meeting, and all the rest of the blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of burnt offering, which is at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
4:19	All its fat he shall remove from it and burn on the altar.
4:20	He shall do with the bull just as he did with the bull of the sin offering; so he shall do with it. The priest shall make atonement for them, and they will be forgiven.
4:21	He shall carry the bull outside the camp and burn it as he burned the first bull; it is the sin offering for the assembly.
4:22	When a leader sins and unintentionally does any one of the commandments of Yahweh his God about things that must not be done, and becomes guilty,
4:23	or the sin he has committed is made known to him, he shall bring as his offering a male goat without blemish.
4:24	He shall lay his hand on the goat's head and slaughter it at the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered before Yahweh; it is a sin offering.
4:25	The priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and pour out the rest of its blood at the base of the altar of burnt offering.
4:26	All its fat he shall burn on the altar, like the fat of the sacrifice of peace offering. So the priest shall make atonement for him for his sin, and he will be forgiven.
4:27	If any one of the common people sins unintentionally by doing any one of Yahweh's commandments about things that must not be done, and becomes guilty,
4:28	or his sin that he has committed is made known to him, he shall bring as his offering a female goat without blemish for the sin he has committed.
4:29	He shall lay his hand on the head of the sin offering and slaughter the sin offering at the place of the burnt offering.
4:30	The priest shall take some of its blood with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and all the rest of its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar.
4:31	All its fat he shall remove, just as the fat is removed from the sacrifice of peace offering, and the priest shall burn it on the altar as a soothing aroma to Yahweh. So the priest shall make atonement for him, and he will be forgiven.
4:32	If he brings a lamb as his offering for a sin offering, he shall bring a female without blemish.
4:33	He shall lay his hand on the head of the sin offering and slaughter it as a sin offering at the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered.
4:34	The priest shall take some of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and all the rest of its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar.
4:35	All its fat he shall remove, just as the fat of the lamb is removed from the sacrifice of peace offering, and the priest shall burn them on the altar on top of Yahweh's food offerings. So the priest shall make atonement for him for the sin he has committed, and he will be forgiven.
