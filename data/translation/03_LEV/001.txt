1:1	Yahweh called to Moses and spoke to him from the tent of meeting, saying,
1:2	"Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When any of you brings an offering to Yahweh, you shall bring your offering from the livestock, from the herd or from the flock.
1:3	If his offering is a burnt offering from the herd, he shall offer a male without blemish; he shall bring it to the entrance of the tent of meeting, so that he may be accepted before Yahweh.
1:4	He shall lay his hand on the head of the burnt offering, and it will be accepted on his behalf to make atonement for him.
1:5	He shall slaughter the bull before Yahweh, and Aaron's sons the priests shall present the blood and splash the blood against all sides of the altar that is at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
1:6	He shall skin the burnt offering and cut it into its pieces.
1:7	The sons of Aaron the priest shall put fire on the altar and arrange wood on the fire.
1:8	Aaron's sons the priests shall arrange the pieces, the head, and the fat on the wood that is on the fire on the altar.
1:9	Its entrails and its legs he shall wash with water, and the priest shall burn all of it on the altar as a burnt offering, a food offering, a soothing aroma to Yahweh.
1:10	If his offering for a burnt offering is from the flock, from the sheep or from the goats, he shall offer a male without blemish.
1:11	He shall slaughter it on the north side of the altar before Yahweh, and Aaron's sons the priests shall splash its blood against all sides of the altar.
1:12	He shall cut it into its pieces, with its head and its fat, and the priest shall arrange them on the wood that is on the fire on the altar.
1:13	The entrails and the legs he shall wash with water, and the priest shall offer all of it and burn it on the altar; it is a burnt offering, a food offering, a soothing aroma to Yahweh.
1:14	If his offering to Yahweh is a burnt offering of birds, he shall bring his offering from the turtledoves or from the young pigeons.
1:15	The priest shall bring it to the altar, wring off its head, and burn it on the altar; and its blood shall be drained out against the side of the altar.
1:16	He shall remove its crop with its contents and throw it beside the altar on the east side, in the place for the ashes.
1:17	He shall tear it open by its wings without severing it, and the priest shall burn it on the altar, on the wood that is on the fire; it is a burnt offering, a food offering, a soothing aroma to Yahweh.
