10:1	Now Aaron's sons Nadab and Abihu each took his firepan, put fire in it, and laid incense on it, and they offered before Yahweh unauthorized fire, which He had not commanded them.
10:2	Fire came out from before Yahweh and consumed them, and they died before Yahweh.
10:3	Then Moses said to Aaron, "This is what Yahweh spoke: 'Among those who are near Me I will show Myself holy, and before all the people I will be glorified.'" And Aaron was silent.
10:4	Moses called Mishael and Elzaphan, the sons of Uzziel, Aaron's uncle, and said to them, "Come here; carry your kinsmen away from the front of the sanctuary to outside the camp."
10:5	They came near and carried them in their tunics outside the camp, as Moses had said.
10:6	Moses said to Aaron and to his sons Eleazar and Ithamar, "Do not let your hair hang loose or tear your garments, or you will die and He will be angry with the whole congregation. But your kinsmen, the whole house of Israel, may weep over the burning that Yahweh has kindled.
10:7	Do not go out from the entrance of the tent of meeting, or you will die, for Yahweh's anointing oil is on you." And they did as Moses said.
10:8	Yahweh spoke to Aaron, saying,
10:9	"Do not drink wine or beer, you or your sons with you, when you enter the tent of meeting, so that you do not die. It is a perpetual statute throughout your generations,
10:10	so that you may distinguish between the holy and the common, and between the unclean and the clean,
10:11	and so that you may teach the Israelites all the statutes that Yahweh has spoken to them through Moses."
10:12	Moses spoke to Aaron and to his remaining sons, Eleazar and Ithamar: "Take the grain offering that is left from Yahweh's food offerings and eat it unleavened beside the altar, for it is most holy.
10:13	You shall eat it in a holy place, because it is your due and your sons' due from Yahweh's food offerings; for so I have been commanded.
10:14	But the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution you may eat in any clean place, you and your sons and your daughters with you; for they have been given as your due and your sons' due from the sacrifices of the Israelites' peace offerings.
10:15	The thigh of the contribution and the breast of the wave offering they shall bring with the food offerings of the fat portions, to wave as a wave offering before Yahweh; and they shall be yours and your sons' with you as a perpetual due, as Yahweh has commanded."
10:16	Then Moses inquired diligently about the goat of the sin offering, and found that it had been burned. He was angry with Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron's remaining sons, and said,
10:17	"Why did you not eat the sin offering in the holy place? For it is most holy, and He has given it to you to bear the iniquity of the congregation, to make atonement for them before Yahweh.
10:18	Look, its blood was not brought into the inner part of the sanctuary. You certainly should have eaten it in the sanctuary, as I commanded."
10:19	Aaron said to Moses, "See, today they offered their sin offering and their burnt offering before Yahweh, and such things as these have happened to me. If I had eaten a sin offering today, would it have been pleasing in Yahweh's sight?"
10:20	When Moses heard that, he was satisfied.
