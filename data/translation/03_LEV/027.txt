27:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
27:2	"Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When anyone makes a special vow to Yahweh involving the valuation of persons,
27:3	the valuation of a male from twenty to sixty years old shall be fifty shekels of silver, by the shekel of the sanctuary.
27:4	If the person is a female, the valuation shall be thirty shekels.
27:5	If the person is from five to twenty years old, the valuation shall be twenty shekels for a male and ten shekels for a female.
27:6	If the person is from a month to five years old, the valuation shall be five shekels of silver for a male, and for a female the valuation shall be three shekels of silver.
27:7	If the person is sixty years old or over, the valuation shall be fifteen shekels for a male and ten shekels for a female.
27:8	But if someone is too poor to pay the valuation, he shall be presented before the priest, and the priest shall set a value for him; the priest shall value him according to what the one who vowed can afford.
27:9	If the vow is an animal that may be offered as an offering to Yahweh, all of it that one gives to Yahweh is holy.
27:10	He shall not exchange it or substitute for it, good for bad or bad for good; and if he does substitute one animal for another, then both it and the substitute shall be holy.
27:11	If it is any unclean animal that may not be offered as an offering to Yahweh, then he shall present the animal before the priest,
27:12	and the priest shall value it as either good or bad; as the priest values it, so it shall be.
27:13	But if he wishes to redeem it, he shall add a fifth to the valuation.
27:14	When a man dedicates his house as holy to Yahweh, the priest shall value it as either good or bad; as the priest values it, so it shall stand.
27:15	And if the one who dedicates it wishes to redeem his house, he shall add a fifth to the valuation price, and it shall be his.
27:16	If a man dedicates to Yahweh part of the land that is his family property, the valuation shall be in proportion to its seed: a homer of barley seed at fifty shekels of silver.
27:17	If he dedicates his field from the year of jubilee, the valuation shall stand,
27:18	but if he dedicates his field after the jubilee, then the priest shall calculate the price according to the years that remain until the year of jubilee, and a deduction shall be made from the valuation.
27:19	And if he who dedicates the field wishes to redeem it, then he shall add a fifth to its valuation price, and it shall remain his.
27:20	But if he does not wish to redeem the field, or if he has sold the field to another man, it shall not be redeemed anymore.
27:21	But the field, when it is released in the jubilee, shall be holy to Yahweh, like a field that has been devoted; the priest shall be in possession of it.
27:22	If he dedicates to Yahweh a field that he has bought, which is not a part of his family property,
27:23	then the priest shall calculate the amount of the valuation for it up to the year of jubilee, and the man shall give the valuation on that day as a holy gift to Yahweh.
27:24	In the year of jubilee the field shall return to him from whom it was bought, to whom the land belongs as a family property.
27:25	Every valuation shall be by the shekel of the sanctuary: twenty gerahs shall make a shekel.
27:26	But a firstborn of animals, which as a firstborn belongs to Yahweh, no man may dedicate; whether ox or sheep, it is Yahweh's.
27:27	If it is an unclean animal, then he shall buy it back at the valuation and add a fifth to it; or, if it is not redeemed, it shall be sold at the valuation.
27:28	But no devoted thing that a man devotes to Yahweh, of anything that he has, whether man or beast or of his family land, shall be sold or redeemed; every devoted thing is most holy to Yahweh.
27:29	No one devoted, who is to be devoted to destruction from among mankind, shall be ransomed; he shall surely be put to death.
27:30	Every tithe of the land, whether of the seed of the land or of the fruit of the trees, is Yahweh's; it is holy to Yahweh.
27:31	If a man wishes to redeem some of his tithe, he shall add a fifth to it.
27:32	And every tithe of herds and flocks, every tenth animal of all that pass under the herdsman's staff, shall be holy to Yahweh.
27:33	One shall not inquire whether it is good or bad, nor shall he exchange it; and if he does exchange it, then both it and the substitute shall be holy; it shall not be redeemed."
27:34	These are the commandments that Yahweh commanded Moses for the Israelites on Mount Sinai.
