7:1	"This is the law of the guilt offering; it is most holy.
7:2	In the place where they slaughter the burnt offering they shall slaughter the guilt offering, and its blood shall be splashed against all sides of the altar.
7:3	All its fat shall be offered from it: the fat tail and the fat that covers the entrails,
7:4	the two kidneys with the fat that is on them at the loins, and the lobe on the liver, which shall be removed with the kidneys.
7:5	The priest shall burn them on the altar as a food offering to Yahweh; it is a guilt offering.
7:6	Every male among the priests may eat it; it shall be eaten in a holy place; it is most holy.
7:7	The guilt offering is like the sin offering; there is one law for them: it belongs to the priest who makes atonement with it.
7:8	As for the priest who offers anyone's burnt offering, the hide of the burnt offering that he has offered shall belong to that priest.
7:9	Every grain offering that is baked in an oven, and all that is prepared in a pan or on a griddle, shall belong to the priest who offers it.
7:10	And every grain offering, whether mixed with oil or dry, shall belong to all the sons of Aaron, to one as much as another.
7:11	This is the law of the sacrifice of peace offerings that one may offer to Yahweh.
7:12	If he offers it as a thanksgiving, then along with the sacrifice of thanksgiving he shall offer unleavened loaves mixed with oil, unleavened wafers smeared with oil, and loaves of fine flour well soaked and mixed with oil.
7:13	Along with loaves of leavened bread he shall present his offering with his sacrifice of peace offerings for thanksgiving.
7:14	From it he shall offer one of each kind of offering as a contribution to Yahweh; it shall belong to the priest who splashes the blood of the peace offerings.
7:15	The flesh of his sacrifice of peace offerings for thanksgiving shall be eaten on the day it is offered; he shall leave none of it until morning.
7:16	But if the sacrifice he offers is a vow or a freewill offering, it shall be eaten on the day he offers his sacrifice, and on the next day what is left of it may be eaten.
7:17	What remains of the flesh of the sacrifice on the third day shall be burned with fire.
7:18	If any of the flesh of his sacrifice of peace offerings is eaten on the third day, it will not be accepted; it will not be credited to the one who offered it. It is a desecrated thing, and the person who eats of it shall bear his iniquity.
7:19	Flesh that touches anything unclean shall not be eaten; it shall be burned with fire. As for other flesh, anyone who is clean may eat flesh.
7:20	But the person who eats flesh from the sacrifice of peace offerings belonging to Yahweh while his uncleanness is on him, that person shall be cut off from his people.
7:21	And if anyone touches anything unclean—human uncleanness, or an unclean animal, or any unclean detestable thing—and then eats some of the flesh of the sacrifice of peace offerings belonging to Yahweh, that person shall be cut off from his people."
7:22	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
7:23	"Speak to the Israelites, saying: You shall not eat any fat of ox, sheep, or goat.
7:24	The fat of an animal that died of itself and the fat of one torn by beasts may be used for any other purpose, but you must certainly not eat it.
7:25	For whoever eats the fat of an animal from which a food offering may be presented to Yahweh, the person who eats it shall be cut off from his people.
7:26	You shall not eat any blood, whether of bird or of animal, in any of your dwellings.
7:27	Any person who eats any blood, that person shall be cut off from his people."
7:28	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
7:29	"Speak to the Israelites, saying: Whoever offers his sacrifice of peace offerings to Yahweh shall bring his offering to Yahweh from his sacrifice of peace offerings.
7:30	His own hands shall bring Yahweh's food offerings: he shall bring the fat together with the breast, the breast to be waved as a wave offering before Yahweh.
7:31	The priest shall burn the fat on the altar, but the breast shall belong to Aaron and his sons.
7:32	The right thigh you shall give to the priest as a contribution from your sacrifices of peace offerings.
7:33	Whichever of Aaron's sons offers the blood of the peace offerings and the fat shall have the right thigh as his portion.
7:34	For I have taken from the Israelites, from their sacrifices of peace offerings, the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution, and have given them to Aaron the priest and his sons as a perpetual due from the Israelites.
7:35	This is the portion of Aaron and the portion of his sons from Yahweh's food offerings, from the day they were presented to serve Yahweh as priests,
7:36	which Yahweh commanded to be given them by the Israelites on the day he anointed them, a perpetual statute throughout their generations."
7:37	This is the law for the burnt offering, the grain offering, the sin offering, the guilt offering, the ordination offering, and the sacrifice of peace offerings,
7:38	which Yahweh commanded Moses on Mount Sinai on the day He commanded the Israelites to bring their offerings to Yahweh, in the wilderness of Sinai.
