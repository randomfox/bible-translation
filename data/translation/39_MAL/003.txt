3:1	"Look, I am sending My messenger, and he will clear the way before Me. Then suddenly the Lord Whom you are seeking will come to His temple; the messenger of the covenant, in Whom you delight—look, He is coming," says Yahweh of Armies.
3:2	But who can endure the day of His coming, and who can stand when He appears? For He will be like a refiner's fire and like launderers' soap.
3:3	He will sit as a refiner and purifier of silver; He will purify the sons of Levi and refine them like gold and silver, and they will present offerings to Yahweh in righteousness.
3:4	Then the offering of Judah and Jerusalem will be pleasing to Yahweh, as in the days of old and as in former years.
3:5	"I will draw near to you for judgment, and I will be quick to testify against the sorcerers, the adulterers, those who swear falsely, those who cheat the hired worker of his wages and oppress the widow and the orphan, and those who thrust aside the foreigner and do not fear Me," says Yahweh of Armies.
3:6	"For I, Yahweh, do not change; and you, children of Jacob, have not come to an end.
3:7	From the days of your ancestors you have turned aside from My statutes and have not kept them. Return to Me, and I will return to you," says Yahweh of Armies. But you say, "How shall we return?"
3:8	"Will a man rob God? Yet you are robbing Me. But you say, 'How have we robbed You?' In tithes and contributions.
3:9	You are cursed with a curse, for you are robbing Me—the whole nation of you.
3:10	Bring the whole tithe into the storehouse, so that there may be food in My house, and test Me in this," says Yahweh of Armies, "and see whether I will not open the windows of heaven for you and pour out on you blessing until there is no more need.
3:11	I will rebuke the devourer for you, so that it will not destroy the produce of your soil, and the vine in your field will not fail to bear," says Yahweh of Armies.
3:12	"Then all the nations will call you blessed, for you will be a delightful land," says Yahweh of Armies.
3:13	"Your words against Me have been harsh," says Yahweh. "Yet you say, 'What have we said against You?'
3:14	You have said, 'It is useless to serve God. What have we gained by keeping His charge and walking about in mourning before Yahweh of Armies?
3:15	So now we call the arrogant blessed; evildoers not only prosper, they test God and escape.'"
3:16	Then those who feared Yahweh spoke with one another, and Yahweh paid attention and heard, and a book of remembrance was written before Him for those who feared Yahweh and esteemed His name.
3:17	"They will be Mine," says Yahweh of Armies, "My treasured possession, on the day that I act, and I will spare them as a man spares his son who serves him.
3:18	Then you will again see the difference between the righteous and the wicked, between one who serves God and one who does not serve Him.
3:19	For look, the day is coming, burning like a furnace, when all the arrogant and all evildoers will be stubble; the day that is coming will set them ablaze," says Yahweh of Armies, "so that it will leave them neither root nor branch.
3:20	But for you who fear My name the sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its wings, and you will go out and leap like calves from the stall.
3:21	You will trample the wicked, for they will be ashes under the soles of your feet on the day that I act," says Yahweh of Armies.
3:22	"Remember the instruction of My servant Moses, the statutes and ordinances that I commanded him at Horeb for all Israel.
3:23	Look, I am sending you Elijah the prophet before the great and terrible day of Yahweh comes.
3:24	He will turn the hearts of fathers to their children and the hearts of children to their fathers, so that I do not come and strike the land with a ban of destruction."
