1:1	An oracle: the word of Yahweh to Israel through Malachi.
1:2	"I have loved you," says Yahweh. But you say, "How have You loved us?" "Was not Esau Jacob's brother?" declares Yahweh. "Yet I loved Jacob,
1:3	but Esau I hated. I have made his hill country a desolation and given his inheritance to the jackals of the wilderness."
1:4	If Edom says, "We have been shattered, but we will rebuild the ruins," this is what Yahweh of Armies says: "They may build, but I will tear down. They will be called the Wicked Territory, and the people with whom Yahweh is angry forever.
1:5	Your own eyes will see it, and you will say, 'Great is Yahweh, even beyond the borders of Israel!'"
1:6	"A son honors his father, and a slave his master. If I am a father, where is the honor due Me? And if I am a master, where is the reverence due Me?" says Yahweh of Armies to you priests who despise My name. But you say, "How have we despised Your name?"
1:7	"By offering defiled food on My altar." But you say, "How have we defiled You?" "By saying that the table of Yahweh is contemptible.
1:8	When you offer blind animals in sacrifice, is that not wrong? And when you offer the lame and the sick, is that not wrong? Try presenting it to your governor! Would he be pleased with you or show you favor?" says Yahweh of Armies.
1:9	"Now then, implore God's favor, that He may be gracious to us! With such offerings from your hands, will He show favor to any of you?" says Yahweh of Armies.
1:10	"Oh, that one of you would shut the doors, so that you would not light fires on My altar for nothing! I take no pleasure in you," says Yahweh of Armies, "and I will not accept an offering from your hands.
1:11	For from the rising of the sun to its setting My name is great among the nations, and in every place incense is offered to My name, and a pure offering; for My name is great among the nations," says Yahweh of Armies.
1:12	"But you profane it by saying that the table of the Lord is defiled, and that its fruit, its food, is contemptible.
1:13	And you say, 'What a weariness this is!' and you sniff at it," says Yahweh of Armies. "You bring what was stolen, the lame and the sick, and you bring that as your offering! Should I accept it from your hands?" says Yahweh.
1:14	"Cursed is the cheat who has a male in his flock and vows it, but then sacrifices a blemished animal to the Lord. For I am a great King," says Yahweh of Armies, "and My name is feared among the nations."
