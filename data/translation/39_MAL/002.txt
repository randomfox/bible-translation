2:1	"And now, you priests, this command is for you.
2:2	If you will not listen, and if you will not take it to heart to give honor to my name," says Yahweh of Armies, "then I will send the curse on you and curse your blessings. Indeed, I have already cursed them, because you are not taking it to heart.
2:3	Look, I am going to rebuke your offspring, and I will spread dung on your faces, the dung of your festival sacrifices, and you will be carried off with it.
2:4	Then you will know that I sent this command to you so that my covenant with Levi may continue," says Yahweh of Armies.
2:5	"My covenant with him was one of life and peace, and I gave them to him; it called for reverence, and he revered me and stood in awe of my name.
2:6	True instruction was in his mouth, and no wrong was found on his lips. He walked with me in peace and uprightness, and he turned many away from iniquity.
2:7	For the lips of a priest should guard knowledge, and people should seek instruction from his mouth, for he is the messenger of Yahweh of Armies.
2:8	But you have turned aside from the way; you have caused many to stumble by your instruction; you have corrupted the covenant of Levi," says Yahweh of Armies.
2:9	"So I in turn have made you despised and humiliated before all the people, because you do not keep my ways but show partiality in your instruction."
2:10	Do we not all have one father? Did not one God create us? Why then are we faithless to one another, profaning the covenant of our ancestors?
2:11	Judah has been faithless, and an abomination has been committed in Israel and in Jerusalem; for Judah has profaned the sanctuary of Yahweh, which he loves, and has married the daughter of a foreign god.
2:12	May Yahweh cut off from the tents of Jacob any man who does this, whoever he may be, even one who brings an offering to Yahweh of Armies.
2:13	And this is a second thing you do: you cover the altar of Yahweh with tears, with weeping and groaning, because he no longer regards the offering or accepts it with favor from your hands.
2:14	You ask, "Why?" Because Yahweh was a witness between you and the wife of your youth, to whom you have been faithless, though she is your companion and your wife by covenant.
2:15	Did not the One make you, and is not a remnant of spirit his? And what was the One seeking? Godly offspring. So guard yourselves in your spirit, and let no one be faithless to the wife of his youth.
2:16	"For the man who hates and divorces," says Yahweh the God of Israel, "covers his garment with violence," says Yahweh of Armies. So guard yourselves in your spirit, and do not be faithless.
2:17	You have wearied Yahweh with your words. Yet you ask, "How have we wearied him?" By saying, "Everyone who does evil is good in the eyes of Yahweh, and he delights in them," or by asking, "Where is the God of justice?"
