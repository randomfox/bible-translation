22:1	You must not see your brother's ox or his sheep straying and ignore them; you shall surely take them back to your brother.
22:2	And if your brother does not live near you, or you do not know who he is, you shall bring it home to your house, and it shall stay with you until your brother comes looking for it; then you shall return it to him.
22:3	You shall do the same with his donkey, the same with his cloak, and the same with anything your brother loses and you find. You may not ignore it.
22:4	You must not see your brother's donkey or his ox fallen down on the road and ignore them; you shall surely help him lift them up.
22:5	A woman shall not wear a man's gear, nor shall a man put on a woman's garment, for whoever does these things is detestable to Yahweh your God.
22:6	If you happen to come across a bird's nest along the road, in any tree or on the ground, with young ones or eggs, and the mother sitting on the young or on the eggs, you must not take the mother with the young.
22:7	You shall surely let the mother go, but the young you may take for yourself, so that it may go well with you and you may prolong your days.
22:8	When you build a new house, you shall make a parapet for your roof, so that you do not bring bloodguilt on your house if anyone falls from it.
22:9	You must not sow your vineyard with two kinds of seed, or the whole yield will be forfeited, both the seed you have sown and the produce of the vineyard.
22:10	You must not plow with an ox and a donkey together.
22:11	You must not wear cloth of mixed fibers, wool and linen together.
22:12	You shall make tassels for yourself on the four corners of the garment with which you cover yourself.
22:13	If a man takes a wife and goes in to her, and then dislikes her,
22:14	and accuses her of shameful conduct and gives her a bad name, saying, "I took this woman, and when I came near her I did not find in her the evidence of virginity,"
22:15	then the girl's father and her mother shall take the evidence of the girl's virginity and bring it out to the elders of the city at the gate.
22:16	And the girl's father shall say to the elders, "I gave my daughter to this man as wife, and he dislikes her;
22:17	and now he has accused her of shameful conduct, saying, 'I did not find in your daughter the evidence of virginity.' But here is the evidence of my daughter's virginity." And they shall spread out the cloth before the elders of the city.
22:18	Then the elders of that city shall take the man and punish him,
22:19	and they shall fine him a hundred shekels of silver and give them to the girl's father, because he has given a virgin of Israel a bad name. She shall remain his wife; he may not divorce her as long as he lives.
22:20	But if the charge is true and evidence of virginity was not found for the girl,
22:21	then they shall bring the girl out to the entrance of her father's house, and the men of her city shall stone her with stones until she dies, because she has done an outrageous thing in Israel by acting like a prostitute in her father's house. So you shall purge the evil from among you.
22:22	If a man is found lying with a woman who is married to a husband, then both of them shall die, the man who lay with the woman and the woman. So you shall purge the evil from Israel.
22:23	If there is a young woman, a virgin, engaged to a man, and another man meets her in the city and lies with her,
22:24	you shall bring both of them out to the gate of that city and stone them with stones until they die: the young woman because she did not cry out in the city, and the man because he violated his neighbor's wife. So you shall purge the evil from among you.
22:25	But if the man meets the engaged young woman in the open country, and the man seizes her and lies with her, then only the man who lay with her shall die.
22:26	You shall do nothing to the young woman; the young woman has committed no sin worthy of death, for this case is like that of a man who rises against his neighbor and murders him.
22:27	For he met her in the open country; the engaged young woman cried out, but there was no one to rescue her.
22:28	If a man meets a young woman, a virgin who is not engaged, and seizes her and lies with her, and they are found,
22:29	then the man who lay with her shall give the girl's father fifty shekels of silver, and she shall become his wife, because he has violated her; he may not divorce her as long as he lives.
