14:1	You are children of Yahweh your God. You must not gash yourselves or shave a bald patch on your foreheads for the dead.
14:2	For you are a people holy to Yahweh your God, and Yahweh has chosen you to be His treasured people out of all the peoples on the face of the earth.
14:3	You must not eat anything detestable.
14:4	These are the animals you may eat: the ox, the sheep, and the goat,
14:5	the deer, the gazelle, the roebuck, the wild goat, the ibex, the antelope, and the mountain sheep.
14:6	Any animal that has a divided hoof, split into two parts, and that chews the cud among the animals, you may eat.
14:7	But of those that chew the cud or have the split, divided hoof you must not eat these: the camel, the hare, and the rock badger, because they chew the cud but do not have a divided hoof; they are unclean for you.
14:8	And the pig, because it has a divided hoof but does not chew the cud; it is unclean for you. You must not eat their meat or touch their carcasses.
14:9	Of all that live in the water you may eat these: whatever has fins and scales you may eat.
14:10	But whatever does not have fins and scales you must not eat; it is unclean for you.
14:11	Every clean bird you may eat.
14:12	But these are the ones you must not eat: the eagle, the bearded vulture, the black vulture,
14:13	the red kite, the falcon, and the kite of every kind,
14:14	every raven of every kind,
14:15	the ostrich, the nighthawk, the seagull, the hawk of every kind,
14:16	the little owl, the great owl, the barn owl,
14:17	the desert owl, the carrion vulture, the cormorant,
14:18	the stork, the heron of every kind, the hoopoe, and the bat.
14:19	And every winged swarming creature is unclean for you; they must not be eaten.
14:20	Every clean winged creature you may eat.
14:21	You must not eat anything that has died of itself. You may give it to the resident foreigner who is in your towns, and he may eat it, or you may sell it to a foreigner; for you are a people holy to Yahweh your God. You must not boil a young goat in its mother's milk.
14:22	You must surely set aside a tenth of all the yield of your seed that comes from the field year by year.
14:23	And you shall eat before Yahweh your God, in the place He chooses to make His name dwell, the tithe of your grain, your new wine, and your olive oil, and the firstborn of your herds and your flocks, so that you may learn to fear Yahweh your God always.
14:24	But if the journey is too long for you, so that you cannot carry it, because the place Yahweh your God chooses to set His name there is too far from you, when Yahweh your God blesses you,
14:25	then you shall turn it into money, wrap up the money in your hand, and go to the place Yahweh your God chooses.
14:26	Then you shall spend the money on whatever you desire: cattle, sheep, wine, beer, or whatever you crave. And you shall eat there before Yahweh your God and rejoice, you and your household.
14:27	And you must not neglect the Levite who is in your towns, for he has no portion or inheritance with you.
14:28	At the end of every three years you shall bring out the whole tithe of your produce in that year and store it in your towns.
14:29	And the Levite, because he has no portion or inheritance with you, and the resident foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow who are in your towns shall come and eat and be satisfied, so that Yahweh your God may bless you in all the work of your hands that you do.
