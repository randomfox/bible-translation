17:1	You must not sacrifice to Yahweh your God an ox or a sheep that has a defect, anything seriously wrong, for that is detestable to Yahweh your God.
17:2	If there is found among you, in any of your towns that Yahweh your God is giving you, a man or a woman who does what is evil in the eyes of Yahweh your God by transgressing His covenant,
17:3	and has gone and served other gods and bowed down to them, or to the sun or the moon or any of the host of heaven, which I have not commanded,
17:4	and it is reported to you and you hear of it, then you shall investigate thoroughly; and if it is true and the matter is established that this detestable thing has been done in Israel,
17:5	then you shall bring out to your gates that man or that woman who has done this evil thing, the man or the woman, and you shall stone them with stones until they die.
17:6	On the testimony of two witnesses or three witnesses the one who is to die shall be put to death; he shall not be put to death on the testimony of one witness.
17:7	The hand of the witnesses shall be the first against him to put him to death, and afterward the hand of all the people. So you shall purge the evil from among you.
17:8	If a case is too difficult for you to judge, between one kind of bloodshed and another, between one claim and another, or between one kind of injury and another, matters of dispute within your towns, then you shall arise and go up to the place that Yahweh your God chooses.
17:9	And you shall come to the Levitical priests and to the judge who is in office in those days, and you shall inquire, and they will declare to you the verdict.
17:10	Then you shall act according to the decision they declare to you from that place that Yahweh chooses, and you shall be careful to do all that they instruct you.
17:11	According to the instruction they teach you and according to the judgment they tell you, you shall act; you must not turn aside from the decision they declare to you, to the right or to the left.
17:12	The man who acts presumptuously by not listening to the priest who stands to minister there before Yahweh your God, or to the judge, that man shall die. So you shall purge the evil from Israel.
17:13	And all the people will hear and be afraid, and will not act presumptuously again.
17:14	When you come into the land that Yahweh your God is giving you, and you possess it and live in it, and you say, "I will set a king over me, like all the nations around me,"
17:15	you may indeed set over yourself a king whom Yahweh your God chooses. You shall set as king over yourself one from among your brothers; you may not put a foreigner over yourself, one who is not your brother.
17:16	Only he must not acquire many horses for himself or make the people return to Egypt in order to acquire many horses, since Yahweh has said to you, "You shall never again return that way."
17:17	And he must not acquire many wives for himself, so that his heart does not turn aside, nor shall he acquire for himself excessive silver and gold.
17:18	And when he sits on the throne of his kingdom, he shall write for himself a copy of this law in a book, from the one in the charge of the Levitical priests.
17:19	It shall be with him, and he shall read in it all the days of his life, so that he may learn to fear Yahweh his God by keeping all the words of this law and these statutes and doing them,
17:20	so that his heart may not be lifted up above his brothers and that he may not turn aside from the commandment to the right or to the left, so that he may prolong his days in his kingdom, he and his sons, in the midst of Israel.
