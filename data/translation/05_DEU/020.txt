20:1	When you go out to battle against your enemies and see horses and chariots, an army larger than yours, do not be afraid of them, for Yahweh your God, Who brought you up out of the land of Egypt, is with you.
20:2	When you draw near to the battle, the priest shall come forward and speak to the people.
20:3	He shall say to them, "Hear, O Israel: today you are drawing near to battle against your enemies. Do not let your heart be faint; do not be afraid, do not panic, and do not tremble before them,
20:4	for Yahweh your God is the one Who goes with you to fight for you against your enemies, to save you."
20:5	Then the officers shall speak to the people, saying, "Is there any man who has built a new house and has not dedicated it? Let him go back to his house, or he may die in the battle and another man dedicate it.
20:6	Is there any man who has planted a vineyard and has not begun to enjoy its fruit? Let him go back to his house, or he may die in the battle and another man enjoy its fruit.
20:7	Is there any man who is engaged to a woman and has not married her? Let him go back to his house, or he may die in the battle and another man marry her."
20:8	And the officers shall speak further to the people and say, "Is there any man who is afraid and fainthearted? Let him go back to his house, so that he does not make his brothers' hearts melt like his own."
20:9	When the officers have finished speaking to the people, they shall appoint commanders of troops at the head of the people.
20:10	When you draw near to a city to fight against it, offer it terms of peace.
20:11	If it responds to you peaceably and opens to you, then all the people found in it shall become forced laborers for you and serve you.
20:12	But if it does not make peace with you and makes war against you, then you shall besiege it.
20:13	And when Yahweh your God gives it into your hand, you shall strike all its males with the edge of the sword.
20:14	But the women, the little ones, the livestock, and everything in the city, all its plunder, you may take as spoil for yourself, and you may enjoy the spoil of your enemies, which Yahweh your God has given you.
20:15	So you shall do to all the cities that are very far from you, which are not cities of these nations here.
20:16	But in the cities of these peoples that Yahweh your God is giving you as an inheritance, you must not let anything that breathes remain alive.
20:17	You shall utterly devote them to destruction: the Hittites and the Amorites, the Canaanites and the Perizzites, the Hivites and the Jebusites, as Yahweh your God has commanded you,
20:18	so that they may not teach you to do all the detestable things they have done for their gods, and so you sin against Yahweh your God.
20:19	When you besiege a city for a long time, fighting against it to capture it, you must not destroy its trees by swinging an axe against them. You may eat from them, but you must not cut them down. Is the tree of the field a human being, that it should be besieged by you?
20:20	Only the trees that you know are not trees for food you may destroy and cut down, to build siegeworks against the city that is making war with you, until it falls.
