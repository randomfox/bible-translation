24:1	Suppose a man takes a wife and marries her, and then she does not find favor in his eyes because he has found something indecent in her, and he writes her a certificate of divorce, puts it in her hand, and sends her out of his house,
24:2	and she leaves his house and goes and becomes another man's wife,
24:3	and the second husband dislikes her, writes her a certificate of divorce, puts it in her hand, and sends her out of his house, or the second husband who took her as his wife dies;
24:4	then her first husband, who sent her away, may not take her again to be his wife after she has been defiled, for that is detestable before Yahweh. You must not bring sin on the land that Yahweh your God is giving you as an inheritance.
24:5	When a man takes a new wife, he shall not go out with the army or be charged with any duty; he shall be free at home for one year to bring joy to the wife he has taken.
24:6	No one shall take a pair of millstones, or even the upper millstone, in pledge, for that would be taking a life in pledge.
24:7	If a man is found kidnapping one of his brothers from the people of Israel, and treats him as a slave or sells him, that kidnapper shall die. So you shall purge the evil from among you.
24:8	Take care in a case of a skin disease to be very careful to do all that the Levitical priests instruct you; as I have commanded them, so you shall be careful to do.
24:9	Remember what Yahweh your God did to Miriam on the way when you came out of Egypt.
24:10	When you make your neighbor a loan of any kind, you must not go into his house to take his pledge.
24:11	You shall stand outside, and the man to whom you are making the loan shall bring the pledge out to you.
24:12	And if he is a poor man, you must not go to sleep with his pledge.
24:13	You shall surely return the pledge to him at sunset, so that he may sleep in his cloak and bless you; and it will be righteousness for you before Yahweh your God.
24:14	You must not oppress a hired worker who is poor and needy, whether one of your brothers or one of the resident foreigners who are in your land within your towns.
24:15	You shall give him his wages on the same day, before the sun sets, for he is poor and is counting on it; otherwise he will cry out to Yahweh against you, and it will be sin in you.
24:16	Fathers shall not be put to death for their children, nor shall children be put to death for their fathers; each one shall be put to death for his own sin.
24:17	You must not pervert the justice due to the resident foreigner or the fatherless, and you must not take a widow's garment in pledge.
24:18	You shall remember that you were a slave in Egypt and Yahweh your God redeemed you from there; that is why I am commanding you to do this.
24:19	When you reap your harvest in your field and forget a sheaf in the field, you must not go back to get it; it shall be for the resident foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow, so that Yahweh your God may bless you in all the work of your hands.
24:20	When you beat your olive trees, you must not go over the branches again; what remains shall be for the resident foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow.
24:21	When you gather the grapes of your vineyard, you must not glean it afterward; what remains shall be for the resident foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow.
24:22	You shall remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt; that is why I am commanding you to do this.
