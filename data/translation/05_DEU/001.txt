1:1	These are the words that Moses spoke to all Israel beyond the Jordan, in the wilderness, in the Arabah opposite Suph, between Paran and Tophel, Laban, Hazeroth, and Di-zahab.
1:2	It is eleven days' journey from Horeb by the Mount Seir road to Kadesh-barnea.
1:3	In the fortieth year, on the first day of the eleventh month, Moses spoke to the Israelites according to all that Yahweh had commanded him concerning them,
1:4	after he had defeated Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth, at Edrei.
1:5	Beyond the Jordan, in the land of Moab, Moses undertook to expound this law, saying:
1:6	"Yahweh our God spoke to us at Horeb: 'You have stayed long enough at this mountain.
1:7	Turn and set out, and go to the hill country of the Amorites and to all their neighbors in the Arabah, the hill country, the lowland, the Negev, and the seacoast, the land of the Canaanites, and Lebanon, as far as the great river, the river Euphrates.
1:8	See, I have set the land before you. Go in and take possession of the land that Yahweh swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give to them and to their descendants after them.'
1:9	At that time I said to you, 'I cannot carry you by myself.
1:10	Yahweh your God has multiplied you, and here you are today as numerous as the stars of the sky.
1:11	May Yahweh, the God of your fathers, make you a thousand times as many as you are and bless you, as He has promised you!
1:12	How can I bear by myself the weight and burden of you and your disputes?
1:13	Choose for yourselves wise, discerning, and experienced men from each of your tribes, and I will appoint them as your heads.'
1:14	You answered me, 'What you have proposed to do is good.'
1:15	So I took the heads of your tribes, wise and experienced men, and set them as heads over you: commanders of thousands, commanders of hundreds, commanders of fifties, commanders of tens, and officers for your tribes.
1:16	At that time I charged your judges: 'Hear the cases between your brothers, and judge rightly between a man and his brother or the resident alien with him.
1:17	You shall not show partiality in judgment; you shall hear the small and the great alike. You shall not be afraid of anyone, for the judgment belongs to God. Any case too hard for you, bring to me, and I will hear it.'
1:18	At that time I commanded you all the things that you should do.
1:19	Then we set out from Horeb and went through all that great and terrible wilderness that you saw, on the way to the hill country of the Amorites, as Yahweh our God commanded us, and we came to Kadesh-barnea.
1:20	I said to you, 'You have come to the hill country of the Amorites, which Yahweh our God is giving us.
1:21	See, Yahweh your God has set the land before you. Go up and take possession, as Yahweh, the God of your fathers, has told you. Do not fear or be dismayed.'
1:22	Then all of you came to me and said, 'Let us send men ahead of us to search out the land for us and bring us back word about the route by which we should go up and the cities we will come to.'
1:23	The proposal seemed good to me, and I took twelve men from among you, one man from each tribe.
1:24	They turned and went up into the hill country, came to the Valley of Eshcol, and spied it out.
1:25	They took some of the fruit of the land in their hands and brought it down to us, and they brought us back word and said, 'The land that Yahweh our God is giving us is good.'
1:26	Yet you were unwilling to go up, and you rebelled against the command of Yahweh your God.
1:27	You grumbled in your tents and said, 'Because Yahweh hates us He has brought us out of the land of Egypt, to hand us over to the Amorites to destroy us.
1:28	Where are we going up to? Our brothers have made our hearts melt, saying, "The people are greater and taller than we are; the cities are great and fortified up to the sky; and we even saw the Anakim there."'
1:29	Then I said to you, 'Do not be terrified or afraid of them.
1:30	Yahweh your God, Who goes before you, will Himself fight for you, just as He did for you in Egypt before your eyes,
1:31	and in the wilderness, where you saw how Yahweh your God carried you, as a man carries his son, all the way that you went until you came to this place.'
1:32	Yet in spite of this you did not trust Yahweh your God,
1:33	Who went before you on the way to seek out a place for you to camp, in fire by night to show you the way you should go, and in the cloud by day.
1:34	Yahweh heard the sound of your words and was angry, and He swore:
1:35	'Not one of these men of this evil generation shall see the good land that I swore to give to your fathers,
1:36	except Caleb son of Jephunneh. He shall see it, and to him and to his sons I will give the land on which he has trodden, because he has followed Yahweh wholeheartedly.'
1:37	Even with me Yahweh was angry on your account, and said, 'You also shall not go in there.
1:38	Joshua son of Nun, who stands before you, he shall go in there. Encourage him, for he will cause Israel to inherit it.
1:39	And your little ones, whom you said would become plunder, and your children, who today do not yet know good from bad, they shall go in there. To them I will give it, and they shall possess it.
1:40	But as for you, turn and set out for the wilderness by the way of the Red Sea.'
1:41	Then you answered me, 'We have sinned against Yahweh. We will go up and fight, just as Yahweh our God commanded us.' Every one of you strapped on his weapons and thought it easy to go up into the hill country.
1:42	But Yahweh said to me, 'Say to them: Do not go up and do not fight, for I am not among you; otherwise you will be defeated before your enemies.'
1:43	So I spoke to you, but you would not listen; you rebelled against the command of Yahweh, and in your presumption you went up into the hill country.
1:44	Then the Amorites who lived in that hill country came out against you and chased you as bees do, and they beat you down in Seir as far as Hormah.
1:45	You came back and wept before Yahweh, but Yahweh did not listen to your voice or give ear to you.
1:46	So you stayed at Kadesh many days, for as long as you stayed there.
