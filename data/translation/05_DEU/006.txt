6:1	"Now this is the commandment, the statutes and the rules, that Yahweh your God commanded me to teach you, so that you may do them in the land that you are crossing over to possess,
6:2	so that you may fear Yahweh your God by keeping all His statutes and His commandments that I command you, you and your son and your grandson, all the days of your life, and so that your days may be long.
6:3	Hear, therefore, Israel, and be careful to do them, so that it may go well with you and that you may multiply greatly, as Yahweh, the God of your fathers, has promised you, in a land flowing with milk and honey.
6:4	Hear, Israel: Yahweh our God, Yahweh is one.
6:5	You shall love Yahweh your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your strength.
6:6	These words that I command you today shall be on your heart.
6:7	Repeat them to your children and talk about them when you sit in your house and when you walk by the way, when you lie down and when you rise.
6:8	Bind them as a sign on your hand, and they shall be as emblems between your eyes.
6:9	Write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates.
6:10	When Yahweh your God brings you into the land that He swore to your fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give you, a land with great and good cities that you did not build,
6:11	houses full of all good things that you did not fill, cisterns hewn out that you did not hew, vineyards and olive trees that you did not plant, and when you eat and are satisfied,
6:12	take care that you do not forget Yahweh, Who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.
6:13	Yahweh your God you shall fear; Him you shall serve, and by His name you shall swear.
6:14	You shall not go after other gods, any of the gods of the peoples who are around you,
6:15	for Yahweh your God in your midst is a jealous God, lest the anger of Yahweh your God be kindled against you and He destroy you from the face of the earth.
6:16	You shall not put Yahweh your God to the test, as you tested Him at Massah.
6:17	You shall diligently keep the commandments of Yahweh your God, and His testimonies and His statutes, which He has commanded you.
6:18	Do what is right and good in the sight of Yahweh, so that it may go well with you and that you may go in and take possession of the good land that Yahweh swore to your fathers,
6:19	by thrusting out all your enemies before you, as Yahweh has promised.
6:20	When your son asks you in time to come, 'What do the testimonies, the statutes, and the rules mean that Yahweh our God has commanded you?'
6:21	then you shall say to your son, 'We were slaves of Pharaoh in Egypt, and Yahweh brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand.
6:22	Yahweh performed signs and wonders, great and grievous, against Egypt, against Pharaoh and all his household, before our eyes.
6:23	And He brought us out from there in order to bring us in and give us the land that He swore to our fathers.
6:24	Yahweh commanded us to do all these statutes, to fear Yahweh our God, for our good always, that He might keep us alive, as we are today.
6:25	And it will be righteousness for us, if we are careful to do all this commandment before Yahweh our God, as He has commanded us.'
