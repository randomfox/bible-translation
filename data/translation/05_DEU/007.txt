7:1	"When Yahweh your God brings you into the land that you are entering to possess, and clears away many nations before you, the Hittites, the Girgashites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, seven nations more numerous and mightier than you,
7:2	and when Yahweh your God hands them over to you and you defeat them, then you must devote them completely to destruction. You shall make no covenant with them and show them no mercy.
7:3	You shall not intermarry with them, giving your daughters to their sons or taking their daughters for your sons,
7:4	for they would turn your sons away from following me to serve other gods. Then the anger of Yahweh would be kindled against you, and he would quickly destroy you.
7:5	But this is how you shall deal with them: you shall tear down their altars, smash their standing stones, cut down their Asherah poles, and burn their carved images with fire.
7:6	For you are a people holy to Yahweh your God. Yahweh your God has chosen you out of all the peoples on the face of the earth to be a people for his treasured possession.
7:7	It was not because you were more numerous than all the peoples that Yahweh set his heart on you and chose you, for you were the fewest of all peoples.
7:8	It was because Yahweh loved you and kept the oath that he swore to your fathers that Yahweh brought you out with a mighty hand and redeemed you from the house of slavery, from the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt.
7:9	Know therefore that Yahweh your God is God, the faithful God who keeps covenant and steadfast love with those who love him and keep his commandments, to a thousand generations,
7:10	and who repays those who hate him to their face by destroying them. He will not delay with one who hates him; he will repay him to his face.
7:11	So keep the commandment, the statutes, and the rules that I command you today, by doing them.
7:12	And because you listen to these rules and keep and do them, Yahweh your God will keep with you the covenant and the steadfast love that he swore to your fathers.
7:13	He will love you, bless you, and multiply you. He will bless the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your ground, your grain, your new wine, and your oil, the offspring of your cattle and the young of your flock, in the land that he swore to your fathers to give you.
7:14	You will be blessed above all peoples. There will be no infertile man or woman among you, or among your livestock.
7:15	Yahweh will take away from you all sickness, and none of the evil diseases of Egypt that you knew will he inflict on you, but he will lay them on all who hate you.
7:16	You shall devour all the peoples that Yahweh your God is handing over to you. Your eye shall not pity them, and you shall not serve their gods, for that would be a snare to you.
7:17	If you say in your heart, 'These nations are more numerous than I; how can I dispossess them?'
7:18	you shall not be afraid of them. Remember well what Yahweh your God did to Pharaoh and to all Egypt,
7:19	the great trials that your eyes saw, the signs and the wonders, the mighty hand and the outstretched arm by which Yahweh your God brought you out. So will Yahweh your God do to all the peoples of whom you are afraid.
7:20	Moreover, Yahweh your God will send the hornet among them until those who are left and hide themselves from you are destroyed.
7:21	You shall not be terrified of them, for Yahweh your God is in your midst, a great and awesome God.
7:22	Yahweh your God will clear away these nations before you little by little. You will not be able to make an end of them quickly, or the wild animals would grow too numerous for you.
7:23	But Yahweh your God will hand them over to you and throw them into great confusion until they are destroyed.
7:24	He will hand their kings over to you, and you shall wipe out their name from under heaven. No one will be able to stand against you until you have destroyed them.
7:25	The carved images of their gods you shall burn with fire. You shall not covet the silver or the gold on them and take it for yourself, lest you be ensnared by it, for it is an abomination to Yahweh your God.
7:26	You shall not bring an abomination into your house, or you will become devoted to destruction like it. You shall utterly detest and abhor it, for it is devoted to destruction.
