21:1	If someone is found slain, lying in the open country in the land that Yahweh your God is giving you to possess, and it is not known who struck him down,
21:2	then your elders and your judges shall go out and measure the distance to the cities around the slain man.
21:3	And the city nearest to the slain man, the elders of that city shall take a heifer from the herd that has never been worked and has never pulled in a yoke.
21:4	The elders of that city shall bring the heifer down to a wadi with a perennial stream, in a place that is neither plowed nor sown, and there in the wadi they shall break the heifer's neck.
21:5	Then the priests, the sons of Levi, shall come forward, for Yahweh your God has chosen them to minister to him and to bless in the name of Yahweh, and by their word every dispute and every case of injury shall be settled.
21:6	And all the elders of that city nearest to the slain man shall wash their hands over the heifer whose neck was broken in the wadi,
21:7	and they shall declare, "Our hands did not shed this blood, and our eyes did not see it.
21:8	Make atonement, Yahweh, for your people Israel, whom you redeemed, and do not set the guilt of innocent blood in the midst of your people Israel." Then the bloodguilt will be atoned for them.
21:9	So you shall purge the innocent blood from among you, for you will do what is right in the eyes of Yahweh.
21:10	When you go out to battle against your enemies, and Yahweh your God gives them into your hand and you take them captive,
21:11	and you see among the captives a beautiful woman and desire her and take her as your wife,
21:12	you shall bring her into your house, and she shall shave her head and trim her nails.
21:13	She shall put off the clothing of her captivity and live in your house and mourn her father and her mother for a full month. After that you may go in to her and be her husband, and she shall be your wife.
21:14	But if you no longer delight in her, you shall let her go wherever she wishes. You must not sell her for money; you must not treat her as a slave, since you have humiliated her.
21:15	If a man has two wives, one loved and the other unloved, and both the loved and the unloved have borne him sons, and the firstborn son belongs to the unloved one,
21:16	then on the day he wills his property to his sons, he may not treat the son of the loved wife as the firstborn in preference to the son of the unloved wife, who is the firstborn.
21:17	Rather, he shall acknowledge the firstborn, the son of the unloved wife, by giving him a double portion of all that he has, for he is the firstfruits of his strength; the right of the firstborn belongs to him.
21:18	If a man has a stubborn and rebellious son who will not obey his father's voice or his mother's voice, and though they discipline him he will not listen to them,
21:19	then his father and his mother shall take hold of him and bring him out to the elders of his city, to the gate of his town,
21:20	and they shall say to the elders of his city, "This son of ours is stubborn and rebellious; he will not obey our voice; he is a glutton and a drunkard."
21:21	Then all the men of his city shall stone him with stones so that he dies. So you shall purge the evil from among you, and all Israel will hear and be afraid.
21:22	If a man has committed a sin deserving death and he is put to death, and you hang him on a tree,
21:23	his body must not remain all night on the tree, but you shall surely bury him that same day, for a hanged man is cursed by God. You must not defile your land that Yahweh your God is giving you as an inheritance.
