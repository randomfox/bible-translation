23:1	A man shall not take his father's wife; he shall not uncover his father's garment.
23:2	No one whose testicles are crushed or whose male organ is cut off shall enter the assembly of Yahweh.
23:3	No one born of a forbidden union shall enter the assembly of Yahweh; even to the tenth generation none of his descendants shall enter the assembly of Yahweh.
23:4	No Ammonite or Moabite shall enter the assembly of Yahweh; even to the tenth generation none of them shall ever enter the assembly of Yahweh,
23:5	because they did not meet you with bread and water on the way when you came out of Egypt, and because he hired against you Balaam son of Beor from Pethor in Aram-naharaim to curse you.
23:6	But Yahweh your God was not willing to listen to Balaam; instead Yahweh your God turned the curse into a blessing for you, because Yahweh your God loved you.
23:7	You shall never seek their peace or their prosperity all your days.
23:8	You must not detest an Edomite, for he is your brother. You must not detest an Egyptian, because you were a resident foreigner in his land.
23:9	Children born to them in the third generation may enter the assembly of Yahweh.
23:10	When you go out as an army camp against your enemies, you shall keep yourself from every evil thing.
23:11	If there is a man among you who is unclean because of a nocturnal emission, he shall go outside the camp; he shall not come inside the camp.
23:12	When evening comes he shall bathe in water, and at sunset he may come inside the camp.
23:13	You shall have a place outside the camp, and you shall go out there.
23:14	And you shall have a digging tool among your equipment, and when you squat outside, you shall dig a hole with it, and turn and cover up your excrement.
23:15	For Yahweh your God walks about in the midst of your camp to deliver you and to give up your enemies before you; so your camp must be holy, so that he does not see anything indecent among you and turn away from you.
23:16	You must not hand over to his master a slave who has escaped from his master to you.
23:17	He shall live with you, in your midst, in the place he chooses within one of your towns, wherever suits him best. You must not oppress him.
23:18	None of the daughters of Israel shall be a cult prostitute, and none of the sons of Israel shall be a cult prostitute.
23:19	You must not bring the wages of a prostitute or the price of a dog into the house of Yahweh your God for any vow, for both of them are detestable to Yahweh your God.
23:20	You must not charge interest to your brother, interest on money, interest on food, interest on anything that is lent at interest.
23:21	To a foreigner you may charge interest, but to your brother you must not charge interest, so that Yahweh your God may bless you in all that you put your hand to in the land you are entering to possess.
23:22	When you make a vow to Yahweh your God, you must not delay to fulfill it, for Yahweh your God will surely require it of you, and it would be sin in you.
23:23	But if you refrain from vowing, there will be no sin in you.
23:24	What has passed your lips you shall be careful to do, just as you have freely vowed to Yahweh your God what you promised with your mouth.
23:25	When you enter your neighbor's vineyard, you may eat grapes as many as you wish, until you are satisfied, but you must not put any in your container.
23:26	When you enter your neighbor's standing grain, you may pluck ears with your hand, but you must not put a sickle to your neighbor's standing grain.
