33:1	This is the blessing with which Moses the man of God blessed the Israelites before his death.
33:2	He said: "Yahweh came from Sinai and dawned on them from Seir; He shone forth from Mount Paran and came from the myriads of holy ones; from His right hand went a fiery law for them.
33:3	Indeed, He loves the peoples; all His holy ones are in Your hand; they bow down at Your feet, each receiving of Your words.
33:4	Moses commanded us a law, a possession for the assembly of Jacob.
33:5	So He became king in Jeshurun when the heads of the people gathered, the tribes of Israel together.
33:6	"Let Reuben live and not die, though his men be few."
33:7	And this is for Judah; he said: "Hear, Yahweh, the voice of Judah, and bring him to his people. With his hands he contends for himself; be a help against his foes."
33:8	And of Levi he said: "Your Thummim and Your Urim belong to Your faithful one, whom You tested at Massah, with whom You contended at the waters of Meribah;
33:9	who said of his father and his mother, 'I have not seen them,' and did not acknowledge his brothers or know his own children. For they kept Your word and guarded Your covenant.
33:10	They will teach Jacob Your ordinances and Israel Your law; they will put incense before You and whole burnt offerings on Your altar.
33:11	Bless, Yahweh, his strength, and accept the work of his hands; strike the loins of those who rise against him, and of those who hate him, so that they rise no more."
33:12	Of Benjamin he said: "The beloved of Yahweh will dwell securely beside Him; He shields him all day long, and he dwells between his shoulders."
33:13	Of Joseph he said: "Blessed by Yahweh be his land, with the choicest gifts of heaven, with the dew, and of the deep that crouches beneath;
33:14	with the choicest yield of the sun, and the choicest produce of the months;
33:15	with the finest of the ancient mountains, and the choicest of the everlasting hills;
33:16	with the choicest gifts of the earth and its fullness, and the favor of Him Who dwelt in the bush. Let these come upon the head of Joseph, upon the crown of the one set apart among his brothers.
33:17	His firstborn bull has majesty, and his horns are the horns of a wild ox; with them he gores the peoples, all together, to the ends of the earth. These are the myriads of Ephraim, and these are the thousands of Manasseh."
33:18	Of Zebulun he said: "Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out, and Issachar, in your tents.
33:19	They call peoples to the mountain; there they offer right sacrifices; for they suck the abundance of the seas and the hidden treasures of the sand."
33:20	Of Gad he said: "Blessed is he who enlarges Gad! Like a lion he lies in wait, and tears the arm, and the crown of the head too.
33:21	He chose the best part for himself, for there a commander's portion was reserved; he came with the heads of the people; he carried out the justice of Yahweh and His judgments with Israel."
33:22	Of Dan he said: "Dan is a lion's cub that leaps out from Bashan."
33:23	Of Naphtali he said: "Naphtali, sated with favor and full of the blessing of Yahweh, take possession of the west and the south."
33:24	Of Asher he said: "Most blessed of sons is Asher; let him be the favorite of his brothers, and let him dip his foot in oil.
33:25	Your bolts will be iron and bronze, and as your days, so will your strength be.
33:26	"There is none like God, O Jeshurun, Who rides the heavens to your help, and the clouds in His majesty.
33:27	The eternal God is a dwelling place, and underneath are the everlasting arms. He drove out the enemy before you and said, 'Destroy!'
33:28	So Israel dwells in safety, the fountain of Jacob alone, in a land of grain and new wine, where his heavens drip dew.
33:29	Happy are you, Israel! Who is like you, a people saved by Yahweh, the shield of your help and the sword of your majesty! Your enemies will cringe before you, and you will tread upon their high places."
