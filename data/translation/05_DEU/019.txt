19:1	When Yahweh your God cuts off the nations whose land Yahweh your God is giving you, and you dispossess them and live in their cities and their houses,
19:2	you shall set apart for yourself three cities in the midst of your land that Yahweh your God is giving you to possess.
19:3	You shall prepare the roads for yourself and divide into three parts the territory of your land that Yahweh your God gives you as an inheritance, so that any killer can flee there.
19:4	This is the case of the killer who may flee there and live: one who strikes his neighbor without intent and had not hated him previously,
19:5	as when someone goes into the forest with his neighbor to cut wood, and his hand swings the axe to cut down the tree, and the head slips off the handle and strikes his neighbor so that he dies; he may flee to one of these cities and live,
19:6	so that the avenger of blood does not pursue the killer in the heat of his anger and overtake him, because the way is long, and strike him dead, though he did not deserve death, since he had not hated him previously.
19:7	That is why I am commanding you: you shall set apart three cities for yourself.
19:8	And if Yahweh your God enlarges your territory, as he swore to your fathers, and gives you all the land that he promised to give to your fathers,
19:9	provided you carefully keep all this commandment that I am commanding you today, to love Yahweh your God and to walk in his ways always, then you shall add three more cities for yourself to these three,
19:10	so that innocent blood is not shed in the midst of your land that Yahweh your God is giving you as an inheritance, and bloodguilt is on you.
19:11	But if a man hates his neighbor and lies in wait for him, rises up against him, and strikes him fatally so that he dies, and he flees to one of these cities,
19:12	then the elders of his city shall send and take him from there and hand him over to the avenger of blood, and he shall die.
19:13	Your eye shall not pity him, but you shall purge the innocent blood from Israel, so that it may go well with you.
19:14	You must not move your neighbor's boundary marker, which the former generations set, in the inheritance you will receive in the land that Yahweh your God is giving you to possess.
19:15	A single witness shall not prevail against a man for any iniquity or any sin, in any offense he may commit; on the testimony of two witnesses or on the testimony of three witnesses a matter shall be established.
19:16	If a malicious witness rises up against a man to accuse him of wrongdoing,
19:17	then both men who have the dispute shall stand before Yahweh, before the priests and the judges who are in office in those days.
19:18	The judges shall investigate thoroughly, and if the witness is a false witness and has testified falsely against his brother,
19:19	then you shall do to him as he plotted to do to his brother. So you shall purge the evil from among you.
19:20	And the rest will hear and be afraid, and will never again commit any such evil among you.
19:21	Your eye shall not pity: life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.
