15:1	At the end of every seven years you shall grant a release.
15:2	And this is the manner of the release: every creditor shall release what he has lent to his neighbor. He must not press his neighbor, his brother, for payment, because a release has been proclaimed for Yahweh.
15:3	From a foreigner you may demand payment, but whatever of yours is with your brother your hand shall release.
15:4	There will, however, be no poor among you, for Yahweh will surely bless you in the land that Yahweh your God is giving you as an inheritance to possess,
15:5	if only you carefully listen to the voice of Yahweh your God, being careful to do all this commandment that I am commanding you today.
15:6	For Yahweh your God will bless you as he promised you: you will lend to many nations, but you will not borrow; you will rule over many nations, but they will not rule over you.
15:7	If there is a poor man among you, one of your brothers, in any of your towns in your land that Yahweh your God is giving you, you must not harden your heart or shut your hand against your poor brother.
15:8	Rather, you must freely open your hand to him and generously lend him enough for his need, whatever he lacks.
15:9	Be careful that there is no base thought in your heart, saying, "The seventh year, the year of release, is near," and your eye is evil toward your poor brother and you give him nothing; he will cry out to Yahweh against you, and it will be sin in you.
15:10	You shall surely give to him, and your heart must not be grudging when you give to him, for because of this Yahweh your God will bless you in all your work and in all that you put your hand to.
15:11	For the poor will never cease out of the land; therefore I am commanding you: you shall freely open your hand to your brother, to your needy and to your poor in your land.
15:12	If your brother, a Hebrew man or a Hebrew woman, is sold to you, he shall serve you six years, and in the seventh year you shall let him go free from you.
15:13	And when you let him go free from you, you must not let him go empty-handed.
15:14	You shall load him generously from your flock, from your threshing floor, and from your winepress; as Yahweh your God has blessed you, you shall give to him.
15:15	You shall remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt and Yahweh your God redeemed you; that is why I am commanding you this today.
15:16	But if he says to you, "I will not go out from you," because he loves you and your household, since he is well off with you,
15:17	then you shall take an awl and put it through his ear into the door, and he shall be your slave for life. And to your female slave you shall do the same.
15:18	It must not seem hard in your eyes when you let him go free from you, for he has served you six years at double the cost of a hired worker; and Yahweh your God will bless you in all that you do.
15:19	Every firstborn male that is born in your herd and in your flock you shall consecrate to Yahweh your God. You must not work with the firstborn of your ox or shear the firstborn of your flock.
15:20	You shall eat it before Yahweh your God year by year in the place Yahweh chooses, you and your household.
15:21	But if it has a defect, if it is lame or blind or has any serious defect, you must not sacrifice it to Yahweh your God.
15:22	You shall eat it within your towns, the unclean and the clean alike, as with the gazelle and the deer.
15:23	Only you must not eat its blood; you shall pour it out on the ground like water.
