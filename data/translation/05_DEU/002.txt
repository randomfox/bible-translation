2:1	"Then we turned and set out for the wilderness by the way of the Red Sea, as Yahweh had told me, and for many days we went around Mount Seir.
2:2	Then Yahweh said to me,
2:3	'You have gone around this mountain long enough. Turn north.
2:4	And command the people: You are about to pass through the territory of your brothers, the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir. They will be afraid of you, so be very careful.
2:5	Do not provoke them, for I will not give you any of their land, not even enough for the sole of a foot to tread on, because I have given Mount Seir to Esau as a possession.
2:6	You shall buy food from them with money, so that you may eat, and you shall also buy water from them with money, so that you may drink.
2:7	For Yahweh your God has blessed you in all the work of your hands. He has known your going through this great wilderness. These forty years Yahweh your God has been with you; you have lacked nothing.'
2:8	So we passed on, away from our brothers the descendants of Esau who live in Seir, away from the Arabah road, from Elath and Ezion-geber. Then we turned and went by the way of the wilderness of Moab.
2:9	And Yahweh said to me, 'Do not harass Moab or provoke them to battle, for I will not give you any of their land as a possession, because I have given Ar to the descendants of Lot as a possession.'
2:10	(The Emim formerly lived there, a people great, numerous, and tall as the Anakim.
2:11	Like the Anakim, they too are counted as Rephaim, but the Moabites call them Emim.
2:12	The Horites also formerly lived in Seir, but the descendants of Esau dispossessed them and destroyed them before them and settled in their place, just as Israel did to the land of its possession, which Yahweh gave to them.)
2:13	'Now rise up and cross the Wadi Zered.' So we crossed the Wadi Zered.
2:14	The time from when we left Kadesh-barnea until we crossed the Wadi Zered was thirty-eight years, until the whole generation of fighting men had perished from the camp, as Yahweh had sworn to them.
2:15	Indeed, the hand of Yahweh was against them, to root them out from the camp until they were gone.
2:16	So when all the fighting men had died off from among the people,
2:17	Yahweh spoke to me:
2:18	'Today you are to pass through the territory of Moab, through Ar.
2:19	When you come near the Ammonites, do not harass them or provoke them, for I will not give you any of the land of the Ammonites as a possession, because I have given it to the descendants of Lot as a possession.'
2:20	(It too is counted as a land of Rephaim. Rephaim formerly lived there, but the Ammonites call them Zamzummim,
2:21	a people great, numerous, and tall as the Anakim; but Yahweh destroyed them before the Ammonites, and they dispossessed them and settled in their place,
2:22	just as he did for the descendants of Esau who live in Seir, when he destroyed the Horites before them, and they dispossessed them and have settled in their place to this day.
2:23	As for the Avvim, who lived in villages as far as Gaza, the Caphtorim, who came from Caphtor, destroyed them and settled in their place.)
2:24	'Rise up, set out, and cross the Wadi Arnon. See, I have handed over to you Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land. Begin to take possession, and engage him in battle.
2:25	This day I will begin to put the dread and fear of you upon the peoples everywhere under heaven, who will hear the report of you and tremble and writhe in anguish because of you.'
2:26	So I sent messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon king of Heshbon with words of peace, saying,
2:27	'Let me pass through your land. I will go only by the road; I will not turn aside to the right or to the left.
2:28	You shall sell me food for money, so that I may eat, and give me water for money, so that I may drink. Only let me pass through on foot,
2:29	just as the descendants of Esau who live in Seir and the Moabites who live in Ar did for me, until I cross the Jordan into the land that Yahweh our God is giving us.'
2:30	But Sihon king of Heshbon would not let us pass through his land, for Yahweh your God hardened his spirit and made his heart obstinate, in order to hand him over to you, as he is today.
2:31	Yahweh said to me, 'See, I have begun to give Sihon and his land over to you. Begin to take possession, so as to possess his land.'
2:32	Then Sihon came out against us, he and all his people, for battle at Jahaz.
2:33	And Yahweh our God handed him over to us, and we struck him down, with his sons and all his people.
2:34	At that time we captured all his cities and devoted every city to destruction, men, women, and little ones; we left no survivor.
2:35	Only the livestock we took as plunder for ourselves, along with the spoil of the cities we had captured.
2:36	From Aroer, which is on the edge of the Wadi Arnon, and the city that is in the valley, as far as Gilead, there was no town too high for us. Yahweh our God gave them all over to us.
2:37	Only you did not go near the land of the Ammonites, all along the Wadi Jabbok, and the cities of the hill country, and wherever Yahweh our God had forbidden us.
