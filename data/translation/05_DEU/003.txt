3:1	"Then we turned and went up the road to Bashan, and Og king of Bashan came out against us, he and all his people, for battle at Edrei.
3:2	But Yahweh said to me, 'Do not fear him, for I have handed him over to you, with all his people and his land; you shall do to him as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon.'
3:3	So Yahweh our God handed over to us Og king of Bashan as well, and all his people, and we struck him down until no survivor was left to him.
3:4	At that time we captured all his cities; there was not a town we did not take from them: sixty cities, the whole region of Argob, the kingdom of Og in Bashan.
3:5	All these were cities fortified with high walls, gates, and bars, besides a great many unwalled villages.
3:6	We devoted them to destruction, as we had done to Sihon king of Heshbon, devoting every city to destruction, men, women, and little ones.
3:7	But all the livestock and the spoil of the cities we took as plunder for ourselves.
3:8	So at that time we took the land from the hand of the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan, from the Wadi Arnon to Mount Hermon
3:9	(the Sidonians call Hermon Sirion, and the Amorites call it Senir),
3:10	all the cities of the tableland, all Gilead, and all Bashan as far as Salecah and Edrei, cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan.
3:11	(For only Og king of Bashan was left of the remnant of the Rephaim. His bed, an iron bed, is it not still in Rabbah of the Ammonites? Nine cubits was its length and four cubits its width, by the common cubit.)
3:12	Of this land that we took possession of at that time, I gave to the Reubenites and the Gadites the territory from Aroer, which is on the Wadi Arnon, and half the hill country of Gilead with its cities.
3:13	The rest of Gilead and all Bashan, the kingdom of Og, I gave to the half-tribe of Manasseh: the whole region of Argob. (All that Bashan is called the land of Rephaim.
3:14	Jair the Manassite took the whole region of Argob as far as the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and he named them after himself, Havvoth-jair, as Bashan is called to this day.)
3:15	To Machir I gave Gilead.
3:16	To the Reubenites and the Gadites I gave the territory from Gilead as far as the Wadi Arnon, with the middle of the wadi as a boundary, and up to the Wadi Jabbok, the border of the Ammonites,
3:17	and the Arabah, with the Jordan as a boundary, from Chinnereth as far as the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, under the slopes of Pisgah on the east.
3:18	At that time I commanded you: 'Yahweh your God has given you this land to possess. All your capable warriors shall cross over armed before your brothers the Israelites.
3:19	Only your wives, your little ones, and your livestock (I know that you have much livestock) shall stay in your cities that I have given you,
3:20	until Yahweh gives rest to your brothers as to you, and they too take possession of the land that Yahweh your God is giving them beyond the Jordan. Then each of you may return to his possession that I have given you.'
3:21	And I commanded Joshua at that time: 'Your own eyes have seen all that Yahweh your God has done to these two kings; so Yahweh will do to all the kingdoms into which you are crossing.
3:22	Do not fear them, for it is Yahweh your God Who fights for you.'
3:23	At that time I pleaded with Yahweh:
3:24	'Lord Yahweh, You have only begun to show Your servant Your greatness and Your strong hand; for what god is there in heaven or on earth who can do such works and mighty deeds as Yours?
3:25	Please let me cross over and see the good land beyond the Jordan, that good hill country, and Lebanon.'
3:26	But Yahweh was angry with me on your account and would not listen to me. Yahweh said to me, 'Enough! Speak to Me no more of this matter.
3:27	Go up to the top of Pisgah and lift up your eyes to the west, the north, the south, and the east, and look with your own eyes, for you shall not cross this Jordan.
3:28	But commission Joshua, and encourage and strengthen him, for he shall cross over at the head of this people, and he shall give them as an inheritance the land that you will see.'
3:29	So we stayed in the valley opposite Beth-peor.
