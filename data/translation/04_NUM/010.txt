10:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
10:2	"Make two silver trumpets; make them of hammered work. You shall use them for summoning the congregation and for breaking camp.
10:3	When both are blown, the whole congregation shall gather to you at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
10:4	But if only one is blown, then the chiefs, the heads of the clans of Israel, shall gather to you.
10:5	When you sound an alarm, the camps that are on the east side shall set out.
10:6	When you sound an alarm a second time, the camps that are on the south side shall set out. An alarm is to be sounded for their setting out.
10:7	But when the assembly is to be gathered, you shall blow, but not sound an alarm.
10:8	The sons of Aaron, the priests, shall blow the trumpets. This shall be a perpetual statute for you throughout your generations.
10:9	When you go to war in your land against the adversary who oppresses you, you shall sound an alarm with the trumpets, so that you may be remembered before Yahweh your God and be saved from your enemies.
10:10	Also on your days of rejoicing, at your appointed feasts, and at the beginnings of your months, you shall blow the trumpets over your burnt offerings and over your sacrifices of peace offerings; they shall serve as a reminder of you before your God. I am Yahweh your God."
10:11	In the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth day of the month, the cloud lifted from over the tabernacle of the testimony,
10:12	and the people of Israel set out by stages from the wilderness of Sinai, and the cloud settled in the wilderness of Paran.
10:13	They set out for the first time at the command of Yahweh through Moses.
10:14	The standard of the camp of the sons of Judah set out first by their companies, and over its company was Nahshon son of Amminadab.
10:15	Over the company of the tribe of the sons of Issachar was Nethanel son of Zuar,
10:16	and over the company of the tribe of the sons of Zebulun was Eliab son of Helon.
10:17	Then the tabernacle was taken down, and the sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari, who carried the tabernacle, set out.
10:18	The standard of the camp of Reuben set out by their companies, and over its company was Elizur son of Shedeur.
10:19	Over the company of the tribe of the sons of Simeon was Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai,
10:20	and over the company of the tribe of the sons of Gad was Eliasaph son of Deuel.
10:21	Then the Kohathites set out, carrying the holy things, and the tabernacle was set up before they arrived.
10:22	The standard of the camp of the sons of Ephraim set out by their companies, and over its company was Elishama son of Ammihud.
10:23	Over the company of the tribe of the sons of Manasseh was Gamaliel son of Pedahzur,
10:24	and over the company of the tribe of the sons of Benjamin was Abidan son of Gideoni.
10:25	The standard of the camp of the sons of Dan, the rear guard of all the camps, set out by their companies, and over its company was Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai.
10:26	Over the company of the tribe of the sons of Asher was Pagiel son of Ochran,
10:27	and over the company of the tribe of the sons of Naphtali was Ahira son of Enan.
10:28	This was the order of march of the people of Israel by their companies when they set out.
10:29	Moses said to Hobab son of Reuel the Midianite, Moses' father-in-law, "We are setting out for the place of which Yahweh said, 'I will give it to you.' Come with us, and we will treat you well, for Yahweh has promised good things to Israel."
10:30	But he said to him, "I will not go; I will go back to my own land and to my kindred."
10:31	Moses said, "Please do not leave us, for you know where we should camp in the wilderness, and you will be our eyes.
10:32	And if you go with us, whatever good Yahweh does for us, we will do the same for you."
10:33	So they set out from the mountain of Yahweh three days' journey, and the ark of the covenant of Yahweh went before them three days' journey to seek out a resting place for them.
10:34	And the cloud of Yahweh was over them by day when they set out from the camp.
10:35	Whenever the ark set out, Moses said, "Arise, Yahweh, and let your enemies be scattered, and let those who hate you flee before you."
10:36	And when it came to rest, he said, "Return, Yahweh, to the countless thousands of Israel."
