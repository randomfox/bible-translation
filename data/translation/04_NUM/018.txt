18:1	Yahweh said to Aaron, "You and your sons and your ancestral house with you shall bear the guilt connected with the sanctuary, and you and your sons with you shall bear the guilt connected with your priesthood.
18:2	Also bring near with you your kinsmen, the tribe of Levi, your ancestral tribe, so that they may be joined to you and serve you while you and your sons with you are in front of the tent of the testimony.
18:3	They shall perform their duty to you and their duty for the whole tent, but they must not come near the holy furnishings or the altar, or both they and you will die.
18:4	They shall be joined to you and carry out the duties of the tent of meeting, all the work of the tent, and no outsider shall come near you.
18:5	You yourselves shall carry out the duties of the sanctuary and the duties of the altar, so that there will be no more wrath upon the Israelites.
18:6	See, I Myself have taken your kinsmen the Levites from among the Israelites; they are a gift to you, given to Yahweh, to do the work of the tent of meeting.
18:7	But you and your sons with you shall guard your priesthood in everything concerning the altar and what is inside the curtain, and you shall serve. I give you your priesthood as a service of gift; and any outsider who comes near shall be put to death."
18:8	Yahweh spoke to Aaron: "See, I Myself have given you charge of the contributions made to Me—all the holy gifts of the Israelites. I have given them to you as a portion, and to your sons, as a perpetual due.
18:9	This shall be yours from the most holy things, from what is kept back from the fire: every offering of theirs—every grain offering, every sin offering, and every guilt offering that they render to Me—shall be most holy for you and for your sons.
18:10	You shall eat it in a most holy place; every male may eat it; it shall be holy to you.
18:11	This also is yours: the contribution of their gifts, all the wave offerings of the Israelites. I have given them to you and to your sons and daughters with you as a perpetual due; everyone in your household who is clean may eat it.
18:12	All the best of the fresh oil and all the best of the new wine and grain, the first portion of them that they give to Yahweh, I have given to you.
18:13	The first ripe fruits of all that is in their land, which they bring to Yahweh, shall be yours; everyone in your household who is clean may eat it.
18:14	Everything devoted to destruction in Israel shall be yours.
18:15	Every firstborn of the womb, of all living things, which they offer to Yahweh, whether human or animal, shall be yours. But you must redeem the firstborn of humans, and you shall redeem the firstborn of unclean animals.
18:16	Their redemption price—from a month old you shall redeem them—at your valuation, five shekels of silver by the sanctuary shekel, which is twenty gerahs.
18:17	But the firstborn of an ox, or the firstborn of a sheep, or the firstborn of a goat you shall not redeem; they are holy. You shall dash their blood on the altar and burn their fat as a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.
18:18	Their meat shall be yours, just as the breast of the wave offering and the right thigh are yours.
18:19	All the holy contributions that the Israelites set aside for Yahweh I have given to you and to your sons and daughters with you as a perpetual due. It is a perpetual covenant of salt before Yahweh for you and for your descendants with you."
18:20	Yahweh said to Aaron, "You shall have no inheritance in their land, nor shall you have any share among them. I am your share and your inheritance among the Israelites.
18:21	To the Levites I have given every tithe in Israel as an inheritance, in return for the service that they perform, the service of the tent of meeting.
18:22	The Israelites shall no longer come near the tent of meeting, or they will incur sin and die.
18:23	The Levites alone shall do the service of the tent of meeting, and they shall bear their guilt. It is a perpetual statute throughout your generations; and among the Israelites they shall have no inheritance.
18:24	For the tithe of the Israelites, which they set aside as a contribution to Yahweh, I have given to the Levites as an inheritance. That is why I have said of them that they shall have no inheritance among the Israelites."
18:25	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
18:26	"Speak to the Levites and say to them: When you receive from the Israelites the tithe that I have given you from them as your inheritance, you shall set aside from it a contribution to Yahweh, a tithe of the tithe.
18:27	Your contribution will be credited to you as though it were grain from the threshing floor or the full yield of the winepress.
18:28	So you too shall set aside a contribution to Yahweh from all your tithes that you receive from the Israelites, and from them you shall give Yahweh's contribution to Aaron the priest.
18:29	From all the gifts you receive you shall set aside every contribution due to Yahweh, from all the best of it, the holiest part of it.
18:30	Say to them: When you have set aside the best of it, it shall be credited to the Levites as produce of the threshing floor and produce of the winepress.
18:31	You may eat it anywhere, you and your households, for it is your wages in return for your service in the tent of meeting.
18:32	You will incur no sin over it when you have set aside the best of it. But you must not profane the holy gifts of the Israelites, or you will die."
