21:1	When the Canaanite, the king of Arad, who lived in the Negev, heard that Israel was coming by the way of Atharim, he fought against Israel and took some of them captive.
21:2	Then Israel made a vow to Yahweh and said, "If You will indeed give this people into my hand, I will devote their towns to destruction."
21:3	Yahweh listened to Israel's voice and handed over the Canaanites, and they devoted them and their towns to destruction. So the place was named Hormah.
21:4	They set out from Mount Hor by the way of the Red Sea, to go around the land of Edom, and the people grew impatient on the way.
21:5	The people spoke against God and against Moses: "Why have you brought us up out of Egypt to die in the wilderness? For there is no bread and no water, and we loathe this worthless food."
21:6	Then Yahweh sent the fiery serpents among the people, and they bit the people, and many people of Israel died.
21:7	The people came to Moses and said, "We have sinned, for we have spoken against Yahweh and against you. Pray to Yahweh that He take the serpents away from us." So Moses prayed for the people.
21:8	Yahweh said to Moses, "Make yourself a fiery serpent and set it on a pole, and everyone who has been bitten, when he looks at it, shall live."
21:9	So Moses made a bronze serpent and set it on the pole, and whenever a serpent bit someone, he would look at the bronze serpent and live.
21:10	The Israelites set out and camped at Oboth.
21:11	They set out from Oboth and camped at Iye-abarim, in the wilderness that faces Moab toward the sunrise.
21:12	From there they set out and camped in the Wadi Zered.
21:13	From there they set out and camped on the other side of the Arnon, which is in the wilderness extending from the territory of the Amorites, for the Arnon is the border of Moab, between Moab and the Amorites.
21:14	That is why it is said in the Book of the Wars of Yahweh: "Waheb in Suphah, and the wadis, the Arnon,
21:15	and the slope of the wadis that extends toward the site of Ar and leans against the border of Moab."
21:16	From there they went on to Beer; that is the well of which Yahweh said to Moses, "Gather the people together, and I will give them water."
21:17	Then Israel sang this song: "Spring up, O well!—Sing to it!—
21:18	the well that princes dug, that the nobles of the people excavated, with the scepter, with their staffs." And from the wilderness they went on to Mattanah,
21:19	and from Mattanah to Nahaliel, and from Nahaliel to Bamoth,
21:20	and from Bamoth to the valley that is in the country of Moab, at the top of Pisgah, which overlooks the wasteland.
21:21	Then Israel sent messengers to Sihon king of the Amorites, saying,
21:22	"Let me pass through your land. We will not turn aside into field or vineyard; we will not drink water from a well. We will go by the King's Highway until we have passed through your territory."
21:23	But Sihon would not allow Israel to pass through his territory. Sihon gathered all his people and went out against Israel into the wilderness; he came to Jahaz and fought against Israel.
21:24	Israel struck him with the edge of the sword and took possession of his land from the Arnon to the Jabbok, as far as the Ammonites, for the border of the Ammonites was strong.
21:25	Israel took all these towns, and Israel settled in all the towns of the Amorites, in Heshbon and all its villages.
21:26	For Heshbon was the city of Sihon king of the Amorites; he had fought against the former king of Moab and taken all his land out of his hand, as far as the Arnon.
21:27	That is why the ballad singers say: "Come to Heshbon! Let it be built, let the city of Sihon be established!
21:28	For fire went out from Heshbon, a flame from the town of Sihon; it consumed Ar of Moab, the lords of the heights of the Arnon.
21:29	Woe to you, Moab! You are lost, O people of Chemosh! He has made his sons fugitives and his daughters captives to an Amorite king, Sihon.
21:30	But we shot them down; Heshbon is lost as far as Dibon, and we laid waste as far as Nophah, which reaches to Medeba."
21:31	So Israel settled in the land of the Amorites.
21:32	Moses sent men to spy out Jazer, and they captured its villages and drove out the Amorites who were there.
21:33	Then they turned and went up by the way of Bashan, and Og king of Bashan came out against them, he and all his people, to battle at Edrei.
21:34	But Yahweh said to Moses, "Do not fear him, for I have given him into your hand, with all his people and his land. You shall do to him as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon."
21:35	So they struck him down, and his sons and all his people, until no survivor was left to him, and they took possession of his land.
