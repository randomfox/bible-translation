25:1	While Israel was staying at Shittim, the people began to commit sexual immorality with the women of Moab.
25:2	These invited the people to the sacrifices of their gods, and the people ate and bowed down to their gods.
25:3	So Israel yoked itself to the Baal of Peor, and Yahweh's anger burned against Israel.
25:4	Yahweh said to Moses, "Take all the chiefs of the people and impale them before Yahweh in the sun, so that the fierce anger of Yahweh may turn away from Israel."
25:5	And Moses said to the judges of Israel, "Each of you kill those of his men who have yoked themselves to the Baal of Peor."
25:6	Just then one of the Israelites came and brought a Midianite woman to his kinsmen, in the sight of Moses and in the sight of the whole congregation of the Israelites, while they were weeping at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
25:7	When Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, saw it, he got up from among the congregation, took a spear in his hand,
25:8	and went after the Israelite man into the inner chamber, and pierced the two of them, the Israelite man and the woman, through her belly. So the plague was stopped from among the Israelites.
25:9	Those who died in the plague were twenty-four thousand.
25:10	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
25:11	"Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, has turned back my wrath from the Israelites by being zealous with my zeal among them, so that I did not wipe out the Israelites in my zeal.
25:12	Therefore say: I hereby give him my covenant of peace.
25:13	It shall be for him and for his descendants after him a covenant of perpetual priesthood, because he was zealous for his God and made atonement for the Israelites."
25:14	The name of the Israelite man who was struck down, who was struck down with the Midianite woman, was Zimri son of Salu, a leader of an ancestral house of the Simeonites.
25:15	The name of the Midianite woman who was struck down was Cozbi daughter of Zur; he was the head of a clan, an ancestral house in Midian.
25:16	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
25:17	"Treat the Midianites as enemies and strike them down,
25:18	for they have treated you as enemies by their deceit with which they deceived you in the matter of Peor and in the matter of Cozbi, the daughter of a leader of Midian, their sister, who was struck down on the day of the plague over the matter of Peor."
