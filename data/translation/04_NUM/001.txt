1:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tent of meeting, on the first day of the second month, in the second year after they had come out of the land of Egypt, saying,
1:2	"Take a census of the whole congregation of the Israelites, by clans, by fathers' houses, according to the number of names, every male, head by head.
1:3	From twenty years old and upward, all in Israel who are able to go to war, you and Aaron shall list them by their divisions.
1:4	And there shall be with you a man from each tribe, each man the head of his fathers' house.
1:5	These are the names of the men who shall assist you: from Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur;
1:6	from Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai;
1:7	from Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab;
1:8	from Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar;
1:9	from Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon;
1:10	from the sons of Joseph: from Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud, and from Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur;
1:11	from Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni;
1:12	from Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai;
1:13	from Asher, Pagiel son of Ochran;
1:14	from Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel;
1:15	from Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan."
1:16	These were the ones called from the congregation, the chiefs of their ancestral tribes, the heads of the clans of Israel.
1:17	Moses and Aaron took these men who had been designated by name,
1:18	and on the first day of the second month they assembled the whole congregation, and they registered their descent by clans, by fathers' houses, according to the number of names from twenty years old and upward, head by head,
1:19	as Yahweh commanded Moses. So he listed them in the wilderness of Sinai.
1:20	Of the people of Reuben, Israel's firstborn, their generations by their clans, by their fathers' houses, according to the number of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
1:21	those listed of the tribe of Reuben were forty-six thousand five hundred.
1:22	Of the people of Simeon, their generations by their clans, by their fathers' houses, according to the number of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
1:23	those listed of the tribe of Simeon were fifty-nine thousand three hundred.
1:24	Of the people of Gad, their generations by their clans, by their fathers' houses, according to the number of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
1:25	those listed of the tribe of Gad were forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.
1:26	Of the people of Judah, their generations by their clans, by their fathers' houses, according to the number of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
1:27	those listed of the tribe of Judah were seventy-four thousand six hundred.
1:28	Of the people of Issachar, their generations by their clans, by their fathers' houses, according to the number of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
1:29	those listed of the tribe of Issachar were fifty-four thousand four hundred.
1:30	Of the people of Zebulun, their generations by their clans, by their fathers' houses, according to the number of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
1:31	those listed of the tribe of Zebulun were fifty-seven thousand four hundred.
1:32	Of the people of Joseph, namely of Ephraim, their generations by their clans, by their fathers' houses, according to the number of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
1:33	those listed of the tribe of Ephraim were forty thousand five hundred.
1:34	Of the people of Manasseh, their generations by their clans, by their fathers' houses, according to the number of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
1:35	those listed of the tribe of Manasseh were thirty-two thousand two hundred.
1:36	Of the people of Benjamin, their generations by their clans, by their fathers' houses, according to the number of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
1:37	those listed of the tribe of Benjamin were thirty-five thousand four hundred.
1:38	Of the people of Dan, their generations by their clans, by their fathers' houses, according to the number of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
1:39	those listed of the tribe of Dan were sixty-two thousand seven hundred.
1:40	Of the people of Asher, their generations by their clans, by their fathers' houses, according to the number of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
1:41	those listed of the tribe of Asher were forty-one thousand five hundred.
1:42	Of the people of Naphtali, their generations by their clans, by their fathers' houses, according to the number of names, head by head, every male from twenty years old and upward, all who were able to go to war:
1:43	those listed of the tribe of Naphtali were fifty-three thousand four hundred.
1:44	These are those who were listed, whom Moses and Aaron listed with the help of the chiefs of Israel, twelve men, each representing his fathers' house.
1:45	So all those listed of the Israelites, by their fathers' houses, from twenty years old and upward, every man able to go to war in Israel—
1:46	all those listed were six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty.
1:47	But the Levites were not listed along with them by their ancestral tribe.
1:48	For Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
1:49	"Only the tribe of Levi you shall not list, and you shall not take a census of them among the Israelites.
1:50	But appoint the Levites over the tabernacle of the testimony, and over all its furnishings, and over all that belongs to it. They are to carry the tabernacle and all its furnishings, and they shall take care of it and camp around the tabernacle.
1:51	When the tabernacle is to set out, the Levites shall take it down, and when the tabernacle is to be pitched, the Levites shall set it up. And any outsider who comes near shall be put to death.
1:52	The Israelites shall camp each in his own camp and each by his own standard, by their divisions.
1:53	But the Levites shall camp around the tabernacle of the testimony, so that there may be no wrath on the congregation of the Israelites. And the Levites shall keep guard over the tabernacle of the testimony."
1:54	Thus the Israelites did; according to all that Yahweh commanded Moses, so they did.
