2:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,
2:2	"The Israelites shall camp each by his own standard, with the banners of their fathers' houses. They shall camp facing the tent of meeting on every side.
2:3	Those to camp on the east side, toward the sunrise, shall be of the standard of the camp of Judah by their divisions, the chief of the people of Judah being Nahshon son of Amminadab,
2:4	his division as listed being seventy-four thousand six hundred.
2:5	Those to camp next to him shall be the tribe of Issachar, the chief of the people of Issachar being Nethanel son of Zuar,
2:6	his division as listed being fifty-four thousand four hundred.
2:7	Then the tribe of Zebulun, the chief of the people of Zebulun being Eliab son of Helon,
2:8	his division as listed being fifty-seven thousand four hundred.
2:9	All those listed of the camp of Judah, by their divisions, were one hundred eighty-six thousand four hundred. They shall set out first on the march.
2:10	On the south side shall be the standard of the camp of Reuben by their divisions, the chief of the people of Reuben being Elizur son of Shedeur,
2:11	his division as listed being forty-six thousand five hundred.
2:12	Those to camp next to him shall be the tribe of Simeon, the chief of the people of Simeon being Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai,
2:13	his division as listed being fifty-nine thousand three hundred.
2:14	Then the tribe of Gad, the chief of the people of Gad being Eliasaph son of Reuel,
2:15	his division as listed being forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.
2:16	All those listed of the camp of Reuben, by their divisions, were one hundred fifty-one thousand four hundred and fifty. They shall set out second.
2:17	Then the tent of meeting shall set out, with the camp of the Levites in the midst of the camps; as they camp, so shall they set out, each in position, standard by standard.
2:18	On the west side shall be the standard of the camp of Ephraim by their divisions, the chief of the people of Ephraim being Elishama son of Ammihud,
2:19	his division as listed being forty thousand five hundred.
2:20	Next to him shall be the tribe of Manasseh, the chief of the people of Manasseh being Gamaliel son of Pedahzur,
2:21	his division as listed being thirty-two thousand two hundred.
2:22	Then the tribe of Benjamin, the chief of the people of Benjamin being Abidan son of Gideoni,
2:23	his division as listed being thirty-five thousand four hundred.
2:24	All those listed of the camp of Ephraim, by their divisions, were one hundred eight thousand one hundred. They shall set out third.
2:25	On the north side shall be the standard of the camp of Dan by their divisions, the chief of the people of Dan being Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai,
2:26	his division as listed being sixty-two thousand seven hundred.
2:27	Those to camp next to him shall be the tribe of Asher, the chief of the people of Asher being Pagiel son of Ochran,
2:28	his division as listed being forty-one thousand five hundred.
2:29	Then the tribe of Naphtali, the chief of the people of Naphtali being Ahira son of Enan,
2:30	his division as listed being fifty-three thousand four hundred.
2:31	All those listed of the camp of Dan were one hundred fifty-seven thousand six hundred. They shall set out last, standard by standard."
2:32	These are the Israelites as listed by their fathers' houses. All those listed in the camps by their divisions were six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty.
2:33	But the Levites were not listed among the Israelites, as Yahweh commanded Moses.
2:34	Thus the Israelites did according to all that Yahweh commanded Moses: so they camped by their standards, and so they set out, each by his clan, according to his fathers' house.
