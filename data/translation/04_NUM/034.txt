34:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses:
34:2	"Command the Israelites and say to them: When you enter the land of Canaan, this is the land that will fall to you as an inheritance, the land of Canaan with its borders.
34:3	Your southern side shall run from the wilderness of Zin alongside Edom; your southern border shall begin in the east at the end of the Salt Sea.
34:4	Your border shall turn south of the Ascent of Akrabbim and pass on to Zin, and its limit shall be south of Kadesh-barnea; then it shall go on to Hazar-addar and pass on to Azmon.
34:5	From Azmon the border shall turn to the Wadi of Egypt, and its limit shall be at the Sea.
34:6	For your western border you shall have the Great Sea and its coast; this shall be your western border.
34:7	This shall be your northern border: from the Great Sea you shall mark out your line to Mount Hor;
34:8	from Mount Hor you shall mark it out to Lebo-hamath, and the limit of the border shall be at Zedad.
34:9	Then the border shall go on to Ziphron, and its limit shall be at Hazar-enan; this shall be your northern border.
34:10	For your eastern border you shall mark out your line from Hazar-enan to Shepham.
34:11	The border shall go down from Shepham to Riblah on the east side of Ain; then the border shall go down and reach the slope east of the Sea of Chinnereth.
34:12	The border shall go down to the Jordan, and its limit shall be at the Salt Sea. This shall be your land with its borders all around."
34:13	Moses commanded the Israelites: "This is the land you shall receive as an inheritance by lot, which Yahweh has commanded to be given to the nine tribes and the half-tribe.
34:14	For the tribe of the Reubenites by their ancestral houses and the tribe of the Gadites by their ancestral houses have received their inheritance, and so has the half-tribe of Manasseh.
34:15	The two tribes and the half-tribe have received their inheritance beyond the Jordan opposite Jericho, eastward, toward the sunrise."
34:16	Yahweh spoke to Moses:
34:17	"These are the names of the men who shall apportion the land to you as an inheritance: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun.
34:18	You shall also take one chief from each tribe to apportion the land.
34:19	These are the names of the men: of the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;
34:20	of the tribe of the Simeonites, Shemuel son of Ammihud;
34:21	of the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Chislon;
34:22	of the tribe of the Danites, a chief, Bukki son of Jogli;
34:23	of the descendants of Joseph: of the tribe of the Manassites, a chief, Hanniel son of Ephod;
34:24	of the tribe of the Ephraimites, a chief, Kemuel son of Shiphtan;
34:25	of the tribe of the Zebulunites, a chief, Elizaphan son of Parnach;
34:26	of the tribe of the Issacharites, a chief, Paltiel son of Azzan;
34:27	of the tribe of the Asherites, a chief, Ahihud son of Shelomi;
34:28	of the tribe of the Naphtalites, a chief, Pedahel son of Ammihud."
34:29	These are the men whom Yahweh commanded to apportion the inheritance to the Israelites in the land of Canaan.
