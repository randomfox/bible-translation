17:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
17:2	"Tell Eleazar son of Aaron the priest to take up the censers from the blaze, and scatter the fire far and wide, for they have become holy—
17:3	the censers of these men who sinned at the cost of their lives. Let them be made into hammered sheets as a plating for the altar, for they offered them before Yahweh and so they became holy. They shall be a sign to the Israelites."
17:4	So Eleazar the priest took the bronze censers that those who were burned had offered, and they hammered them out as a plating for the altar,
17:5	a reminder to the Israelites that no outsider, who is not of the descendants of Aaron, should come near to burn incense before Yahweh, so that he would not become like Korah and his company—just as Yahweh had spoken to him through Moses.
17:6	On the next day the whole congregation of the Israelites grumbled against Moses and Aaron, saying, "You have killed Yahweh's people."
17:7	When the congregation had assembled against Moses and Aaron, they turned toward the tent of meeting, and there the cloud covered it, and the glory of Yahweh appeared.
17:8	Moses and Aaron came to the front of the tent of meeting,
17:9	and Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
17:10	"Get away from among this congregation, so that I may consume them in an instant." And they fell on their faces.
17:11	Moses said to Aaron, "Take the censer, put fire on it from the altar, lay incense on it, and carry it quickly to the congregation and make atonement for them, for wrath has gone out from before Yahweh; the plague has begun."
17:12	Aaron took it as Moses had said and ran into the midst of the assembly, and the plague had already begun among the people. He put on the incense and made atonement for the people.
17:13	He stood between the dead and the living, and the plague was stopped.
17:14	Those who died in the plague were fourteen thousand seven hundred, besides those who died over the matter of Korah.
17:15	Then Aaron returned to Moses at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and the plague was stopped.
17:16	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
17:17	"Speak to the Israelites and take from them a staff for each ancestral house, from all their leaders according to their ancestral houses, twelve staffs. Write each man's name on his staff.
17:18	Write Aaron's name on the staff of Levi, for there is to be one staff for the head of each ancestral house.
17:19	Then deposit them in the tent of meeting in front of the testimony, where I meet with you.
17:20	The staff of the man I choose will sprout, and I will rid myself of the grumblings of the Israelites, which they keep grumbling against you."
17:21	So Moses spoke to the Israelites, and all their leaders gave him a staff, one staff for each leader according to their ancestral houses, twelve staffs; and Aaron's staff was among their staffs.
17:22	Moses deposited the staffs before Yahweh in the tent of the testimony.
17:23	The next day Moses went into the tent of the testimony, and there the staff of Aaron for the house of Levi had sprouted: it had put out buds, produced blossoms, and borne ripe almonds.
17:24	Then Moses brought out all the staffs from before Yahweh to all the Israelites; they looked, and each man took his staff.
17:25	Yahweh said to Moses, "Put Aaron's staff back in front of the testimony, to be kept as a sign for the rebels, so that you may put an end to their grumblings against me, and they will not die."
17:26	Moses did so; as Yahweh commanded him, so he did.
17:27	The Israelites said to Moses, "Look, we are perishing, we are lost, we are all lost!
17:28	Everyone who comes near, who comes near the tabernacle of Yahweh, dies. Are we all to perish?"
