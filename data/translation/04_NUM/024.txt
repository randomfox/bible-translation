24:1	When Balaam saw that it pleased Yahweh to bless Israel, he did not go, as at the other times, to seek omens, but set his face toward the wilderness.
24:2	Balaam lifted up his eyes and saw Israel encamped tribe by tribe, and the spirit of God came upon him.
24:3	He took up his oracle and said: "The utterance of Balaam son of Beor, the utterance of the man whose eye is opened,
24:4	the utterance of one who hears the words of God, who sees the vision of the Almighty, falling down, but with eyes uncovered:
24:5	How lovely are your tents, O Jacob, your dwellings, O Israel!
24:6	Like valleys that stretch out, like gardens beside a river, like aloes that Yahweh has planted, like cedars beside the waters.
24:7	Water shall flow from his buckets, and his seed shall be by abundant waters; his king shall be higher than Agag, and his kingdom shall be exalted.
24:8	God, who brings him out of Egypt, is for him like the horns of a wild ox. He shall devour the nations, his foes, and crush their bones, and strike them through with his arrows.
24:9	He crouched, he lay down like a lion, like a lioness; who will rouse him? Blessed is everyone who blesses you, and cursed is everyone who curses you!"
24:10	Balak's anger burned against Balaam, and he struck his hands together. Balak said to Balaam, "I summoned you to curse my enemies, and here you have done nothing but bless them these three times!
24:11	Now flee to your own place! I said I would reward you richly, but Yahweh has kept you from reward."
24:12	Balaam said to Balak, "Did I not tell your messengers whom you sent to me,
24:13	'If Balak were to give me his house full of silver and gold, I could not go beyond the command of Yahweh to do either good or bad of my own will; what Yahweh speaks, that is what I will speak'?
24:14	And now I am going to my people. Come, let me advise you what this people will do to your people in days to come."
24:15	He took up his oracle and said: "The utterance of Balaam son of Beor, the utterance of the man whose eye is opened,
24:16	the utterance of one who hears the words of God and knows the knowledge of the Most High, who sees the vision of the Almighty, falling down, but with eyes uncovered:
24:17	I see him, but not now; I behold him, but not near. A star shall march forth out of Jacob, and a scepter shall rise out of Israel; it shall crush the brow of Moab and tear down all the sons of Sheth.
24:18	Edom shall become a possession, Seir, his enemies, shall become a possession, while Israel does valiantly.
24:19	One from Jacob shall rule, and he shall destroy the survivors of the city."
24:20	Then he looked at Amalek and took up his oracle and said: "Amalek was first among the nations, but his end is to perish forever."
24:21	Then he looked at the Kenites and took up his oracle and said: "Enduring is your dwelling place, and your nest is set in the rock;
24:22	yet Kain shall be burned. How long until Asshur takes you captive?"
24:23	Then he took up his oracle and said: "Alas, who shall live when God does this?
24:24	Ships shall come from the coast of Kittim, and they shall afflict Asshur and afflict Eber; and he too shall perish forever."
24:25	Then Balaam rose and went back to his place, and Balak also went his way.
