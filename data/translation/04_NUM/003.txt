3:1	These are the generations of Aaron and Moses on the day Yahweh spoke with Moses on Mount Sinai.
3:2	These are the names of the sons of Aaron: Nadab the firstborn, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.
3:3	These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the anointed priests, whom he ordained to serve as priests.
3:4	But Nadab and Abihu died before Yahweh when they offered unauthorized fire before Yahweh in the wilderness of Sinai, and they had no sons. So Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests in the presence of Aaron their father.
3:5	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
3:6	"Bring the tribe of Levi near and present them before Aaron the priest, so that they may serve him.
3:7	They shall perform duties for him and for the whole congregation before the tent of meeting, doing the service of the tabernacle.
3:8	They shall take charge of all the furnishings of the tent of meeting and perform duties for the people of Israel, doing the service of the tabernacle.
3:9	You shall give the Levites to Aaron and his sons; they are wholly given to him from among the people of Israel.
3:10	You shall appoint Aaron and his sons, and they shall keep their priesthood; but any outsider who comes near shall be put to death."
3:11	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
3:12	"Look, I Myself have taken the Levites from among the people of Israel in place of every firstborn who opens the womb among the people of Israel. The Levites shall be Mine,
3:13	for every firstborn is Mine. On the day I struck down every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I consecrated to Myself every firstborn in Israel, both human and animal. They shall be Mine; I am Yahweh."
3:14	Yahweh spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying,
3:15	"Register the sons of Levi by their fathers' houses and by their clans; you shall register every male from a month old and upward."
3:16	So Moses registered them according to the word of Yahweh, as he was commanded.
3:17	These were the sons of Levi by their names: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.
3:18	These are the names of the sons of Gershon by their clans: Libni and Shimei.
3:19	The sons of Kohath by their clans: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.
3:20	The sons of Merari by their clans: Mahli and Mushi. These are the clans of the Levites by their fathers' houses.
3:21	To Gershon belonged the clan of the Libnites and the clan of the Shimeites; these are the clans of the Gershonites.
3:22	Those registered, counting every male from a month old and upward, numbered seven thousand five hundred.
3:23	The clans of the Gershonites were to camp behind the tabernacle, on the west.
3:24	The chief of the fathers' house of the Gershonites was Eliasaph son of Lael.
3:25	The responsibility of the sons of Gershon in the tent of meeting was the tabernacle and the tent, its covering, the screen for the entrance of the tent of meeting,
3:26	the hangings of the court, the screen for the entrance of the court that surrounds the tabernacle and the altar, and its cords, with all the work connected with them.
3:27	To Kohath belonged the clan of the Amramites, the clan of the Izharites, the clan of the Hebronites, and the clan of the Uzzielites; these are the clans of the Kohathites.
3:28	Counting every male from a month old and upward, there were eight thousand six hundred, keeping charge of the sanctuary.
3:29	The clans of the sons of Kohath were to camp on the south side of the tabernacle.
3:30	The chief of the fathers' house of the clans of the Kohathites was Elizaphan son of Uzziel.
3:31	Their responsibility was the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, the vessels of the sanctuary with which they minister, the screen, and all the work connected with them.
3:32	The chief over the chiefs of the Levites was Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, with oversight of those keeping charge of the sanctuary.
3:33	To Merari belonged the clan of the Mahlites and the clan of the Mushites; these are the clans of Merari.
3:34	Those registered, counting every male from a month old and upward, numbered six thousand two hundred.
3:35	The chief of the fathers' house of the clans of Merari was Zuriel son of Abihail. They were to camp on the north side of the tabernacle.
3:36	The assigned responsibility of the sons of Merari was the frames of the tabernacle, its bars, its pillars, its bases, all its equipment, and all the work connected with them,
3:37	and the pillars of the court all around, with their bases, their pegs, and their cords.
3:38	Those who were to camp in front of the tabernacle on the east, in front of the tent of meeting toward the sunrise, were Moses and Aaron and his sons, keeping charge of the sanctuary on behalf of the people of Israel; any outsider who came near was to be put to death.
3:39	All the Levites registered, whom Moses and Aaron registered at the command of Yahweh by their clans, every male from a month old and upward, were twenty-two thousand.
3:40	Yahweh said to Moses, "Register every firstborn male of the people of Israel from a month old and upward, and take the count of their names.
3:41	You shall take the Levites for Me, I am Yahweh, in place of all the firstborn among the people of Israel, and the livestock of the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the livestock of the people of Israel."
3:42	So Moses registered all the firstborn among the people of Israel, as Yahweh commanded him.
3:43	All the firstborn males, by the number of names from a month old and upward, as registered, were twenty-two thousand two hundred and seventy-three.
3:44	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
3:45	"Take the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the people of Israel, and the livestock of the Levites in place of their livestock. The Levites shall be Mine; I am Yahweh.
3:46	And as the redemption price for the two hundred and seventy-three of the firstborn of the people of Israel who exceed the number of the Levites,
3:47	you shall take five shekels apiece, per head; you shall take them by the sanctuary shekel, the shekel being twenty gerahs.
3:48	Give the money to Aaron and his sons as the redemption price for those who are in excess among them."
3:49	So Moses took the redemption money from those who exceeded the number redeemed by the Levites.
3:50	From the firstborn of the people of Israel he took the money, one thousand three hundred and sixty-five shekels, by the sanctuary shekel.
3:51	Moses gave the redemption money to Aaron and his sons according to the word of Yahweh, as Yahweh commanded Moses.
