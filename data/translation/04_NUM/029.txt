29:1	"In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, you shall have a holy assembly; you shall do no regular work. It shall be a day of trumpet blasts for you.
29:2	You shall offer a burnt offering as a pleasing aroma to Yahweh: one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old without defect;
29:3	and their grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths for the bull, two tenths for the ram,
29:4	and one tenth for each of the seven lambs;
29:5	and one male goat as a purification offering, to make atonement for you;
29:6	in addition to the burnt offering of the new moon and its grain offering, and the regular burnt offering and its grain offering, and their drink offerings, according to their ordinance, as a pleasing aroma, a fire offering to Yahweh.
29:7	On the tenth day of this seventh month you shall have a holy assembly, and you shall humble yourselves; you shall do no work.
29:8	You shall offer a burnt offering to Yahweh, a pleasing aroma: one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old; they shall be without defect.
29:9	Their grain offering shall be of fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths for the bull, two tenths for the one ram,
29:10	a tenth for each of the seven lambs;
29:11	and one male goat as a purification offering, in addition to the purification offering of atonement and the regular burnt offering and its grain offering, and their drink offerings.
29:12	On the fifteenth day of the seventh month you shall have a holy assembly; you shall do no regular work, and you shall celebrate a festival to Yahweh for seven days.
29:13	You shall offer a burnt offering, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh: thirteen young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old; they shall be without defect.
29:14	Their grain offering shall be of fine flour mixed with oil: three tenths for each of the thirteen bulls, two tenths for each of the two rams,
29:15	and a tenth for each of the fourteen lambs;
29:16	also one male goat as a purification offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.
29:17	On the second day: twelve young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old without defect,
29:18	with their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams, and the lambs, according to their number, as prescribed;
29:19	also one male goat as a purification offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering and its grain offering, and their drink offerings.
29:20	On the third day: eleven bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old without defect,
29:21	with their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams, and the lambs, according to their number, as prescribed;
29:22	also one male goat as a purification offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offering.
29:23	On the fourth day: ten bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old without defect,
29:24	with their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams, and the lambs, according to their number, as prescribed;
29:25	also one male goat as a purification offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.
29:26	On the fifth day: nine bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old without defect,
29:27	with their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams, and the lambs, according to their number, as prescribed;
29:28	also one male goat as a purification offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offering.
29:29	On the sixth day: eight bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old without defect,
29:30	with their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams, and the lambs, according to their number, as prescribed;
29:31	also one male goat as a purification offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offerings.
29:32	On the seventh day: seven bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old without defect,
29:33	with their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bulls, the rams, and the lambs, according to their number, as prescribed for them;
29:34	also one male goat as a purification offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.
29:35	On the eighth day you shall have a solemn closing assembly; you shall do no regular work.
29:36	You shall offer a burnt offering, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh: one bull, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old without defect,
29:37	with their grain offering and their drink offerings for the bull, the ram, and the lambs, according to their number, as prescribed;
29:38	also one male goat as a purification offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering and its grain offering and its drink offering.
29:39	These you shall offer to Yahweh at your appointed festivals, in addition to your vowed offerings and your freewill offerings, whether your burnt offerings, your grain offerings, your drink offerings, or your offerings of well-being."
