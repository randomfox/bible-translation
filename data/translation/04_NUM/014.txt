14:1	Then the whole congregation raised a loud cry, and the people wept that night.
14:2	All the people of Israel grumbled against Moses and Aaron, and the whole congregation said to them, "If only we had died in the land of Egypt! Or if only we had died in this wilderness!
14:3	Why is Yahweh bringing us into this land to fall by the sword? Our wives and our little ones will become plunder. Would it not be better for us to go back to Egypt?"
14:4	And they said to one another, "Let us appoint a leader and go back to Egypt."
14:5	Then Moses and Aaron fell on their faces before the whole assembly of the congregation of the people of Israel.
14:6	And Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh, who were among those who had scouted out the land, tore their clothes
14:7	and said to the whole congregation of the people of Israel, "The land we passed through to scout it out is an exceedingly good land.
14:8	If Yahweh delights in us, He will bring us into this land and give it to us, a land that flows with milk and honey.
14:9	Only do not rebel against Yahweh, and do not fear the people of the land, for they are bread for us. Their protection has been removed from them, and Yahweh is with us; do not fear them."
14:10	But the whole congregation said to stone them with stones. Then the glory of Yahweh appeared at the tent of meeting to all the people of Israel.
14:11	And Yahweh said to Moses, "How long will this people despise Me? And how long will they not believe in Me, in spite of all the signs I have done among them?
14:12	I will strike them with pestilence and disinherit them, and I will make of you a nation greater and mightier than they."
14:13	But Moses said to Yahweh, "Then the Egyptians will hear of it, for You brought up this people in Your might from among them,
14:14	and they will tell the inhabitants of this land. They have heard that You, Yahweh, are in the midst of this people, that You, Yahweh, are seen eye to eye, that Your cloud stands over them, and that You go before them in a pillar of cloud by day and in a pillar of fire by night.
14:15	If You kill this people as one man, then the nations who have heard of Your fame will say,
14:16	'Because Yahweh was not able to bring this people into the land that He swore to give them, He slaughtered them in the wilderness.'
14:17	And now, please let the power of the Lord be great, as You have spoken, saying,
14:18	'Yahweh is slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love, forgiving iniquity and transgression, but He will by no means clear the guilty, visiting the iniquity of the fathers on the children, to the third and the fourth generation.'
14:19	Please pardon the iniquity of this people according to the greatness of Your steadfast love, just as You have forgiven this people from Egypt until now."
14:20	Then Yahweh said, "I have pardoned, as you have asked.
14:21	But truly, as I live, and as all the earth shall be filled with the glory of Yahweh,
14:22	none of the men who have seen My glory and My signs that I did in Egypt and in the wilderness, and yet have tested Me these ten times and have not listened to My voice,
14:23	shall see the land that I swore to give to their fathers; none of those who despised Me shall see it.
14:24	But My servant Caleb, because he has a different spirit and has followed Me fully, I will bring into the land into which he went, and his descendants shall possess it.
14:25	Now the Amalekites and the Canaanites live in the valleys; tomorrow turn and set out into the wilderness by the way to the Red Sea."
14:26	Yahweh spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,
14:27	"How long shall this wicked congregation grumble against Me? I have heard the grumblings of the people of Israel, which they grumble against Me.
14:28	Say to them, 'As I live, declares Yahweh, what you have said in My hearing I will do to you:
14:29	your dead bodies shall fall in this wilderness, all of you who were registered in your whole number, from twenty years old and upward, who have grumbled against Me.
14:30	Not one of you shall come into the land where I swore with uplifted hand to settle you, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.
14:31	But your little ones, who you said would become plunder, I will bring in, and they shall know the land that you have rejected.
14:32	But as for you, your dead bodies shall fall in this wilderness.
14:33	And your children shall be shepherds in the wilderness forty years and shall bear the consequences of your unfaithfulness, until the last of your dead bodies lies in the wilderness.
14:34	According to the number of the days in which you scouted out the land, forty days, a year for each day, you shall bear your iniquity forty years, and you shall know My opposition.'
14:35	I, Yahweh, have spoken. Surely I will do this to all this wicked congregation who have banded together against Me: in this wilderness they shall come to an end, and there they shall die."
14:36	And the men whom Moses sent to scout out the land, who returned and made the whole congregation grumble against him by spreading a bad report about the land,
14:37	those men who spread the bad report about the land died by a plague before Yahweh.
14:38	Of those men who went to scout out the land, only Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh remained alive.
14:39	When Moses told these words to all the people of Israel, the people mourned greatly.
14:40	They rose early in the morning and went up to the top of the hill country, saying, "Here we are; we will go up to the place that Yahweh has promised, for we have sinned."
14:41	But Moses said, "Why are you transgressing the command of Yahweh? It will not succeed.
14:42	Do not go up, for Yahweh is not among you, so that you will not be struck down before your enemies.
14:43	For the Amalekites and the Canaanites are there facing you, and you shall fall by the sword. Because you have turned back from following Yahweh, Yahweh will not be with you."
14:44	But they presumed to go up to the top of the hill country, although neither the ark of the covenant of Yahweh nor Moses departed from the camp.
14:45	Then the Amalekites and the Canaanites who lived in that hill country came down and struck them and crushed them as far as Hormah.
