36:1	The heads of the ancestral houses of the clan of the descendants of Gilead son of Machir son of Manasseh, of the clans of the descendants of Joseph, came forward and spoke before Moses and before the chiefs, the heads of the ancestral houses of the Israelites.
36:2	They said, "Yahweh commanded my lord to give the land as an inheritance by lot to the Israelites, and my lord was commanded by Yahweh to give the inheritance of our brother Zelophehad to his daughters.
36:3	But if they marry any of the men of the other Israelite tribes, their inheritance will be taken away from the inheritance of our ancestors and added to the inheritance of the tribe into which they marry; so it will be taken away from the allotted portion of our inheritance.
36:4	And when the Jubilee of the Israelites comes, their inheritance will be added to the inheritance of the tribe into which they marry, and their inheritance will be taken away from the inheritance of our ancestral tribe."
36:5	So Moses commanded the Israelites at Yahweh's word: "The tribe of the descendants of Joseph is right in what it says.
36:6	This is what Yahweh commands concerning the daughters of Zelophehad: Let them marry whom they think best, only they must marry within a clan of their father's tribe,
36:7	so that no inheritance of the Israelites passes from tribe to tribe; for each of the Israelites shall hold on to the inheritance of his ancestral tribe.
36:8	Every daughter who possesses an inheritance in any tribe of the Israelites shall marry someone from a clan of her father's tribe, so that each of the Israelites may possess the inheritance of his ancestors.
36:9	No inheritance shall pass from one tribe to another, for each of the tribes of the Israelites shall hold on to its own inheritance."
36:10	The daughters of Zelophehad did as Yahweh had commanded Moses.
36:11	Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah, the daughters of Zelophehad, married sons of their uncles.
36:12	They married into the clans of the descendants of Manasseh son of Joseph, and their inheritance remained in the tribe of their father's clan.
36:13	These are the commandments and the rules that Yahweh commanded the Israelites through Moses on the plains of Moab by the Jordan opposite Jericho.
