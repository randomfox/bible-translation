11:1	Now the people became like those who complain bitterly in the hearing of Yahweh, and Yahweh heard it, and His anger blazed; the fire of Yahweh burned among them and consumed some of the outskirts of the camp.
11:2	Then the people cried out to Moses, and Moses prayed to Yahweh, and the fire died down.
11:3	So the name of that place was called Taberah, because the fire of Yahweh burned among them.
11:4	The rabble that was among them had a strong craving, and the people of Israel also wept again and said, "If only we had meat to eat!
11:5	We remember the fish we ate in Egypt for nothing, the cucumbers, the melons, the leeks, the onions, and the garlic.
11:6	But now our appetite is dried up; there is nothing at all except this manna before our eyes."
11:7	The manna was like coriander seed, and its appearance was like the appearance of bdellium resin.
11:8	The people went about and gathered it, ground it in mills or pounded it in mortars, boiled it in pots, and made cakes of it; its taste was like the taste of cakes made with oil.
11:9	When the dew fell on the camp at night, the manna fell with it.
11:10	Moses heard the people weeping, clan by clan, each at the entrance of his tent. Yahweh's anger blazed hotly, and Moses was displeased.
11:11	Moses said to Yahweh, "Why have You treated Your servant so badly? Why have I not found favor in Your sight, that You lay the burden of all this people on me?
11:12	Did I conceive all this people? Did I give birth to them, that You should say to me, 'Carry them in your bosom, as a nurse carries a nursing child,' to the land that You swore to their fathers?
11:13	Where am I to get meat to give to all this people? For they weep before me and say, 'Give us meat, that we may eat.'
11:14	I am not able to carry all this people by myself; it is too heavy for me.
11:15	If this is how You will treat me, kill me at once, if I have found favor in Your sight, and do not let me see my misery."
11:16	Then Yahweh said to Moses, "Gather for Me seventy men of the elders of Israel, whom you know to be elders of the people and officers over them, and bring them to the tent of meeting, and let them take their stand there with you.
11:17	I will come down and speak with you there, and I will take some of the spirit that is on you and put it on them, and they shall bear the burden of the people with you, so that you will not bear it by yourself alone.
11:18	And say to the people: 'Consecrate yourselves for tomorrow, and you shall eat meat, for you have wept in the hearing of Yahweh, saying, "If only we had meat to eat! For it was better for us in Egypt." So Yahweh will give you meat, and you shall eat.
11:19	You shall eat not just one day, or two days, or five days, or ten days, or twenty days,
11:20	but a whole month, until it comes out of your nostrils and becomes loathsome to you, because you have rejected Yahweh Who is among you and have wept before Him, saying, "Why did we ever come out of Egypt?"'"
11:21	But Moses said, "The people among whom I am number six hundred thousand on foot, and You have said, 'I will give them meat, so that they may eat for a whole month'!
11:22	If flocks and herds were slaughtered for them, would it be enough for them? Or if all the fish of the sea were gathered for them, would it be enough for them?"
11:23	Yahweh said to Moses, "Is Yahweh's arm too short? Now you shall see whether My word comes true for you or not."
11:24	So Moses went out and told the people the words of Yahweh. He gathered seventy men of the elders of the people and stationed them around the tent.
11:25	Then Yahweh came down in the cloud and spoke to him, and took some of the spirit that was on him and put it on the seventy elders. And when the spirit rested on them, they prophesied, but they did not do so again.
11:26	Two men had remained in the camp, one named Eldad and the other named Medad, and the spirit rested on them. They were among those registered, but they had not gone out to the tent, and they prophesied in the camp.
11:27	A young man ran and told Moses, "Eldad and Medad are prophesying in the camp."
11:28	And Joshua son of Nun, Moses' assistant from his youth, answered, "My lord Moses, stop them!"
11:29	But Moses said to him, "Are you jealous for my sake? Would that all Yahweh's people were prophets, that Yahweh would put His spirit on them!"
11:30	And Moses and the elders of Israel returned to the camp.
11:31	Then a wind went out from Yahweh and drove quail in from the sea and let them fall around the camp, about a day's journey on this side and a day's journey on the other side, all around the camp, about two cubits above the ground.
11:32	The people rose up all that day and all night and all the next day and gathered the quail; the one who gathered least gathered ten homers. And they spread them out for themselves all around the camp.
11:33	While the meat was still between their teeth, before it was chewed, the anger of Yahweh blazed against the people, and Yahweh struck the people with a very great plague.
11:34	So the name of that place was called Kibroth-hattaavah, because there they buried the people who had craved.
11:35	From Kibroth-hattaavah the people set out for Hazeroth, and they stayed at Hazeroth.
