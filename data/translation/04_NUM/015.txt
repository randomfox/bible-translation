15:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
15:2	"Speak to the people of Israel and say to them: When you come into the land where you are to live, which I am giving you,
15:3	and you make a food offering to Yahweh, a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to fulfil a special vow, or as a freewill offering, or at your appointed feasts, to make a pleasing aroma to Yahweh from the herd or from the flock,
15:4	then the one who brings his offering shall present to Yahweh a grain offering of a tenth of an ephah of fine flour, mixed with a quarter of a hin of oil;
15:5	and you shall prepare a quarter of a hin of wine for the drink offering with the burnt offering or the sacrifice, for each lamb.
15:6	Or for a ram, you shall prepare as a grain offering two tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a third of a hin of oil,
15:7	and for the drink offering you shall present a third of a hin of wine, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.
15:8	And when you prepare a bull as a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to fulfil a special vow or as peace offerings to Yahweh,
15:9	then one shall present with the bull a grain offering of three tenths of an ephah of fine flour, mixed with half a hin of oil,
15:10	and you shall present as the drink offering half a hin of wine, a food offering with a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.
15:11	This is how it shall be done for each bull or ram, or for each of the male lambs or young goats.
15:12	However many you offer, so you shall do for each one, according to their number.
15:13	Every native-born Israelite shall do these things in this way, in presenting a food offering with a pleasing aroma to Yahweh.
15:14	And if a foreigner is residing with you, or anyone who is among you throughout your generations, and he wishes to make a food offering with a pleasing aroma to Yahweh, he shall do just as you do.
15:15	For the assembly, there shall be one statute for you and for the foreigner who resides with you, a perpetual statute throughout your generations: as you are, so shall the foreigner be before Yahweh.
15:16	One law and one rule shall apply to you and to the foreigner who resides with you."
15:17	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
15:18	"Speak to the people of Israel and say to them: When you come into the land to which I am bringing you
15:19	and you eat of the bread of the land, you shall present a contribution to Yahweh.
15:20	From the first of your dough you shall present a loaf as a contribution; you shall present it like the contribution from the threshing floor.
15:21	From the first of your dough you shall give to Yahweh a contribution throughout your generations.
15:22	But if you unintentionally fail to keep all these commandments that Yahweh has spoken to Moses,
15:23	all that Yahweh has commanded you through Moses, from the day that Yahweh gave the commandment and onward throughout your generations,
15:24	then if it was done unintentionally, without the knowledge of the congregation, the whole congregation shall offer one young bull as a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma to Yahweh, with its grain offering and its drink offering according to the rule, and one male goat as a sin offering.
15:25	The priest shall make atonement for the whole congregation of the people of Israel, and they shall be forgiven, because it was a mistake, and they have brought their offering, a food offering to Yahweh, and their sin offering before Yahweh for their mistake.
15:26	The whole congregation of the people of Israel shall be forgiven, and the foreigner who resides among them, because the whole people was involved in the mistake.
15:27	If one person sins unintentionally, he shall offer a female goat a year old as a sin offering.
15:28	The priest shall make atonement before Yahweh for the person who errs, when he sins unintentionally, to make atonement for him, and he shall be forgiven.
15:29	You shall have one law for whoever acts unintentionally, for the native among the people of Israel and for the foreigner who resides among them.
15:30	But the person who acts defiantly, whether native or foreigner, is blaspheming Yahweh, and that person shall be cut off from among his people.
15:31	Because he has despised the word of Yahweh and has broken His commandment, that person shall be utterly cut off; his iniquity shall be on him."
15:32	While the people of Israel were in the wilderness, they found a man gathering sticks on the Sabbath day.
15:33	Those who found him gathering sticks brought him to Moses and Aaron and to the whole congregation.
15:34	They put him in custody, because it had not been made clear what should be done to him.
15:35	Then Yahweh said to Moses, "The man shall be put to death; the whole congregation shall stone him with stones outside the camp."
15:36	So the whole congregation brought him outside the camp and stoned him to death with stones, as Yahweh commanded Moses.
15:37	Yahweh said to Moses,
15:38	"Speak to the people of Israel and tell them to make tassels on the corners of their garments throughout their generations, and to put a cord of blue on the tassel of each corner.
15:39	It shall be a tassel for you to look at, so that you remember all the commandments of Yahweh and do them, and do not follow your own heart and your own eyes, after which you prostitute yourselves.
15:40	So you shall remember and do all My commandments, and be holy to your God.
15:41	I am Yahweh your God, Who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God. I am Yahweh your God."
