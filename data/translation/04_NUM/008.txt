8:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
8:2	"Speak to Aaron and say to him: When you set up the lamps, the seven lamps shall give light in front of the lampstand."
8:3	Aaron did so: he set up its lamps to face the front of the lampstand, as Yahweh commanded Moses.
8:4	This was how the lampstand was made: hammered work of gold; from its base to its blossoms it was hammered work. According to the pattern that Yahweh had shown Moses, so he made the lampstand.
8:5	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
8:6	"Take the Levites from among the people of Israel and purify them.
8:7	This is what you shall do to them to purify them: sprinkle the water of purification on them, and let them pass a razor over their whole body, wash their clothes, and so purify themselves.
8:8	Then let them take a young bull and its grain offering of fine flour mixed with oil, and you shall take a second young bull for a sin offering.
8:9	You shall bring the Levites before the tent of meeting and assemble the whole congregation of the people of Israel.
8:10	When you bring the Levites before Yahweh, the people of Israel shall lay their hands on the Levites,
8:11	and Aaron shall present the Levites before Yahweh as a wave offering from the people of Israel, so that they may do the service of Yahweh.
8:12	Then the Levites shall lay their hands on the heads of the bulls, and you shall offer the one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering to Yahweh, to make atonement for the Levites.
8:13	You shall set the Levites before Aaron and his sons and present them as a wave offering to Yahweh.
8:14	In this way you shall separate the Levites from among the people of Israel, and the Levites shall be mine.
8:15	After that the Levites shall go in to serve at the tent of meeting, once you have purified them and presented them as a wave offering.
8:16	For they are wholly given to me from among the people of Israel; in place of all who open the womb, the firstborn of all the people of Israel, I have taken them for myself.
8:17	For every firstborn among the people of Israel is mine, both human and animal; on the day I struck down every firstborn in the land of Egypt I consecrated them for myself,
8:18	and I have taken the Levites in place of all the firstborn among the people of Israel.
8:19	I have given the Levites as a gift to Aaron and his sons from among the people of Israel, to do the service for the people of Israel at the tent of meeting and to make atonement for the people of Israel, so that no plague may strike the people of Israel when the people of Israel come near the sanctuary."
8:20	Moses and Aaron and the whole congregation of the people of Israel did this to the Levites; according to all that Yahweh commanded Moses concerning the Levites, so the people of Israel did to them.
8:21	The Levites purified themselves from sin and washed their clothes, and Aaron presented them as a wave offering before Yahweh, and Aaron made atonement for them to purify them.
8:22	After that the Levites went in to do their service in the tent of meeting before Aaron and his sons. As Yahweh had commanded Moses concerning the Levites, so they did to them.
8:23	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
8:24	"This is what applies to the Levites: from twenty-five years old and upward they shall come to serve in the work of the tent of meeting.
8:25	From the age of fifty years they shall withdraw from the work of the service and serve no more.
8:26	They may assist their brothers in the tent of meeting in keeping guard, but they shall do no labor. This is how you shall arrange the duties of the Levites."
