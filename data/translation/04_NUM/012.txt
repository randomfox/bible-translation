12:1	Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses because of the Cushite woman whom he had married, for he had married a Cushite woman.
12:2	They said, "Has Yahweh spoken only through Moses? Has He not spoken through us too?" And Yahweh heard it.
12:3	Now the man Moses was very humble, more than any person on the face of the earth.
12:4	Suddenly Yahweh said to Moses, Aaron, and Miriam, "Come out, you three, to the tent of meeting." And the three of them came out.
12:5	Yahweh came down in a pillar of cloud and stood at the entrance of the tent, and called Aaron and Miriam, and they both came forward.
12:6	He said, "Hear My words: If there is a prophet among you, I, Yahweh, make Myself known to him in a vision; I speak with him in a dream.
12:7	Not so with My servant Moses; he is entrusted with all My house.
12:8	With him I speak mouth to mouth, clearly and not in riddles, and he beholds the form of Yahweh. Why then were you not afraid to speak against My servant Moses?"
12:9	The anger of Yahweh blazed against them, and He departed.
12:10	When the cloud withdrew from over the tent, there was Miriam, diseased in her skin, white as snow. Aaron turned toward Miriam, and there she was with the skin disease.
12:11	Aaron said to Moses, "Please, my lord, do not hold against us the sin that we have so foolishly committed.
12:12	Please do not let her be like a stillborn child, whose flesh is half eaten away when it comes out of its mother's womb."
12:13	And Moses cried out to Yahweh, "O God, please heal her!"
12:14	But Yahweh said to Moses, "If her father had merely spat in her face, would she not be in disgrace for seven days? Let her be shut out of the camp for seven days, and after that she may be brought back."
12:15	So Miriam was shut out of the camp for seven days, and the people did not set out until Miriam was brought back.
12:16	After that the people set out from Hazeroth and camped in the wilderness of Paran.
