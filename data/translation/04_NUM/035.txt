35:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses on the plains of Moab by the Jordan opposite Jericho:
35:2	"Command the Israelites to give the Levites, from the inheritance they possess, towns to live in; and you shall give the Levites pasturelands around the towns.
35:3	The towns shall be theirs to live in, and their pasturelands shall be for their cattle, their livestock, and all their animals.
35:4	The pasturelands of the towns that you give the Levites shall extend outward from the town wall a thousand cubits all around.
35:5	You shall measure outside the town on the east side two thousand cubits, on the south side two thousand cubits, on the west side two thousand cubits, and on the north side two thousand cubits, with the town in the middle. This shall be their town pasturelands.
35:6	Among the towns that you give the Levites shall be the six cities of refuge, which you shall provide for a killer to flee to; and in addition to them you shall give forty-two towns.
35:7	All the towns you give the Levites shall be forty-eight, together with their pasturelands.
35:8	As for the towns you give from the possession of the Israelites, from the larger tribe you shall take more and from the smaller you shall take fewer; each shall give some of its towns to the Levites in proportion to the inheritance it receives."
35:9	Yahweh spoke to Moses:
35:10	"Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan,
35:11	you shall select towns to be cities of refuge for you, so that a killer who strikes someone down unintentionally may flee there.
35:12	The towns shall be a refuge for you from the avenger, so that the killer may not die before he stands trial before the congregation.
35:13	The towns that you give shall be your six cities of refuge.
35:14	You shall give three towns beyond the Jordan and three towns in the land of Canaan; they shall be cities of refuge.
35:15	These six towns shall be a refuge for the Israelites and for the resident alien and the settler among them, so that anyone who strikes someone down unintentionally may flee there.
35:16	But if he struck him with an iron object, so that he died, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.
35:17	If he struck him with a stone in the hand that could cause death, and he died, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.
35:18	Or if he struck him with a wooden object in the hand that could cause death, and he died, he is a murderer; the murderer shall surely be put to death.
35:19	The avenger of blood shall himself put the murderer to death; when he meets him, he shall put him to death.
35:20	If someone pushes another out of hatred, or throws something at him with intent, and he dies,
35:21	or in enmity strikes him with his hand, and he dies, the one who struck him shall surely be put to death; he is a murderer. The avenger of blood shall put the murderer to death when he meets him.
35:22	But if he pushed him suddenly without enmity, or threw any object at him without intent,
35:23	or, without seeing him, dropped on him any stone that could cause death, and he died, though he was not his enemy and did not seek his harm,
35:24	then the congregation shall judge between the one who struck him and the avenger of blood according to these rules.
35:25	The congregation shall rescue the killer from the hand of the avenger of blood, and the congregation shall return him to his city of refuge to which he fled, and he shall live there until the death of the high priest who was anointed with the holy oil.
35:26	But if the killer ever goes outside the limits of his city of refuge to which he fled,
35:27	and the avenger of blood finds him outside the limits of his city of refuge and the avenger of blood kills the killer, he incurs no bloodguilt.
35:28	For he must stay in his city of refuge until the death of the high priest; after the death of the high priest the killer may return to the land of his possession.
35:29	These shall be a statute of law for you throughout your generations in all your dwellings.
35:30	If anyone kills a person, the murderer shall be put to death on the testimony of witnesses; but a single witness shall not testify against a person so that he is put to death.
35:31	You shall accept no ransom for the life of a murderer who is guilty of death; he shall surely be put to death.
35:32	Nor shall you accept ransom for one who has fled to his city of refuge, so that he may return to live in the land before the death of the priest.
35:33	You shall not pollute the land in which you live, for blood pollutes the land, and no atonement can be made for the land for the blood that is shed in it except by the blood of the one who shed it.
35:34	You shall not defile the land in which you live, in the midst of which I dwell; for I, Yahweh, dwell among the Israelites."
