20:1	The Israelites, the whole congregation, came into the wilderness of Zin in the first month, and the people stayed at Kadesh. Miriam died there and was buried there.
20:2	There was no water for the congregation, and they assembled against Moses and Aaron.
20:3	The people quarreled with Moses and said, "If only we had perished when our kinsmen perished before Yahweh!
20:4	Why have you brought Yahweh's assembly into this wilderness, for us and our livestock to die here?
20:5	Why did you bring us up out of Egypt to bring us to this evil place? It is no place for grain or figs or vines or pomegranates, and there is no water to drink."
20:6	Moses and Aaron went from the presence of the assembly to the entrance of the tent of meeting and fell on their faces, and the glory of Yahweh appeared to them.
20:7	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
20:8	"Take the staff, and assemble the congregation, you and Aaron your brother, and speak to the rock before their eyes, and it will yield its water. So you will bring water out of the rock for them and give the congregation and their livestock drink."
20:9	Moses took the staff from before Yahweh, as He had commanded him.
20:10	Moses and Aaron gathered the assembly in front of the rock, and he said to them, "Listen, you rebels! Must we bring water out of this rock for you?"
20:11	Then Moses raised his hand and struck the rock twice with his staff, and abundant water came out, and the congregation and their livestock drank.
20:12	But Yahweh said to Moses and Aaron, "Because you did not trust Me, to treat Me as holy in the eyes of the Israelites, therefore you shall not bring this assembly into the land that I have given them."
20:13	These are the waters of Meribah, where the Israelites quarreled with Yahweh, and He showed Himself holy among them.
20:14	Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom: "Thus says your brother Israel: You know all the hardship that has befallen us—
20:15	how our ancestors went down to Egypt, and we lived in Egypt a long time, and the Egyptians mistreated us and our ancestors.
20:16	When we cried out to Yahweh, He heard our voice and sent a messenger and brought us out of Egypt. Now here we are at Kadesh, a town on the edge of your territory.
20:17	Please let us pass through your land. We will not pass through field or vineyard, nor drink water from a well. We will travel by the King's Highway; we will not turn aside to the right or to the left until we have passed through your territory."
20:18	But Edom said to him, "You shall not pass through me, or I will come out against you with the sword."
20:19	The Israelites said to him, "We will go up by the highway, and if we drink any of your water, I and my livestock, I will pay its price. It is nothing at all; only let me pass through on foot."
20:20	But he said, "You shall not pass through." And Edom came out against them with a large force and a strong hand.
20:21	So Edom refused to let Israel pass through his territory, and Israel turned away from him.
20:22	They set out from Kadesh, and the Israelites, the whole congregation, came to Mount Hor.
20:23	At Mount Hor, on the border of the land of Edom, Yahweh said to Moses and Aaron,
20:24	"Aaron shall be gathered to his people, for he shall not enter the land that I have given to the Israelites, because you both rebelled against My command at the waters of Meribah.
20:25	Take Aaron and Eleazar his son and bring them up Mount Hor.
20:26	Strip Aaron of his garments and put them on Eleazar his son; and Aaron shall be gathered to his people and shall die there."
20:27	Moses did as Yahweh had commanded; they went up Mount Hor in the sight of the whole congregation.
20:28	Moses stripped Aaron of his garments and put them on Eleazar his son, and Aaron died there on the top of the mountain. Then Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain.
20:29	When the whole congregation saw that Aaron had perished, all the house of Israel wept for Aaron thirty days.
