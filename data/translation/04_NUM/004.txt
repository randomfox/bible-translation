4:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,
4:2	"Take a census of the sons of Kohath from among the sons of Levi, by their clans and their fathers' houses,
4:3	from thirty years old and upward to fifty years old, everyone who enters the service to do work in the tent of meeting.
4:4	This is the service of the sons of Kohath in the tent of meeting: the most holy things.
4:5	When the camp sets out, Aaron and his sons shall go in and take down the screening curtain and cover the ark of the testimony with it.
4:6	Then they shall put on it a covering of fine leather, spread over it a cloth entirely of blue, and put its poles in place.
4:7	Over the table of the presence they shall spread a blue cloth and put on it the plates, the dishes, the bowls, and the jugs for the drink offering; the regular bread shall also be on it.
4:8	They shall spread over them a scarlet cloth, cover it with a covering of fine leather, and put its poles in place.
4:9	They shall take a blue cloth and cover the lampstand for the light, with its lamps, its tongs, its trays, and all the vessels for its oil with which they tend it.
4:10	They shall put it and all its utensils in a covering of fine leather and put it on the carrying frame.
4:11	Over the golden altar they shall spread a blue cloth, cover it with a covering of fine leather, and put its poles in place.
4:12	They shall take all the utensils of service with which they minister in the sanctuary, put them in a blue cloth, cover them with a covering of fine leather, and put them on the carrying frame.
4:13	They shall clear the ashes from the altar and spread a purple cloth over it.
4:14	They shall put on it all its utensils with which they minister at it: the firepans, the forks, the shovels, and the basins, all the utensils of the altar; and they shall spread over it a covering of fine leather and put its poles in place.
4:15	When Aaron and his sons have finished covering the sanctuary and all the furnishings of the sanctuary, as the camp sets out, after that the sons of Kohath shall come to carry them; but they must not touch the holy things, or they will die. These are the things in the tent of meeting that the sons of Kohath are to carry.
4:16	Eleazar son of Aaron the priest shall have charge of the oil for the light, the fragrant incense, the regular grain offering, and the anointing oil, with charge of the whole tabernacle and all that is in it, the sanctuary and its furnishings."
4:17	Yahweh spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,
4:18	"Do not let the tribe of the clans of the Kohathites be cut off from among the Levites.
4:19	But do this for them, so that they may live and not die when they approach the most holy things: Aaron and his sons shall go in and assign each of them to his task and to his load.
4:20	But they shall not go in to look at the holy things even for a moment, or they will die."
4:21	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
4:22	"Take a census of the sons of Gershon also, by their fathers' houses and by their clans.
4:23	From thirty years old and upward to fifty years old you shall register them, everyone who comes to serve in the service, to do work in the tent of meeting.
4:24	This is the service of the clans of the Gershonites, in serving and in carrying:
4:25	they shall carry the curtains of the tabernacle and the tent of meeting, its covering and the covering of fine leather that is on top of it, and the screen for the entrance of the tent of meeting,
4:26	the hangings of the court, the screen for the entrance of the gate of the court that surrounds the tabernacle and the altar, their cords, and all the equipment for their service; and they shall do all that needs to be done with them.
4:27	All the service of the sons of the Gershonites, in all their carrying and in all their work, shall be at the command of Aaron and his sons; and you shall assign to them as their charge all that they are to carry.
4:28	This is the service of the clans of the sons of the Gershonites in the tent of meeting, and their duties shall be under the direction of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.
4:29	As for the sons of Merari, you shall register them by their clans and their fathers' houses.
4:30	From thirty years old and upward to fifty years old you shall register them, everyone who enters the service to do the work of the tent of meeting.
4:31	This is what they are charged to carry, as all their service in the tent of meeting: the frames of the tabernacle, its bars, its pillars, and its bases,
4:32	and the pillars of the court all around with their bases, pegs, and cords, with all their equipment and all their service; and you shall assign by name the items they are charged to carry.
4:33	This is the service of the clans of the sons of Merari, all their service in the tent of meeting, under the direction of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest."
4:34	So Moses and Aaron and the chiefs of the congregation registered the sons of the Kohathites by their clans and their fathers' houses,
4:35	from thirty years old and upward to fifty years old, everyone who entered the service for work in the tent of meeting.
4:36	Those registered by their clans were two thousand seven hundred and fifty.
4:37	These were those registered of the clans of the Kohathites, all who served in the tent of meeting, whom Moses and Aaron registered according to the command of Yahweh through Moses.
4:38	Those registered of the sons of Gershon, by their clans and their fathers' houses,
4:39	from thirty years old and upward to fifty years old, everyone who entered the service for work in the tent of meeting,
4:40	those registered by their clans and their fathers' houses were two thousand six hundred and thirty.
4:41	These were those registered of the clans of the sons of Gershon, all who served in the tent of meeting, whom Moses and Aaron registered according to the command of Yahweh.
4:42	Those registered of the clans of the sons of Merari, by their clans and their fathers' houses,
4:43	from thirty years old and upward to fifty years old, everyone who entered the service for work in the tent of meeting,
4:44	those registered by their clans were three thousand two hundred.
4:45	These were those registered of the clans of the sons of Merari, whom Moses and Aaron registered according to the command of Yahweh through Moses.
4:46	All those registered of the Levites, whom Moses and Aaron and the chiefs of Israel registered, by their clans and their fathers' houses,
4:47	from thirty years old and upward to fifty years old, everyone who came to do the work of service and the work of carrying in the tent of meeting,
4:48	those registered were eight thousand five hundred and eighty.
4:49	According to the command of Yahweh through Moses they were assigned, each to his service and to his load; thus they were registered by him, as Yahweh commanded Moses.
