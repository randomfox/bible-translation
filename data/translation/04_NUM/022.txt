22:1	The Israelites set out and camped in the plains of Moab, across the Jordan from Jericho.
22:2	Balak son of Zippor saw all that Israel had done to the Amorites.
22:3	Moab was very much afraid of the people, because they were numerous; Moab was filled with dread of the Israelites.
22:4	Moab said to the elders of Midian, "Now this horde will lick up everything around us, as an ox licks up the green growth of the field." Balak son of Zippor was king of Moab at that time.
22:5	He sent messengers to Balaam son of Beor at Pethor, which is by the River, in the land of his kinsfolk, to summon him, saying, "Look, a people has come out of Egypt; they cover the face of the land, and they are settled opposite me.
22:6	So now, please come and curse this people for me, for they are too strong for me. Perhaps I will be able to defeat them and drive them out of the land, for I know that whoever you bless is blessed, and whoever you curse is cursed."
22:7	So the elders of Moab and the elders of Midian went with the fees for divination in their hands, came to Balaam, and told him Balak's words.
22:8	He said to them, "Spend the night here, and I will bring you back word as Yahweh speaks to me." So the officials of Moab stayed with Balaam.
22:9	God came to Balaam and said, "Who are these men with you?"
22:10	Balaam said to God, "Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, has sent to me:
22:11	'Look, the people that has come out of Egypt covers the face of the land. Now come, put a curse on them for me; perhaps I will be able to fight against them and drive them out.'"
22:12	God said to Balaam, "You shall not go with them. You shall not curse the people, for they are blessed."
22:13	Balaam got up in the morning and said to Balak's officials, "Go back to your own land, for Yahweh has refused to let me go with you."
22:14	So the officials of Moab rose and went to Balak and said, "Balaam refused to come with us."
22:15	Balak again sent officials, more numerous and more distinguished than these.
22:16	They came to Balaam and said to him, "Thus says Balak son of Zippor: 'Please do not let anything hold you back from coming to me,
22:17	for I will reward you very richly, and whatever you tell me I will do. Please come, put a curse on this people for me.'"
22:18	But Balaam answered Balak's servants, "Even if Balak were to give me his house full of silver and gold, I could not go beyond the command of Yahweh my God, to do anything small or great.
22:19	Now, please, you also stay here tonight, so that I may find out what more Yahweh will say to me."
22:20	God came to Balaam at night and said to him, "If the men have come to summon you, get up and go with them; but only the word I speak to you shall you do."
22:21	So Balaam got up in the morning, saddled his donkey, and went with the officials of Moab.
22:22	But God's anger burned because he was going, and the angel of Yahweh took his stand in the road as an adversary against him. He was riding on his donkey, and his two servants were with him.
22:23	The donkey saw the angel of Yahweh standing in the road with his sword drawn in his hand, and the donkey turned off the road and went into the field. Balaam struck the donkey to turn her back onto the road.
22:24	Then the angel of Yahweh stood in a narrow path between the vineyards, with a wall on this side and a wall on that side.
22:25	When the donkey saw the angel of Yahweh, she pressed herself against the wall and pressed Balaam's foot against the wall, and he struck her again.
22:26	Then the angel of Yahweh went on ahead and stood in a narrow place where there was no room to turn either right or left.
22:27	When the donkey saw the angel of Yahweh, she lay down under Balaam, and Balaam's anger burned, and he struck the donkey with his stick.
22:28	Then Yahweh opened the donkey's mouth, and she said to Balaam, "What have I done to you that you have struck me these three times?"
22:29	Balaam said to the donkey, "Because you have made a fool of me! If only I had a sword in my hand, I would kill you right now."
22:30	The donkey said to Balaam, "Am I not your donkey, which you have ridden all your life to this day? Have I ever been in the habit of doing this to you?" And he said, "No."
22:31	Then Yahweh opened Balaam's eyes, and he saw the angel of Yahweh standing in the road with his sword drawn in his hand; and he bowed down and prostrated himself on his face.
22:32	The angel of Yahweh said to him, "Why have you struck your donkey these three times? Look, I have come out as an adversary, because your way is reckless before me.
22:33	The donkey saw me and turned aside from me these three times. If she had not turned aside from me, I would surely have killed you by now and let her live."
22:34	Balaam said to the angel of Yahweh, "I have sinned, for I did not know that you were standing in the road to meet me. Now, if it is displeasing to you, I will go back."
22:35	The angel of Yahweh said to Balaam, "Go with the men, but only the word that I speak to you shall you speak." So Balaam went on with Balak's officials.
22:36	When Balak heard that Balaam had come, he went out to meet him at Ir-moab, on the border formed by the Arnon, at the edge of the territory.
22:37	Balak said to Balaam, "Did I not send urgently to summon you? Why did you not come to me? Am I really not able to honor you?"
22:38	Balaam said to Balak, "Look, I have come to you now. Am I able to say anything at all? The word that God puts in my mouth, that is what I will speak."
22:39	Then Balaam went with Balak, and they came to Kiriath-huzoth.
22:40	Balak sacrificed cattle and sheep and sent some to Balaam and to the officials who were with him.
22:41	In the morning Balak took Balaam and brought him up to Bamoth-baal, and from there he saw the edge of the people.
