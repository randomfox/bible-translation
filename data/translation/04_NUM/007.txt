7:1	On the day when Moses had finished setting up the tabernacle, he anointed it and consecrated it with all its furnishings, and the altar with all its utensils; he anointed them and consecrated them.
7:2	Then the chiefs of Israel, the heads of their fathers' houses, made offerings; these were the chiefs of the tribes, who were over those registered.
7:3	They brought their offering before Yahweh: six covered wagons and twelve oxen, a wagon for every two chiefs and an ox for each one. They presented them in front of the tabernacle.
7:4	Then Yahweh said to Moses,
7:5	"Accept these from them, and let them be used in doing the service of the tent of meeting, and give them to the Levites, to each according to his service."
7:6	So Moses took the wagons and the oxen and gave them to the Levites.
7:7	Two wagons and four oxen he gave to the sons of Gershon, according to their service,
7:8	and four wagons and eight oxen he gave to the sons of Merari, according to their service, under the direction of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.
7:9	But to the sons of Kohath he gave none, because they were in charge of the service of the holy things, which had to be carried on the shoulder.
7:10	The chiefs also brought offerings for the dedication of the altar on the day it was anointed; the chiefs presented their offering before the altar.
7:11	And Yahweh said to Moses, "Let them present their offerings for the dedication of the altar, one chief each day."
7:12	The one who presented his offering on the first day was Nahshon son of Amminadab, of the tribe of Judah.
7:13	His offering was one silver plate weighing a hundred and thirty shekels and one silver basin of seventy shekels, by the sanctuary shekel, both of them full of fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering;
7:14	one gold dish of ten shekels, full of incense;
7:15	one bull from the herd, one ram, and one male lamb a year old, for a burnt offering;
7:16	one male goat for a sin offering;
7:17	and for the sacrifice of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five male goats, and five male lambs a year old. This was the offering of Nahshon son of Amminadab.

7:85	each silver plate weighing a hundred and thirty shekels and each basin seventy; all the silver of the vessels was two thousand four hundred shekels by the sanctuary shekel.
7:86	The twelve gold dishes full of incense weighed ten shekels apiece by the sanctuary shekel; all the gold of the dishes was a hundred and twenty shekels.
7:87	All the cattle for the burnt offering were twelve bulls, with twelve rams, twelve male lambs a year old, and their grain offering; and twelve male goats for a sin offering.
7:88	All the cattle for the sacrifice of peace offerings were twenty-four bulls, with sixty rams, sixty male goats, and sixty male lambs a year old. This was the dedication offering for the altar after it was anointed.
7:89	When Moses went into the tent of meeting to speak with Yahweh, he heard the voice speaking to him from above the atonement cover that was on the ark of the testimony, from between the two cherubim; and so he spoke to him.
