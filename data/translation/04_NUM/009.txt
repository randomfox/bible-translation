9:1	Yahweh spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the first month of the second year after they had come out of the land of Egypt, saying,
9:2	"Let the people of Israel keep the Passover at its appointed time.
9:3	On the fourteenth day of this month, at twilight, you shall keep it at its appointed time; you shall keep it according to all its statutes and all its rules."
9:4	So Moses told the people of Israel to keep the Passover.
9:5	They kept the Passover in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, at twilight, in the wilderness of Sinai; according to all that Yahweh commanded Moses, so the people of Israel did.
9:6	But there were some men who were unclean through contact with a human corpse, and they could not keep the Passover on that day. They came before Moses and Aaron on that day,
9:7	and those men said to him, "We are unclean through contact with a human corpse. Why should we be kept from presenting Yahweh's offering at its appointed time among the people of Israel?"
9:8	Moses said to them, "Wait, so that I may hear what Yahweh will command concerning you."
9:9	Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,
9:10	"Speak to the people of Israel, saying: If any one of you or of your descendants is unclean through a corpse or is away on a long journey, he may still keep the Passover to Yahweh.
9:11	In the second month, on the fourteenth day, at twilight, they shall keep it; they shall eat it with unleavened bread and bitter herbs.
9:12	They shall leave none of it until morning, nor break any of its bones; according to every statute of the Passover they shall keep it.
9:13	But the man who is clean and is not on a journey, yet refrains from keeping the Passover, that person shall be cut off from his people, because he did not present Yahweh's offering at its appointed time; that man shall bear his sin.
9:14	And if a foreigner residing among you would keep the Passover to Yahweh, he shall keep it according to the statute of the Passover and according to its rule. You shall have one statute, both for the resident foreigner and for the native of the land."
9:15	On the day that the tabernacle was set up, the cloud covered the tabernacle, the tent of the testimony; and in the evening it was over the tabernacle like the appearance of fire until morning.
9:16	So it was continually: the cloud covered it, and the appearance of fire by night.
9:17	Whenever the cloud lifted from over the tent, after that the people of Israel set out; and in the place where the cloud settled, there the people of Israel camped.
9:18	At the command of Yahweh the people of Israel set out, and at the command of Yahweh they camped; as long as the cloud rested over the tabernacle, they remained in camp.
9:19	Even when the cloud lingered over the tabernacle many days, the people of Israel kept Yahweh's charge and did not set out.
9:20	Sometimes the cloud was over the tabernacle only a few days; at the command of Yahweh they camped, and at the command of Yahweh they set out.
9:21	Sometimes the cloud remained from evening until morning, and when the cloud lifted in the morning, they set out; or if it continued for a day and a night, when the cloud lifted they set out.
9:22	Whether it was two days, or a month, or a year that the cloud lingered over the tabernacle, resting upon it, the people of Israel remained in camp and did not set out; but when it lifted, they set out.
9:23	At the command of Yahweh they camped, and at the command of Yahweh they set out; they kept Yahweh's charge, at the command of Yahweh through Moses.
