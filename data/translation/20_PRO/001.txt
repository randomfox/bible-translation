1:1	The proverbs of Solomon son of David, king of Israel:
1:2	for knowing wisdom and discipline, for understanding words of insight,
1:3	for receiving instruction in wise dealing, in righteousness, justice, and equity;
1:4	to give shrewdness to the simple, knowledge and discretion to the youth—
1:5	let the wise hear and increase in learning, and the one who understands acquire guidance—
1:6	to understand a proverb and an enigma, the words of the wise and their riddles.
1:7	The fear of Yahweh is the beginning of knowledge; fools despise wisdom and discipline.
1:8	Hear, my son, your father's instruction, and do not forsake your mother's teaching,
1:9	for they are a graceful garland for your head and pendants for your neck.
1:10	My son, if sinners entice you, do not consent.
1:11	If they say, "Come with us, let us lie in wait for blood; let us ambush the innocent without cause;
1:12	like Sheol let us swallow them alive, and whole, like those who go down to the pit;
1:13	we shall find all kinds of precious wealth; we shall fill our houses with plunder;
1:14	throw in your lot among us; we will all have one purse"—
1:15	my son, do not walk in the way with them; hold back your foot from their paths,
1:16	for their feet run to evil, and they hasten to shed blood.
1:17	For in vain is a net spread in the sight of any bird,
1:18	but these men lie in wait for their own blood; they ambush their own lives.
1:19	Such are the ways of everyone who is greedy for unjust gain; it takes away the life of its possessors.
1:20	Wisdom cries aloud in the street; in the squares she raises her voice;
1:21	at the head of the noisy streets she calls out; at the entrance of the city gates she speaks her words:
1:22	"How long, O simple ones, will you love being simple? How long will scoffers delight in their scoffing and fools hate knowledge?
1:23	Turn back at my reproof; behold, I will pour out my spirit to you; I will make my words known to you.
1:24	Because I called and you refused, I stretched out my hand and no one paid attention,
1:25	and you ignored all my counsel and would have none of my reproof,
1:26	I also will laugh at your calamity; I will mock when terror comes upon you,
1:27	when terror comes upon you like a storm and your calamity comes like a whirlwind, when distress and anguish come upon you.
1:28	Then they will call upon me, but I will not answer; they will seek me diligently but will not find me.
1:29	Because they hated knowledge and did not choose the fear of Yahweh,
1:30	would have none of my counsel and despised all my reproof,
1:31	therefore they shall eat the fruit of their way and have their fill of their own devices.
1:32	For the turning away of the simple kills them, and the complacency of fools destroys them;
1:33	but whoever listens to me will dwell secure and will be at ease, without dread of disaster."
