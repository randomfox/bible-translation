4:1	"For behold, in those days and at that time, when I restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem,
4:2	I will gather all the nations and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat. And I will enter into judgment with them there, on behalf of my people and my heritage Israel, because they have scattered them among the nations and have divided up my land,
4:3	and have cast lots for my people, and have traded a boy for a prostitute, and have sold a girl for wine and have drunk it.
4:4	What are you to me, O Tyre and Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia? Are you paying me back for something? If you are paying me back, I will return your payment on your own head swiftly and speedily.
4:5	For you have taken my silver and my gold, and have carried my rich treasures into your temples.
4:6	You have sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks in order to remove them far from their own border.
4:7	Behold, I will stir them up from the place to which you have sold them, and I will return your payment on your own head.
4:8	I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hand of the people of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabeans, to a nation far away, for Yahweh has spoken."
4:9	Proclaim this among the nations: Consecrate for war; stir up the mighty men. Let all the men of war draw near; let them come up.
4:10	Beat your plowshares into swords, and your pruning hooks into spears; let the weak say, "I am a warrior."
4:11	Hasten and come, all you surrounding nations, and gather yourselves there. Bring down your warriors, O Yahweh.
4:12	Let the nations stir themselves up and come up to the Valley of Jehoshaphat; for there I will sit to judge all the surrounding nations.
4:13	Put in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe. Go in, tread, for the winepress is full. The vats overflow, for their evil is great.
4:14	Multitudes, multitudes, in the valley of decision! For the day of Yahweh is near in the valley of decision.
4:15	The sun and the moon are darkened, and the stars withdraw their shining.
4:16	Yahweh roars from Zion, and utters his voice from Jerusalem, and the heavens and the earth quake. But Yahweh is a refuge to his people, a stronghold to the people of Israel.
4:17	"So you shall know that I am Yahweh your God, who dwells in Zion, my holy mountain. And Jerusalem shall be holy, and strangers shall never again pass through it.
4:18	And in that day the mountains shall drip sweet wine, and the hills shall flow with milk, and all the streambeds of Judah shall flow with water; and a fountain shall come forth from the house of Yahweh and water the Valley of Shittim.
4:19	Egypt shall become a desolation and Edom a desolate wilderness, for the violence done to the people of Judah, because they have shed innocent blood in their land.
4:20	But Judah shall be inhabited forever, and Jerusalem to all generations.
4:21	I will declare innocent their blood, which I had not declared innocent, for Yahweh dwells in Zion."
