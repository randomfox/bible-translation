39:1	Now Joseph had been brought down to Egypt, and Potiphar, an official of Pharaoh, the captain of the guard, an Egyptian, bought him from the Ishmaelites who had brought him down there.
39:2	Yahweh was with Joseph, and he became a successful man; he was in the house of his Egyptian master.
39:3	His master saw that Yahweh was with him and that Yahweh made everything he did succeed in his hand.
39:4	So Joseph found favor in his eyes and attended him personally; he put him in charge of his household and entrusted to him everything he owned.
39:5	From the time he put him in charge of his house and over all that he owned, Yahweh blessed the Egyptian's house for Joseph's sake; Yahweh's blessing was on everything he owned, in the house and in the field.
39:6	So he left everything he owned in Joseph's hands, and with him there he gave no thought to anything except the food he ate. Now Joseph was well built and handsome.
39:7	After these things his master's wife cast her eyes on Joseph and said, "Lie with me."
39:8	But he refused and said to his master's wife, "Look, with me here my master gives no thought to anything in the house, and he has entrusted to me everything he owns.
39:9	No one in this house is greater than I, and he has withheld nothing from me except you, because you are his wife. How then could I do this great evil and sin against God?"
39:10	Though she spoke to Joseph day after day, he would not listen to her, to lie beside her or to be with her.
39:11	One day he went into the house to do his work, and none of the men of the household was there in the house.
39:12	She caught him by his garment, saying, "Lie with me." But he left his garment in her hand, fled, and went outside.
39:13	When she saw that he had left his garment in her hand and fled outside,
39:14	she called to the men of her household and said to them, "Look, he has brought us a Hebrew to make sport of us! He came in to me to lie with me, and I screamed loudly.
39:15	When he heard me raise my voice and scream, he left his garment beside me, fled, and went outside."
39:16	She kept his garment beside her until his master came home.
39:17	Then she told him the same story: "The Hebrew slave whom you brought us came in to me to make sport of me.
39:18	But when I raised my voice and screamed, he left his garment beside me and fled outside."
39:19	When his master heard the words his wife spoke to him, "This is what your slave did to me," his anger burned.
39:20	Joseph's master took him and put him in the prison, the place where the king's prisoners were confined; and he remained there in the prison.
39:21	But Yahweh was with Joseph and showed him steadfast love, and gave him favor in the eyes of the prison warden.
39:22	The prison warden put all the prisoners who were in the prison in Joseph's charge; whatever was done there, he was the one who did it.
39:23	The prison warden paid no attention to anything in his charge, because Yahweh was with him; and whatever he did, Yahweh made it succeed.
