3:1	Now the serpent was more cunning than any wild animal of the field that Yahweh God had made. He said to the woman, "Did God really say, 'You must not eat from any tree of the garden'?"
3:2	The woman said to the serpent, "We may eat from the fruit of the trees of the garden,
3:3	but about the fruit of the tree that is in the middle of the garden God said, 'You must not eat from it, and you must not touch it, or you will die.'"
3:4	The serpent said to the woman, "You will not surely die.
3:5	For God knows that on the day you eat from it your eyes will be opened, and you will be like God, knowing good and evil."
3:6	When the woman saw that the tree was good for food, and that it was a delight to the eyes, and that the tree was desirable for gaining insight, she took some of its fruit and ate; and she also gave some to her husband, who was with her, and he ate.
3:7	Then the eyes of both of them were opened, and they knew that they were naked; so they sewed fig leaves together and made themselves loincloths.
3:8	They heard the sound of Yahweh God walking about in the garden at the breezy time of the day, and the man and his wife hid themselves from the presence of Yahweh God among the trees of the garden.
3:9	But Yahweh God called to the man and said to him, "Where are you?"
3:10	He said, "I heard the sound of you in the garden, and I was afraid because I was naked, so I hid."
3:11	He said, "Who told you that you were naked? Have you eaten from the tree I commanded you not to eat from?"
3:12	The man said, "The woman whom you put with me, she gave me some from the tree, and I ate."
3:13	Then Yahweh God said to the woman, "What is this you have done?" And the woman said, "The serpent deceived me, and I ate."
3:14	Yahweh God said to the serpent, "Because you have done this, cursed are you above all livestock and above every wild animal of the field. On your belly you will go, and dust you will eat all the days of your life.
3:15	I will put hostility between you and the woman, and between your offspring and her offspring. He will strike your head, and you will strike his heel."
3:16	To the woman he said, "I will greatly increase your pain and your pregnancies; in pain you will bear children. Your desire will be for your husband, and he will rule over you."
3:17	And to Adam he said, "Because you listened to the voice of your wife and ate from the tree about which I commanded you, 'You must not eat from it,' cursed is the ground because of you; in painful toil you will eat from it all the days of your life.
3:18	Thorns and thistles it will sprout for you, and you will eat the plants of the field.
3:19	By the sweat of your face you will eat bread until you return to the ground, for out of it you were taken; for dust you are, and to dust you will return."
3:20	The man named his wife Eve, because she became the mother of all the living.
3:21	And Yahweh God made garments of skin for Adam and his wife and clothed them.
3:22	Then Yahweh God said, "Look, the man has become like one of us, knowing good and evil. And now, he must not reach out his hand and take also from the tree of life and eat and live forever."
3:23	So Yahweh God sent him out from the garden of Eden to work the ground from which he had been taken.
3:24	He drove out the man, and east of the garden of Eden he stationed the cherubim and the flame of the whirling sword to guard the way to the tree of life.
