5:1	This is the record of the generations of Adam. On the day God created humankind, he made them in the likeness of God.
5:2	Male and female he created them, and he blessed them and named them "Humankind" on the day they were created.
5:3	When Adam had lived a hundred and thirty years, he fathered a son in his own likeness, according to his image, and named him Seth.
5:4	After he fathered Seth, Adam lived eight hundred years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
5:5	So all the days that Adam lived were nine hundred and thirty years, and he died.
5:6	When Seth had lived a hundred and five years, he fathered Enosh.
5:7	After he fathered Enosh, Seth lived eight hundred and seven years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
5:8	So all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years, and he died.
5:9	When Enosh had lived ninety years, he fathered Kenan.
5:10	After he fathered Kenan, Enosh lived eight hundred and fifteen years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
5:11	So all the days of Enosh were nine hundred and five years, and he died.
5:12	When Kenan had lived seventy years, he fathered Mahalalel.
5:13	After he fathered Mahalalel, Kenan lived eight hundred and forty years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
5:14	So all the days of Kenan were nine hundred and ten years, and he died.
5:15	When Mahalalel had lived sixty-five years, he fathered Jared.
5:16	After he fathered Jared, Mahalalel lived eight hundred and thirty years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
5:17	So all the days of Mahalalel were eight hundred and ninety-five years, and he died.
5:18	When Jared had lived a hundred and sixty-two years, he fathered Enoch.
5:19	After he fathered Enoch, Jared lived eight hundred years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
5:20	So all the days of Jared were nine hundred and sixty-two years, and he died.
5:21	When Enoch had lived sixty-five years, he fathered Methuselah.
5:22	After he fathered Methuselah, Enoch walked with God three hundred years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
5:23	So all the days of Enoch were three hundred and sixty-five years.
5:24	Enoch walked with God, and then he was no more, because God took him.
5:25	When Methuselah had lived a hundred and eighty-seven years, he fathered Lamech.
5:26	After he fathered Lamech, Methuselah lived seven hundred and eighty-two years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
5:27	So all the days of Methuselah were nine hundred and sixty-nine years, and he died.
5:28	When Lamech had lived a hundred and eighty-two years, he fathered a son.
5:29	He named him Noah, saying, "This one will comfort us in our work and in the painful toil of our hands, arising from the ground that Yahweh has cursed."
5:30	After he fathered Noah, Lamech lived five hundred and ninety-five years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
5:31	So all the days of Lamech were seven hundred and seventy-seven years, and he died.
5:32	When Noah was five hundred years old, Noah fathered Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
