11:1	Now the whole earth had one language and the same words.
11:2	As people journeyed in the east, they found a plain in the land of Shinar and settled there.
11:3	They said to one another, "Come, let us make bricks and fire them thoroughly." So they had brick for stone, and bitumen for mortar.
11:4	Then they said, "Come, let us build ourselves a city and a tower with its top in the heavens, and let us make a name for ourselves, so that we are not scattered over the face of the whole earth."
11:5	Yahweh came down to see the city and the tower that the humans had built.
11:6	And Yahweh said, "Look, they are one people, and they all have one language, and this is only the beginning of what they will do; now nothing they plan to do will be beyond them.
11:7	Come, let us go down and confuse their language there, so that they will not understand one another's speech."
11:8	So Yahweh scattered them from there over the face of the whole earth, and they stopped building the city.
11:9	That is why it was called Babel, because there Yahweh confused the language of the whole earth, and from there Yahweh scattered them over the face of the whole earth.
11:10	These are the generations of Shem. When Shem was a hundred years old, he fathered Arpachshad, two years after the flood.
11:11	After he fathered Arpachshad, Shem lived five hundred years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
11:12	When Arpachshad had lived thirty-five years, he fathered Shelah.
11:13	After he fathered Shelah, Arpachshad lived four hundred and three years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
11:14	When Shelah had lived thirty years, he fathered Eber.
11:15	After he fathered Eber, Shelah lived four hundred and three years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
11:16	When Eber had lived thirty-four years, he fathered Peleg.
11:17	After he fathered Peleg, Eber lived four hundred and thirty years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
11:18	When Peleg had lived thirty years, he fathered Reu.
11:19	After he fathered Reu, Peleg lived two hundred and nine years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
11:20	When Reu had lived thirty-two years, he fathered Serug.
11:21	After he fathered Serug, Reu lived two hundred and seven years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
11:22	When Serug had lived thirty years, he fathered Nahor.
11:23	After he fathered Nahor, Serug lived two hundred years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
11:24	When Nahor had lived twenty-nine years, he fathered Terah.
11:25	After he fathered Terah, Nahor lived a hundred and nineteen years, and he fathered other sons and daughters.
11:26	When Terah had lived seventy years, he fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran.
11:27	These are the generations of Terah. Terah fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran; and Haran fathered Lot.
11:28	Haran died in the lifetime of his father Terah, in the land of his birth, in Ur of the Chaldeans.
11:29	Abram and Nahor took wives for themselves. The name of Abram's wife was Sarai, and the name of Nahor's wife was Milcah, the daughter of Haran, the father of Milcah and Iscah.
11:30	Now Sarai was barren; she had no child.
11:31	Terah took Abram his son, and Lot the son of Haran, his grandson, and Sarai his daughter-in-law, the wife of his son Abram, and they set out together from Ur of the Chaldeans to go to the land of Canaan; but when they came to Haran, they settled there.
11:32	The days of Terah were two hundred and five years, and Terah died in Haran.
