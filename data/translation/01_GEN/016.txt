16:1	Now Sarai, Abram's wife, had borne him no children. She had an Egyptian slave woman whose name was Hagar.
16:2	So Sarai said to Abram, "Look, Yahweh has kept me from bearing children. Please go in to my slave woman; perhaps I can build a family through her." And Abram listened to what Sarai said.
16:3	So after Abram had lived ten years in the land of Canaan, Sarai, Abram's wife, took Hagar the Egyptian, her slave woman, and gave her to Abram her husband as a wife.
16:4	He went in to Hagar, and she conceived. When she saw that she had conceived, her mistress became contemptible in her eyes.
16:5	Then Sarai said to Abram, "May the wrong done to me be on you! I put my slave woman in your arms, and now that she sees she has conceived, I am contemptible in her eyes. May Yahweh judge between me and you!"
16:6	Abram said to Sarai, "Look, your slave woman is in your hands; do to her whatever you think best." Then Sarai mistreated her, and she ran away from her.
16:7	The angel of Yahweh found her by a spring of water in the wilderness, the spring on the road to Shur.
16:8	He said, "Hagar, slave of Sarai, where have you come from, and where are you going?" She said, "I am running away from my mistress Sarai."
16:9	The angel of Yahweh said to her, "Go back to your mistress and submit to her authority."
16:10	The angel of Yahweh also said to her, "I will greatly multiply your offspring, so that they will be too many to count."
16:11	And the angel of Yahweh said to her, "Look, you are pregnant and will bear a son, and you will name him Ishmael, for Yahweh has heard of your affliction.
16:12	He will be a wild donkey of a man; his hand will be against everyone, and everyone's hand against him, and he will live in defiance of all his brothers."
16:13	So she called the name of Yahweh who spoke to her, "You are a God who sees me," for she said, "Have I really seen here the one who sees me?"
16:14	That is why the well was called Beer-lahai-roi; it lies between Kadesh and Bered.
16:15	Hagar bore Abram a son, and Abram named his son, whom Hagar bore, Ishmael.
16:16	Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore Ishmael to Abram.
