7:1	Then Yahweh said to Noah, "Go into the ark, you and all your household, for I have seen that you are righteous before me in this generation.
7:2	Of every clean animal take with you seven pairs, a male and its mate, and of the animals that are not clean, one pair, a male and its mate;
7:3	also of the birds of the sky seven pairs, male and female, to keep their offspring alive on the face of all the earth.
7:4	For in seven more days I will send rain on the earth forty days and forty nights, and I will wipe out from the face of the ground every living thing that I have made."
7:5	And Noah did all that Yahweh commanded him.
7:6	Noah was six hundred years old when the flood came, waters upon the earth.
7:7	Noah went into the ark, and his sons and his wife and his sons' wives with him, to escape the waters of the flood.
7:8	Of the clean animals and of the animals that are not clean, and of the birds, and of everything that creeps on the ground,
7:9	two by two they came to Noah into the ark, male and female, as God had commanded Noah.
7:10	And after the seven days the waters of the flood came upon the earth.
7:11	In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on that day all the springs of the great deep burst open, and the windows of the heavens were opened.
7:12	And rain fell on the earth forty days and forty nights.
7:13	On that very day Noah, and Shem, Ham, and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and Noah's wife and the three wives of his sons with them, went into the ark,
7:14	they and every wild animal after its kind, and all livestock after their kind, and every creeping thing that creeps on the earth after its kind, and every bird after its kind, every winged creature of every sort.
7:15	They came to Noah into the ark, two by two of all flesh in which there is the breath of life.
7:16	Those that entered, male and female of all flesh, went in as God had commanded him. Then Yahweh shut him in.
7:17	The flood continued forty days on the earth; the waters increased and lifted up the ark, and it rose high above the earth.
7:18	The waters prevailed and increased greatly on the earth, and the ark floated on the surface of the waters.
7:19	The waters prevailed so greatly over the earth that all the high mountains under the whole heavens were covered.
7:20	The waters prevailed fifteen cubits higher, and the mountains were covered.
7:21	And all flesh that moved on the earth perished: birds, livestock, wild animals, all the swarming things that swarm on the earth, and all humankind.
7:22	Everything on dry land that had the breath of the spirit of life in its nostrils died.
7:23	He wiped out every living thing that was on the face of the ground, from people to animals to creeping things to birds of the sky; they were wiped out from the earth. Only Noah was left, and those who were with him in the ark.
7:24	And the waters prevailed over the earth a hundred and fifty days.
