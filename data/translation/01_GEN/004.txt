4:1	Now the man knew Eve his wife, and she conceived and gave birth to Cain, and she said, "I have gotten a man with the help of Yahweh."
4:2	Then she gave birth again, to his brother Abel. Abel became a keeper of sheep, while Cain worked the ground.
4:3	In the course of time Cain brought some of the fruit of the ground as an offering to Yahweh.
4:4	And Abel too brought some of the firstborn of his flock and of their fat portions. Yahweh looked with favour on Abel and his offering,
4:5	but on Cain and his offering he did not look with favour. So Cain was very angry, and his face fell.
4:6	Yahweh said to Cain, "Why are you angry, and why has your face fallen?
4:7	If you do well, will there not be a lifting up? But if you do not do well, sin is crouching at the door; its desire is for you, but you must master it."
4:8	Cain spoke to Abel his brother. And when they were in the field, Cain rose up against Abel his brother and killed him.
4:9	Then Yahweh said to Cain, "Where is Abel your brother?" He said, "I do not know. Am I my brother's keeper?"
4:10	And he said, "What have you done? The voice of your brother's blood is crying out to me from the ground.
4:11	And now you are cursed from the ground, which opened its mouth to receive your brother's blood from your hand.
4:12	When you work the ground, it will no longer yield its strength to you. You will be a restless wanderer on the earth."
4:13	Cain said to Yahweh, "My punishment is too great to bear.
4:14	Look, today you have driven me from the face of the ground, and from your face I will be hidden. I will be a restless wanderer on the earth, and whoever finds me will kill me."
4:15	But Yahweh said to him, "Very well then, whoever kills Cain will suffer vengeance sevenfold." And Yahweh put a sign on Cain, so that no one who found him would strike him down.
4:16	Then Cain went out from the presence of Yahweh and settled in the land of Nod, east of Eden.
4:17	Cain knew his wife, and she conceived and gave birth to Enoch. He was building a city, and he named the city after the name of his son, Enoch.
4:18	To Enoch was born Irad, and Irad fathered Mehujael, and Mehujael fathered Methushael, and Methushael fathered Lamech.
4:19	Lamech took two wives; the name of the one was Adah, and the name of the second was Zillah.
4:20	Adah gave birth to Jabal; he was the father of those who live in tents and keep livestock.
4:21	His brother's name was Jubal; he was the father of all who play the lyre and pipe.
4:22	Zillah too gave birth, to Tubal-cain, who forged all kinds of tools of bronze and iron. And the sister of Tubal-cain was Naamah.
4:23	Lamech said to his wives, "Adah and Zillah, listen to my voice; wives of Lamech, give ear to my words: I have killed a man for wounding me, a young man for striking me.
4:24	If Cain is avenged sevenfold, then Lamech seventy-sevenfold."
4:25	Adam knew his wife again, and she gave birth to a son and named him Seth, "for God has appointed me another offspring in place of Abel, because Cain killed him."
4:26	To Seth also a son was born, and he named him Enosh. At that time people began to call on the name of Yahweh.
