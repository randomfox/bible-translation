27:1	When Isaac was old and his eyes were too dim to see, he called Esau his older son and said to him, "My son." And he said to him, "Here I am."
27:2	He said, "Look, I am old; I do not know the day of my death.
27:3	Now then, take your gear, your quiver and your bow, and go out into the open country and hunt game for me.
27:4	Then make me a tasty dish such as I love, and bring it to me so that I may eat, so that I may bless you before I die."
27:5	Now Rebekah was listening when Isaac spoke to his son Esau. So when Esau went to the open country to hunt for game and bring it,
27:6	Rebekah said to her son Jacob, "I have just heard your father speaking to your brother Esau, saying,
27:7	'Bring me game and make me a tasty dish so that I may eat, and I will bless you before Yahweh before I die.'
27:8	Now, my son, listen to my voice as I command you.
27:9	Go to the flock and get me two good young goats from there, and I will make them into a tasty dish for your father, such as he loves.
27:10	Then you shall bring it to your father so that he may eat, so that he may bless you before he dies."
27:11	But Jacob said to Rebekah his mother, "Look, my brother Esau is a hairy man, and I am a smooth man.
27:12	Perhaps my father will feel me, and I will seem to him to be mocking him, and I will bring a curse on myself and not a blessing."
27:13	His mother said to him, "Let your curse be on me, my son; only listen to my voice, and go, get them for me."
27:14	So he went and got them and brought them to his mother, and his mother made a tasty dish such as his father loved.
27:15	Then Rebekah took the best garments of Esau her older son, which she had with her in the house, and put them on Jacob her younger son.
27:16	And she put the skins of the young goats on his hands and on the smooth part of his neck.
27:17	And she put the tasty dish and the bread that she had prepared into the hand of her son Jacob.
27:18	He went in to his father and said, "My father." He said, "Here I am. Who are you, my son?"
27:19	Jacob said to his father, "I am Esau your firstborn. I have done as you told me. Please sit up and eat some of my game, so that you may bless me."
27:20	But Isaac said to his son, "How is it that you found it so quickly, my son?" He said, "Because Yahweh your God made it happen for me."
27:21	Then Isaac said to Jacob, "Come near, so that I may feel you, my son, to know whether you really are my son Esau or not."
27:22	So Jacob came near to Isaac his father, and he felt him and said, "The voice is Jacob's voice, but the hands are the hands of Esau."
27:23	He did not recognize him, because his hands were hairy like his brother Esau's hands. So he blessed him.
27:24	He said, "Are you really my son Esau?" He said, "I am."
27:25	Then he said, "Bring it to me, so that I may eat of my son's game and bless you." So he brought it to him, and he ate; and he brought him wine, and he drank.
27:26	Then his father Isaac said to him, "Come near and kiss me, my son."
27:27	So he came near and kissed him, and he smelled the smell of his garments and blessed him and said, "See, the smell of my son is like the smell of a field that Yahweh has blessed.
27:28	May God give you of the dew of heaven and of the fatness of the earth, and plenty of grain and new wine.
27:29	Let peoples serve you, and nations bow down to you. Be lord over your brothers, and may your mother's sons bow down to you. Cursed be those who curse you, and blessed be those who bless you."
27:30	As soon as Isaac had finished blessing Jacob, and Jacob had scarcely gone out from the presence of Isaac his father, Esau his brother came in from his hunting.
27:31	He too made a tasty dish and brought it to his father. And he said to his father, "Let my father sit up and eat of his son's game, so that you may bless me."
27:32	His father Isaac said to him, "Who are you?" He said, "I am your son, your firstborn, Esau."
27:33	Then Isaac trembled violently and said, "Who then was it that hunted game and brought it to me, and I ate of it all before you came, and I blessed him? Yes, and blessed he shall be."
27:34	When Esau heard his father's words, he cried out with a great and exceedingly bitter cry and said to his father, "Bless me, me also, my father!"
27:35	But he said, "Your brother came with deceit and has taken away your blessing."
27:36	Esau said, "Is he not rightly named Jacob? For he has cheated me these two times. He took my birthright, and now he has taken my blessing." Then he said, "Have you not reserved a blessing for me?"
27:37	Isaac answered and said to Esau, "I have made him lord over you, and all his brothers I have given to him as servants, and with grain and new wine I have sustained him. What then can I do for you, my son?"
27:38	Esau said to his father, "Have you only one blessing, my father? Bless me, me also, my father." And Esau lifted up his voice and wept.
27:39	Then Isaac his father answered and said to him, "See, away from the fatness of the earth shall your dwelling be, and away from the dew of heaven above.
27:40	By your sword you shall live, and you shall serve your brother; but when you grow restless, you shall break his yoke from off your neck."
27:41	Esau held a grudge against Jacob because of the blessing with which his father had blessed him, and Esau said to himself, "The days of mourning for my father are approaching; then I will kill my brother Jacob."
27:42	But the words of Esau her older son were told to Rebekah. So she sent and called Jacob her younger son and said to him, "Your brother Esau is consoling himself by planning to kill you.
27:43	Now, my son, listen to my voice: get up and flee to Laban my brother in Haran,
27:44	and stay with him a while, until your brother's fury subsides,
27:45	until your brother's anger turns away from you and he forgets what you have done to him. Then I will send and bring you from there. Why should I be bereaved of you both in one day?"
27:46	Then Rebekah said to Isaac, "I loathe my life because of the Hittite women. If Jacob takes a wife from the Hittite women like these, from the women of the land, what good will my life be to me?"
