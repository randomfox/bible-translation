15:1	After these things the word of Yahweh came to Abram in a vision: "Do not be afraid, Abram. I am your shield; your reward will be very great."
15:2	But Abram said, "Lord Yahweh, what will You give me, since I go on childless, and the heir of my household is Eliezer of Damascus?"
15:3	And Abram said, "Look, You have given me no offspring, and a member of my household will be my heir."
15:4	Then the word of Yahweh came to him: "This man will not be your heir; but one who comes from your own body will be your heir."
15:5	He brought him outside and said, "Look up at the sky and count the stars, if you are able to count them." Then He said to him, "So shall your offspring be."
15:6	And he trusted in Yahweh, and He counted it to him as righteousness.
15:7	He said to him, "I am Yahweh Who brought you out of Ur of the Chaldeans to give you this land to possess."
15:8	But he said, "Lord Yahweh, how am I to know that I will possess it?"
15:9	He said to him, "Bring Me a three-year-old heifer, a three-year-old female goat, a three-year-old ram, a turtledove, and a young pigeon."
15:10	He brought him all these and cut them in two, and laid each half opposite the other; but he did not cut the birds in two.
15:11	When birds of prey came down on the carcasses, Abram drove them away.
15:12	As the sun was going down, a deep sleep fell on Abram, and a terror, a great darkness, fell on him.
15:13	Then He said to Abram, "Know for certain that your offspring will be foreigners in a land that is not theirs, and they will be enslaved and oppressed for four hundred years.
15:14	But I will judge the nation they serve, and afterward they will come out with great possessions.
15:15	As for you, you will go to your fathers in peace; you will be buried at a good old age.
15:16	In the fourth generation they will return here, for the iniquity of the Amorites is not yet complete."
15:17	When the sun had gone down and it was dark, a smoking firepot and a flaming torch passed between these pieces.
15:18	On that day Yahweh made a covenant with Abram, saying, "To your offspring I give this land, from the river of Egypt to the great river, the river Euphrates:
15:19	the land of the Kenites, the Kenizzites, the Kadmonites,
15:20	the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Rephaim,
15:21	the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Girgashites, and the Jebusites."
