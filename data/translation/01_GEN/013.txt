13:1	So Abram went up from Egypt into the Negev, he and his wife and all that he had, and Lot with him.
13:2	Now Abram was very rich in livestock, in silver, and in gold.
13:3	He went on by stages from the Negev as far as Bethel, to the place where his tent had been at the beginning, between Bethel and Ai,
13:4	to the place of the altar that he had made there at first; and there Abram called on the name of Yahweh.
13:5	Lot, who was travelling with Abram, also had flocks and herds and tents,
13:6	so that the land could not support them living together; their possessions were so great that they could not live together.
13:7	And there was quarrelling between the herdsmen of Abram's livestock and the herdsmen of Lot's livestock. At that time the Canaanites and the Perizzites were living in the land.
13:8	Abram said to Lot, "Please, let there be no quarrel between me and you, or between my herdsmen and your herdsmen, for we are kinsmen.
13:9	Is not the whole land before you? Please separate from me. If you go to the left, I will go to the right; and if you go to the right, I will go to the left."
13:10	Lot looked up and saw that the whole plain of the Jordan was well watered everywhere, like the garden of Yahweh, like the land of Egypt, as far as Zoar; this was before Yahweh destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah.
13:11	So Lot chose for himself the whole plain of the Jordan, and Lot journeyed east; thus they separated from each other.
13:12	Abram settled in the land of Canaan, while Lot settled among the cities of the plain and moved his tent as far as Sodom.
13:13	Now the men of Sodom were wicked, great sinners against Yahweh.
13:14	Yahweh said to Abram after Lot had separated from him, "Look up and gaze from the place where you are, northward and southward, eastward and westward,
13:15	for all the land that you see I will give to you and to your offspring forever.
13:16	I will make your offspring like the dust of the earth, so that if anyone could count the dust of the earth, your offspring too could be counted.
13:17	Get up, walk through the length and breadth of the land, for I will give it to you."
13:18	So Abram moved his tent and came and settled by the oaks of Mamre, which are at Hebron, and there he built an altar to Yahweh.
