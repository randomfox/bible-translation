45:1	Then Joseph could no longer control himself in front of all who stood by him, and he cried out, "Make everyone leave my presence!" So no one stayed with him when Joseph made himself known to his brothers.
45:2	He wept so loudly that the Egyptians heard it, and the household of Pharaoh heard it.
45:3	Joseph said to his brothers, "I am Joseph! Is my father still alive?" But his brothers could not answer him, for they were terrified at his presence.
45:4	Then Joseph said to his brothers, "Please come near to me." And they came near. He said, "I am your brother Joseph, whom you sold into Egypt.
45:5	And now do not be distressed or angry with yourselves because you sold me here, for God sent me ahead of you to preserve life.
45:6	For the famine has been in the land these two years, and there are still five years in which there will be neither plowing nor harvest.
45:7	God sent me ahead of you to preserve for you a remnant on the earth and to keep you alive by a great deliverance.
45:8	So it was not you who sent me here, but God. He has made me a father to Pharaoh, lord of all his household, and ruler over all the land of Egypt.
45:9	Hurry and go up to my father and say to him, 'Thus says your son Joseph: God has made me lord of all Egypt. Come down to me; do not delay.
45:10	You shall live in the land of Goshen and be near me, you and your sons and your sons' sons, your flocks and your herds, and all that you have.
45:11	I will provide for you there, for there are still five years of famine to come, so that you, your household, and all that you have will not fall into poverty.'
45:12	Your eyes can see, and the eyes of my brother Benjamin, that it is my own mouth speaking to you.
45:13	Tell my father about all my honor in Egypt and about all that you have seen, and hurry and bring my father down here."
45:14	Then he fell on his brother Benjamin's neck and wept, and Benjamin wept on his neck.
45:15	He kissed all his brothers and wept over them, and after that his brothers talked with him.
45:16	The news was heard in Pharaoh's house: "Joseph's brothers have come." And it pleased Pharaoh and his servants.
45:17	Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Tell your brothers, 'Do this: load your animals and go back to the land of Canaan,
45:18	and take your father and your households and come to me, and I will give you the best of the land of Egypt, and you shall eat the fat of the land.'
45:19	And you are commanded to say, 'Do this: take wagons from the land of Egypt for your little ones and your wives, and bring your father, and come.
45:20	Do not be concerned about your belongings, for the best of all the land of Egypt is yours.'"
45:21	The sons of Israel did so. Joseph gave them wagons, as Pharaoh had ordered, and gave them provisions for the journey.
45:22	To each of them he gave a change of clothes, but to Benjamin he gave three hundred pieces of silver and five changes of clothes.
45:23	And to his father he sent the following: ten donkeys loaded with the good things of Egypt, and ten female donkeys loaded with grain, bread, and provisions for his father for the journey.
45:24	Then he sent his brothers away, and as they left he said to them, "Do not quarrel on the way."
45:25	So they went up out of Egypt and came to the land of Canaan, to their father Jacob.
45:26	They told him, "Joseph is still alive, and he is ruler over all the land of Egypt." But his heart went numb, for he did not believe them.
45:27	But when they told him all the words that Joseph had spoken to them, and when he saw the wagons that Joseph had sent to carry him, the spirit of their father Jacob revived.
45:28	And Israel said, "Enough! My son Joseph is still alive. I will go and see him before I die."
