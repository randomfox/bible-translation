41:1	At the end of two full years, Pharaoh dreamed that he was standing by the Nile.
41:2	Up out of the Nile came seven cows, sleek and fat, and they grazed among the reeds.
41:3	Then seven other cows came up after them out of the Nile, ugly and thin, and stood beside the other cows on the bank of the Nile.
41:4	And the ugly, thin cows ate up the seven sleek, fat cows. Then Pharaoh woke up.
41:5	He fell asleep and dreamed a second time: seven ears of grain, plump and good, were growing on a single stalk.
41:6	Then seven ears, thin and scorched by the east wind, sprouted after them.
41:7	And the thin ears swallowed up the seven plump, full ears. Then Pharaoh woke up, and it was a dream.
41:8	In the morning his spirit was troubled, and he sent and summoned all the magicians of Egypt and all its wise men. Pharaoh told them his dreams, but there was no one who could interpret them for Pharaoh.
41:9	Then the chief cupbearer spoke to Pharaoh: "Today I must recall my offenses.
41:10	Pharaoh was angry with his servants and put me in custody in the house of the captain of the guard, me and the chief baker.
41:11	We dreamed dreams on the same night, he and I, each of us dreaming a dream with its own interpretation.
41:12	A young Hebrew was there with us, a slave of the captain of the guard. We told him, and he interpreted our dreams for us, giving each man the interpretation of his dream.
41:13	And just as he interpreted for us, so it happened: I was restored to my post, and he was hanged."
41:14	Then Pharaoh sent and summoned Joseph, and they quickly brought him out of the pit. He shaved, changed his clothes, and came in to Pharaoh.
41:15	Pharaoh said to Joseph, "I have dreamed a dream, and there is no one who can interpret it. I have heard it said of you that when you hear a dream you can interpret it."
41:16	Joseph answered Pharaoh, "It is not in me; God will give Pharaoh an answer for his welfare."
41:17	Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, "In my dream I was standing on the bank of the Nile.
41:18	Up out of the Nile came seven cows, fat and sleek, and they grazed among the reeds.
41:19	Then seven other cows came up after them, poor and very ugly and lean; I have never seen such ugly ones in all the land of Egypt.
41:20	The lean, ugly cows ate up the first seven fat cows,
41:21	but when they had swallowed them, no one could tell that they had swallowed them, for they looked just as ugly as before. Then I woke up.
41:22	I also saw in my dream seven ears of grain, full and good, growing on a single stalk.
41:23	Then seven ears, withered, thin, and scorched by the east wind, sprouted after them,
41:24	and the thin ears swallowed up the seven good ears. I told it to the magicians, but there was no one who could explain it to me."
41:25	Joseph said to Pharaoh, "Pharaoh's dreams are one and the same. God has told Pharaoh what He is about to do.
41:26	The seven good cows are seven years, and the seven good ears are seven years; it is one dream.
41:27	The seven lean, ugly cows that came up after them are seven years, and the seven empty ears scorched by the east wind are seven years of famine.
41:28	It is as I told Pharaoh: God has shown Pharaoh what He is about to do.
41:29	Seven years of great plenty are coming throughout all the land of Egypt,
41:30	but after them seven years of famine will arise, and all the plenty in the land of Egypt will be forgotten, and the famine will consume the land.
41:31	The plenty will be unknown in the land because of the famine that follows, for it will be very severe.
41:32	As for the dream being repeated to Pharaoh twice, it means that the matter is firmly decided by God, and God will soon bring it about.
41:33	Now therefore let Pharaoh look for a discerning and wise man and set him over the land of Egypt.
41:34	Let Pharaoh act and appoint overseers over the land, and take a fifth of the produce of the land of Egypt during the seven years of plenty.
41:35	Let them gather all the food of these good years that are coming and store up grain under Pharaoh's authority as food in the cities, and let them guard it.
41:36	That food will be a reserve for the land against the seven years of famine that will come upon the land of Egypt, so that the land may not be wiped out by the famine."
41:37	The proposal seemed good to Pharaoh and to all his servants.
41:38	Pharaoh said to his servants, "Can we find anyone like this, a man in whom is the spirit of God?"
41:39	Then Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Since God has made all this known to you, there is no one as discerning and wise as you.
41:40	You shall be over my house, and all my people shall be governed by your word; only with regard to the throne will I be greater than you."
41:41	Pharaoh said to Joseph, "See, I have set you over all the land of Egypt."
41:42	Then Pharaoh took his signet ring from his hand and put it on Joseph's hand, clothed him in garments of fine linen, and put the gold chain around his neck.
41:43	He had him ride in the chariot of his second-in-command, and they cried out before him, "Abrek!" Thus he set him over all the land of Egypt.
41:44	Pharaoh said to Joseph, "I am Pharaoh, but without your consent no one shall lift hand or foot in all the land of Egypt."
41:45	Pharaoh gave Joseph the name Zaphenath-paneah, and he gave him Asenath the daughter of Potiphera, priest of On, as his wife. And Joseph went out over the land of Egypt.
41:46	Joseph was thirty years old when he entered the service of Pharaoh king of Egypt. Joseph went out from Pharaoh's presence and traveled through all the land of Egypt.
41:47	During the seven years of plenty the land produced abundantly.
41:48	He gathered all the food of the seven years that came upon the land of Egypt and put the food in the cities; in each city he put the food from the fields around it.
41:49	Joseph stored up grain in great abundance, like the sand of the sea, until he stopped measuring it, because it was beyond measure.
41:50	Before the year of famine came, two sons were born to Joseph, whom Asenath the daughter of Potiphera, priest of On, bore to him.
41:51	Joseph named the firstborn Manasseh, "For God has made me forget all my hardship and all my father's house."
41:52	The second he named Ephraim, "For God has made me fruitful in the land of my affliction."
41:53	The seven years of plenty that had been in the land of Egypt came to an end,
41:54	and the seven years of famine began to come, just as Joseph had said. There was famine in all the lands, but in all the land of Egypt there was bread.
41:55	When all the land of Egypt became hungry, the people cried to Pharaoh for bread. Pharaoh said to all the Egyptians, "Go to Joseph; do whatever he tells you."
41:56	The famine spread over the whole face of the earth. Joseph opened all the storehouses and sold grain to the Egyptians, for the famine grew severe in the land of Egypt.
41:57	And all the world came to Egypt to Joseph to buy grain, because the famine was severe over all the earth.
