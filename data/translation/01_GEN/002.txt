2:1	So the heavens and the earth were completed, and all their host.
2:2	By the seventh day God had finished the work that He had done, and He rested on the seventh day from all the work that He had done.
2:3	God blessed the seventh day and made it holy, because on it He rested from all His work that God had created and made.
2:4	These are the generations of the heavens and the earth when they were created. On the day that Yahweh God made earth and heavens,
2:5	no shrub of the field was yet on the earth and no plant of the field had yet sprouted, for Yahweh God had not sent rain on the earth, and there was no human to work the ground.
2:6	But a stream would rise from the earth and water the whole surface of the ground.
2:7	Then Yahweh God formed the man from the dust of the ground and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, and the man became a living being.
2:8	And Yahweh God planted a garden in Eden, in the east, and there He put the man whom He had formed.
2:9	Out of the ground Yahweh God made every tree grow that is pleasing to the sight and good for food, with the tree of life in the middle of the garden, and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.
2:10	A river flows out of Eden to water the garden, and from there it divides and becomes four branches.
2:11	The name of the first is Pishon; it is the one that winds through the whole land of Havilah, where there is gold.
2:12	The gold of that land is good; bdellium and onyx stone are there.
2:13	The name of the second river is Gihon; it is the one that winds through the whole land of Cush.
2:14	The name of the third river is Tigris; it is the one that flows east of Assyria. And the fourth river is the Euphrates.
2:15	Yahweh God took the man and settled him in the garden of Eden to work it and to keep it.
2:16	And Yahweh God commanded the man, saying, "From every tree of the garden you may freely eat,
2:17	but from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil you must not eat, for on the day that you eat from it you will surely die."
2:18	Then Yahweh God said, "It is not good for the man to be alone. I will make him a helper corresponding to him."
2:19	So out of the ground Yahweh God formed every wild animal of the field and every bird of the sky, and He brought them to the man to see what he would call each one; and whatever the man called each living creature, that was its name.
2:20	The man gave names to all the livestock and to the birds of the sky and to every wild animal of the field, but for the man there was not found a helper corresponding to him.
2:21	So Yahweh God caused a deep sleep to fall on the man, and he slept. Then He took one of his ribs and closed up the flesh in its place.
2:22	And Yahweh God built the rib that He had taken from the man into a woman and brought her to the man.
2:23	The man said, "This one at last is bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh; this one shall be called Woman, because from Man this one was taken."
2:24	That is why a man leaves his father and his mother and clings to his wife, and they become one flesh.
2:25	And the two of them were naked, the man and his wife, and they were not ashamed.
