6:1	When humankind began to multiply on the face of the ground and daughters were born to them,
6:2	the sons of God saw that the daughters of humankind were beautiful, and they took as wives for themselves any they chose.
6:3	Then Yahweh said, "My spirit will not remain in humankind forever, since they are indeed flesh; their days will be a hundred and twenty years."
6:4	The Nephilim were on the earth in those days, and also afterward, when the sons of God went in to the daughters of humankind and they bore children to them. These were the mighty men of old, the men of renown.
6:5	Yahweh saw that the wickedness of humankind was great on the earth, and that every inclination of the thoughts of their heart was only evil all the time.
6:6	And Yahweh regretted that He had made humankind on the earth, and He was grieved to His heart.
6:7	So Yahweh said, "I will wipe out humankind, whom I have created, from the face of the ground, people together with animals, creeping things, and birds of the sky, for I regret that I made them."
6:8	But Noah found favour in the eyes of Yahweh.
6:9	These are the generations of Noah. Noah was a righteous man, blameless among the people of his time; Noah walked with God.
6:10	Noah fathered three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
6:11	Now the earth was corrupt in God's sight, and the earth was filled with violence.
6:12	God looked at the earth, and indeed it was corrupt, for all flesh had corrupted its way on the earth.
6:13	So God said to Noah, "I have decided to put an end to all flesh, for the earth is filled with violence because of them. I am about to destroy them along with the earth.
6:14	Make yourself an ark of gopher wood; make the ark with compartments, and coat it inside and out with pitch.
6:15	This is how you are to make it: the length of the ark three hundred cubits, its width fifty cubits, and its height thirty cubits.
6:16	Make a roof for the ark, and finish it to a cubit from the top; put the door of the ark in its side; make it with lower, second, and third decks.
6:17	For My part, I am about to bring the flood of waters on the earth, to destroy from under the heavens all flesh in which there is the breath of life; everything that is on the earth will perish.
6:18	But I will establish My covenant with you, and you will go into the ark, you and your sons and your wife and your sons' wives with you.
6:19	And of every living thing, of all flesh, you are to bring two of every kind into the ark to keep them alive with you; they are to be male and female.
6:20	Of the birds after their kind, and of the livestock after their kind, of every creeping thing of the ground after its kind, two of every kind will come to you to be kept alive.
6:21	And take for yourself some of every kind of food that is eaten, and store it up; it will serve as food for you and for them."
6:22	Noah did this; he did all that God commanded him.
