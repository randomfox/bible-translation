32:1	Early in the morning Laban rose and kissed his grandsons and his daughters and blessed them. Then Laban departed and returned home.
32:2	Jacob went on his way, and the angels of God met him.
32:3	When Jacob saw them he said, "This is God's camp!" So he called the name of that place Mahanaim.
32:4	Jacob sent messengers ahead of him to Esau his brother in the land of Seir, the country of Edom,
32:5	instructing them, "Thus you shall say to my lord Esau: Thus says your servant Jacob, 'I have lived as a foreigner with Laban and stayed until now.
32:6	I have oxen, donkeys, flocks, male servants, and female servants. I have sent to tell my lord, in order to find favor in your eyes.'"
32:7	The messengers returned to Jacob, saying, "We came to your brother Esau, and he is coming to meet you, and four hundred men are with him."
32:8	Then Jacob was greatly afraid and distressed. He divided the people who were with him, and the flocks and herds and camels, into two camps,
32:9	thinking, "If Esau comes to one camp and attacks it, then the camp that is left will escape."
32:10	And Jacob said, "O God of my father Abraham and God of my father Isaac, Yahweh, who said to me, 'Return to your country and to your kindred, and I will do you good,'
32:11	I am not worthy of the least of all the acts of steadfast love and all the faithfulness that you have shown your servant, for with only my staff I crossed this Jordan, and now I have become two camps.
32:12	Please deliver me from the hand of my brother, from the hand of Esau, for I fear him, that he may come and attack me, the mothers along with the children.
32:13	But you said, 'I will surely do you good and make your offspring as the sand of the sea, which cannot be counted for multitude.'"
32:14	So he stayed there that night, and from what he had with him he took a present for Esau his brother:
32:15	two hundred female goats and twenty male goats, two hundred ewes and twenty rams,
32:16	thirty milking camels and their young, forty cows and ten bulls, twenty female donkeys and ten male donkeys.
32:17	He put them in the charge of his servants, each drove by itself, and said to his servants, "Pass on ahead of me and keep a space between drove and drove."
32:18	He instructed the first, "When Esau my brother meets you and asks you, 'To whom do you belong? Where are you going? And whose are these ahead of you?'
32:19	then you shall say, 'They belong to your servant Jacob. They are a present sent to my lord Esau. And look, he himself is behind us.'"
32:20	He likewise instructed the second and the third and all who followed the droves, "You shall say the same thing to Esau when you find him,
32:21	and you shall say, 'Moreover, your servant Jacob is behind us.'" For he thought, "I may appease him with the present that goes ahead of me, and afterward I shall see his face; perhaps he will accept me."
32:22	So the present passed on ahead of him, while he himself stayed that night in the camp.
32:23	That same night he rose and took his two wives, his two female servants, and his eleven children, and crossed the ford of the Jabbok.
32:24	He took them and sent them across the stream, and sent across everything he had.
32:25	And Jacob was left alone. And a man wrestled with him until the break of dawn.
32:26	When the man saw that he could not prevail against him, he struck the hollow of his thigh, and Jacob's hip was put out of joint as he wrestled with him.
32:27	Then he said, "Let me go, for the dawn has broken." But Jacob said, "I will not let you go unless you bless me."
32:28	He said to him, "What is your name?" And he said, "Jacob."
32:29	Then he said, "Your name shall no longer be called Jacob, but Israel, for you have striven with God and with men, and have prevailed."
32:30	Then Jacob asked him, "Please tell me your name." But he said, "Why is it that you ask my name?" And he blessed him there.
32:31	So Jacob called the name of the place Peniel, saying, "For I have seen God face to face, and yet my life has been spared."
32:32	The sun rose upon him as he passed Penuel, limping because of his hip.
32:33	Therefore to this day the people of Israel do not eat the sinew of the thigh that is on the hollow of the thigh, because he struck Jacob's hip on the sinew of the thigh.
