18:1	Yahweh appeared to him by the oaks of Mamre as he sat at the entrance of the tent in the heat of the day.
18:2	He looked up and saw three men standing near him. When he saw them, he ran from the tent entrance to meet them and bowed to the ground,
18:3	and said, "My lord, if I have found favor in your sight, please do not pass by your servant.
18:4	Let a little water be brought, and wash your feet, and rest yourselves under the tree,
18:5	while I bring a bit of bread so you can refresh yourselves; after that you may go on, since you have come to your servant." They said, "Do as you have said."
18:6	Abraham hurried into the tent to Sarah and said, "Quick! Three seahs of fine flour—knead it and make cakes."
18:7	Abraham ran to the herd, took a calf, tender and good, and gave it to the young man, who hurried to prepare it.
18:8	Then he took curds and milk and the calf that he had prepared and set it before them, and he stood by them under the tree while they ate.
18:9	They said to him, "Where is Sarah your wife?" He said, "There, in the tent."
18:10	He said, "I will surely return to you about this time next year, and Sarah your wife shall have a son." Sarah was listening at the tent entrance, which was behind Him.
18:11	Now Abraham and Sarah were old, advanced in years; the way of women had ceased for Sarah.
18:12	So Sarah laughed to herself, saying, "After I am worn out, shall I have pleasure, and my lord is old?"
18:13	Yahweh said to Abraham, "Why did Sarah laugh and say, 'Shall I really bear a child, now that I am old?'
18:14	Is anything too wonderful for Yahweh? At the appointed time I will return to you, about this time next year, and Sarah shall have a son."
18:15	But Sarah denied it, saying, "I did not laugh," for she was afraid. He said, "No, you did laugh."
18:16	Then the men set out from there and looked down toward Sodom, and Abraham went with them to send them on their way.
18:17	Yahweh said, "Shall I hide from Abraham what I am about to do,
18:18	seeing that Abraham shall surely become a great and mighty nation, and all the nations of the earth shall be blessed in him?
18:19	For I have chosen him, so that he may command his children and his household after him to keep the way of Yahweh by doing righteousness and justice, so that Yahweh may bring to Abraham what He has promised him."
18:20	Then Yahweh said, "The outcry against Sodom and Gomorrah is great indeed, and their sin is very grave.
18:21	I will go down now and see whether they have done altogether according to the outcry that has come to Me; and if not, I will know."
18:22	So the men turned from there and went toward Sodom, while Abraham still stood before Yahweh.
18:23	Abraham drew near and said, "Will You really sweep away the righteous with the wicked?
18:24	Suppose there are fifty righteous within the city. Will You really sweep it away and not spare the place for the sake of the fifty righteous who are in it?
18:25	Far be it from You to do such a thing, to put the righteous to death with the wicked, so that the righteous fare like the wicked! Far be it from You! Shall not the Judge of all the earth do what is just?"
18:26	Yahweh said, "If I find in Sodom fifty righteous in the city, I will spare the whole place for their sake."
18:27	Abraham answered, "Look, I have taken it upon myself to speak to the Lord, though I am but dust and ashes.
18:28	Suppose the fifty righteous lack five. Will You destroy the whole city for lack of five?" He said, "I will not destroy it if I find forty-five there."
18:29	Again he spoke to Him and said, "Suppose forty are found there." He said, "For the sake of the forty I will not do it."
18:30	Then he said, "Let not the Lord be angry, and I will speak. Suppose thirty are found there." He said, "I will not do it if I find thirty there."
18:31	He said, "Look, I have taken it upon myself to speak to the Lord. Suppose twenty are found there." He said, "For the sake of the twenty I will not destroy it."
18:32	Then he said, "Let not the Lord be angry, and I will speak just once more. Suppose ten are found there." He said, "For the sake of the ten I will not destroy it."
18:33	And Yahweh went His way when He had finished speaking to Abraham, and Abraham returned to his place.
