9:1	God blessed Noah and his sons and said to them, "Be fruitful and multiply and fill the earth.
9:2	The fear and dread of you will be on every wild animal of the earth and on every bird of the sky, on everything that creeps on the ground and on all the fish of the sea; into your hand they are given.
9:3	Every moving thing that lives will be food for you; as I gave you the green plants, I now give you everything.
9:4	Only you must not eat flesh with its life, that is, its blood.
9:5	And for your own lifeblood I will surely demand an accounting: from every animal I will demand it, and from every human; from each one for the life of his fellow human I will demand an accounting.
9:6	Whoever sheds the blood of a human, by a human his blood shall be shed, for in the image of God He made humankind.
9:7	As for you, be fruitful and multiply; swarm on the earth and multiply in it."
9:8	Then God said to Noah and to his sons with him,
9:9	"As for Me, I am establishing My covenant with you and with your offspring after you,
9:10	and with every living creature that is with you, the birds, the livestock, and every wild animal of the earth with you, all that came out of the ark, every animal of the earth.
9:11	I establish My covenant with you: never again will all flesh be cut off by the waters of a flood, and never again will there be a flood to destroy the earth."
9:12	And God said, "This is the sign of the covenant that I am making between Me and you and every living creature that is with you, for all generations to come:
9:13	I have set My bow in the clouds, and it will be the sign of the covenant between Me and the earth.
9:14	Whenever I bring clouds over the earth and the bow appears in the clouds,
9:15	I will remember My covenant that is between Me and you and every living creature of all flesh, and the waters will never again become a flood to destroy all flesh.
9:16	When the bow is in the clouds, I will see it and remember the everlasting covenant between God and every living creature of all flesh that is on the earth."
9:17	God said to Noah, "This is the sign of the covenant that I have established between Me and all flesh that is on the earth."
9:18	The sons of Noah who came out of the ark were Shem, Ham, and Japheth. Ham was the father of Canaan.
9:19	These three were the sons of Noah, and from them the whole earth was populated.
9:20	Noah, a man of the soil, was the first to plant a vineyard.
9:21	He drank some of the wine and became drunk, and he lay uncovered inside his tent.
9:22	Ham, the father of Canaan, saw his father's nakedness and told his two brothers outside.
9:23	Then Shem and Japheth took the garment, laid it on both their shoulders, walked backward, and covered their father's nakedness; their faces were turned away, and they did not see their father's nakedness.
9:24	When Noah awoke from his wine and learned what his youngest son had done to him,
9:25	he said, "Cursed be Canaan; the lowest of slaves he shall be to his brothers."
9:26	He also said, "Blessed be Yahweh, the God of Shem; and let Canaan be his slave.
9:27	May God make room for Japheth, and let him dwell in the tents of Shem; and let Canaan be his slave."
9:28	After the flood Noah lived three hundred and fifty years.
9:29	So all the days of Noah were nine hundred and fifty years, and he died.
