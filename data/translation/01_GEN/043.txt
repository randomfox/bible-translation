43:1	Now the famine was severe in the land.
43:2	When they had finished eating the grain they had brought from Egypt, their father said to them, "Go back and buy us a little food."
43:3	But Judah said to him, "The man solemnly warned us, 'You shall not see my face unless your brother is with you.'
43:4	If you will send our brother with us, we will go down and buy you food.
43:5	But if you will not send him, we will not go down, for the man said to us, 'You shall not see my face unless your brother is with you.'"
43:6	Israel said, "Why did you do me such harm by telling the man that you had another brother?"
43:7	They said, "The man questioned us closely about ourselves and our family, saying, 'Is your father still alive? Do you have another brother?' We answered him according to these questions. How could we possibly know that he would say, 'Bring your brother down'?"
43:8	Judah said to Israel his father, "Send the boy with me, and we will get up and go, so that we may live and not die, we and you and our little ones.
43:9	I myself will be surety for him; you may hold me responsible for him. If I do not bring him back to you and set him before you, then let me bear the blame before you forever.
43:10	For if we had not delayed, we could by now have gone there and back twice."
43:11	Then their father Israel said to them, "If it must be so, then do this: take some of the choicest products of the land in your containers and carry a gift down to the man — a little balm and a little honey, gum and resin, pistachio nuts and almonds.
43:12	Take double the silver with you, and take back with you the silver that was returned in the mouths of your bags; perhaps it was an oversight.
43:13	Take your brother too, and get up and go back to the man.
43:14	May God Almighty grant you mercy before the man, so that he may release to you your other brother and Benjamin. As for me, if I am bereaved, I am bereaved."
43:15	So the men took this gift, and they took double the silver with them, and Benjamin. They got up and went down to Egypt and stood before Joseph.
43:16	When Joseph saw Benjamin with them, he said to the steward of his house, "Bring the men into the house, slaughter an animal and prepare it, for the men are to eat with me at noon."
43:17	The man did as Joseph said and brought the men into Joseph's house.
43:18	The men were afraid because they were brought into Joseph's house, and they said, "It is because of the silver that was put back in our bags the first time that we are being brought in, so that he can attack us, fall upon us, and take us as slaves, along with our donkeys."
43:19	So they approached the steward of Joseph's house and spoke to him at the entrance of the house.
43:20	They said, "Please, my lord, we really did come down the first time to buy food.
43:21	When we came to the lodging place and opened our bags, there was each man's silver in the mouth of his bag, our silver in its full weight; so we have brought it back with us.
43:22	We have brought other silver down with us to buy food. We do not know who put our silver in our bags."
43:23	He said, "Peace be to you; do not be afraid. Your God and the God of your father has put treasure in your bags for you; I received your silver." Then he brought Simeon out to them.
43:24	The man brought the men into Joseph's house and gave them water, and they washed their feet, and he gave their donkeys fodder.
43:25	They got the gift ready for Joseph's arrival at noon, for they had heard that they were to eat a meal there.
43:26	When Joseph came home, they brought into the house to him the gift that they had with them, and bowed down to him to the ground.
43:27	He asked them how they were and said, "Is your old father well, of whom you spoke? Is he still alive?"
43:28	They said, "Your servant our father is well; he is still alive." And they bowed their heads and prostrated themselves.
43:29	He looked up and saw his brother Benjamin, his mother's son, and said, "Is this your youngest brother, of whom you spoke to me?" And he said, "May God be gracious to you, my son."
43:30	Then Joseph hurried out, for he was deeply moved over his brother, and he was on the verge of tears. He went into an inner room and wept there.
43:31	Then he washed his face and came out, and controlling himself he said, "Serve the meal."
43:32	They served him by himself, them by themselves, and the Egyptians who ate with him by themselves, because the Egyptians could not eat with the Hebrews, for that is an abomination to the Egyptians.
43:33	They were seated before him, the firstborn according to his birthright and the youngest according to his youth; and the men looked at one another in amazement.
43:34	Portions were carried to them from his table, but Benjamin's portion was five times as much as any of theirs. And they drank and drank freely with him.
