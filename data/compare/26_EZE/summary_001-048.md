# Ezekiel 1–48: summary

- **Coverage:** 48 chapters, 1,273 verses (Hebrew numbering).
- **Flags:** 47 flags on 45 verses.
- **By category:** LEXICAL 23, INTERP 10, NAME 5, MEANING 3, TEXT 3, ARCHAIC 3.
- **By severity:** 42 at sev 1, 5 at sev 2, none at sev 3.

## Systematic differences (not flagged per verse)

- **Divine names:**
  - *Adonai YHWH* ("the Lord Yahweh") appears over 200 times. The KJV gives it as "the Lord GOD"; this translation has "the Lord Yahweh".
  - *YHWH tseva'ot* does not occur in Ezekiel.
- **"Son of man"** (*ben-adam*): about 93 times. Both versions translate it literally.
- **Sheol** (31:15–17, 32:21, 32:27): the KJV has "hell" or "the grave".
- **"Meat offering"** (*minhah*, chs. 42–46): this is the grain offering. "Meat" in 1611 meant "food".
- **Hebrew/KJV numbering:** the chapter divisions differ in ch. 21 (Heb 21:1–5 = KJV 20:45–49).
- **Textual base:** the KJV follows the Masoretic Text closely. Both versions keep the MT wherever the LXX differs (e.g. 40:44 "singers", 41:22, 45:1), so TEXT flags are rare. They are confined to ketiv/qere choices and one 1QIsa-style reading.

## Most important differences

1. **27:6**
   - KJV: "the company of the Ashurites".
   - Mine: "ivory inlaid in cypress".
   - The KJV split *bit'ashurim* ("in cypress wood") into two words and so invented a guild of Assyrian craftsmen.
2. **28:13**
   - KJV: "thy tabrets and … thy pipes".
   - Mine: "your settings and mountings".
   - These are jeweller's terms for the gemstones just listed, not musical instruments. KJV-based readings that give the king of Tyre (later read as Satan) musical talents rest on this.
3. **22:16**
   - KJV: "take thine inheritance in thyself".
   - Mine: "be profaned".
   - The verb is from *halal* ("profane"), not *nahal* ("inherit").
4. **39:2**
   - KJV: "leave but the sixth part of thee".
   - Mine: "drive you forward".
   - This is a hapax that the KJV linked to *shesh* ("six"). No "sixth" survives in the Hebrew.
5. **21:32** (KJV 21:27)
   - KJV: "until he come whose right it is".
   - Mine: "the one to whom judgment belongs".
   - *Mishpat* means judgment. The KJV wording adds a messianic echo of Gen 49:10.
6. **14:14, 28:3**
   - KJV: "Daniel".
   - Mine: "Danel".
   - The consonants *dn'l* point to the legendary sage known from Ugarit, not Ezekiel's younger contemporary.
7. **39:11**
   - KJV: "stop the [noses] of the passengers".
   - Mine: "block the travelers".
   - The KJV's italic supply invents a stench.
8. **29:10, 30:6**
   - KJV: "from the tower of Syene".
   - Mine: "from Migdol to Syene".
   - This is a north-to-south merism naming two towns. The KJV read *migdol* as the common noun "tower".

## Assessment

Ezekiel's KJV is faithful to the Masoretic Text, and no flag reaches severity 3. Most of the divergences come in two places:

- **Rare technical vocabulary:** gems, ship-building, temple architecture, the altar hearth and the Tyre trade list (ch. 27). Seventeenth-century lexicography could only guess at these words.
- **Wrong word divisions or roots:** *bit'ashurim*, *shishshe'tikha*, *venihalt*.

None of these changes the book's theology, but several have fed later speculation, such as the "musical" fallen cherub (28:13). The legal and visionary chapters (18, 33, 36–37, 40–48) come through almost unchanged.

**Caveat:** the comparison checked selected KJV verses against my translation rather than reading every KJV verse, so minor differences may be undercounted.
