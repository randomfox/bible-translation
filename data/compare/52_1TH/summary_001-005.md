# 1 Thessalonians 1–5: summary

**Coverage:** 5 chapters, 89 verses. Same as the KJV.

**Flags:** 7 flags.
- By category: TEXT 3, MEANING 1, INTERP 1, LEXICAL 1, ARCHAIC 1.
- By severity: all 7 at sev 1.

## Systematic

- **Textual base.** Three minor TR differences:
  - an expanded greeting (1:1);
  - "their own prophets" (2:15);
  - a softened "minister of God" in place of the bold "God's coworker" (3:2).

## Most important differences

1. **5:22.** "Abstain from all appearance of evil". *Eidos* means "form" or "kind". Paul says to avoid every kind of evil, not things that merely look bad. The KJV wording has been used to support rules about appearances.
2. **4:15.** "shall not prevent them which are asleep". In 1611 "prevent" meant "go before". A modern reader takes it to mean "stop".
3. **4:4.** "possess his vessel". The KJV keeps an ambiguity that is in the Greek: Paul may mean controlling one's own body or taking a wife.
4. **5:14.** "comfort the feebleminded". Paul means the discouraged.

## Assessment

The KJV renders Paul's earliest letter accurately. The passage on the Lord's coming (4:13–18) reads the same as in a modern critical translation, apart from the archaic "prevent". The two most-quoted phrases that differ are "appearance of evil" and "pray without ceasing". The second is fine; the first has had an outsized influence on later teaching about conduct.
