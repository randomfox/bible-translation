# 1 Peter 1–5: summary

**Coverage:** 5 chapters, 105 verses. Same as the KJV.

**Flags:** 12 flags.
- By category: TEXT 6, LEXICAL 3, ARCHAIC 2, INTERP 1.
- By severity: all 12 at sev 1.

## Systematic

- **Textual base.** The TR differs from the earliest manuscripts in five places:
  - "the Lord God" for "Christ the Lord" (3:15). The early text applies Isaiah 8:13 to Christ.
  - "on this behalf" for "in this name" (4:16). The early reading points back to the name "Christian".
  - an added sentence in 4:14;
  - "into salvation" omitted at 2:2;
  - "Jesus. Amen" added at 5:14.
- **Imperial context.** In 2:13 and 2:17 the KJV renders *basileus* as "the king". In a letter to Roman provinces this means the emperor.

## Most important differences

1. **3:15.** The KJV has "sanctify the Lord God". The early text reads "honor Christ the Lord as holy", which is a high Christology drawn from Isaiah.
2. **3:21.** The KJV has "the answer of a good conscience". *Eperotema* means an appeal or pledge, possibly the baptismal vow.
3. **2:2.** The KJV has "the sincere milk of the word". The adjective means "spiritual". The early text adds "into salvation".
4. **5:3.** The KJV has "lords over God's heritage". The Greek refers to the congregations allotted to each elder. The word *kleros* is also the root of "clergy".

## Assessment

1 Peter is rendered faithfully. Its key passages read as in a modern critical translation: the living hope (1:3–9), the royal priesthood (2:9) and the suffering Christ (2:21–25). The difficult descent passage (3:18–20) is rendered accurately in both versions, and neither resolves it. Most flags here are small TR variants.
