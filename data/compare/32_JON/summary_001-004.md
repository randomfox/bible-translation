# Jonah 1–4: summary

**Coverage:** 4 chapters, 48 verses in Hebrew numbering. Heb 2:1 is KJV 1:17, and Heb 2:2–11 is KJV 2:1–10.

**Flags:** 2 flags, on 2 verses.
- By category: ARCHAIC 1, LEXICAL 1.
- By severity: both are severity 1.

## Systematic differences

- **Divine names:** the KJV renders YHWH as "LORD". The narrator alternates *YHWH*, *Elohim* and *YHWH Elohim*, and both versions keep this pattern.
- **"Great fish":** the Hebrew is *dag gadol*, and both versions translate it that way. The KJV says "whale" only in Matt 12:40, where the Greek has *ketos*.
- **Textual base:** the KJV follows the Masoretic Text throughout.

## Differences

1. **3:10 and 4:2**
   - KJV: "God repented of the evil".
   - Mine: "God relented of the disaster".
   - In 1611 the wording was accurate. Today it suggests that God had sinned.
2. **4:6–10**
   - KJV: "gourd".
   - Mine: "castor-oil plant".
   - The KJV follows the LXX here. The Hebrew *qiqayon* is probably the castor-oil plant.

## Assessment

Jonah is simple Hebrew narrative, and the KJV translates it with real fidelity, including the narrator's repetitions:
- "went down" in 1:3–5
- "hurled" in 1:4–15
- "appointed" in 2:1 and 4:6–8

Neither flag affects the story. The only point a modern reader is likely to misunderstand is the archaic phrasing of God "repenting of evil".
