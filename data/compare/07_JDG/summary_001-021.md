# Judges 1–21: summary

**Coverage:** 21 chapters, 618 verses.

**Flags:** 80 flags.
- By category: INTERP 29, LEXICAL 20, MEANING 13, ARCHAIC 11, NAME 6, TEXT 1.
- By severity: 68 at sev 1, 12 at sev 2.

## Systematic

- **The Song of Deborah (chapter 5).** This is among the oldest Hebrew poetry, and its text is very difficult. Many flags fall here (5:2, 5:5, 5:13, 5:26). In these places both versions are making educated guesses.
- **"Groves" for the Asherahs (3:7)** and **"tribute" for forced labour (1:28)**. As in the Pentateuch, these are technical terms that the KJV misread.

## Most important differences

1. **11:40.** "to lament the daughter of Jephthah". The verb means to recount or commemorate. The difference matters to readings of her fate.
2. **13:18.** "seeing it is secret". The angel's name is *pil'i*, "wonderful", from the same root as "Wonderful" in Isa 9:6.
3. **5:2.** "Praise ye the LORD for the avenging of Israel". The Hebrew probably means "when the leaders took the lead" (or "let their hair loose").
4. **5:26.** "she smote off his head". Jael crushed Sisera's head with the tent peg; she did not behead him.
5. **20:18.** "the house of God". This is the town Bethel, where the ark then stood.
6. **12:14.** "thirty nephews". In 1611 "nephew" could mean grandson.

## Assessment

The KJV's Judges is vivid and generally faithful. Its translators met the same obstacles every translator meets in the Song of Deborah. The flags concentrate there and in place names that the KJV translated as if they were common words (Bethel, Lehi).
