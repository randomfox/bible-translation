# 1 Kings 1–22: summary

**Coverage:** 22 chapters, 817 verses, counted by Hebrew numbering. The Hebrew 4:21–5:32 corresponds to KJV 4:21–5:18 (a two-verse shift between 4:21 and 5:1), and the Hebrew 22:44 is KJV 22:43b.

**Flags:** 78 flags.
- By category: LEXICAL 26, INTERP 16, MEANING 16, ARCHAIC 15, NAME 4, TEXT 1.
- By severity: 67 at sev 1, 11 at sev 2.

## Systematic

- **Cult vocabulary.** The terms for the Canaanite cult are regularly misrendered:
  - "groves" for *asherim*, which are cult poles of the goddess Asherah (14:15);
  - "sodomites" for *qedeshim*, which are male shrine functionaries, literally "consecrated ones" (14:24, 15:12, 22:47).

  These renderings came to shape readers' moral vocabulary.
- **"Of the lowest of the people" for *miqtsot ha'am* (12:31, 13:33).** The phrase means "from all the people". Jeroboam's fault was choosing priests who were not Levites, not choosing low-born ones.

## Most important differences

1. **19:12.** "a still small voice" for *qol demamah daqqah*. The Hebrew is an oxymoron, "a sound of thin silence". The KJV phrase is famous and expressive, but the Hebrew paradox is stranger than it.
2. **22:38.** "they washed his armour". The Hebrew says the prostitutes bathed there. The KJV follows a rabbinic reading.
3. **10:28.** "linen yarn". This is the place name Kue in Cilicia, known as a horse market.
4. **10:5.** "his ascent by which he went up". The phrase means the burnt offerings Solomon offered.
5. **22:10.** "in a void place". The Hebrew word means a threshing floor.

## Assessment

The narrative of the divided kingdom is rendered faithfully. The KJV's main weakness here is the cult vocabulary of the 1611 translators. "Groves" and "sodomites" carried later moral categories into texts about Canaanite religion.
