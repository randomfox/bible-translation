# Obadiah: summary

**Coverage:** 1 chapter, 21 verses. The verse numbering is the same in Hebrew and the KJV.

**Flags:** 2 flags on 2 verses.
- By category: LEXICAL 1, INTERP 1.
- By severity: both are severity 1.

## Systematic differences

- **Divine names.** The Hebrew *Adonai YHWH* appears in the KJV as "the Lord GOD", and YHWH appears as "the LORD".
- **Textual base.** Both versions follow the Masoretic Text.
- **Shared material with Jeremiah.** Verses 1–9 closely parallel Jer 49:7–22, and both versions translate the two passages consistently.

## Differences

1. **1:12–14**
   - **KJV:** "thou shouldest not have looked … in the day that he became a stranger".
   - **Mine:** "do not gloat … in the day of his misfortune".
   - **Why they differ:**
     - The verbs (*al* with a jussive) are prohibitions. The KJV's past-tense rebuke is a possible reading, but it changes the tone.
     - *nokhro* means "his calamity". It does not mean "his becoming a stranger".
2. **1:7**
   - **KJV:** "laid a wound under thee".
   - **Mine:** "set a trap".
   - **Why they differ:** in this context *mazor* means a snare.

## Assessment

The book is short and largely straightforward, and the KJV renders it faithfully. The two flagged points are minor. They concern the sense of an obscure noun and the mood of a run of prohibitions. Neither changes the oracle's meaning: Edom will be punished for its betrayal of Judah, and "the kingdom shall be the LORD's."
