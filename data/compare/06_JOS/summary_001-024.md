# Joshua 1–24: summary

**Coverage:** 24 chapters, 658 verses.

**Flags:** 50 flags.
- By category: INTERP 14, ARCHAIC 10, NAME 8, LEXICAL 8, MEANING 7, TEXT 3.
- By severity: 47 at sev 1, 3 at sev 2.

## Systematic

- **Place names.** The KJV transliterates them, sometimes translates them, and sometimes uses Latinate spellings. These account for the NAME flags.
- **"Accursed" for *cherem*.** The *cherem* is the ban: something devoted irrevocably to God, usually by destruction (6:17–18, 7:1). "Accursed" loses its sacral character.

## Most important differences

1. **6:17.** "the city shall be accursed". Jericho is *devoted* to Yahweh. This is the key concept of the conquest narrative.
2. **11:13.** "the cities that stood still in their strength". The Hebrew *'al-tillam* means "on their mounds", the tells on which earlier cities stood. It describes archaeology, not strength.
3. **24:2.** "on the other side of the flood". The Hebrew means "beyond the River", that is, the Euphrates.

## Assessment

Joshua is accurately rendered, with only three flags at sev 2. The book's moral difficulty, the ban on the Canaanite cities, is present in both versions. The KJV's "accursed" obscures that the ban was a form of sacral dedication, but it does not soften what was done.
