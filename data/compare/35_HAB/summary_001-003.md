# Habakkuk 1–3: summary

**Coverage:** 3 chapters, 56 verses. The Hebrew and KJV verse numbering agree.

**Flags:** 4 flags on 4 verses.
- By category: LEXICAL 3, INTERP 1.
- By severity: 1 flag at sev 2, 3 at sev 1.

## Systematic differences

- **Divine names:** the KJV renders YHWH as "LORD", as usual.
- **1:1:** *massa* is rendered "burden".
- **Music terms:** *Shigionoth* (3:1) and *Selah* are left untranslated in both versions.
- **1:12:** the Masoretic Text reads "we shall not die". Jewish tradition counts this as a scribal correction of an original "you shall not die". Both versions follow the Masoretic Text.

## Differences

1. **2:4**
   - KJV: "the just shall live by his faith".
   - Mine: "the righteous shall live by his faithfulness".
   - Note: *emunah* means steadfast fidelity. "By faith" is how Paul reads the verse (Rom 1:17, Gal 3:11), and that reading became the motto of the Reformation. The KJV's wording lines the prophet up with the New Testament reading.
2. **2:6**
   - KJV: "thick clay".
   - Mine: "pledges".
   - Note: the Hebrew is a single rare word that the KJV divided wrongly.
3. **3:4**
   - KJV: "horns coming out of his hand".
   - Mine: "rays flashed from his hand".
4. **3:5**
   - KJV: "burning coals".
   - Mine: "plague".
   - Note: *resheph* parallels "pestilence" in the same verse.

## Assessment

Habakkuk is rendered faithfully. The dialogue of chs. 1–2, the woes, and the great confession of 3:17–18 all carry over intact. The one significant point is 2:4. There the KJV's "faith" is a legitimate but theologically loaded choice. The Hebrew stresses faithful endurance rather than belief. The other flags are poetic imagery in the theophany of chapter 3, where the KJV took the literal sense of a word such as "horns" or "coals" and missed the figure.

*Caveat: the KJV was checked at selected verses only.*
