# 3 John: summary

**Coverage:** 1 chapter. The SBLGNT has 15 verses because it divides KJV 1:14 into two (1:14–15). The content is the same.

**Flags:** 2 flags. INTERP 1, TEXT 1. Both are severity 1.

## Differences

1. **1:2.** KJV: "I wish above all things that thou mayest prosper and be in health". *Peri panton* means "in all respects". The line is the conventional health-wish that opened Greek letters. It has nonetheless been used as a key text for the "prosperity gospel".
2. **1:7.** KJV: "for his name's sake". The early text reads "for the sake of the Name", which was an early Christian way of referring to Christ.

## Assessment

3 John is rendered accurately. Its one influential misreading, at 1:2, comes from an English idiom ("above all things"), not from the Greek.
