# Micah 1–7: summary

- **Coverage:** 7 chapters, 105 verses, in Hebrew numbering. Heb 4:14 = KJV 5:1, and Heb 5:1–14 = KJV 5:2–15.
- **Flags:** 5 flags on 5 verses.
  - By category: INTERP 3, LEXICAL 1, NAME 1.
  - By severity: 1 at sev 2, the rest at sev 1. None at sev 3.

## Systematic

- **Divine names:** YHWH is rendered "LORD", and Adonai YHWH is rendered "Lord GOD".
- **Recurring terms:**
  - "groves" stands for the Asherah poles (5:13).
  - "mercy" stands for *hesed* (6:8, 7:18, 7:20).
- **Textual base:** the Masoretic Text. Micah 4:1–3 is the same oracle as Isa 2:2–4, and both versions render it the same way in both places.
- **Wordplay in 1:10–15:** the lament puns on the names of Judean towns (Beth-leaphrah "house of dust", Shaphir "beauty", Achzib "deception"). Both versions keep the names, so the puns are lost in each.

## Differences

1. **5:1 (KJV 5:2)**
   - KJV: "whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting".
   - Mine: "whose origins are from of old, from ancient days".
   - *mime olam* refers to ancient times, as in 7:14. Matthew 2:6 quotes this verse. The KJV's "everlasting" supports reading it as divine pre-existence.
2. **2:12**
   - KJV: "the sheep of Bozrah".
   - Mine: "sheep in a fold".
   - *botsrah* here is the common noun for an enclosure, not the city.
3. **1:8**
   - KJV: "dragons … owls".
   - Mine: "jackals … ostriches".
4. **7:11**
   - KJV: "the decree be far removed".
   - Mine: "the boundary shall be far extended".
5. **7:12**
   - KJV: "the fortified cities … the fortress".
   - Mine: "Egypt".
   - *matsor* is a poetic name for Egypt, paired with Assyria.

## Assessment

Micah's KJV is faithful. The great texts come through unchanged:

- swords into plowshares (4:3)
- "what doth the LORD require of thee" (6:8)
- "Who is a God like unto thee" (7:18–19)

The one theologically weighted divergence is "from everlasting" in the Bethlehem oracle. There the Hebrew points to the ancient Davidic line rather than eternity. The KJV reading is a legitimate possibility, coloured by the verse's use in Matthew. The remaining flags are place-name and animal identifications.

*Caveat: the KJV was compared only at selected verses.*
