# 2 John: summary

**Coverage:** 1 chapter, 13 verses. Same as the KJV.

**Flags:** 3 flags. All are TEXT, at severity 1.

## Differences

1. **1:9.** The KJV reads "Whosoever transgresseth". The early text reads "Everyone who goes on ahead". The warning is aimed at teachers who claim to have moved beyond the teaching of Christ, not at transgressors in general.
2. **1:8.** The KJV reads "that we lose not … that we receive". The early text is in the second person: "you may not lose".
3. **1:3.** The KJV reads "with you … the Lord Jesus Christ". The early text reads "with us … Jesus Christ".

## Assessment

The KJV renders this short note faithfully. The three differences are small TR variants. Of these, only "goes on ahead" (1:9) changes what the warning is about.
