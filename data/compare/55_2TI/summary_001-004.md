# 2 Timothy 1–4: summary

**Coverage:** 4 chapters, 83 verses. Same as the KJV.

**Flags:** 7 flags.
- By category: TEXT 3, LEXICAL 3, ARCHAIC 1.
- By severity: all at sev 1.

## Systematic

- **Textual base.** Small TR additions: "of the Gentiles" (1:11), "Jesus Christ" and "Amen" (4:22), and "the Lord" (4:1).
- **Wording.** The flags are mainly 1611 words that modern readers misread: "study", "dividing", "sound mind", "canker", "reprobate".

## Most important differences

1. **2:15.** "Study to shew thyself approved … rightly dividing the word of truth". The verb means "make every effort", not "study". The participle means "handling correctly", not "dividing". Both words have shaped popular uses of the verse.
2. **1:7.** "a sound mind". *Sophronismos* means self-control. The verse is now often read as being about mental health.
3. **3:16.** "given by inspiration of God". This accurately renders *theopneustos* ("God-breathed"); the two versions agree.

## Assessment

Paul's last letter, his farewell (4:6–8) and the statement on Scripture (3:16–17) are faithfully rendered. Every flag is minor. The most influential one is 2:15, where the KJV's wording ("study", "dividing") gave a slogan to Bible study groups and to dispensationalist schemes. The Greek supports neither.
