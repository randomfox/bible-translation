# Philemon: summary

**Coverage:** 1 chapter, 25 verses. Same as the KJV.

**Flags:** 3 flags.
- By category: TEXT 2, ARCHAIC 1.
- By severity: all at sev 1.

## Differences

1. **1:7, 1:12, 1:20.** The KJV renders *splanchna* as "bowels". In 1611 that word meant the seat of the emotions. Today it reads as plain anatomy, so Paul's tender "my very heart" becomes comic.
2. **1:12.** The TR adds "receive him" to complete Paul's sentence, which is broken off in the Greek.
3. **1:25.** The TR adds "Amen".

## Assessment

The KJV renders this personal letter faithfully. That includes the wordplay on Onesimus' name: "unprofitable … profitable" (1:11). Apart from "bowels", a modern reader loses nothing.
