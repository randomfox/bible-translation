# Nehemiah 1–13: summary

**Coverage:** 13 chapters, 405 verses. The Hebrew numbering differs from the KJV in 3:33–4:17 (KJV 4:1–23) and 9:38–10:1.

**Flags:** 13 flags.
- By category: INTERP 5, TEXT 3, LEXICAL 3, NAME 2.
- By severity: all at sev 1.

## Differences

1. **7:67–68.** The Masoretic Text of Nehemiah lacks the horses-and-mules verse found in Ezra 2:66. The KJV supplies it as 7:68, following the parallel.
2. **4:17 (Heb 4:23).** The KJV has "saving that every one put them off for washing". This is a guess at an obscure line. The Hebrew probably means each man kept his weapon in his right hand.
3. **7:69 (Heb 7:69/70).** The KJV leaves "the Tirshatha" untranslated. It is the Persian title for the governor.
4. **8:15.** The KJV has "pine branches". The Hebrew means wild olive.

## Assessment

Nehemiah is one of the most accurately rendered books in the comparison. No flag is above severity 1, and the KJV's memoir voice matches the Hebrew closely.
