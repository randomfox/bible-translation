# Jude: summary

**Coverage:** 1 chapter, 25 verses. Same as the KJV.

**Flags:** 6 flags.
- By category: TEXT 4, INTERP 1, LEXICAL 1.
- By severity: 5 at sev 1, 1 at sev 2.

## Most important differences

1. **1:5 (sev 2).** "the Lord, having saved the people out of the land of Egypt". SBLGNT reads "Jesus", with Alexandrinus, Vaticanus and early versions. On that reading Jude names Jesus as the one who delivered Israel at the exodus. It is one of the strongest early statements of Christ's pre-existence in the NT. The TR has the easier word, "Lord".
2. **1:4.** "denying the only Lord God, and our Lord Jesus Christ". The TR adds "God", which makes two persons. The early text calls Jesus Christ "our only Master and Lord".
3. **1:25.** "To the only wise God our Saviour". The TR omits "through Jesus Christ our Lord" and "before all time".
4. **1:12.** "spots in your feasts of charity". The Greek word means hidden reefs, which wreck ships.
5. **1:22.** "making a difference". The Greek means "those who doubt".

## Assessment

Jude is short and its imagery is vivid. The KJV renders the denunciations well. The doxology (24–25) is one of the most beloved in the KJV, but its TR form omits Christ's mediation. As in Titus 2:13 and 2 Pet 1:1, the textual differences in Jude run against the usual assumption that the TR always strengthens Christology. Here the early text is the more explicit about Christ, at 1:4, 1:5 and 1:25.
