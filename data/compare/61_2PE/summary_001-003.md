# 2 Peter 1–3: summary

**Coverage:** 3 chapters, 61 verses. Same as the KJV.

**Flags:** 7 flags.
- By category: TEXT 3, INTERP 2, LEXICAL 2.
- By severity: 6 at sev 1, 1 at sev 2.

## Most important differences

1. **3:10 (sev 2).** KJV: "the earth also and the works that are therein shall be burned up".
   - The early text reads "will be found", meaning laid bare or exposed to judgment. The TR's "burned up" is an easier reading.
   - It turns judgment into annihilation of the earth, which shaped later ideas that the material world will be destroyed.
2. **1:1.** KJV: "God and our Saviour Jesus Christ".
   - The same construction appears in 1:11, 2:20 and 3:18. There the KJV renders it as one person ("Lord and Saviour").
   - In 1:1 it allows two, so the verse loses its most natural reading: Jesus called "our God and Savior".
3. **1:20.** KJV: "of any private interpretation".
   - The verse is about where prophecy comes from (1:21).
   - It is not about the reader's interpretation, though it has been used to argue against private Bible reading.
4. **2:4.** KJV: "hell" for *tartaroo*, a distinct Greek concept (Tartarus).
5. **3:12.** KJV: "hasting unto the coming".
   - Peter says believers "hasten", that is, speed, the coming of the day.

## Assessment

The KJV renders 2 Peter accurately on the whole. Two points carry weight:
- **3:10** is a textual issue that bears on cosmology.
- **1:1** is a translation inconsistency that bears on Christology.

On 1:1 the KJV is less explicit than the Greek, as in Titus 2:13.
