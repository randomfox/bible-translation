# John 1–21: summary

**Coverage:** 21 chapters and 878 SBLGNT verses. The KJV has 879. One KJV verse has no counterpart in the critical text: 5:4.

**Flags:** 48 flags.
- By category: TEXT 30, INTERP 9, LEXICAL 7, MEANING 1, ARCHAIC 1.
- By severity: 43 at sev 1, 4 at sev 2, 1 at sev 3.

## Systematic

- **Textual base.** John has fewer TR harmonisations than the Synoptics, because there is less parallel material to borrow from. The TR still:
  - assimilates names and titles to Matthew ("son of Jona" for "son of John" at 1:42 and 21:15–17; Peter's confession at 6:69),
  - adds explanatory phrases (3:13, 5:3b–4, 11:41, 16:16), and
  - prefers easier readings where the early papyri (P66, P75) are harder (1:18, 7:8, 9:35).
- **Recurring terms.** Not flagged per verse: "Holy Ghost"; "devil(s)". Flagged once and applied throughout:
  - "only begotten" for *monogenes* (1:14), which means "only, unique".
  - "Comforter" for *parakletos* (14:16), which means "Advocate, helper".
- **"The Jews".** Both versions render *hoi Ioudaioi* literally. In John it often means the Judean authorities, not the Jewish people; neither version marks this.

## Most important differences

1. **7:53–8:11 (sev 3).** The woman taken in adultery. Absent from P66, P75, Sinaiticus, Vaticanus and the early versions, and placed at different points in later manuscripts. SBLGNT double-brackets it; the KJV prints it as part of John.
2. **1:18 (sev 2).** KJV "the only begotten Son" against "the only God" (*monogenes theos*) in P66, P75, Sinaiticus and Vaticanus. The early text calls the Son "God" a second time in the prologue, so the KJV here is the weaker witness to John's Christology.
3. **5:4 (sev 2).** The angel stirring the pool. A later explanation of 5:7, absent from the earliest manuscripts.
4. **6:69 (sev 2).** KJV "that Christ, the Son of the living God" (from Mt 16:16) against "the Holy One of God".
5. **10:16 (sev 2).** KJV "one fold, and one shepherd" against "one flock". The Greek uses two different words, *aule* (fold) and *poimne* (flock); the KJV, following the Vulgate, merges them.
6. **3:3 and 1:5.** "Born again" for *anothen* ("from above" as well as "again"), and "comprehended it not" for *katelaben* ("did not overcome it").
7. **9:35.** KJV "Son of God" against "Son of Man", a confessional title replacing John's.
8. **20:17.** "Touch me not" against "Do not cling to me", a present imperative meaning "stop holding on".

## Assessment

John's KJV is one of its finest achievements, and the comparison shows why: the Greek is simple and the KJV follows it closely. Only 18 of the 48 flags concern translation, and most are word choices whose 1611 sense has shifted ("mansions", "firkins", "reprove", "purgeth"). The textual differences are fewer than in the Synoptics but include the single largest passage in the New Testament absent from the earliest manuscripts (7:53–8:11), and a reading at 1:18 where the TR is the less exalted Christology, not the more. Readers who assume the KJV's base text always "adds" doctrine should note that case.

*Caveat: TR differences were checked at known variant sites and selected verses.*
