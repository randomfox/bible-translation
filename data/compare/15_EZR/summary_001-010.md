# Ezra 1–10: summary

**Coverage:** 10 chapters, 280 verses. Ezra 4:8–6:18 and 7:12–26 are in Aramaic; both versions translate them like the Hebrew.

**Flags:** 15 flags.
- By category: NAME 4, LEXICAL 4, MEANING 3, INTERP 2, TEXT 1, ARCHAIC 1.
- By severity: 14 at sev 1, 1 at sev 2.

## Most important difference

**10:15.** The KJV has "were employed about this matter". The Hebrew *'amdu 'al-zot* most likely means the four men "opposed this", that is, the dissolution of the mixed marriages. The KJV makes them its supporters. This removes the only recorded dissent from Ezra's policy.

## Assessment

Ezra's lists and documents are accurately rendered. Most of the flags concern names and Persian administrative terms. The single substantive point, 10:15, matters to how readers judge the book's harshest episode.
