# Zephaniah 1–3: summary

**Coverage:** 3 chapters, 53 verses. The Hebrew and KJV verse numbering are the same.

**Flags:** 2 flags on 2 verses.
- By category: LEXICAL 1, INTERP 1.
- By severity: both are severity 1.

## Systematic

- **Divine names:** YHWH is rendered "LORD", and YHWH tseva'ot is rendered "LORD of hosts".
- **Proper names:** the KJV transliterates *kemarim* (1:4, idolatrous priests) as "Chemarims" and *malkam* (1:5) as "Malcham". Neither is flagged; both are acceptable renderings of a name.
- **Textual base:** both versions follow the Masoretic Text. I had emended 3:17 in my first draft and corrected it back to the MT.

## Differences

1. **2:14**
   - KJV: "the cormorant and the bittern".
   - Mine: "the owl and the hedgehog".
   - The KJV puts water birds in a dry ruin.
2. **2:1**
   - KJV: "O nation not desired".
   - Mine: "O shameless nation".
   - The Hebrew verb here is obscure, so either reading is a guess.

## Assessment

This is one of the cleanest books in the comparison. The KJV renders Zephaniah's Day-of-Yahweh poem and its closing song faithfully. The two most memorable passages come through unchanged:
- 1:15, the *dies irae* that inspired the medieval hymn.
- 3:17, "he will rejoice over thee with joy … he will joy over thee with singing".

The only points of difference are the identity of two animals and one rare verb.

*Caveat: I compared the KJV at selected verses only.*
