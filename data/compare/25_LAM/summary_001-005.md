## Systematic differences (not flagged per verse)

- LORD for YHWH.
- "Bowels" for *me'ayim*, the inner parts as the seat of emotion.
- The KJV, like most English versions, does not reproduce the alphabetic acrostics of chs. 1–4. Neither does this translation.

## Most important differences

1. **3:22: "It is of the LORD's mercies that we are not consumed"** follows the MT. The Targum and Syriac read "The steadfast love of Yahweh never ceases", which parallels v. 22b.
2. **5:22:** the KJV ends the book with "But thou hast utterly rejected us". The Hebrew *ki 'im*, "unless", is more naturally a fearful condition on the prayer of v. 21.
3. **4:3: "sea monsters"** (ketiv) should be "jackals" (qere).
4. **4:7: "Nazarites"** may be "princes".
5. **2:20: "children of a span long"** should be "children they have cherished".
6. **3:65: "sorrow of heart"** should be "dullness (covering) of heart".

## Assessment

The KJV Lamentations is a close and moving rendering. It has only a handful of divergences, all minor. The most notable is the tone of the final verse, which the KJV makes despairing rather than conditional.
