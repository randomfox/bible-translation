# Zechariah 1–8 — summary

**Coverage:** 8 chapters and 121 verses, counted by the Hebrew numbering. Heb 2:1–4 corresponds to KJV 1:18–21, and Heb 2:5–17 to KJV 2:1–13.

**Flags:** 4 flags on 4 verses.
- By category: LEXICAL 2, INTERP 1, NAME 1.
- By severity: all four are severity 1.

## Systematic

- **Divine name.** *YHWH tseva'ot* appears over 40 times in these chapters. The KJV renders it "the LORD of hosts".
- **Heavenly figures.** The KJV uses "angel" for *mal'akh* in the night visions, and so does my translation.
- **Messianic titles.** "My servant the Branch" (3:8, 6:12) is a messianic title in both versions. I found no difference between them here.
- **Textual base.** Both versions follow the Masoretic Text. At 5:6 the MT has *eynam*, "their appearance". Both keep it rather than the LXX's "their iniquity".

## Differences

1. **3:1–2**
   - KJV: "Satan standing at his right hand to resist him".
   - Mine: "the Accuser … to accuse him".
   - The Hebrew *hassatan* has the article, so it names a role in the heavenly court, as in Job. Its pun with *lesitno* ("to accuse him") is lost in the KJV.
2. **7:2**
   - KJV: "sent unto the house of God".
   - Mine: "the people of Bethel had sent".
   - The KJV turns the town that sent the delegation into the place the delegation went to.
3. **1:8**
   - KJV: "speckled".
   - Mine: "sorrel".
4. **2:3** (KJV 1:20)
   - KJV: "carpenters".
   - Mine: "craftsmen".

## Assessment

The night visions are rendered accurately. These are the horsemen, the horns and craftsmen, the measuring line, Joshua's investiture, the lampstand and olive trees, the flying scroll, the woman in the ephah, and the chariots. So are the sermons on fasting in chs. 7–8. The famous lines come through intact: "Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit" (4:6), "who hath despised the day of small things?" (4:10), and the ten men holding the skirt of a Jew (8:23). The only difference with some theological weight is the KJV's "Satan" at 3:1, which makes the courtroom accuser a proper name. Together with summary_009-014 (chs. 9–14), this completes Zechariah.
