## Systematic differences

- **Divine titles:** the KJV has "the LORD God of hosts" where this translation has "Yahweh, the God of Armies", and "the Lord GOD" for "the Lord Yahweh".
- **Recurring formula:** "I will not revoke it" (*lo ashivennu*) is rendered by the KJV as "I will not turn away [the punishment] thereof". The KJV's italic supply matches the sense, so this is not flagged.
- **Textual base:** both versions follow the Masoretic Text. Where the New Testament quotes Amos from the LXX, the KJV Old Testament keeps the Hebrew except at 5:26. The two NT quotations are:
  - 5:25–27 in Acts 7:42–43
  - 9:11–12 in Acts 15:16–17, where the LXX reads "remnant of men" and the MT "remnant of Edom"

## Most important differences

1. **5:26**
   - KJV: "the tabernacle of your Moloch and Chiun".
   - Mine: "Sikkuth your king, and Kiyyun your star-god".
   - These are the Assyrian astral gods Sakkut and Kaiwan (Saturn). The KJV mixes in the LXX "Moloch" as quoted in Acts.
2. **3:6**
   - KJV: "shall there be evil in a city, and the LORD hath not done it?"
   - Mine: "disaster".
   - The word means calamity, not moral evil.
3. **5:9**
   - KJV: "strengtheneth the spoiled against the strong".
   - Mine: "makes destruction flash forth against the strong".
4. **6:13**
   - KJV: "a thing of nought … horns".
   - Mine: "Lo-debar … Karnaim".
   - These are puns on real towns that the KJV translates away.
5. **8:14**
   - KJV: "the sin of Samaria".
   - Mine: "Ashimah of Samaria".
   - Ashimah is a goddess whose name puns on "guilt".
6. **3:12**
   - KJV: "in Damascus in a couch".
   - Mine: "part of a bed".
7. **9:6**
   - KJV: "his troop in the earth".
   - Mine: "his vault upon the earth".

## Assessment

Amos is vigorous, mostly clear Hebrew, and the KJV is faithful to it. The well-known lines come through with their meaning intact:

- "Can two walk together, except they be agreed?" (3:3)
- "prepare to meet thy God" (4:12)
- "let judgment run down as waters" (5:24)
- "I was no prophet" (7:14)
- "a famine … of hearing the words of the LORD" (8:11)

The flags are mostly about the KJV translating proper names as common words, or the reverse: Lo-debar, Ashimah, Damascus, Harmon. They also include a few rare architectural and cosmological terms. Only 5:26, a textual mixture with theological colour, rises to severity 2.

**Caveat:** the KJV was checked only at selected verses.
