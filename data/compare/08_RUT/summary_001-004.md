## Most important difference

**4:5 (sev 2).** "thou must buy it also of Ruth the Moabitess". The verse turns on a written/read variant (ketiv/qere) in the Masoretic Text. The KJV's "buy it of Ruth" follows one reading. The other makes Ruth herself part of the redemption: "you also acquire Ruth". That second reading is what makes the near kinsman refuse.

## Assessment

Ruth is short and its prose is clear. The KJV renders it faithfully, including the loyalty speech "whither thou goest, I will go" (1:16–17), which is accurate. The one textual point, at 4:5, concerns the legal mechanism of the story.
