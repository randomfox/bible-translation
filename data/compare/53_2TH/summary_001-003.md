# 2 Thessalonians 1–3: summary

**Coverage:** 3 chapters, 47 verses. Same as the KJV.

**Flags:** 7 flags.
- By category: TEXT 4, MEANING 1, ARCHAIC 1, INTERP 1.
- By severity: all 7 at sev 1.

## Systematic

- **Textual base.** Small TR differences:
  - "Christ" added (1:8).
  - "man of sin" for "man of lawlessness" (2:3).
  - "from the beginning" for "as firstfruits" (2:13). The two readings differ by only one letter division.
- **Changed English.** "Letteth" (2:7) meant "hinders" in 1611. Today it reads as "allows", which reverses the sense.

## Most important differences

1. **2:2.** KJV: "the day of Christ is at hand". The Thessalonians were being told the day of the Lord *had already come*; that is the error Paul corrects. "At hand" says something close to the opposite and undermines the point of the chapter.
2. **2:7.** KJV: "he who now letteth will let". This means "he who now restrains". A modern reader hears "allows".
3. **2:3.** KJV: "man of sin". The critical text reads "man of lawlessness", which ties the figure to "the mystery of lawlessness" (2:7) and "the lawless one" (2:8).
4. **3:5.** KJV: "patient waiting for Christ". The Greek refers to Christ's own steadfastness.

## Assessment

The eschatological chapter, 2:1–12, is where the KJV most affects readers here. One verb tense in 2:2 and one archaic word in 2:7 combine to blur Paul's argument against those who said the day had already come. The rest of the letter is rendered accurately.
