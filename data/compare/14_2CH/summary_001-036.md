# 2 Chronicles 1–36: summary

**Coverage:** 36 chapters and 822 verses. Verse numbers follow the Hebrew text, which differs from the KJV at 1:18–2:17 and 13:23–14:14.

**Flags:** 53 flags.
- By category: INTERP 16, TEXT 14, LEXICAL 9, MEANING 8, ARCHAIC 4, NAME 2.
- By severity: 47 at sev 1, 6 at sev 2.

## Systematic

- **Textual problems.** Chronicles has many numerical and textual problems, often visible against the parallels in Kings. That is why the TEXT count (14) is high. Both versions mostly follow the Masoretic Text.
- **Recurring terms.** As in Kings:
  - "Linen yarn" stands for the place name Kue (1:16).
  - "Tribute" stands for forced labour (8:8).
  - "Devils" stands for goat-demons (11:15).
  - "College" stands for the Second Quarter of Jerusalem (34:22).

## Most important differences

1. **22:2.** "Forty and two years old was Ahaziah". This would make Ahaziah older than his father (21:20). The parallel at 2 Kgs 8:26 reads 22. Both versions keep the Masoretic number; this translation marks the problem.
2. **33:11.** "took Manasseh among the thorns". The Hebrew means hooks, the hooks put through captives' noses, as shown in Assyrian reliefs.
3. **8:8.** "pay tribute". This is forced labour; 8:9 contrasts it with slavery.
4. **11:15.** "devils" for the *se'irim*, which are goat-demons.

## Assessment

The KJV's 2 Chronicles is faithful to the Masoretic Text, including its errors. The flags are mostly technical. None has the theological weight of the parallel issues in 2 Samuel.
