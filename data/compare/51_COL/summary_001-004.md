# Colossians 1–4: summary

**Coverage:** 4 chapters, 95 verses. Same as the KJV.

**Flags:** 11 flags.
- By category: TEXT 8, LEXICAL 2, INTERP 1.
- By severity: all 11 at sev 1.

## Systematic

- **Textual base.** Most flags are TR additions or substitutions:
  - phrases borrowed from Ephesians ("through his blood" at 1:14; "and the Lord Jesus Christ" at 1:2)
  - "God" for "Christ" (3:15)
  - "of the sins" (2:11)
  - a conflated title for "the mystery of God" (2:2)
- **Most consequential.** At 2:18 the TR adds the word "not", turning "what he has seen" (the false teacher's visions) into "things which he hath not seen". This reverses the sense.

## Most important differences

1. **2:18.** "intruding into those things which he hath not seen". One added *me* ("not") reverses Paul's description of the false teacher, who boasts of his visions.
2. **1:15.** "firstborn of every creature" can suggest that Christ is himself a creature, the Arian reading. "Firstborn of all creation" is a title of rank, and the next verse explains it: "by him were all things created".
3. **4:15.** "Nymphas … his house". The best manuscripts read Nympha, a woman hosting a house church.
4. **1:14.** "through his blood" is imported from Eph 1:7.
5. **2:2.** "of God, and of the Father, and of Christ" is a TR conflation. The early reading is "God's mystery, Christ".

## Assessment

The KJV renders the Christ hymn (1:15–20) and the "fullness of the Godhead bodily" (2:9) accurately and memorably. Every flag in Colossians is minor. They are textual for the most part, plus a few archaic words ("concupiscence", "sister's son"). The one reversal of meaning, at 2:18, comes from a single word in the TR, not from the translators.
