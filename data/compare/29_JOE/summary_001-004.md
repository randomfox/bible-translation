# Joel 1–4 — summary

**Coverage:** 4 chapters, 73 verses, in Hebrew numbering.
- Heb 3:1–5 = KJV 2:28–32.
- Heb 4 = KJV 3.

**Flags:** 3 flags, on 3 verses.
- By category: LEXICAL 1, MEANING 1, INTERP 1.
- By severity: all three are sev 1.

## Systematic

- **Divine name:** YHWH is rendered "LORD" throughout. The KJV "the LORD" stands where my text has "Yahweh", including the much-quoted 3:5 (KJV 2:32): "whosoever shall call on the name of the LORD shall be delivered".
- **"Spirit":** "my Spirit" in 3:1–2 is the same in both versions.
- **Textual base:** the Masoretic Text, in both versions. In my first draft of 4:21 I had used the LXX reading "avenge"; I corrected it back to the MT, so the two versions now follow the same text.
- **Peter's citation:** Acts 2:17–21 quotes 3:1–5. Both translations are consistent with that use.

## Most important differences

1. **1:4 and 2:25 (the locusts)**
   - KJV: "palmerworm, locust, cankerworm, caterpiller".
   - Mine: four kinds or stages of locust.
   - All four Hebrew words are locust terms. The KJV's "worms" and "caterpillar" suggest a plague of different pests.
2. **2:23**
   - KJV: "the former rain moderately".
   - Mine: "the early rain for your vindication".
   - *litsdaqah* means "in righteousness / for vindication". Some read it as "the teacher of righteousness". It does not mean "moderately".
3. **4:21** (KJV 3:21)
   - KJV: "cleanse their blood".
   - Mine: "declare innocent their blood".
   - *niqqah* is a legal verb meaning "acquit". The KJV's "cleanse" leaves the line opaque.

## Assessment

Joel is short and comparatively clear Hebrew, and the KJV renders it faithfully: there are no sev 2 or 3 flags. The famous passages are rendered without substantive difference:
- "rend your heart, and not your garments" (2:13)
- the outpouring of the Spirit (3:1–2)
- "beat your plowshares into swords" (4:10)
- "the valley of decision" (4:14)

The only reader-facing problems are the 1611 insect names and one mistranslated adverb ("moderately").

**Caveat:** the KJV was compared at selected verses, not read line by line.
