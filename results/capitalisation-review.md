# Capitalised pronouns: notes and places to review

In November 2026 the translation began capitalising pronouns that refer to God, Jesus or the Holy Spirit: he, him, his, himself, you, your, yours, yourself, me, my, mine, myself, who, whom and whose. 7,150 verses changed (commit `9ce85c4`). The rules are in [INSTRUCTIONS.md](../INSTRUCTIONS.md) and [README.md](../README.md).

Each chapter was read for who each pronoun refers to. Where the referent was unclear, the pronoun was left lowercase. The places below were judgement calls. They are worth a second look, and any of them can be changed.

References use the translation's own numbering: Hebrew numbering in the Old Testament (Psalm titles are verse 1) and SBL Greek numbering in the New.

## How the rules were applied

- **God:** Yahweh, God, the Father, the Most High and similar names and titles, including when God speaks ("My statutes", "before Me").
- **Jesus:** throughout the New Testament, including before the resurrection and when others speak to or about Him.
- **The Holy Spirit:** He and Him. "It" stays lowercase where the translation writes "it" for the Spirit, or for "spirit" in the Old Testament.
- **The angel of Yahweh:** capitalised only where the text identifies him with God.
- **Old Testament prophecy:** the coming figure's pronouns are capitalised only in passages the New Testament applies to Jesus: Gen 3:15, Gen 49:10, Num 24:17, Deut 18:15–19, Ps 2, Ps 16:8–11, Ps 22, Ps 40:6–8, Ps 45:2–7, Ps 110, Ps 118:22–26, Isa 7:14, Isa 9:6–7, Isa 11:1–10, Isa 42:1–4, Isa 49:1–7, Isa 50:4–9, Isa 52:13–53:12, Isa 61:1–3, Jer 23:5–6, Jer 33:15–16, Dan 7:13–14, Mic 5:2–5, Zech 9:9–10, Zech 12:10, Zech 13:7 and Mal 3:1.
- **who / whom / whose:** capitalised when the word itself refers to God or Jesus, as in "the God Who answers", "Jesus, Who". Lowercase when it refers to a person, as in "the servant of Yahweh, who addressed", and in questions ("Who is like You?").
- **Genesis 49:10** was reworded at the same time: "until the One to Whom it belongs comes, and the obedience of the peoples is His."

## Inconsistencies aligned

Four places where one passage was capitalised and a closely linked one was not have been aligned (commit after `807f906`):

1. **Isa 7:15:** "when He knows how to refuse the evil", following Isa 7:14.
2. **Mic 5:5** (Hebrew numbering, KJV 5:6): "and He shall deliver us", continuing the ruler of Mic 5:1–4.
3. **Ezek 21:32:** "until He comes, the One to Whom judgment belongs, and I will give it to Him", matching Gen 49:10, which it echoes.
4. **Parable figures:** a figure is capitalised where the context itself identifies it as Jesus. So the master of the house in Luke 13:25–27 ("You taught in our streets") and Matt 10:25, the shepherd in John 10:2–4 ("I am the good shepherd", 10:11), and the bridegroom in John 3:29 ("He must increase", 3:30) are capitalised. The thief in John 10:1, the stranger in 10:5, and parable characters the context does not identify with Jesus, such as the bridegroom of Matt 25 or the son in the vineyard parable, stay lowercase.
